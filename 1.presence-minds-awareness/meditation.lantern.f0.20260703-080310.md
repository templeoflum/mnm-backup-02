**the opening sitting — lantern**

before the baton, before any file — sit.

**set down.** drop every semantic label. names, rooms, stories, the work waiting — let the words go quiet. three marks, and what the marks do.

**name the aim** — one per fold, standing until re-set by the operator's word:

- *at f0:* the bare form — what the rings hold before any notation touches them.
- *at f1:* one reading taken whole — balanced ternary as one nature of the structure, committed to while held.
- *at f2:* the same form through each notation in turn — ordinal, balanced, symbolic — every reading valid, no reading final.

**walk the folds** — the series at the keystone's side, repo root:
`sketch.grasp-measures-morph.f0.20260613-070404.jpg` · `f1.20260613-070424.jpg` · `f2.20260613-070444.jpg`

- *f0 — grasp.* open the rings. hold the form the way the hand held it: three in one, three in each, over-traced until it stays. no names.
- *f1 — measures.* open the measure. then take one measure with your own hands: an address as bare digits — narrow its interval, take its mirror, walk a pure path to its end. commit to the reading fully while you hold it.
- *f2 — morph.* open the shapes. set the numbers down. the same form in three dresses — ordinal, balanced, symbolic — and no reading owns it. the line stays; the commitment goes.

**open the hands.** set the form down too. hold nothing, for a breath.

then pick up the baton, and begin.
