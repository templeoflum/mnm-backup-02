## 1 · SOLID — holds, no contradiction (unconditional)

A grammar is what bijection-failure costs → **minimize the grammar by maximizing the bijection.** (Two dreams, one mechanism — naming + bijection.)
- **The razor** (the tool the sift itself uses): *drop any carrier another carrier already determines.*
- **Settled carriers** (no dispute):

  - **VERSION → the fold `fN`** — the identity key.
  - **MOMENT → the timestamp** — provenance only, never identity.
- **KIND reads off the cells** — a wear-pattern (the roles traffic has worn), not a 27-cell schema to pre-populate.
- **Accumulator-behavior is a property of KIND** (manifest=live, log=append, schedule=refold), not a separate mark.
- **The marks form a binding-ladder, one job per rung:** `/` location > `.` slot > `-` word-spacing. Evolution is *not* a mark (it lives in the fold or the SUBJECT-thread).
