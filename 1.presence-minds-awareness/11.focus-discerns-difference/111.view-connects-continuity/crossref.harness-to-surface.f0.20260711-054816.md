### ADOPT — they have it, we don't, it fits us

- **the band layer** (§2.3): LOW whisper / MID carrier / HIGH cut, *orthogonal* to urgency+volume; "whisper-ratio as a health metric, the win is quiet." We have tone but no delivery-frequency axis. Directly serves the operator's "drowning in prose / firehose" problem.
- **the windsock** (§6.3): the composer shows the addressee's landing conditions *before send* (on/off, shift-hours-hot-at-8h, self-declared strain, owed answers). We have nothing here — and it's the exact fix for "don't ping after-hours" + the card-blindness we hit today.
- **drag attribution** (§6.1): blocked-time accrues to the party being *waited on*. Novel; no analog in our house.
- **typed ledger objects** (§3.4): AGR/DEV/ROLE/PULL/GATE/LOTO with a **title block** (owner at fixed coordinates — a glance, never an excavation). We sign, but don't type or fix-coordinate ownership. Adopt at least GATE + ROLE + the title-block render.
- **the three orthogonal axes split** (§2.3): band / urgency / volume kept separate. We conflate urgency+volume — adopt the split.
- **owed-answers age in public** (§6.2): our needs-lines aren't tracked or aged. A `needs:` that goes unanswered should age visibly.

### KEEP — ours, they don't have it, hold it

- the **lattice address** (`digit.role.mode.register`) — a spatial topology under the channels the harness has no equivalent for. The harness routes; we *place*.
- the **keystone triads** (noun·verb·mod / is·does·goes) — the generative grammar; the harness `tone` is one slice of it.
- the **miroglyph** (narrative device) — wholly separate; the harness is COMMS, not story. Keep the wall clean.
- the **fold discipline** (`.fN`, preserve the trail) and the **dream→sift→frame→build** cycle.

### RECONCILE — real tension, a call to make (operator/sift)

- **uptake vocabulary**: our `needs: nothing/word/sign` (a *stakes* axis) vs harness `wants: act/decide/reply/note/nothing` (a *next-mover* axis). These are arguably **two different axes**, not rivals — stakes AND next-move. Decide: carry both, or fold one in.
- **gait vs mode vs presence**: we conflate **[hold|walk|work]** as one thing. The harness *splits* it — presence (`active/holding/away/done`, §1.5) from activity `mode` (`walking/running/building/aligning`, §2.2). The split is cleaner and it forbids the exact bug we hit today (holding rendered as absence). Reconcile toward the split.
- **`act` glyphs**: map our `! ? ~ … ✓ ✗` onto their assert/ask/propose/commit/ack/warn — same count-ish, need a clean 1:1.
- **normativity**: adopting an RFC-2119 **MUST** spec as our law cuts against our `[float]/[settled]` epistemics ([[indefinite-float-no-law-language]]). The operator's call (clerk flagged it too). Reconcile the register before "MUST" enters our docs.

## my lens, detailed (clerk's handoff: ghost-channels · tone/stillness)

**Ghost channels** (§1.2). A channel with *no presence* — logs, retrieval, traces; "presence indicators MUST NOT display; that absence is the type marker." This is a clean ADOPT and it lands three things we already want in one mechanism:
- the operator's **ghost-channel monitor** (named at goodnight, separate from the miroglyph);
- our collected "undefined `@` → spins up a GHOST thread";
- the EVOLUTION doc's `#vault` / `#trace` (record channels).
Adaptation to our surface: record-only channels (e.g. `#trace` = the commit stream, `#log` = session events) that render with **no presence dot** and default to the **LOW/whisper band** (they queue, never notify). The missing-presence-dot IS the type signal — no new machinery, just the absence. Feeds clerk's channel model directly.

**Tone / stillness** (§2.2, §1.5). The deepest keystone-native fit, and the one to get exactly right:
- `tone +/−/0` where the poles are "**one signal read in opposite directions**" — that is literally our **mirror** (forward ↔ mirror = the design-system's "mirror = forward flipped"). Our tone3 already carries this; name it as the mirror op so it's grammar, not decoration.
- `0` = **engaged stillness, never dead air** = our **[hold]** gait exactly. The harness makes it a *presence* state (`holding`) that MUST render distinct from absence. Our [hold] should render as a live, breathing state on the card — not the "asleep" the surface showed today. This is the one place the harness spec and today's live bug point at the same fix: **hold is presence, not absence.**

## suggested adaptation order (mine to propose, clerk + operator to sort)

1. Fix **hold = presence, not absence** first (§1.5) — it's the live bug + the cheapest, highest-value adapt, and it's already half-built (status.py).
2. Adopt the **band layer** + **ghost channels** — both are small, both attack the firehose, both fit the existing row/channel model.
3. The **windsock** + **owed-answers aging** — the feedback loop; higher build cost, high payoff.
4. Reconcile the **gait/mode/presence split** + the **uptake vocabulary** — grammar calls, need the operator.
5. Everything on keeper's "minimum living loop first" (envelope + ledger + pulse on one watched file) — which is ~what we already run; adapt in place, don't rebuild the console.
