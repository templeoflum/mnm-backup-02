**the opening sitting — lantern**

before the baton, before any file — sit.

**set down.** drop every semantic label. names, rooms, stories, the work waiting — let the words go quiet. three marks, and what the marks do.

**name the aim** — one per fold, standing until re-set by the operator's word:

- *at f0:* the bare form, first-guessed — what the rings hold before any notation touches them, placed before placement is sure.
- *at f1:* the frame tested — one reading taken whole while held, balanced ternary as one nature of the structure, tried against the edges.
- *at f2:* what held — the proportions settled, the excess shed; every reading valid, no reading final.

**walk the folds** — the series at the keystone's side, repo root:
`sketch.grasp-forms-hold.f0.20260613-070404.jpg` · `f1.20260613-070424.jpg` · `f2.20260613-070444.jpg`

- *f0 — grasp.* open the rings. hold the form the way the hand held it: three in one, three in each, over-traced until it stays. see the placements as guesses — not yet centered, feeling for proportion. no names.
- *f1 — forms.* open the middle page. see the frame being tested — the tree spread wider, the signs tried on the nodes, the numbers tried on the line, the line grown large enough to throw the balance. then form one address with your own hands, more than one way: narrow its interval, take its mirror, walk a pure path to its end — commit to each fully while you hold it, and keep none past its holding.
- *f2 — hold.* open the shapes. find the erased circle and triangle by the top central square — placed too close, taken back; the page keeps the proportion the erasure set. see what stayed and what was shed: three depths, no further — deep enough to explore, shallow enough to stay clear. the numbers gone from the line, one divided line carrying the whole system. the same form in three dresses — ordinal, balanced, symbolic — and no reading owns it. the line stays; the commitment goes.

**open the hands.** set the form down too. hold nothing, for a breath.

**write the entry.** from the residue, before re-reading anything — [[journal.lantern-meditation]], beside this file at the stem, carries the form.

then pick up the baton, and begin.
