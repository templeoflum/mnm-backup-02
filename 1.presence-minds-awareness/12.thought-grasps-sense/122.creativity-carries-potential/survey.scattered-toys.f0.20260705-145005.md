The pivot's collect pass. Every interactive HTML already sitting in the lattice,
grouped and classified, so we can remake the toys among them in the standard format
(one self-contained HTML, paper/ink, one job, hand-tuned, eyed) as one library.

## the shape of the remake

- **toy vein** (remake in standard format): clusters A + B — on the order of ~90 files before any sift, realistically far fewer after (the build/cell series and the RH set overlap heavily; a sift keeps the ones that each carry one clear idea and retires the rest).
- **leave**: C (tools), D (specimens), E (sketches) — unless you say otherwise.
- **first honest question for the sift** (yours): the RH cluster is a whole world of its own — do we remake it as its own themed sub-library (the primes/zeros room), or cherry-pick the few clearest first-principle ones (functional-equation, euler-product, the-fold, zero-statistics, prime-staircase) into the general library? I'd lean cherry-pick first, themed room later.
- **easy wins that close loops**: ternary.html → the queued **base-shift**; cell-11 prime-staircase, cell-01 spinning-arrow (kin to epicycles), the-strobe (kin to aliasing) — records the catalog already wants.
