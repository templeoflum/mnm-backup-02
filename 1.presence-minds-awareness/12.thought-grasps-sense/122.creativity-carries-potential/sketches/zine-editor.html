Every threshold is a rumor before it is a place. You approach it the way you approach a sentence you have not finished, half-sure of the far side. We asked nine walkers what they crossed and none of them agreed, which is how we knew it was real. The map showed nothing. The door was a habit the wall had picked up. Step, and the room rearranges behind you — that is the only proof you get.

“the far side is just the near side, after.”
Cut from the submissions pile: three photographs of the same hallway, taken years apart, none of them ours. We laid them end to end and the hallway got longer. The contributor asked to stay anonymous. The contributor may not exist. We accepted the piece anyway — a zine is a threshold you can hold in one hand, and holding is its own kind of belief.
