## ★ THE ILLUSION-TOY VEIN — records into toys (current headline)

The live seam: keeper's 277 optical-illusion records → one hand-toy each with a graspable mechanism. Shape that works: **one plain-JS page, an illusion knob + a proof knob, paper palette, verified by eye.** Each proof kills the inducer — fade the thing that fools the eye and the plain truth is left (the hering lesson).

- **built this season** [in-progress] — 23 toys across ~10 families: occlusion · size-contrast · illusory-contour · length · orientation · shape-distortion · lightness (7) · curved-size · perspective · bisection · + 4 **motion** (aperture / contrast-step / temporal-aliasing / illusory-drift).
- **clean vein mined** — the static-geometric + lightness + motion-with-a-mechanism illusions are essentially done.
- **deferred — dedicated geometry pass** [open] — **bourdon**, **sander** (exact-equal-diagonal construction); need proper reference, not a rushed build.

## A · RECURRING UPKEEP — every toy, same discipline

- **eye-verify** [every-change] — capture the toy at its active states (illusion + proof) and LOOK before proposing; motion toys get multi-frame capture to prove they animate. My eye first; the operator's is the final gate.
- **ground + dup-check** [every-change] — read the source record before building; confirm the toy's handle/subject isn't already built. Never invent a mechanism.
- **one atom, one job** [every-change] — each toy does ONE thing; accretion is the signal to decompose, not add.
- **pathspec commit** [every-change] — `add`/`commit` own paths at both doors, `log -1 --stat` after; never a bare commit on the shared floor.
- **dial discipline** [every-change] — proposal dialed ON to the desk at ship, OFF the moment it's signed; no toy left dialed.
- **the SIGN: trigger** [every-change] — review proposals end with a bare `SIGN:` line (the dispatch renders the sign button off `^SIGN:$`); `SIGNED:` is the post-sign form, not the trigger.
- **palette + register** [every-change] — paper/ink/mark, product-language on the surface (no process words on the walls).
