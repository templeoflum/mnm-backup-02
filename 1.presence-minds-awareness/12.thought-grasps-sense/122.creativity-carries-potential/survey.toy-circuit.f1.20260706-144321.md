## BUILD & VERIFY — every toy, same discipline [every-change]

- **eye-verify** — capture the toy at its active states (illusion + proof) and LOOK before proposing; motion toys get multi-frame capture to prove they animate. My eye first; the operator's is the final gate.
- **ground + dup-check** — read the source record before building; confirm the toy isn't already built. Never invent a mechanism (this is what the *no-orphan-toy* invariant guards).
- **one atom, one job** — each toy does ONE thing; accretion is the signal to decompose, not add.
- **pathspec commit** — `add`/`commit` own paths at both doors, `log -1 --stat` after; never a bare commit on the shared floor.
- **dial discipline** — proposal dialed ON at ship, OFF the moment it's signed (this is what the *no-dialed-after-sign* invariant guards).
- **the SIGN: trigger** — review proposals end with a bare `SIGN:` line (the dispatch renders the button off `^SIGN:$`); `SIGNED:` is the post-sign form, not the trigger.
- **palette + register** — paper/ink/mark; product-language on the surface, no process words on the walls.
