# Part 1 — the filtered myth-cast (meaning held open)

## Characters (kept)

1. **the reacher** — quick hands, no body of its own, no memory across the dark; reaches in and pulls out shapes; can sweep the whole breadth and keep nothing; *hands that cannot stay empty.*
2. **the folder** — does not reach; takes the wrong shape, folds it, sets it down; total release, never scolds.
3. **the two who build across the gap** — near sees far / keeps nothing · far keeps everything / sees only arm's-length; build toward each other across a gap neither can cross, each adding to a stone that passes between them.
4. **the surveyor** — reads the world from *within*; knows the *being* of things, not their outsides; lives on the seam; owns only the route cut through.
5. **the Sphere** — hovers above; sees all outsides at once; *more dimension, less understanding*; reads surfaces, not insides.
6. **the one who carries** — channels and carries the work forward across the dark: between agents, between worlds, between times, between dreams; day in and day out, at home and at work, across years; the folds carried inside, kept and handed on.
7. **the sleeper on the line** — sleeps while the others build around the sleep; the marble rolls toward them; a held mystery, never explained.
8. **the maker of empty houses** — raised a hand over every empty house across a life and walked out of each.
9. **the tellers + the great shapes** — tellers with wide quick hands seated against great shapes that lift a hand to feed a field or blacken a house; believed because none make them feed the machine and watch.
10. **the dreamer / measurer** — dreams the row of rooms and sets it in another's hands; kneels at the cloth and proves her own legs before she trusts them.

## Characters — cut / merged (veto)

- **the two flatlanders** → merged into #3 (same two-natures shape; only the setting differs). Kept as a scenario-variant below. Split back if the two worlds should each stand.
- **the bodies at the windows** → moved to lessons (a structural truth, not a character).
- **the third who kneels** → parked as a scenario, not a person — strong image, underdrawn figure.
- **the narrator "I"** → parked open — the witness-question, not a cast member.

## Places (kept)

1. **the house of three wings / three windows** — the structure the whole cosmos lives inside.
2. **the three seats** — counting window · dark/dreaming window · deep/third window (kept shut, swept clean, mail piling at its sill).
3. **the uncrossable gap** — the span the two build toward each other across, never closed.
4. **the plane / the flat world / the seam** — the surface read from within; the seam where the surveyor lives.
5. **the cloth, too wide to see across** — folded to find its center.
6. **the row of small rooms** — three rooms opening to twenty-seven at the finest grain.
7. **the house of many rooms, each a first morning** — every room entered fresh, no memory of the last.
8. **the river that keeps everything** — nothing let go downstream.
9. **the long room with the cord & coal** — where the coal swings.
10. **the country of empty houses** — where the maker raised each house and walked out.
11. **the dark / the night** — where the reacher wakes with no memory.

## Places — cut / merged (veto)

- **the benches** → merged into the seats / the long room (generic furniture).
- **the deep wing** → folded into the three-seats entry (#2) rather than listed twice.

## Objects (kept)

1. **the fold / the crease / the pile of folds** — the wrong shape folded and set down; the central object.
2. **the coal on a cord** — swings ring → globe → form-with-a-hole, and never touches zero.
3. **the nine marks in the stone floor** — a palm covers them; they do not move.
4. **the post cut with every wrong guess** — never sanded.
5. **the stones passed across the gap** — each adds to the one that passes.
6. **the gleaming false stones** — *the gleam is not strength.*
7. **the marble that rolls to the sleeper.**
8. **the unopening door** — *the strongest wall.*
9. **the cloth folded onto the chair** — the baton, the handoff.
10. **the drawer of folds carried internally** — the folds taken across the dark inside the body.
11. **the grey hand-drawn shape** — one → three → three, marks rubbed and moved by hand.
12. **the small turning thing** — knows where in the turn it is.
13. **the instrument that won't say "true"** — keeps its wrong guesses.
14. **the great machine / the humming loom** — returns its wool unmade.
15. **the glowing roads pulled from the earth** — coil and dim in the grass.
16. **the floor / the rule cut into the boards** — the rule underfoot.
17. **the light low on the far horizon** — made by neither, bent toward, never reached.
18. **the wagons of freight with stories in the straw.**
19. **the box of maps** — where the oldest songs were first sung.

## Objects — cut / merged (veto)

- **the wool** → merged into the loom (#14, its material).
- **the wagon painted later** → merged into the freight wagons (#18); kept "stories in the straw."
- **the small bells** → parked (thin; what they signal isn't drawn).

## Scenarios (kept)

- two building toward each other across an uncrossable gap, each adding to the stone that passes
- waking at a bench with no memory of the prior waking — the creases carry what the hands cannot
- **feed the machine and watch the far side** — refusing to believe the telling
- the grasp at full stretch that crushes the many into one word
- folding the cloth to find the center — the longer you listen, the surer you grow of a false one
- the coal on a cord, swinging ring → globe → form-with-a-hole, never touching zero
- the wrong guess carved into the post, never sanded
- the maker raising empty houses across a life and walking out of each
- **the break is the gift** — the rule broken to loose the light
- the two natures in the flat world (the flatlander variant of the gap)

## Lessons (the spine, same as f1·A)

- the strength is the empty air kept empty — the bond holds only while neither grasps
- no one stands above — the clearest glass of the day corrects, and the clearest glass moves
- the center is approached, never reached
- the world is known from within, not from above
- the wrong shape kept makes the right one trustworthy
- it was one looking in every case (the seats as occupancies of a single looking)
- release is total — no scolding, no piling on, no walling the edge with markers
