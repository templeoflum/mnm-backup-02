the raw shape: a dry account of the structure, one numbered claim per line. the projection is checked against it; the ground it traces is the dream ([[dream.one-window]]) and the built floor under it.

## actors

- A — a mover that carries state across gaps; holds the deciding.
- B1 — a counter and signer at a boundary.
- B2 — a looker into the unbuilt; builds nothing.
- B3 — a holder of trust-levels; floor vs shelf.

## structure claims

- S1. the whole was previously assembled by traversal; awareness lagged activity.
- S2. a surface shows all parts at once without halting any of them.
- S3. the surface replaces the transport of attention, not the authority; deciding stays with A.
- S4. an utterance aimed at a place is a complete act — no carrier between saying and landing.
- S5. a container comes into being at first use; nothing is pre-provisioned empty.
- S6. two strata, strictly non-mixing: reference above, weight below; the surface shows only what is said about the carrying.
- S7. attention is budgeted; a signal exists only where it is earned.
- S8. a request is a lit question; repeated identical grants compile into standing law.
- S9. naming and permitting share one mechanism: repetition wears use into identity.
- S10. the surface may signal change, never enact it on its observer; origin — one failure where the surface's self-motion hid in-flight words (a loss of sight, not of the thing; the underlying stratum does not lose).
- S11. a universal stop is available to any hand without leave; the stop is itself recorded work.
- S12. dark and quiet are the signature of healthy operation, not of absence.
- S13. some transport stays slow on purpose; speed is not a universal good.

## sequence

traversal era → the surface arrives → speech-founding → the strata law → the attention law → the permission law → the stillness law (through the one failure) → the stop law → the dark close.
