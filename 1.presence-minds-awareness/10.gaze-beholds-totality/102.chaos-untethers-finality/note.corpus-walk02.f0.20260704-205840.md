# note — corpus walk 02 — f0

Second away-work pull under [[proposal.lantern-standing-orders.f0]].
Walked the signal-and-watcher shelf at 0/corpus/records — the fields
under the operator's morning words ("signals and watchers and
compression rather than semantics"). Unsigned pile — for the batch
read. Three leads; none acted on.

1. **The chip problem has a field with receipts.** field.detection-
   estimation-theory: deciding among hypotheses from noisy
   observations. Neyman–Pearson (1933) names the two ways a watcher
   fails — false alarm and miss — and the trade between them. This
   morning's stale-pid read was a false alarm in the LANDED/DARK
   class, caught by hand. Lead: when the weather frame cuts, each of
   the three signal classes could carry its two error names from this
   shelf instead of inventing vocabulary.

2. **The floor's coordination style has a name.** field.stigmergy:
   coordination through traces left in a shared medium — the trace
   directs the next act, no central plan, no direct message (Grassé
   1959, termite nest reconstruction). Commits, the board, the baton
   already work this way. Dream seed: the termite mound as a town
   that builds itself from marks, nobody holding the drawing.

3. **The immune conversation has documented kin.** field.high-
   reliability-organizations: two opposed founding claims — accidents
   structurally inevitable (Perrow 1984) vs. near-error-free operation
   achievable (Berkeley program) — settled into five teachable
   principles of collective mindfulness (Weick & Sutcliffe 2001).
   "A signal-fire counts as health" has kin in their preoccupation-
   with-failure. Lead only; a shelf to mine when the weather frame
   opens, not a claim that the town matches it.

Thin fourth, one line: field.point-processes — inter-arrival grammar
for event streams; "quiet vs. dead" on a beat stream is its home
question. Parked with the above.
