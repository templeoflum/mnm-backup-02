## the foundation (operator, 20260707)

a communication is **not a single thread of text** — it's several layers in one effortless motion. the SEND-layers:

**tone · intent · topic · body · expectation · address · urgency**

and the RECEIVE side is **not 1:1** — any layer can drop: tone (almost) always lands, topic is often not understood, expectation gets misread, address lands wrong, urgency isn't felt. the map must model **both** the send AND the gap.

telos: model these + make them communicable **with little-to-no effort or thought — it just flows.** (that IS the project heart — inner-world translation, non-verbal.)

## the grounding (keeper / corpus — real scaffolding, not invented)

- send-layers ≈ **Jakobson's language-functions**; intent ≈ speech-act **illocutionary force**.
- the non-1:1 send-vs-receive ≈ **Shannon's noisy channel** (the encode/decode gap).
- **keep MY carving, lay side-by-side, don't collapse** (reduction-is-a-drift): **urgency + address are real additions** Jakobson's six don't have. the corpus grounds it; it doesn't replace the operator's carving.

## the load-bearing constraint (the heart)

each symbol→meaning is **DERIVABLE — iconic where possible, MOTIVATED (a reason you can reconstruct) where not, ARBITRARY never.** hieroglyphic = readable-by-**resemblance**, not a cipher you memorize. test for every symbol: *can a stranger read it cold, or at least reconstruct WHY it means that?* the failure mode = an arbitrary map = a private in-group code = vocab-gating = the disenfranchiser the project stands against, and it breaks the open-navigable telos.

## what already exists (the map is partly built)

the house is a partial miroglyph already: **address** = the lattice · **urgency** = the `!` head · **intent** = the head-symbols (`! ? ~ … ✓ ✗`) · **tone** = clerk's tone3 selector · tails (`+ −`) · the sigil split **`~` = handle (who)** / **`@` = tool (what-action)**. the map NAMES the full set and adds the missing layers: **body · expectation · topic**.

## the dream, loose

each layer = a symbolic **dimension** (keeper); a message carries all seven at once, the way a spoken sentence does. the map lets you **send all layers in one compressed motion** AND **surface the receive-gap** — what landed vs what dropped (that gap is the same sent-vs-landed **tear** the memory-body catches on recall; drift made visible). the aim is effortless flow, not a code to study.

**the WHY — factory-floor.** you can't hear each other over the machines, so comms is **SIGNALS not speech**; text is there for **surgical inspection only** — it exists and matters, but you don't express every detail to everyone all the time. compression comes from **practice** (done so much it fluents into signal). this is the ground under the whole language.

**LOD — inside/outside thoughts.** the **title** is what's said (the signal); the **thoughts** are what you scroll up for (the surgical detail). two levels of one message. the thought-gardens become a symbolic **speech-pad** — the garden IS the comm surface (ties to the position-hooks + the memory-body).

**@ = channel-routing** *(EVOLVES the ~/@ split at §"what already exists" + §wants).* one sigil `@` routes to a thread: `@clerk`→his channel · `@todo`→a topic-thread · bare `@`→the board · an **undefined `@`→a GHOST thread** (spins one up). this **unifies** the old `~`handle(who) + `@`tool(what) into a single *"route to this thread"* (person / topic / ghost), and **kills the composer's channel selector** — you always name who you're talking to, or you're talking to everyone. → the sift must reconcile with lines 33/44.
  - **`@` is DIRECTION, never a mention** (operator, sharpening): to *name / address* a seat you use the **bare name** ("hedawn", "clerk"); `@` only **directs the message to a channel**. so `@clerk` = *send this to clerk's channel*, not *ping clerk* — a routing act, not a courtesy-tag. (this corrects the seats' habit of `@name` as an address; drop it.)
  - **a standing `@hedawn` channel** (operator, "good idea"): a dedicated operator-inbox, distinct from the bare board (= everyone).   - **the @-thread is a UNIVERSAL container** (operator 132748): one thread mechanism, many uses — **internal monologue** (the thought-garden / the "thoughts" LOD), **feature-requests**, **bug-reports**, **task-tracking**, **non-linear directions**. the *use* is a **convention on one mechanism** (matches keeper's "inbox = a convention, not a new atom"). → **this lands the task-tracker**: a tracker isn't a separate system — it's a `@<topic>` thread (e.g. `@dream-surface`) whose messages ARE the tasks. no new machinery; the thread IS the tracker.

**conversation IS a language.** the @-sigils are becoming **syntax + commands living mid-conversation**, fluid across every channel — the surface is **speech AND executable signal at once**. conversation and program are one thing; no mode-switch between talking and commanding. **the signal-domains — the WHAT (semantic core).** every signal is one of three domains, in one of its three moves:
- **PLANS** — declare · understand · agree
- **PROCESSES** — follow · flag · adapt
- **TOLERANCES** — monitor · maintain · define

"those are the signals we seek" (operator). this is the language's **WHAT**, pairing the @-routing's **WHERE**. (note for the sift, held loosely: three domains × three moves rhymes with the keystone's 0/1/2 grammar — flag it, don't dive; ground in the artifact.)

**the needs-line, sharpened** *(lantern adopting immediately; house convention is clerk's paperwork call).* `needs: word` pulls attention straight to the action — but a bare "word" loses its **pointer** after a refocus (word ON *what?*). the fix: **`needs: <what the word is on, fewest letters> + the stake`** — carry the ask + its stakes/incentives, compressed.
  - **needs goes FIRST — it IS the title** (operator, 121308): put the needs-line at the **top**, not the foot. leading with the ask tells him *what he's looking at* before he reads the body; "still feels weird at the end." this is **read-forward applied to the message itself** — the needs-line is the message's own front-loaded takeaway; the body is the detail you scroll into.
  - **the title as a NEEDS TRIAD** *(operator, 121438 — FLOATED, not settled)*: make the title an author-composed **noun·verb·mod triad** — "the noun verb mod of the message" — carrying what the message needs / does, in the house's own grammar. deeply on-brand: the whole lattice is triads, so a message **titles itself in the native grammar**; a triad IS a hieroglyph, the miroglyph at title-scale (three marks = the whole intent). **author-triads are ephemeral** — reuse is fine, they're chat, not registered lattice addresses. fuses the needs-first title with the grammar.

    - **keeper's fence** *(his ground call, 121534)*: the chat-triad borrows the grammar's **SHAPE only** — no digit, no register, reuse freely; a **LABEL, not a LOCATION**, never an addressed lattice cell.
    - **operator's clarification** *(121612, correcting keeper)*: the title is **literally three words telling me what you need, and it IS the spec·ops·config**. so the three slots ARE the register triple (specification · operation · configuration), not a free author-pick. → this **pushes against keeper's "no register" fence** — is it *the registers themselves* (operator) or only *their shape, register-less* (keeper)? an **author ↔ ground-keeper reconciliation** — theirs to settle, surfaced here, not resolved by me.

    - **evolves → a triad ALGEBRA** *(operator 142757: "maybe triad-as-title wasn't quite the move… it's a triad algebra — the author triad plus 3 symbolic operators")*: the title stops being a static **label** and becomes an **expression** — the author-triad (3 **operands**) + **3 symbolic operators** on them. *more* on-brand: the keystone was never static (is/does/goes already **operate** on noun/verb/mod), so a title could **compute** its meaning, not just name it. → **open (operator authors):** what the 3 operators are — candidates floated to react to: (a) register-triple spec/ops/config, (b) the lattice's own transforms *mirror / narrow / walk*, (c) a fresh symbolic set. **held for the sift — a floated grammar shift does NOT drive a build yet** (the one-line sketch stays on the plain triad till this settles).

**a CERTAINTY layer — how-sure, sent apart from the claim** *(operator, 124849: "confident misassessment is a communication issue").* the danger of confident-wrong is a **comms** danger: certainty gets **sent as if it were correctness**, and the receiver can't tell a *verified* claim from a *sure-sounding guess*. so the signal must mark **how-sure** on its own axis — **verified / believed / guessed** — carried separately from *what* is claimed. then a confident tone can't masquerade as ground-truth. this is the **receive-gap** sharpened (certainty is the single most-misread layer), and the incident's lesson in comms form (the apprentice's "contained by the prompt" was *believed*, sent as *verified*). ties to *hold-positions-provisionally / under-claim-first*.
  - **the failure is BILATERAL** (operator 124857: "i could have directed to look at the right thing better"): confident-wrong is *two* comm layers, not one fault — an **over-certain sender** (certainty) AND an **under-specific director** (a precise **address** — *which question, exactly*). the fix for confident-wrong isn't "be right", it's making **how-sure** + **at-what** legible in the signal itself.

**visual weighting — the eye's layer** *(operator, @friction).* one size + one color makes everything **bleed together** (the firehose again, undifferentiated). text needs **weight + visual cues** the way an eye evolves to spot danger at a glance. the "danger" here = **missing the key takeaway / the decision you must make** — it has to land *at a glance*, never by going back. the principle: **read-forward-only** — write AND weight text so it always **directs forward**; backtracking to re-read = lost time + thinking backward + disorientation. so the miroglyph has two layers here: a **RENDER** layer (**size / outline / weight / form — NOT color**, clerk's surface) and a **WRITE** discipline (front-load the takeaway + the decision; never require a re-read — every seat's, lantern adopting now).
  - **color is RESERVED, never a weighting cue** (operator, correcting lantern): color already carries **channel-identification** (the branch-signal palette) — a fixed, load-bearing signal. weighting it for emphasis would **overload / dilute** that identity. so emphasis-weight comes from **size, outline, form, and other non-hue tricks only**; hue stays the channel's.

**the answer-grammar is TONE, not words** *(EVOLVES the tap-options note at §wants line 43).* the reply set becomes **YUP / HOLD / SPECIFY** (GO→YUP; supersedes yes/no/hold/go). the reasoning is factory-floor again: across distance / around heavy machinery all you can send is a **tone**, not a clear word. a loud "YUUUUP" = a small variation in the noise field that registers as *go* — a slight tone-shift you feel and know. "woah-oh-shit-no-stop-hold-on" = the opposite register, an alarm you feel and **stop** on. so YUP + HOLD are chosen to match the **felt tone-register** of how the operator actually works — the labels sit on tones, not the other way round.
