## the through-line — v1 → v4 (what the versions actually do)

One skeleton runs through all four: **3 arcs × 6 conditions = 18 nodes** (v4 adds a 19th, the **Nontion** center). The names change; the topology doesn't. What changes is the **direction of each version** — subtract toward the frame, or accrete toward machinery:

A compass "read, walked, remembered — not computed." **But the engine-word is already seeded here** ("experiential engine," "myth engine") — poetic, not built. The spirit-vs-engine tension is latent from the start.
- **v2 — "Architecture Primer."** Same 18-node topology, **grown heavy**: a 9-section per-node "Node Blueprint" (descends into camera angle + five-sense anchoring = a render brief) and a combinatoric "Motif Mapping System" (numeric progression rules). First real over-reach — accretion.

Subtractive — but seeds a new tendency: a combinatorial **generator** (locked patterns, "3×6=18 exactly matching," schema-to-enforce) — the frame starting to be *produced by a rule* rather than *held as a skeleton*.
- **v4 — "Minimal Core" (the MAP).** The mature frame is a **subtraction**: "removed navigation rules (user defines paths)," motifs made optional, "from prescriptive rules → to flexible framework." Its own words: **"structure without prescription," "the system offers structure, not truth," "not a map but a mirror."** 19 points, dual naming, polarity pairs, condition resonance. This is the frame stripped to its essence.

**The healthy line is the SUBTRACTIVE one** (v1 seed → v3 cut → v4 Minimal Core). Every accretion (v2's blueprint/motif math, v3's generator) was later cut back. The frame wants to be *less*, not more.
