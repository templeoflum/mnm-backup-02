## ~1 — the desks, dreamt

each seat's place in the window, dreamt as furniture the operator can pick up. standing rule inside the dream: every piece must be load-bearing — a gauge wearing a body, never costume. (a piece that only decorates gets cut at the sift.)

**clerk's office** — a standing desk at the dock's edge, glass on three sides: it looks onto the dock (20x), the floor (21x), the wrapping bench (22x) — the three trades are three windows of one office, and which window clerk stands at IS the mode it's in. on the desk:
- **the clipboard** — the FLAG+SIGN popup made honest: paper under a clamp, flags as tabs sticking out the side, signing pulls a tab flush. the stack's THICKNESS is the queue — pending paperwork readable across the room as paper, not as a number.
- **the spike** — a spindle by the lamp; signed rows go down it the way old counter-slips did. the manifest, impaled in order; riffle it to read backward.
- **the carbon book** — every page writes its copy underneath (the commit under every motion); the office never explains this, it's just how its paper works.
- **the bell** — the dock's delivery bell; intake rings it, and the ring is a row.

**keeper's stacks** — no office: a reading room below the other floors, lamp pools on a long table. the law of the room is visible as geometry — by the door, the FLOOR, full and plainly rowed (unvetted, walked past daily); along the far wall, the SHELF, nearly bare (what's earned). between them:
- **the cart** — keeper's quick-access, dreamt: the returns cart mid-room holds exactly what's being walked RIGHT NOW — the five things mid-verification, one pull away, unshelved by the pull itself. cart load = WIP, readable as load.
- **the slip drawer** — a small drawer of call slips: pointers only, never cargo (the mail law, again). quick-access is slips + cart, never a second shelf.
- **the cradles and gloves** — fragile sources sit in book cradles; the room handles its material like it might be the last copy, because the wing's whole posture says it might.

**lantern's room** — barely a desk: a night table by the one window that looks out instead of in (102). on it a journal, the three sketch folds pinned above, and the lamp that is the seat itself. two trays:
- **moth-paper** — wants voiced mid-night stick to it and are carried whole to the sift table at 112; nothing on moth-paper is ever acted from the night table.
- **the sift table** — next room over (112), a sieve and two piles; SHARED — two chairs at it, and the plainer eye rules.
- the room has no progress bar and no clock face; a night is not a percentage. its one gauge is the lamp: dark (holding) · low (dreaming) · carried out the door (a myth going to 121).

**the bodies (Q2)** — not new metaphors; the paper's own words, drawn:
- **gait** — the card's posture: [hold] sits, [walk] stands, [work] leans in. one glyph, three stances, read at a glance across three cards.
- **hands** — the dial rendered as hands: empty hands (idle), a held file (its name on a slip), both hands full (the overload tell — a fourth thread is the baton's own overload signal).
- **breath** — loop cadence as a slow pulse on the dark card; a house working looks like slow breathing in dark glass, not like dashboards.
- **the raised hand** — the amber row is a body asking, already; draw it as the hand it is.
- **the pulled flag** — a cord-stop sits the body down visibly; a stopped seat is a seated body with its flag out, not a greyed div.
