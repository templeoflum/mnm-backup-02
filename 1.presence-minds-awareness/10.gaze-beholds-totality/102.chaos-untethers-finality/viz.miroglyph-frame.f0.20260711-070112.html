The Miroglyph Frame

3 arcs × 6 conditions = 18 nodes, + 1 center. one skeleton; the names change across versions, the topology doesn’t.
“structure without prescription” — the frame gives you the positions; you choose the paths.
center
Nontion (∅) — the pause / reset / integration-in-absence. not a node; traversable but empty.
arc 1 descent / shadow — fracture, break, shadow-work arc 2 resonance / mirror — reflect, echo, pattern arc 3 emergence / mythogenesis — synthesize, become
polarity pairs: within an arc, condition 1↔4, 2↔5, 3↔6 mirror each other. condition-resonance: the same condition across all three arcs (a row) rhymes. these relationships are what make the structure readable at a glance — author-visible, not computed.
