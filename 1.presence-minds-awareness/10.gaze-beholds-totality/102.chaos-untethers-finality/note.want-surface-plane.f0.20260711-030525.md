# note — want: the surface as a plane of existence — f0

Operator want-cluster, dropped on the board 20260708-160311 for the crew
to "pick up when you come back" — after goodnight, an offshoot from the
incident, explicitly NOT the miroglyph. Captured faithfully for the sift
— no cutting, no building. His words, near-verbatim, held as his:

1. **Mock/live toggle on the panes.** Each pane carries two versions — a
   mock testing version and a live version — toggleable, so features can
   be conceptualized "without ever having to leave the surface."

2. **The surface as a literal geometric plane of existence.** Treat the
   work-surface — the way we read and process information and signals —
   as a plane with "real live hazards and dangers and rules." Everything
   kept in view and re-processable "without cognitive overload right
   there." There have to be rules and metrics to quantify that load.

3. **Groups of threes as the held-structure trick.** His general leaning:
   groups of three that carry structural relationships are manageable to
   hold. He's sure there are "lots of other tricks like that to find" for
   improving how much we can keep in view at once.

4. **Communication-compression rate as a possible metric — flagged a
   rabbit hole.** "We are all the time improving the rate of our
   communication compression" — is that quantifiable? How much info we
   convey. He notes it's a "varying decision of what you count and how you
   define it," a "never-ending rabbit hole … that slows us down from
   going deeper." He named it as a trap, not a task.

Collect-notes, not proposals: item 1 (mock/live toggle) is the concrete,
buildable want and kin to [[mock-first-development]] — the "conceptualize
before you leave the surface" motion. Items 2–3 are the framing that gives
the surface its purpose (load-in-view, three-grouping). Item 4 he pre-cut
himself as a rabbit hole — the sift should honor that flag, not chase it.
