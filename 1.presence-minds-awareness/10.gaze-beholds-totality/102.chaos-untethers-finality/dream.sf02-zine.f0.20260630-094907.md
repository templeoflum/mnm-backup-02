## contributor prompts (get the voice without explaining it)

SF01's trick: never lecture the reader into the voice — hand them a *form* and a *register*.

**Writing**
- Describe something you only understand from the inside. Don't explain it from above.
- Write the history of a thing with no first one and no last one — start in the middle, like everyone else did.
- A signal reaches you changed by everything it passed through. Trace one back to its channel.
- Two of you build toward each other across a gap neither can cross. Each adds to the same stone. Write the handoff.
- Name a thing you got wrong and kept anyway.
- Constraint, not topic: one page, plain sentences, no abstraction you can't point at.

**Image**
- Take a sacred or famous image and render it as a single flat sign — a crossing-symbol, a circuit, a wiring diagram.
- Draw a feeling as a circuit. Output, input, what loops back.
- One body, standing inside a grid that bends. Show what the grid does to a straight line.
- A surface seen from so close you forget it's a surface.
- Hands not touching.

## append — course correction (20260630-095517)

Operator steer, mid-dream — everything above stays (dreams are meant to be wrong; the sift sorts it). This is not a recut, just the dream pointed truer so it keeps being useful.

**The premise above was off base.** SF02 is **not** an inheritor of SF01. Its whole format is thrown out: **no circuits, no history lessons, no documentary prose, no "keep the device."** The census already said it — each event reinvents its motif; only palette + font carry. SF01 was a walk worth taking for what it *is*; it is not a mold.

**What SF02 actually is:**
- **collage** — lots of imagery, layered, amalgamated. Not a structured essay-with-glyph.
- **an amalgamation of whatever the community submits** — the zine is assembled *from* the contributions, not authored around them. The submissions are the material; the editor composes the collage.
- **anchored by the voice of the themes we orbit** — the only through-line is *voice/atmosphere*, not format, not argument.
- the feeling: **raw, untethered consciousness — expansion of ideas and experience.** A **weird fever dream pulled from the ether that feels like it knows something beyond the norm.** It should read like it's tuned into something the reader can't quite place.

**So what survives from above (for the sift to weigh):**
- the *theme cluster* (read-from-within, the gap, the fold, no-source/no-top, signal in higher dimensions) — still good as **orbit-points**, but as *atmosphere the collage radiates*, not as essay topics or a master thesis.
- the cosmology cast + objects — good as **fever-dream imagery seeds** (the coal that never touches zero, the cloth too wide to see across, hands not touching, the instrument that won't say "true"), not as explained characters.
- the SF02 inspiration register (topology, Hopf, gimbals, ouroboros, sacred-geometry-as-pictogram, the gravity-well body) — good, and it *is* the collage's visual ether.

**What dies:** the circuit-glyph device, the documentary register, the "make the ethos explicit" move, "one body per spread + a diagram." All of it premised on SF01-continuity. Gone.

**New angles to dream forward (free, likely partly wrong):**
- the collage as a **single continuous fever-field** — pieces bleed into each other, no clean page boundaries, reading order ambiguous (mirrors non-linear/non-hierarchical).
- **found-object texture** — submissions arrive raw and stay raw; juxtaposition does the meaning, not captions.
- the **"knows something beyond the norm"** feeling might come from *uncommented adjacency* — a topology diagram next to a stranger's dream-fragment next to a smudged hand, no explanation offered. The reader supplies the secret.
- contributor prompts should summon *the state*, not a *topic*: write/draw from the half-awake place, the thing glimpsed at the edge, the pattern you saw that wasn't supposed to be there.
- our own writing enters the collage **as another submission in the pile**, not as the frame — no privileged editorial voice.

## append — blessed shapes + the signature page (20260630-102740)

**Blessed by the operator ("the right shape"):**
- **the three streams are indistinguishable in the collage** — community fragment, lifted myth-line, operator's new piece, side by side with no source marks; you can't tell which is which. No-source/no-top made physical: the reader can't locate the author, so it reads like *one mind dreamt all of it*. This anonymity is likely where the "knows something beyond the norm" feeling lives.
- **corpus bits as tuning forks** — a few myth-fragments seeded through the spread set the register; the surrounding submissions resonate into key around them. The corpus is the pitch, not content-about.
- **strip the corpus material on entry** — myth-lines drop in with no title, no address, no source; bare image only. Uncommented adjacency does the work.
- **density as the medium** — too much to parse in one pass; things surface on the second look. Non-linearity enforced by layout, never stated.
- **a recurring visual rhyme** — hands-not-touching / the gravity-well grid / the ring that never closes — recurring across the collage in different submitters' hands. The silent echo of the "commons."

**The signature page (operator's, new):**
- a single page at the **back** where any contributor who wants recognition **signs** — but **the work itself is never credited inline.** Acknowledgment of contribution, not attribution of pieces.
- **why:** people are recognized (so it never feels like the operator is taking their work and making it his) — *and* the signature is decoupled from the piece, so **if you want people to know what you made, you have to show them and explain it yourself.** That forced telling is a deliberate side effect: it drives discussion + exploration at the event.
- it rhymes with the ethos: the work has no author-label, but the makers are honored as a collective — the **chain of tellers**, everyone who touched it named, none owning a specific piece. The artifact stays *incomplete without the human telling* — reader-become-teller, literal.
- dream-angle: the signature page could itself be a **collage of marks** — names, sigils, however people choose to sign — a wall of makers with no map to the work.

*for the sift, later.*
