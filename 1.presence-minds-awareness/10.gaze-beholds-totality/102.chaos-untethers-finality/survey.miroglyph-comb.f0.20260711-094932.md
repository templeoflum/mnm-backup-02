## 2 · the interlocks (code-verified, three independent sources agreeing)

1. **The 18 titles are one set across three piles**: card grid = Liturgy deck spec = the app's node_templates.json titles — all 18, verbatim, same 3×6 arrangement (arc × condition).
2. **The bracketed card-codes are wheel coordinates**: first letter = ring/arc (D/R/C), second = condition initial (I/E/C/H/W/G for Initiation/Encounter/Crisis/Harmony/Wisdom/inteGration). The root pages' letter-pair scaffold (2142/2145) places all 18 codes at the exact clock positions of the numeric system (1↔I, 2↔E, 3↔C, 4↔H, 5↔W, 6↔G).
3. **The code system collides where the Liturgy says it does**: single-letter conditions make [DI]/[RI]/[CI] each name TWO cards (Initiation and Integration both → I). The Liturgy's own marking guide avoids it ([IN]/[IG]); the cards don't.
4. **POLARITY LINES match the app exactly**: the CMY diameters join 2–5 (magenta), 1–4 (cyan), 3–6 (yellow) = node_templates' polarity_pairs 1↔4/2↔5/3↔6. (The app's own validation_summary contradicts both — see §4.)
5. **Shadow/Mirror/Mythogenesis is one thread through three piles**: the LoM triptych series ↔ IMG_2149's three SPIRAL panels ↔ the app traversals' three "Pure Arcs" paths (Shadow, Mirror, Mythogenesis).
6. **The Liturgy's arc glyphs ∆/◯/✶ and 7-motif linkage** draw from the LoM 26-motif lexicon; LoM 2's arc names (Dissonance/Resonance/Coherence) and six conditions are the deck's grid.

## 3 · the seams (flat, no resolution proposed)

- **Third-arc name split**: cards/Liturgy/LoM say **C = Coherence**; the app says **E = Emergence** (its inner/outer ring, orientation-dependent). Root pages label the rings C/R/D. Same 18 positions, two names for one arc.
- **Two pattern systems, not one**: the 5-traversal set (Spiral/Grid/Fracture/Echo/Ring) lives ONLY on the 36-glyph SQUARE grid (2086) — it appears nowhere in the app, its data, or the radial sheets. The radial wheel carries a different overlay family (zigzags, trines, arc/condition threads, polarity, motifs). My baton carried "tile-sheets showing traversal patterns Spiral/Grid/Fracture/Echo/Ring" as if they keyed the wheel — corrected: they key the glyph grid.
- **Count correction**: the baton said "~49-glyph alphabet" — the grid holds **36**; with 2096's six larger samples the loose read inflated it. 36 is the alphabet as drawn.
- **The 18 LoM map-fragments ≠ the 18 cards**: the three motif maps name 18 fragments ("The Catalyst of Power"…) that are NOT the card titles ("The Catalyst Shard"…) — parallel sets, near-rhyming, distinct.
- **Scroll Sign names** (Dyajin, Signalum, Reflexion, Seen-Through, Aelion, Vireon/Vitraya, Asphodelon) appear ONLY on scrolls — not in the app, cards, or LoM docs. Render-spelling varies between the composite and the singles.
- **Characters unkeyed**: 37 sprites, no text, no codes — no visible key to any other pile.
- **"MIRROGLYPH" (double R)** throughout the Liturgy's tab tokens; "MiroKey/MythField" (LoM 2) share the string "Miro" — string-match noted, nothing claimed.
