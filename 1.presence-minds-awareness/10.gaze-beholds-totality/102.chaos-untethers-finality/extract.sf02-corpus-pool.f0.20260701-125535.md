# extract.sf02-corpus-pool — f0

address: 102 · companion to [[dream.sf02-zine]]
scope: the clustered concept/theme pool drawn from the branch-0 ground corpus (`0/corpus/` — 154 fields, 1014 objects), for SF02 to draw on as tuning-forks.

constraint: **COLLECT, not sift.** Candidates gathered across the whole corpus, broad + inclusive — dry math kept in on purpose, no taste-filter. Grouped into resonance-pools laid out **as plural**: recurrences named, no master key, nothing crowned. Which of these become SF02 tuning-forks is the later **shared** sift. Nothing here is signed.

method: pulled the one-line `essence` of every field + object record, then grouped by cross-field *shape* — a pool earns its place when the same shape rings in math AND myth AND cognition (that ring is what makes a tuning-fork). Members shown as `handle · fragment [fields spanned]`. A concept can sit in more than one pool; the pools braid.
