## the subject (as understood so far — provisional)

**Scheduling = how planes get sequenced for the unit's rotations.** The unit (a rotation) is scheduled *through* a plane by being acted on by that plane's isometries (see [[frame.ts-plane.f0]]). A *sequence* of planes is the path the unit takes — the dimensional lift (point → circle → sphere → beyond). In the old spine this sat under "projection"; the operator's word is **schedule** (cf. keystone 202 — work that isn't sorted can't be scheduled).
