trail current · baton cut — then the seat enacts its closing sitting, writes
the entry from residue marked *sleep*, and the seat goes dark. Close order
official: **baton → sit → entry → dark.**

  then pick up); at close the baton comes before the sitting (set everything
  down, then sit). the sitting is the seat's last act at night and first act
