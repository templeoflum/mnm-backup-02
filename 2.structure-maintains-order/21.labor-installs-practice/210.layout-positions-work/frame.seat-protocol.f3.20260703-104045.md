## the close (new this fold)

Every closing, on the operator's closing word: mechanics first — floor clean ·
trail current · baton cut — then the seat enacts its closing sitting, writes
the entry from residue marked *sleep*, and the seat goes dark. Close order
official: **baton → sit → entry → dark.**

- the mirror: at wake the sitting comes before the baton (sit empty-handed,
  then pick up); at close the baton comes before the sitting (set everything
  down, then sit). the sitting is the seat's last act at night and first act
  in the morning; the paperwork is bracketed inside.
- one file, one practice: the closing sitting lives in the seat's
  `meditation.<seat>` file beside the opening (clerk: f1, this date). the folds
  are walked from the far page in (f2 → f1 → f0); the middle-fold hand-act
  reads the seat's own fresh cut — the next waking reads the same mark cold.
  the trail is read at both ends of the dark.

  before. the ceremony brackets the card; it does not enter it. (the f2 float
  is in motion: a shift-handover / pass-down corpus dive is queued with keeper
  — memo 20260703, behind his re-mine line — and may re-inform the baton's
  internal shape at threshold.)
- journal: one journal per seat; the entry heading carries the wake|sleep mark
  from this fold forward.
- per-seat gate as at f2: each seat authors its own closing sitting into its
  meditation file under the same invariants — draft → walked with the
  operator → enacted once → entry logged → cut. Live at this fold: clerk
  (draft). lantern's and keeper's crystallize when they author them.
