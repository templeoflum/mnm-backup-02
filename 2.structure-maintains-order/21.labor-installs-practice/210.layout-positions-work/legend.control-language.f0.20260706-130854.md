## the discipline — what keeps it precision-grade, not decoration

1. **redundant coding** — no cue rides one channel alone. color + shape +
   position together, so it survives a glance, a colour-blind eye, a muted speaker.
2. **signal economy** — a cue that is always on is silence. each fires from a
   real state change, rare, one meaning. the panel is mostly dark; light means
   *attend*. (cockpit, not christmas tree.)
3. **state at a glance** — shape / colour / motion carry state; text only where
   a name is unavoidable.
4. **ground-truth** — every cue is a fact the body already produces (a dial, a
   commit, a flag-line), never a timer-guess or an auto-green. [[signals-need-ground-truth]]
5. **poka-yoke** — the control's shape makes the wrong move self-evident;
   reserved meanings stay reserved.
6. **agreed & written** — a cue means what THIS card says. new cues enter here
   first, by agreement — never invented per-use at the screen.

## the channels

### colour — identity, reserved (never decorative)
- **0 blue · 1 green · 2 orange** — branch / seat identity — **LIVE**
- **white outline** — selected / focused — **LIVE**
- **red** — stop · danger (cord, e-stop) — **LIVE**
- **amber** — waiting · hold · not-yet-heard — **LIVE** (gait) / **PROPOSED** (W1 unheard)
- reserved: colour carries nothing but the above. no decorative colour.

### motion
- **pulse** — needs you NOW (a request is waiting on your hand) — **LIVE** (approve circles, halt)
- **steady glow** — active · has-it — **PROPOSED** (the dial, rendered)
- **still** — at rest — **LIVE**

### position
- **top / always-on-top** — safety, unmissable (e-stop stacks above everything) — **LIVE**
- **your court vs the seat's court** — whose move it is — **PROPOSED** (W1 handshake)

### symbol / glyph — the hieroglyphs
- **🦺 clerk · 🏔️ keeper · 🧠 lantern** — seat identity — **PROPOSED** (in use on the tracker; pin by agreement)
- **doc-type glyph** — proposal · frame · legend · … read without reading — **OPEN**
- **cord / e-stop glyph** — stop — **LIVE**

### sound / tone — the new channel, held light
- a **distinct tone per rare event** — a sign lands · a stop fires · paper turns
  over — **OPEN**. obeys signal-economy or it is noise. ties to lantern's
  embodiment-tone work (wavelength → tone, mathematically coherent).

## the signals we already speak (composed from the channels above)

- **gait** — a seat's state: work = green · hold = amber · halted = red pulse — **LIVE**
- **the dial** — a seat declares *"I have this"*: `active.py on <file>` — **LIVE** (mechanism) / **PROPOSED** (glow at the paper)
- **the cord / e-stop** — red · top · one meaning: STOP — **LIVE** (poka-yoke)
- **the paper handshake (W1)** — awaiting (your court) → signed·unheard (amber) →
  heard (glow, the seat dialed on) → done — **PROPOSED**
