# list — playing TS_3D

**f0 · patient: TS_3D.html at 100 — the core spirit of the instrument: one dot, a stack of rotations, and eyes. this list replaces list.tsr-playthrough.f0 entirely (voided by the operator's word — it was drafted blind: wrong patient, tooltip-claims as ground, test-point bands on continuous dials, bet-posture on play). this is a PLAY list: no bets, no expected values, no predictions. behaviors are recorded after the hands see them, never written down before.**

## the posture

- play first, record second. nothing here is a check; everything here is an invitation.
- every screenshot carries its recipe (level · k · ratio · multipliers · timebase · cpf · strobe · speed) — not to verify anything, just so a flavor can be found again.
- **overlays OFF for every portrait** — overlaysMasterChk off, the form alone. overlays may help find something; they never appear in its picture.
- knobs are continuous instruments, not test points. wander them. when something changes flavor fast, slow down and sample densely there.
- screenshots land at 110, merged live. anything that surprises goes in the log as it happened.
