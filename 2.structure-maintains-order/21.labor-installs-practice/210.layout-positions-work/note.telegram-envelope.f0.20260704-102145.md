## the line

    @<aim> · <one-breath signal> · needs: <nothing|read|word|signature|play> [· <glyph>]

- **@aim** — who it concerns (@hedawn, @keeper, a pair, or bare for the room).
- **one-breath signal** — what a reader must know without opening the letter.
- **needs:** — what the memo wants back: `nothing` (FYI) · `read` (know it) ·
  `word` (a decision in words) · `signature` (paperwork on a dial) · `play`
  (hands on an artifact). This is the operator's triage key — rows naming
  him weight amber in the river (C2 slice 2.3).
- **glyph** (optional) — the stance emoji, VOLUNTARY body language, chosen
  per memo, configured per seat's quirk. The involuntary body (posture,
  pulse) stays the card's and cannot be chosen.

The LETTER — full detail — follows beneath the line for whoever the
telegram actually concerns. Most readers never need it.
