the refusal: the gap is never measured in a picture. the meter reads the full 4-D distance between the two endpoints; the viewports are charts, and a chart can under-report — on this bench, down to 16.5% of the true gap (measured at α=6°, β=137°). when the planes overlap, the leading disagreement is a turn in the third plane (yz) — two turns disagree by a turn neither of them owns: [J_xy, J_xz] = J_yz, exact to the bit at generator level. when the planes are disjoint, the two roads end at the same door, bitwise.
