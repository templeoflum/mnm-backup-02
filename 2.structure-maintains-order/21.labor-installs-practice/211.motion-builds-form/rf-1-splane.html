The s-plane: a point's location tells you how it behaves in time.

To an engineer, the complex variable s = σ + jω is the language of behavior: σ is growth or decay, ω is how fast it oscillates. This isn't an analogy for ζ — it's the same plane ζ lives in. Drag the point and watch its time-signal. Then drop in ζ's landscape and read it the same way.

The reading. Everything decaying sits in the left half-plane; everything blowing up sits on the right; pure endless oscillation lives exactly on the vertical axis. ζ's pole at s=1 is a resonance that blows up; its zeros are the notches we hunt; the Riemann Hypothesis is the claim that every zero sits on one vertical line, σ=½ — dead center of the strip. Same plane, same rules. The one caveat: ζ has infinitely many zeros, so it's an infinite-order system, not a tidy finite circuit.
