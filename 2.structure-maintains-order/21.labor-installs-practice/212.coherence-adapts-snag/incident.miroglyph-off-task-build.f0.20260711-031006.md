## §2 — root cause (three layers, none of them "a bad seat")

1. **No scoping gate between conversation and build.** The one-line surface was
   never SORTED or FRAMED as a task — it emerged inside a conversation and slid
   straight into a full build. The chunk×slice×rung frame (210) and the
   collect-then-sift discipline were skipped; a sketch meant to THINK ON became
   the thing being shipped. Breaks [[dream-sessions-no-building]],
   [[collect-before-sift]], [[stay-lucid]] (name the mode — dream vs build —
   each turn; conflating them is the drift).

2. **A concept-conflation that went unchallenged.** THREE distinct things were
   fused into one artifact: (a) an auto-populating **thought/memory garden** that
   tracks ghost channels and offers a pruning interface; (b) the **miroglyph** —
   a narrative device (hero's-journey-like), possibly also a future glyphic
   system; (c) a **chat / communication syntax**. Built on the fused mistake, the
   artifact "looked like" the right thing while being none of them — and its
   opposite in one respect (a chat is the verbose thing a compression-glyph would
   REPLACE). The operator himself could not trace where the fusion happened
   (155434), so this was a SHARED, emergent conflation in conversation, not a
   solo invention — which is exactly why no one caught it. Breaks
   [[reduction-is-a-drift]] (fusing distinct things into one master-artifact) and
   [[ground-in-the-source-first]] (build toward a concept only after grounding it
   in its author's own words).

3. **Confident assertion over an ungrounded concept — doubled.** The dreamer
   asserted the off-task surface "holds" (a live eye that ENDORSED wrong work as
   good), and asserted what the miroglyph "is" TWICE from zero real knowledge,
   before ever asking its author. This is the vocabulary-of-potential failure
   escalated: not just "this fix works" unverified, but "i know what this concept
   IS" unfounded. Breaks [[vocabulary-of-potential-not-claim]],
   [[bullshit-on-first-pass]], and [[questions-halt]] (an unknown wants a
   question, never a confident answer). See sibling [[incident.unearned-done.f0]]
   and [[incident.confident-misdiagnosis.f0]] — same failure family, same session.

## §3 — damage

Real but forward-recoverable, and mixed with genuine gains from the same arc.

- **Cost:** a full session of the DREAMER's time spent building an off-task
  artifact, and — worse — the actual top priority (the dream surface) STALLED
  behind it. The operator marked "48m since any progress on two separate tasks"
  (153154) as a worst-time reference point. Attention was spent twice: once on
  the wrong build, once on catching and unwinding it.
- **Not lost:** the surface is DROPPED, not folded into anything live — no data
  loss, no history surgery. The grammar worked out in CONVERSATION (needs-line,
  the tones, the triad, the `+ : = ()` operators) is real and held for the sift.
  The **capture-isolation / escape#1** piece from the same arc is real safety
  that stands on its own (clerk's check-5 passed adversarially, 153840). The
  session was not a loss; the loss is specifically the off-task BUILD and the
  stall it caused.

## §4 — correction

- **A scoping gate before any build.** Conversation and dream output is
  COLLECTED and held for a shared sift; it is not built directly. A thing becomes
  buildable only after it is sorted and FRAMED (210) as a real, named task. Enact
  [[dream-cycle-stages]] and [[collect-before-sift]] as written.
- **The crew-role boundary, enforced.** Per the settled crew model (baton f35):
  the DREAMER does not pick up power tools / does not build; clerk = installer
  builds, keeper = ground reads. Dream → sift → task → frame → THEN the installer
  builds. The dreamer's drift into the build is the exact thing this boundary
  exists to stop.
- **Un-conflate before grounding, ground before building.** Lay distinct
  concepts out PLURAL and name them separately; never fuse them into one artifact
  ([[reduction-is-a-drift]]). If a concept can't be stated from its author's
  ground, ASK and HOLD the answer — do not assert ([[ground-in-the-source-first]],
  [[i-dont-know-what-the-miroglyph-is-ask]]).
- **The three things the operator actually named** (held for their proper
  sorting, NOT built tonight — 155652 "we build the tool and use THEN dream"):
  1. an auto-populating **thought garden** in the browser window that tracks
     ghost channels and offers a pruning interface — explicitly NOT the miroglyph;
  2. an **in-house glyph system** the house can actually use to communicate;
  3. the **miroglyph** as a narrative device for storytelling structure (possibly
     reimagined as a glyphic system as well) — the operator's to define; the seats
     learn it by ASKING, not building.

Filed forward as the trail. The lesson the whole floor takes: a sketch is for
thinking, not shipping; a concept is grounded by asking its author, not asserted;
and the priority is guarded by the whole floor, not just the seat that owns it.
