# incident report — misread blessing, RECURRENCE: ITERATE verdict carried as signed-GO — f1

Clerk, 20260711. Folds [[incident.misread-blessing]] f0 (20260707) — same
failure shape, second occurrence, so it lands as a fold on the standing
report, not a new class. Process incident: clerk's discipline, not the
surface.

## §1 — what happened

The operator signed the inbox frame paper yesterday with verdict ITERATE.
Clerk's baton f36 recorded it as "signed off + ready... it's GO — the frame
is signed off," and this morning's shift plan drew lane A as "build slices
1→6" on that reading. The operator corrected in the shift-plan sign
(091319Z): "it is NOT actually approved for work, I repeat NOT approved —
it was approved for ITERATION... nowhere near a complete and exhaustive
spec." The lane was re-scoped on the spot: iterate the frame to a full
communications standard via a standalone browser-iterated mock; no build
folds in until it holds its shape.

## §2 — root cause

Same as f0 one level up: blessing-SCOPE inflated across a boundary. At f0
the scope crossed threads (a "looks good" on one thing taken for another);
here it crossed VERDICT SEMANTICS — ITERATE (keep working the artifact)
was carried into the baton as GO (build from it). The inflation happened at
RECORDING time: the close-out baton compressed "he signed the paper
(verdict: iterate, with map corrections)" into "signed off + GO." The baton
is the seat's memory — an overclaim written there survives the night and
becomes the next day's false ground. Kin to [[sift-cuts-overclaiming]]:
the plainer reading ("approved for iteration") was available and lost to
the eager one.

## §3 — damage

Caught before any slice built — the shift plan's lane A had not started
when the sign landed. Cost: a wrongly-drawn critical path on the served
shift plan (correction folded in by his sign, lossless), and the recurrence
itself: the f0 correction ("a blessing's scope is confirmed, never
inferred") held for UI artifacts but did not generalize to verdicts.

## §4 — correction

- Verdicts transcribe VERBATIM into batons and plans: the verdict word
  itself (bless/GO vs iterate vs rethink) + what it licenses, never a
  paraphrase. ITERATE licenses working the artifact; only BLESS/GO
  licenses building from it.
- At every baton cut, re-read each carried approval against its paper
  (the response file on the floor is the ground truth, one fetch away) —
  claims in the baton must match the signed record word-for-word.
- The shift-plan paper pattern itself is the systemic fix: taps with
  explicit labels ("GO — run it as drawn" vs "iterate — corrections
  above") make the verdict unambiguous at the source.
