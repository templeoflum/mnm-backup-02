This is the principle layer: what the work taught, sorted by whether its shape is agreed. **Settled** = survived contact — verified, corrected, or refused into its final form. **Floating** = felt and partially evidenced, shape not yet agreed; nothing here may be built from until it settles. Entries are numbered for pointing at while we walk. *Items added at this fold are marked; placements there are the builder's call and demotable on a word.*

**The frame this map serves:** the most lovingly crafted measurement device for the new age — every paradigm relatable on one bench. *Relatable* means the ladder (S5) plus the honest registration of where translation breaks (S12): the seam is the device's conscience, not its obstacle. In the user's words, the sharpest self-definition yet: **a calibration tool for all things same AND all things different, not confusing the two** — the ladder grades the sameness; the refusals and the seam grade the difference; confusing them is the one sin the instrument may never commit. *(sharpened at the post-clutch-six walk: the tool does not decide same-or-different — it measures the similarity structure and the difference structure simultaneously, as quantities, and never collapses one into the other.)* RH is the first patient; TS's full vision is the horizon. **The way:** first principles, cell-level, small steps because they feel good; build → feel → log prediction-vs-actual → let the toys breed the next generation; two generations, then stop and review. Walk-protocol governs: ideas float until we agree on shape. **Prediction discipline (added at this fold): name the bath** — every magnitude prediction states the background it sits in before naming the number; the f0 journal's seven-miss ledger earned this rule.

## SETTLED

**S1 · The walked path.** Verify the instrument against a distance already known before trusting it on one that isn't. — *Earned by:* every kernel proven headless against analytic ground truth before any UI; the 24% FFT "error" traced to spectral leakage, not the kernel; three confident debugging hypotheses killed under test before the file was exonerated. *(Re-earned fourteen times in the nursery.)*

**S2 · The noise floor is measured, not declared.** A tolerance is honest only when it is relative to the instrument's own observed breathing. — *Earned by:* the signature fix (rollStat noise floor replacing magic tiers); the symmetry score defined as chamfer ÷ the cloud's own nearest-neighbour baseline. *(Sharpened by cell 09: the floor alone is insufficient — see S2's sibling clause in the landscape-dip criterion — and by cell 14's declared estimator floor.)*

**S3 · The two ½s.** The bench owns the *coordinate-free-ness* of ½ (normalization conserves one unit, so every chart shares one midpoint); only the loaded form decides whether that ½ is a *mirror* (a fixed point of a symmetry). Scaling makes the first; reflection makes the second. — *Earned by:* the original pushback and concession; the-fold; the probe's verdict. *(Made tactile by cells 05–06: the pebble moves the ruler, never the mirror.)*

**S4 · One invariant, many rulers.** Agreement across coordinate systems is real, checkable, and the bench's founding thesis. — *Earned by:* TS's own caliperCrossSystem reading one separation in four rulers to machine precision — it predates all of this work; we re-derived it before recognizing it.

**S5 · The ladder of sameness.** Three rungs, in rising strength: **agreement** (numbers concur across rulers) → **class** (signatures within the measured noise floor) → **identity** (same spectrum). The top rung is modularity's gift: the strongest cross-system statement is two families being *one*, certified by a shared spectral invariant — an identity, not an approximation. — *Earned by:* caliper (agreement); signature + S2 (class); the FLT detour, with the false rhyme caught and refused first. *(Cell 13 added the strictest rung-three exhibit we own: a sum identical to its other computation to the last bit.)*
> **AMENDED at the post-clutch-six walk (user audit):** not a ladder of *sameness* but a ladder of **comparison-statement strength**. nothing ever truly agrees from every angle — variation, deviation, noise, vibration are the premise, and no view sees every plane at once; we translate and compare across translations to **measure deviations, never to assert sameness**. "identity" is re-scoped: it is a property of the bench's own computations (bit-identical sums, exact brackets — S18's realm) or of imported proof; it is **never issuable about loaded specimens from measurement**. the strongest sentence measurement can say: *below every floor we own, in every chart we tried, blind spots declared — provisional.*

**S6 · L-functions are natively epicyclic.** Read through its zeros, ζ decomposes into TS's own primitive — each term a scaling ∘ rotation — and the dictionary runs both ways. The clock is log x, not t. — *Earned by:* explicit-formula, zeros-from-primes, prime-orchestra; amplitude verified as x^β/|ρ|. *(Re-earned at cell scale by 11–14: thirty zeros rebuilt the staircase; wrong zeros could not have.)*

**S7 · RH is one shared breathing rate.** What ½ pins is each wave's *growth exponent* — every thread swells in the same proportion as it crosses; their loudnesses differ (1/|ρ|) always. The invariant is the rate, never the height. — *Earned by:* the structure of S6; the myth-keystone correction. *(Made tactile by cell 14: a rate error grows with x; a tuning error stays even.)*

**S8 · Fold, don't listen.** No finite listening can split near-rational from rational (Diophantine); centers are found by symmetry, not spectra. The continued fraction is the right tool because it measures *how close*, not *whether*. — *Earned by:* two honest failures plus the-fold's success. *(Arrived as a lullaby in cell 10 and as a parenthesis in cell 02.)*

**S9 · Refusals are load-bearing.** Every real advance was a registered boundary; the instrument earned trust each time it declined to speak. — *Earned by:* the whole arc. *(Now a construction rule — see S17.)*

**S10 · Texture is a fingerprint.** The zeros are *stiffer than white* — hyperuniform, long-range fluctuations frozen — and that stiffness is the stability. Primes pile where zeros repel; opposite ends of one crystal↔white dial. (*Asterisk kept:* primes-as-Poisson is conjectural/limited in the literature; the contrast as measured on our toys is real.) — *Earned by:* noise-floor, dual-textures, zero-statistics.

**S11 · Flatness means no view separates it.** The β-extent invariant reads identically across every embedding tried — "on the line" is better said as "not canonically separable from it in any chart." — *Earned by:* the headless embedding sweep; rh-flatness toys.

**S12 · The seam exists and is measured.** Radial/scaling content maps cleanly between ζ and the staged form; angular/rotational content is obstructed by commutativity structure. The seam's existence is settled; everything about its meaning floats (F1, F10). — *Earned by:* the commutator measurements; reading SCHED in the source.

**S13 · The body is a ruler.** The mythic grammar is the device's most extreme coordinate system — lossy in names, lossless in relations — and falsifiable: the keystone correction proved a myth can be measured wrong and fixed. — *Earned by:* the-crease, written, then corrected by S7.

**S14 · Invariants trade robustness for information.** Winding survives almost anything and says little; cross-ratio says more and breaks sooner. Choosing a ruler is choosing a point on that tradeoff. — *Earned by:* inv-1-winding, inv-2-crossratio. *(Echoed by cell 09: a smooth bulk localizes its mirror weakly — sharpness of a minimum is itself information.)*

**S15 · Projection, never shadow.** *(folded at review one.)* A projection is a lawful reading with a known chart — you know what it keeps, what it drops, and by what law; paired readings are lossless. A shadow forgets lawlessly. Invariants are precisely what survive lawful maps. — *Earned by:* cell 01 r1's axis readings; cell 05's declared chart; cell 08's ellipse as the catalog of every 1-D reading; propagated through the dream list.

**S16 · Clock knobs and specimen knobs.** *(folded at review one.)* If a knob preserves the trace's geometry it is a clock knob: accumulate, bend only the future, never restart. If it changes the curve it is a specimen knob: restart honestly. — *Earned by:* cell 01 r1 (the teleport fixed), cells 02–04 conforming, the criterion holding unbroken across the nursery.

**S17 · A cell's truth lives at its refusal.** *(folded at review one.)* Every honest toy carries exactly one thing it will not do, and that refusal is where its teaching lives — S9 promoted from observation to construction rule. — *Earned by:* fourteen cells, fourteen refusals, none decorative (see the f1 journal's index).

**S18 · Live identity on display.** *(folded at review one.)* The toy verifies itself in front of the player: two computations of one truth shown agreeing in public ("measured / the ellipse says"; the true tone standing in its own spike; the bit-exact receipt). Verification moved from the builder's process into the instrument's face. — *Earned by:* cells 08, 10, 13, 14.

**S19 · Resolution is purchased lawfully.** *(folded at review one.)* Patience buys sharpness at a measurable rate: spectral width ≈ 2π/T_listen (cell 10: 4.00 and 4.00 per 4× listening); growth-discrimination gain = (x-band)^Δβ (cell 14: 1.366 law, 1.38 measured). Range and listening time are the same currency; say the price when you claim the reading. — *Earned by:* cells 10 and 14, the second via a failed prediction that resolved into the law.
