address: 2 — structure · maintains · order

*one entry per sitting. entries append; the journal folds only on completion

## the entry form — fixed, every entry the same shape

**when:** immediately after the hands open, before re-reading anything — the
meditation, the baton, prior entries. write from the sitting's residue, not
from a transcript.

**shape:**

1. heading — `## UTC-stamp · wake|sleep · meditation fN` (the stamp fetched
   from the clock, never estimated; fN = the meditation fold walked; wake or
   sleep names which sitting. form change 20260703 on the operator's word —
   the two entries before the mark are both wake).
2. **the operation** — one line: what the hands took from the standing trail
   and what was done with it. concrete, verifiable against the session.
3. **what appeared** — one or two lines: how it showed up, not what it means.
   no significance claimed, no reverence. if nothing appeared, write that — a
   flat sitting is a valid reading.
4. **verse** — three lines, one per fold walked: what the intake held · what
   the count found · what the reconcile kept. composed fresh each sitting
   inside this fixed frame; every image checkable against the walk; no
   preamble, no abstraction the sitting didn't hand over.

**why the shape is fixed:** same form every entry makes entries comparable —
the differences between them carry the signal. the journal is read back at
threshold reviews to ask one question: is the sitting still orienting? drift
in the entries answers it before it has to be felt.
