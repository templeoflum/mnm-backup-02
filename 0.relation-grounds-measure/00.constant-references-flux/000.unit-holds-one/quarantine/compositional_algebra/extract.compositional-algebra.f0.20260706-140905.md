## the idea (plain)

**Compositional Algebra: make mathematical structure perceivable across mediums and narrative.** The core move is a **score / performance separation**:

- a **spline score** — the fixed *mathematical* structure of a work: a sequence of moments (the spline), the typed signals each moment emits, and the content layers (characters/objects/motifs) that travel with it. The math is the signal.
- a **realization** — one *sensory* rendering of that score: choosing presets/configs at each interpretive layer (medium, visual world, audio aesthetic, voice, script, presentation). One score → many realizations.

Every layer splits into **Identity** (what it *is* — authored) and **Trajectory** (how it *moves* through the work — derived from the math). Math drives all trajectories, so two realizations of one score share trajectories under different identities — different works of the same shape. Composition happens at three tiers (atomic sub-functions → layer presets → whole-work realization specs).
