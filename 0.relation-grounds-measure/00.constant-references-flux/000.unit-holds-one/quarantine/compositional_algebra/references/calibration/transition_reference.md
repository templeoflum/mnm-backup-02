# Transition Reference

Two transitions for *Calibration*. Each transition carries non-derivable authoring: bridging anchors (specific sensory elements that must hold consistent across the join), continuity polish requirements (what must be true of the rendering across the join), and anti-drift constraints (specific prohibitions on what must not happen at the join).

Per-modality continuity content (what carries vs. what shifts across each layer) is *not* authored here — it is re-derivable from comparing the adjacent movements' per-modality signals in the spline. Beat-level continuity grades and felt-quality contributions at the join are authored in the spline's beat-authoring sections, not here.

---

## 1 → 2 (Crossing → At the Window)

- **Grade:** C¹ (direction match, magnitude differs)
- **Felt quality:** shift — the arrival is felt as a redirect rather than as continuous unfolding. The Figure overshoots and settles.

### Bridging elements

- The Figure (continuous presence)
- The Window (visible in 1, foregrounded in 2)
- The room (the space the crossing happened across)
- The footfall ceasing (sonic anchor)

### Continuity polish requirements

- The arrival must be *felt* — not narrated as a separate event, but rendered as the moment forward motion stops
- The Window must remain visually consistent across the join (same window, now at hand rather than distant)
- The shift from rhythm-of-walking to rhythm-of-breath must be audible

### Anti-drift

- Do not let the Figure pause mid-crossing; the crossing is continuous until the join
- Do not render the arrival as triumph or accomplishment; it is settling, not climax
- Do not let the room dissolve at the join; the room persists as context

---

## 2 → 3 (At the Window → Opening)

- **Grade:** C² (direction and magnitude match)
- **Felt quality:** unfold — the opening emerges from within the held state. There is no felt boundary between movements.

### Bridging elements

- The Figure (continuous, gesture forming across the join)
- The Window (state changes but identity persists)
- The latch (sonic anchor — its first sound at end of 2, full sound at start of 3)
- The held drone (carries from 2 into 3, opening as the window opens)

### Continuity polish requirements

- The opening must *unfold* — the join cannot be felt as a cut or shift
- The held state must carry into the opening rather than being interrupted by it
- The air entering is the audible signature of the join; render it gently

### Anti-drift

- Do not narrate the opening as event-with-result; it is process
- Do not introduce new sonic elements unrelated to the opening
- Do not let the Figure step away from the window during the join; the gesture continues across the join

## Cross-Transition Notes

**The two grades verify Shape rules:**

- 1→2 (C¹): direction-only match (both rising) with magnitude mismatch (steep → gradual). Tests that direction-match-only produces *shift*-quality joins.
- 2→3 (C²): direction and magnitude match (both gradual falling). Tests that full match produces *unfold*-quality joins.

A realization that renders 1→2 as smoothly as 2→3 (or vice versa) has failed to read Shape correctly. The continuity grades constrain the felt quality of the joins; this is observable in the output.

**Bridging discipline:**

Both transitions name specific bridging elements (the Figure, the Window, the room/sonic anchors). These are the anti-drift anchors — what must remain consistent across the join even as surface elements shift.
