# Object Reference

One object. Minimum viable scope to exercise the object signal across three movements.

---

## The Window

The threshold the Figure approaches, arrives at, and opens.

> Per framework's Identity/Trajectory split: **Identity invariants** and **Identity flexibility** are the Window's Identity (what the Window is, across the work). The **Per-movement behavior** below is the Window's Trajectory (how the Window's role moves through the work) — coordinated with the spline's math.

### Construction

- **Identity invariants:** a window in a wall; present across all three movements; the central object of the work
- **Identity flexibility:** specific style (any architectural period), specific size, specific glazing, specific view through it — all open to realization-level choice
- **Continuity grade across movements:** C² for the window's physical presence; state changes (closed → opening → open) tracked per movement

### Per-movement behavior

**Movement 1 (Crossing):**
- **Role:** goal — the destination of the crossing
- **State:** closed
- **Presence:** visible at the far side of the room; distant; not yet reached

**Movement 2 (At the Window):**
- **Role:** foregrounded — the central object of the held movement
- **State:** closed (still); the latch present, not yet touched
- **Presence:** at hand; the Figure is at the window; the boundary is felt

**Movement 3 (Opening):**
- **Role:** the site of the act — the window opens
- **State:** opening, then open
- **Presence:** the threshold becoming aperture; what was boundary becomes opening

### Math primitive correspondence

- **Movement 1:** the window as line segment endpoint (the destination point)
- **Movement 2:** the window as threshold (boundary as boundary)
- **Movement 3:** the window as aperture (boundary as opening)

The object embodies the math primitive arc of the work.

### Cross-medium modulation

- **In narrative:** the Window may be named directly or referred to obliquely; described in detail or sketched in outline; the realization chooses
- **In image:** the Window must be visually consistent across the three movements (same window, same style, same position) — only its state changes (closed → opening → open)
- **In audio:** the Window's sonic signature is silence in movements 1 and 2 (it doesn't yet make sound), then the latch and the air entering in movement 3

### Connection to other layers

- **Spline:** receives the Window's per-movement role and state as part of the Object signal
- **Audio surface:** reads the Window's sonic signatures (latch, air) — audio renders the Window's events as the temporal substrate develops
- **Visual surface:** reads the Window's per-movement state; medium and visual world render the Window within the audio substrate
- **Pass 6 (substitution):** reads the Window's continuity material (brass latch, tarnish, consistent material details) and substitutes into all three prompts (image, audio, narrative) cross-modally — same brass latch appears in image prompt, audio prompt, and narrative prompt
- **Pass 5 prompts:** receive the Window's per-movement scene material (state this movement); do not receive continuity material
- **Motif:** the Window is the locus of the Approach-and-Arrive motif's three phases
