## what miroglyph is (plain)

A **navigation topology for mythic transformation** — a fixed skeleton of positions you move through to structure a journey, in any medium (story / ritual / divination / game / performance). *"Myth is not discovered, it is navigated."* Structure without prescription: it gives you the positions and their relationships; you choose the path.

**The skeleton (stable across every version): 19 points.**
- **18 nodes = 3 arcs × 6 conditions.**
  - 3 arcs (thematic modes): **Descent/Shadow (D)** · **Resonance/Mirror (R)** · **Emergence/Mythogenesis (E)**
  - 6 conditions (phases): Dawn/Initiation · Immersion/Encounter · Crucible/Crisis · Alignment/Harmony · Unveiling/Wisdom · Return/Integration
- **1 center: Nontion (∅)** — the pause/reset; integration-in-absence; traversable but not a node.
- Each node: dual naming (functional + evocative) + code (D1…E6) + title ("D1 = The Catalyst Shard") + role + tone.
- Relationships make structure *visible*: **polarity pairs** (conditions 1↔4, 2↔5, 3↔6), **condition-resonance** (D1/R1/E1 share initiation across arcs), an optional **motif/evidence-marker** system (6 categories O/A/Q/B/F/M; primary locked by condition, secondary by arc).
- **Fractal**: the same topology nests at any scale (beat ⊂ scene ⊂ chapter ⊂ book).

## the reconciliation — one skeleton, TWO realizations

The same 3×6+center skeleton exists in two framings:

| | **Authoring map** (`references/v4/`, mythic-library) | **Physics engine** (`references/engine/`, mythos/Temple-of-Lum) |
|---|---|---|
| the 3 | **arcs** — Descent · Resonance · Emergence (narrative *modes*) | **lenses** — Fracture · Echo · Fusion (interaction *verbs*) |
| the 6 | **conditions** — Dawn…Return (narrative *phases*) | **pressures** — Threshold · Gravity · Friction · Resonance · Tension · Silence (*forces*, 0–1 per node) |
| the model | a **static map** you author with — draw connections, save paths | a **dynamical system** — traverse, accumulate pressure, engine detects patterns (4 captures, fluidity, avoidance) and *perturbs the topology*; no win condition |

**DECIDED (operator, 20260706): both — layered. The map is the keystone, the engine is the terrain.** Same relation the lattice has to itself: you impose the map (the 19 positions + relationships — the readable structure) to navigate the terrain (the engine's forces/dynamics — the reality underneath). **Start with the map.** The physics-engine was the ambitious layer — deferred until the map stands. So the rebuild grounds the **authoring-topology first** (positions, dual-naming, polarity/resonance, Nontion, fractal scale); the pressures/lenses/detection engine is the later terrain-layer the map sits over.
