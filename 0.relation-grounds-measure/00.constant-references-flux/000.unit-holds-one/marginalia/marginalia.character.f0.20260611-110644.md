# marginalia — the character
**f0 · companion to the frame, the world, the style, the sound. the iconography, and the one inhabitant. a drawn specimen accompanies this doc.**

## the witness
the page has no narrator, no host, no face. the world is empty of persons except one thing: **a hand attached to no one**, arriving from outside the frame to point. this is what manicules were — interventions from beyond the text, drawn by readers the author never met, hands reaching across centuries into someone else's page. ours is a disembodied, silent witness at the seat of the work. the page reads as first-person exploration of a dark field; the living margin is the HUD, doing the keeping at the edge; the manicule is the only event that is not the mathematics. when it appears, something is being attended to.

## the behavior law
the manicule never decorates and never performs. no walk cycle, no bounce, no idle animation, no mascot duty. it **arrives — settles — points — holds — releases**. supernatural intervention at tending-pace. it is rare; its appearance carries weight, like the rubric. the rest of the time the witness is present only as the margin itself: the seat is there, occupied by silence.

## the mark
the logo depicts the most famous thing in the history of margins: **what wasn't there.**
- a vast dark field, mostly empty — the emptiness does the work, even at 48 pixels.
- one thin vertical rule, dividing the field unevenly. the narrow side is ours.
- on the rule, **four small ticks** — ruling marks to any passerby; to anyone who asks, four points on a line: the minimum drawing that carries a cross-ratio, the invariant Klein's program crowns. the secret rides inside the structural bone. never declared, only answered.
- in the narrow side, the **tiny manicule**, pointing across the rule into the wide emptiness — at the imagined proof the margin was too narrow to contain. the mark claims nothing because it depicts nothing, pointed at, faithfully.

## the invariance law (Klein, for logos)
the mark is not a fixed artwork; it is **an invariant under a declared group**. it may rotate, shear, project, redraw in any piece's phosphor, be traced into existence by a moving point — and it remains the mark so long as the kept things hold:
**kept: that the hand points · that the rule divides unevenly · that the ticks are four · that the field is mostly empty.**
**let go: orientation, scale, color, weight, position, medium.**
the identity is the orbit, not the glyph.

## the reject pile
klein bottles, möbius strips, golden spirals, π, ∞, mandelbrot zooms — the genre's claimed clip-art, gorgeous and worn smooth. not ours. our pointers are quieter: the hand, the rule, the ticks, the dark.

## the wordmark
plain lowercase `marginalia`, IBM Plex Mono, dim ink, small. only when needed. the avatar carries no text at all.

## the character's own refusal
the witness does not speak, does not own, does not appear on demand. a piece that uses the hand as a sticker has lost it. the manicule is spent like the rubric — rarely, and meaning it.
