**origin** (precursors) — Newton (interpolation/quadrature), Gauss (interpolation). **[confirmed as precursor tradition]**

**origin** (modern field) — John von Neumann & Herman H. Goldstine, "Numerical Inverting of Matrices of High Order," *Bull. Amer. Math. Soc.* 53(11) (Nov 1947):1021–1099 — "the birth certificate of numerical analysis" (Bultheel & Cools). [[source.vonneumann-goldstine-1947]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — error analysis founded by the 1947 paper (per Goldstine 1972); systematic backward-error theory by James H. Wilkinson, *Rounding Errors in Algebraic Processes* (1963) and *The Algebraic Eigenvalue Problem* (1965). carry both, in that order. [[source.wilkinson-1963]] [[source.wilkinson-1965]] **[confirmed / confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — Trefethen & Bau, *Numerical Linear Algebra* (SIAM, 1997); or Süli & Mayers, *An Introduction to Numerical Analysis* (Cambridge, 2003). [[source.trefethen-bau-1997]] [[source.suli-mayers-2003]]
