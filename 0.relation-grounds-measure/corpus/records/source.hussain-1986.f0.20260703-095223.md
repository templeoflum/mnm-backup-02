A. K. M. F. Hussain, "Coherent structures and turbulence," *Journal of Fluid Mechanics* 173 (1986): 303–356.

Contains — the influential review/definition of a **coherent structure** as a connected region of the turbulent flow over which a fundamental quantity (typically vorticity) is instantaneously correlated in phase. Establishes coherent structures as measurable, dynamically significant, and manipulable — the conceptual bridge between "turbulence as disorder" and the possibility of re-channeling a turbulent flow by acting on its organized motions.
