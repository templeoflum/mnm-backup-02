Heleen A. Slagter, Antoine Lutz, Lawrence L. Greischar, Andrew D. Francis, Sander Nieuwenhuis, James M. Davis & Richard J. Davidson, "Mental training affects distribution of limited brain resources," *PLoS Biology* 5, no. 6 (2007): e138.

Contains — evidence that three months of intensive mindfulness (Vipassana / open-monitoring) training **reduces the attentional blink**: after training, practitioners more often detected the second of two rapid targets, with a smaller P3b to the first target — read as a more efficient allocation of limited attentional resources, so less of the perceptual stream is missed. A core empirical support for the "open monitoring widens the receptive channel" claim (cf. van Vugt & Slagter follow-ups).
