Bernard Williams and Thomas Nagel, "Moral Luck," *Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society, Supplementary Volumes* 50 (1976): 115–151 — the joint symposium (Williams 115–135, Nagel 137–151) naming **moral luck**: factors beyond an agent's control shape moral judgment, challenging the Kantian control principle. Nagel distinguishes resultant, circumstantial, constitutive, and causal luck. **[confirmed as attribution]**
