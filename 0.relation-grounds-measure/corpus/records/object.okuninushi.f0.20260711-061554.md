**statement (plain)** — **Ōkuninushi-no-Kami** is the great earthly kami who ruled Izumo. A descendant of **Susanoo**, he survived his brothers' murderous plots and, with the dwarf-kami Sukunabikona, built up and healed the land of Izumo. In the **kuni-yuzuri** ("ceding of the land") he handed the terrestrial realm to **Amaterasu**'s envoys in return for the building of a great shrine.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: earth, nation-building, medicine, love and marriage, wealth. He is the ruler and builder of the earthly realm who yields it to the heavenly line. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Ōkuninushi is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant names and accounts of the land-ceding; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
