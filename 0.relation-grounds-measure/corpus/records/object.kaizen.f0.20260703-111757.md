**statement (plain)** — Kaizen (改善, "change for the better") is improvement made continuously, in small increments, by the people who do the work, on the actual place of work (*gemba*) — as opposed to improvement delivered occasionally from above as a large innovation. Each improvement raises the standard, which becomes the new baseline for the next round; the gains are individually tiny but compound relentlessly. It is a mindset and daily practice, not a project.

**relevance** — the improvement engine of TPS, and the reason standardization is not stagnation: kaizen depends on **standardized work** ([[object.standardized-work]]) as the base it ratchets — "where there is no standard there can be no kaizen" (Imai). The disciplinary model for treating a seat's own close/handover practice as a standard to be steadily improved rather than a fixed rule [[source.imai-1986]] **[confirmed as attribution]**.
