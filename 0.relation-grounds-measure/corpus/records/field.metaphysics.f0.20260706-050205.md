**origin** — Aristotle, *Metaphysics* (c. 340 BCE) — the study of "being qua being," substance, and the categories; the name comes from the ordering of Aristotle's works (*ta meta ta physika*).

**codification** — for the personal-identity strand these thought experiments probe: John Locke, *Essay* (1689), Book II.xxvii — identity as continuity of consciousness/memory [[source.locke-1689]]; and Derek Parfit, *Reasons and Persons* (1984) — the reductionist view that identity is not what matters, only psychological continuity ("Relation R") [[source.parfit-1984]].

**door** — Michael Loux & Thomas Crisp, *Metaphysics: A Contemporary Introduction* — universals and particulars, modality, time, persistence, personal identity.

*grade s — origin (Aristotle), codification for the identity strand (Locke, Parfit), door (Loux) present. New field-home for the thought-experiments intake (quarantine, 20260706); homes the teletransporter / personal-identity puzzle (and adjacent identity cases).*
