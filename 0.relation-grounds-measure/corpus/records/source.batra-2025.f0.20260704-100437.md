R. Batra, L. Kim, I. Mandel, A. Kwatra, J. L. E, S. J. Jackson, T. Roumen, "(De)composing Craft: An Elementary Grammar for Sharing Expertise in Craft Workflows," arXiv:2506.10891 (v1 12 Jun 2025; CSCW 2026). **[confirmed]**

Role - a grammar of seven workflow patterns (granularity shifts, reflective loops, note-to-self, external links, segments, branches, revision loops) built on Knight-Stiny's "stuff/doing/sensing/things"; the CraftLink interface (Gemini 1.5 Pro turns craft videos into graphs), evaluated with N=7 expert crocheters. Argues linear step-documentation "reduces craft to a mechanical endeavor" and fails to transmit tacit knowledge (Suchman 1987); anchored in Polanyi, Schon, Ingold, Pye, Sennett.
