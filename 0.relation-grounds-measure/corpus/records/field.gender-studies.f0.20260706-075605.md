**origin** — Simone de Beauvoir, *The Second Sex* (1949) — "one is not born, but rather becomes, a woman": gender as made, not given.

**codification** — Judith Butler, *Gender Trouble* (1990) — **gender performativity**: gender is constituted by the repeated performance of gendered acts within regulatory norms, not expressed by a prior identity. [[source.butler-1990]] Draws on Foucault (power), Austin (speech acts), Derrida (iterability).

**door** — the field's readers and journals (e.g. *The Gender/Sexuality Reader*); Butler's *Gender Trouble* remains the pivotal entry.
