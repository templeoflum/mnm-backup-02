Geza Vermes, *The Complete Dead Sea Scrolls in English* (Allen Lane / Penguin, 1997). The standard accessible one-volume translation of the non-biblical Qumran scrolls — community rules, hymns, commentaries, apocalyptic and liturgical texts — with introductions; the "Complete" edition-line grew out of Vermes's 1962 *The Dead Sea Scrolls in English*.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Apocalypticism, Merkavah and Dead Sea Scrolls. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (Penguin Classics). **[confirmed as attribution]**
