J. Maldacena, "The Large N Limit of Superconformal Field Theories and Supergravity," arXiv:hep-th/9711200; *Adv. Theor. Math. Phys.* 2, 231 (1998).

Role — conjectures the duality: Type IIB string theory on AdS₅ × S⁵ is dual to N=4 supersymmetric SU(N) Yang–Mills in 3+1 dimensions ("The 't Hooft limit of 4-d N=4 super-Yang-Mills at the conformal point is shown to contain strings: they are IIB strings"). Made precise by Gubser–Klebanov–Polyakov (1998) and Witten (1998). The most highly cited paper in high-energy physics — over 10,000 citations by 2015; INSPIRE-HEP announced 15,000 in October 2019. **[confirmed]**
