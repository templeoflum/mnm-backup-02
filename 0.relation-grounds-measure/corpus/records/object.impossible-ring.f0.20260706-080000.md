**statement (plain)** — The Impossible Ring is an impossible object depicting a torus or ring-like structure with a twist or other topological manipulation that creates contradictory depth relationships incompatible with three-dimensional Euclidean space. The figure typically shows a continuous band that loops back on itself to form a closed ring, but with depth cues suggesting that the band passes alternately in front of and behind itself in ways that violate topological constraints. Unlike a Möbius strip, which has a genuine topological twist that can exist in three dimensions, the Impossible Ring presents depth relationships that cannot be consistently maintained in any valid three-dimensional configuration.
