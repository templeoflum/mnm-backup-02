Christopher S. Mackay (trans.), *The Hammer of Witches: A Complete Translation of the Malleus Maleficarum* (Cambridge University Press, 2009). A complete, annotated English translation of the Malleus Maleficarum (1486), the most influential early-modern witch-hunting manual, drawn from Mackay's critical Latin edition (2006). Foundational as the reliable modern translation; the widely available Montague Summers translation (1928) is public domain but unreliable.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Witch Hunts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
