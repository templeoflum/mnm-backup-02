**statement (plain)** — **Nergal** is the god of the underworld, of plague, war, and death. In the Akkadian tale **Nergal and Ereshkigal** he descends to the realm of the dead, and after his union with its queen **Ereshkigal** becomes her husband and shares the rule of the underworld with her. He is associated with the scorching, destructive heat of the sun and with the planet **Mars**, and his weapons and lion imagery mark him as a bringer of death by war and pestilence.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the underworld, plague and pestilence, war, sudden death; the destructive heat of the summer sun. Culture: Sumerian and Akkadian **Nergal**; astral aspect the planet **Mars**. Consort and co-ruler of the underworld with **Ereshkigal**. Chief cult centre: the city of **Kutha** and its temple. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Mesopotamian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Treated in the standard reference by Black & Green [[source.black-green-1992]]; the primary narrative source is the Akkadian tale **Nergal and Ereshkigal**. His underworld rule with Ereshkigal, plague/war domains, and Kutha cult are established; the descent-and-marriage narrative is attested tradition, variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
