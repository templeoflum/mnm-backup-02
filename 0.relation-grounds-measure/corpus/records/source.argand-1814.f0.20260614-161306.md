J.-R. Argand, "Réflexions sur la nouvelle théorie des imaginaires, suivies d'une application à la démonstration d'un théorème d'analyse," *Annales de Mathématiques* (Gergonne) 5 (1814), 197–209. Building on his 1806 *Essai sur une manière de représenter les quantités imaginaires dans les constructions géométriques*.

Role — generally credited as the first **full and rigorous** proof of the [[object.fundamental-theorem-of-algebra]], and the first to state it for polynomials with **complex** coefficients rather than only real ones. Argand's proof (the |p(z)| has a minimum, which must be zero) is the ancestor of the modern minimum-modulus proof. Reproduced (uncredited) in Cauchy's *Cours d'analyse* (1821).

Access — historical, French; digitized (Gergonne's Annales). Attribution corroborated by MacTutor, Wikipedia, and the "Ex Libris / Argand Proves the FTA" source note. **[confirmed]**
