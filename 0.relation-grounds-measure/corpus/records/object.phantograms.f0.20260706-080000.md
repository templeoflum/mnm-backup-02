**statement (plain)** — Phantograms are specialized stereoscopic images designed to create a vivid illusion of three-dimensional objects appearing to float above or project from a flat printed surface when viewed from a specific vantage point. Unlike traditional stereograms that require binocular fusion of two separate images, phantograms present a single anamorphic projection that exploits monocular depth cues including perspective, shading, and size gradients to create compelling depth perception. The technique involves carefully distorting a photographed or rendered object according to precise geometric transformations, such that when the printed image is positioned at a particular angle relative to the viewer, the anamorphic distortion is perceptually corrected and the object appears to occupy real three-dimensional space.
