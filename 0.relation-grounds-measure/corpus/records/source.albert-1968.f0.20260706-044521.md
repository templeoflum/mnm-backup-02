Hans Albert, *Traktat über kritische Vernunft* (Mohr, 1968) — reformulates the ancient Agrippan/Pyrrhonian regress as the **Munchhausen trilemma**: any justification must either regress infinitely, circle, or stop dogmatically. A cornerstone of critical rationalism. **[confirmed as attribution]**
