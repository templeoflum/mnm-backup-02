M. Merleau-Ponty, *Phenomenology of Perception* (*Phenomenologie de la perception*, 1945). **[confirmed]**

Role - the body schema (a "practical diagram of our relationships with the world," sub-reflective, organizing posture toward habitual/novel outcomes) and motor intentionality (skilled bodily understanding distinct from reflective act-intentionality), illustrated via the Schneider case; the intentional arc unifying sensation and action. Tools as extensions of the body (a chisel lets one "feel" the wood). The source for [[object.body-schema]] and [[object.motor-intentionality]].
