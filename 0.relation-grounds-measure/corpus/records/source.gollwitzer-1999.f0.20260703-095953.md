Peter M. Gollwitzer, "Implementation Intentions: Strong Effects of Simple Plans," *American Psychologist* 54, no. 7 (1999): 493–503.

Contains — the founding account of **implementation intentions**: goal intentions ("I intend to reach X") are made far more effective by pairing them with a plan of the form "When situation Y arises, I will perform response Z." This links an anticipated cue to a goal-directed response in advance, so that when the cue appears the response is initiated automatically — passing control to the environment before the moment of temptation or distraction.
