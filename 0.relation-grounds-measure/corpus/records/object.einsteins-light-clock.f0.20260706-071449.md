**statement (plain)** — A clock ticks by bouncing a photon straight up and down between two mirrors. Put it on a moving ship: to an outside observer the mirrors slide sideways, so the photon traces a longer diagonal. Since light's speed is fixed, each tick takes longer — the moving clock runs slow.

**statement (framework)** — the cleanest derivation of **time dilation** from the light-speed postulate; the factor is the Lorentz γ. A staple for teaching special relativity and the relativity of time. Established.
