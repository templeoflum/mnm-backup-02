S. A. Farmer, *Syncretism in the West: Pico's 900 Theses (1486)* (MRTS, 1998). Latin text and English translation of Giovanni Pico della Mirandola's nine hundred theses — the Renaissance program fusing scholasticism, Neoplatonism, Hermetica, and Christian Kabbalah — with Farmer's study of syncretic thought and its evolution.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Renaissance Magic sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
