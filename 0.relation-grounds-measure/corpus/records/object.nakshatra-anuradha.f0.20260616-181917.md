**statement (plain)** — Anurādhā: the seventeenth of the 27 nakṣatras (lunar mansions). Symbol: **lotus / archway**. Deity: **Mitra**. Planetary lord (Vimśottarī): **Saturn**. Theme: friendship, devotion, cooperative success.
