**origin** — (lineage) the quantitative-law tradition: Zipf's rank-frequency law and the **Menzerath–Altmann law** (as a construct grows, its constituents shrink — a scale-relation across linguistic levels, Menzerath 1954 / Altmann 1980). **[confirmed as attribution]** (classical laws named without dedicated citation in the dive; carried as lineage)

**codification** — I. Alabdulmohsin, V. Q. Tran, M. Dehghani, "Fractal Patterns May Illuminate the Success of Next-Token Prediction," arXiv:2402.01825 (NeurIPS 2024) — establishes language is self-similar (Hölder S≈0.59) and long-range dependent (Hurst H≈0.70±0.09) over information-theoretic complexity. [[source.alabdulmohsin-2024]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — V. A. Gromov, N. S. Borodin, A. S. Yerbolova, "A Language and Its Dimensions: Intrinsic Dimensions of Language Fractal Structures," *Complexity* (Wiley, 2024; arXiv:2311.10217) — non-integer intrinsic dimensions (~9) for Russian and English; the multifractal line (Ausloos 2012) is the earlier quantitative door.
