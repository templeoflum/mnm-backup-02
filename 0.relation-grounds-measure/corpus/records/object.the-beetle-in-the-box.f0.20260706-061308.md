**statement (plain)** — Everyone has a box only they can look into and calls what's inside a 'beetle'. The beetles might all differ; some boxes might be empty. Yet 'beetle' works fine in shared talk. So the thing in the box — the private object — plays no role in the word's meaning; it 'drops out of consideration.'

**statement (framework)** — the crux of Wittgenstein's **private-language argument**: words for sensations ('pain', 'beetle') get their meaning from public criteria and shared practice, not by naming a private inner object only the speaker can access. Undercuts the Cartesian inner-theatre picture without denying inner experience. Established; its force against qualia stays **open**.
