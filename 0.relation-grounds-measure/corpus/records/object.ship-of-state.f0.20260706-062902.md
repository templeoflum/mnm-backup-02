**statement (plain)** — A ship's crew, ignorant of navigation, fight over the helm and dismiss the one true navigator — who has studied stars, winds, and seasons — as a useless stargazer. Plato's point: steering a state, like steering a ship, is a *techne* requiring expertise, so rule by the untrained many is a category error.

**statement (framework)** — reframes political authority as **expert knowledge**, undermining the democratic premise of civic competence; diagnoses a self-reinforcing pathology — those lacking expertise decide whether expertise matters. The classic anti-democratic (and pro-epistocracy) argument; a live challenge to democratic theory. Established; contested (**open**).
