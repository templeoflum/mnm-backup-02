**origin** — the oral kami traditions of Japan, codified in the **Kojiki** (712) and the **Nihon Shoki** (720) — the creation of the islands by **Izanagi** and **Izanami**, the sun-goddess **Amaterasu**, and the descent of the imperial line. [[source.kojiki]] [[source.nihon-shoki]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — the two 8th-century chronicles, the *Kojiki* and the *Nihon Shoki*, which fix the classical myth cycle (with the Nihon Shoki preserving variant traditions). [[source.kojiki]] [[source.nihon-shoki]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Michael Ashkenazi, *Handbook of Japanese Mythology* (ABC-CLIO, 2003), the standard modern English reference to the kami and the folk-Buddhist deities. [[source.ashkenazi-2003]]

*grade a — origin + codification (Kojiki / Nihon Shoki), door (Ashkenazi) present. A single-tradition field under [[field.comparative-mythology]]. Field-home for the Japanese pantheon figure intake (20260711). NOTE: the field spans Shinto kami AND Buddhist-syncretic figures (Hachiman, Benzaiten, Jizō, Enma, etc.) per the living syncretic tradition; the Buddhist imports carry their non-Japanese origin in-record.*
