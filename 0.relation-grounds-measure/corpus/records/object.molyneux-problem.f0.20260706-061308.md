**statement (plain)** — A person blind from birth learns to tell a sphere from a cube by touch, then gains sight. Could they say by vision alone, without touching, which is which? Molyneux asked Locke; Locke answered no — the ideas from touch and sight are distinct. Leibniz disagreed, holding that reason bridges the modalities.

**statement (framework)** — a foundational probe in the philosophy of **perception** and the innate/acquired debate: is spatial form amodal, or must cross-modal correspondence be learned? Held et al. (Project Prakash, 2011) give a nuanced answer — no immediate transfer, but it emerges within days. Established as a thought experiment; now partly empirical, its full reading **open**.

**basis** — Molyneux's question in Locke's *Essay* II.ix.8 (2nd ed., 1694) [[source.locke-1689]]; empirical test in Held et al., *Nature Neuroscience* (2011) [[source.held-2011]]. **[confirmed as attribution]**
