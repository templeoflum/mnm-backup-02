**origin** — Virgil's **Aeneid** (c. 29–19 BCE), the Roman national epic of Aeneas' journey from Troy to Italy, together with Ovid's **Fasti**, the poem on the Roman religious calendar. [[source.virgil-aeneid]] [[source.ovid-fasti]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — **Livy**, *Ab Urbe Condita* Book 1, the connected narrative of Rome's legendary foundation (Aeneas, Romulus and Remus, the early kings), with Ovid's **Metamorphoses** Books 14–15 for the Italian material. [[source.livy]] [[source.ovid-metamorphoses]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Pierre Grimal, *The Dictionary of Classical Mythology* (Blackwell, 1986), the standard modern reference to both Greek and Roman myth. [[source.grimal-1986]]
