JCGM 100:2008, *Evaluation of measurement data — Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM)*. The 2008 reissue (minor corrections only) of the GUM first published 1993, corrected and reprinted 1995. Equivalent to ISO/IEC Guide 98-3:2008.

Role — the consolidating standard for measurement uncertainty: Type A / Type B evaluation, combined and expanded uncertainty, coverage factor. Originated in ISO Technical Advisory Group 4 (TAG4); responsibility passed to the JCGM, now maintained by JCGM Working Group 1.

Access — free: JCGM 100:2008 PDF (bipm.org). **[confirmed]**
