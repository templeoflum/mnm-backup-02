**statement (plain)** — The Chromatic Adaptation Illusion encompasses a family of phenomena in which prolonged viewing of a chromatically biased stimulus causes systematic shifts in the perceived color of subsequently viewed stimuli. Following adaptation to a colored field, neutral gray surfaces appear tinted with the complementary color, and colored surfaces undergo predictable shifts in hue and saturation. The magnitude and time course of these shifts depend on the intensity, duration, and spectral composition of the adapting stimulus, as well as the spatial and temporal relationship between adapter and test stimuli.
