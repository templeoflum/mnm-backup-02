F. J. Odling-Smee, K. N. Laland, M. W. Feldman, *Niche Construction: The Neglected Process in Evolution* (Princeton University Press, 2003).

Role — the biological niche-construction theory the cognitive versions draw on: organisms actively modify their own and others' selective environments, and this modification is an inheritable ecological legacy (ecological inheritance). The active-inference niche loop is developed in Constant et al., "A variational approach to niche construction," *J. R. Soc.
