**statement (plain)** — **Persephone** (Greek *Περσεφόνη*), also called **Kore** ("the Maiden"), is the daughter of **Zeus** and **Demeter** and the queen of the underworld. While gathering flowers she is abducted by **Hades**, who carries her below to be his consort; Demeter wanders the earth in grief searching for her, withholding the harvest until the gods intervene (*Homeric Hymn to Demeter*). Because Persephone has eaten the seeds of a **pomegranate** in the underworld, she cannot return wholly to the upper world: she must spend a portion of each year below with Hades and the rest above with her mother, a cycle the tradition ties to the turning of the **seasons** — Demeter's grief being the barren time. She is a central figure of the **Eleusinian Mysteries**, whose promise of a better lot in the afterlife turns on her descent and return.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the underworld and its queenship, the grain and the seasonal cycle of death and renewal, and the mystery cult of Eleusis. Roles: the abducted Maiden, the underworld queen, and the returning goddess whose ascent and descent order the year. Key attribute: the **pomegranate** whose seeds bind her below. Etymology: Greek *Persephónē* is of uncertain, much-varied form and disputed derivation; the Roman *interpretatio* identified her with **Proserpina**. The tying of her cycle to the seasons is the standard mythological reading of the Hymn. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Greek mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested in the Homeric Hymn to Demeter and Hesiod's Theogony [[source.hesiod-theogony]], among the Homeric corpus [[source.homer]], systematized in Apollodorus' Bibliotheca [[source.apollodorus-bibliotheca]]; the standard reference treatment is Hard [[source.hard-2004]]. The abduction, the pomegranate, and the seasonal cycle are attested; the etymology is disputed, and the genealogies and cult details are attested tradition, variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
