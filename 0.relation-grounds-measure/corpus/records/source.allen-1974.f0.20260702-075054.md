Thomas George Allen, *The Book of the Dead or Going Forth by Day: Ideas of the Ancient Egyptians Concerning the Hereafter as Expressed in Their Own Terms* (Oriental Institute, University of Chicago, 1974). A complete scholarly translation of the Book of the Dead spells, long regarded as far more accurate than the older Budge rendering.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Biblical & Ancient Near East. The list entry reads only "Allen - The Egyptian Book of the Dead"; the work recorded here (SAOC 37, 1974) is verified as real and matches the handle year, but the list could conceivably intend T. G. Allen's 1960 *The Egyptian Book of the Dead: Documents in the Oriental Institute Museum* (OIP 82) instead — the mapping is inferred, not confirmed. Oriental Institute (now ISAC) publications, SAOC series included, are free open-access PDFs from the institute's website. **[candidate, unconfirmed]**
