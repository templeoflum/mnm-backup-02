**statement (plain)** — the engram is the memory trace inside the head; the exogram is Donald's name for a memory held *outside* it — a knot, a tally, a written word, a photograph. Exograms outlast engrams, hold more, move across people and media, and can be retrieved and reworked by many means. Human cognition became a hybrid of the two.

**basis** — Donald (1991) introduces the exogram in his three-transitions account of cognitive evolution (mimetic → mythic → theoretic/external-symbolic-storage). Sutton (2009, 2010) develops it within the complementarity frame, noting exogram properties "are not fixed and might change depending on format and implementation."
