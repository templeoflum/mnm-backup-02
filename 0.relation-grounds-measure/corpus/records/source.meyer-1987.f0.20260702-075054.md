Marvin W. Meyer (ed.), *The Ancient Mysteries: A Sourcebook of Sacred Texts* (Harper & Row, 1987). Primary texts in translation on the mystery cults of the ancient Mediterranean — Eleusis, Dionysos, Isis and Osiris, the Great Mother, Mithras — with introductions; the primary-source companion to Burkert's study [[source.burkert-1987]].

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Ancient Greek Mysteries sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
