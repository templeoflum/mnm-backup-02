**statement (plain)** — **Yemoja** (**Yemayá** / **Iemanjá**) is the great mother **Orisha** of the waters, source of the rivers and mother of many of the Orisha. Her name is read as "mother whose children are the fish." She is the patron of motherhood and fertility, and the nurturer and protector of women and children.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: ocean, motherhood, fertility, protection, dreams. Mother of many of the Orisha; in Yorubaland associated with the River Ogun, and in the diaspora with the sea; nurturer and protector of women and children, invoked for fertility; associated with blue and white and with the sea. Diaspora names: **Yemayá** (Santería), **Iemanjá** (Candomblé). **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962) and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; her association with the River Ogun in Yorubaland shifts to the sea in the diaspora traditions of Santería and Candomblé, and her attributes vary accordingly. **[confirmed as attribution]**
