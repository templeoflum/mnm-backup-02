**statement (plain)** — Mach Bands are illusory brightness enhancements and decrements perceived at the boundaries between regions of different luminance gradients. When viewing a pattern that transitions from a uniform dark region through a ramp gradient to a uniform light region, observers perceive a dark band on the dark side of the gradient and a bright band on the light side, despite these regions having uniform luminance. The phenomenon was first systematically described by physicist Ernst Mach in 1865 and has since become one of the foundational illusions in visual neuroscience.

**statement (framework)** — category: brightness lightness contrast; perceptual mechanism(s): lateral-inhibition, spatial-filtering, orientation-selectivity.
