Michael D. Resnik, *Mathematics as a Science of Patterns* (Oxford University Press, 1997).

Contains — the parallel statement of structuralism casting mathematics as the study of **patterns** (structures) and their positions, with mathematical objects having no more identity than their place in a pattern affords. Developed alongside Shapiro's ante rem account; together the two 1997 books are the standard realist-structuralist canon.
