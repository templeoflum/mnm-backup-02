James Clerk Maxwell — the demon that sorts fast from slow molecules to build a temperature gradient without work, introduced in an 1867 letter to Tait and in *Theory of Heat* (1871); the puzzle that linked entropy to information (resolved via Landauer/Bennett: erasing the demon's memory costs entropy). **[confirmed as attribution]**
