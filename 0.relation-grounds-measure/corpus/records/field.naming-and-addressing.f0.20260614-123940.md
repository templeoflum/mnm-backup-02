**(1) encoding-as-number** — **origin** — Kurt Gödel, "Über formal unentscheidbare Sätze der Principia Mathematica und verwandter Systeme I," *Monatshefte für Mathematik und Physik* 38 (1931):173–198 — Gödel numbering. [[source.godel-1931]] **[confirmed]** **door** — Nagel & Newman, *Gödel's Proof* (1958); Hofstadter, *GEB* (1979). [[source.nagel-newman-1958]] [[source.hofstadter-1979]]

**(2) notational classification** — **origin** — Melvil Dewey, *A Classification and Subject Index for Cataloguing and Arranging the Books and Pamphlets of a Library* (Amherst MA, 1876, 44-pp. pamphlet); lineage Dewey → UDC (Otlet & La Fontaine, from 1895) → Ranganathan. [[source.dewey-1876]] **[confirmed]** **door** — Dewey's 1876 pamphlet free at Project Gutenberg (#12513) and archive.org. [[source.dewey-1876]]

**(3) resolving namespaces** — **origin** (file system): Daley & Neumann, "A General-Purpose File System for Secondary Storage," *AFIPS FJCC* (1965) [[source.daley-neumann-1965]] **[confirmed as attribution]**; **origin** (DNS): Paul Mockapetris, RFC 882 & 883 (Nov 1983), superseded by RFC 1034 & 1035 (Nov 1987, "Obsoletes: RFCs 882, 883, 973") [[source.mockapetris-rfc1034]] **[confirmed — RFC numbers/dates verified against the RFCs]**; **origin** (URI/URL): Berners-Lee, RFC 1630 (1994), generic syntax RFC 3986 (Berners-Lee, Fielding & Masinter, 2005) [[source.berners-lee-rfc3986]] **[confirmed as attribution]**. **door** — RFC 1034 (free, rfc-editor.org); RFC 3986 for URIs. [[source.mockapetris-rfc1034]] [[source.berners-lee-rfc3986]]
