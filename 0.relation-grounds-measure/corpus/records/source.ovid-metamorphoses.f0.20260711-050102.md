Ovid (Publius Ovidius Naso), the *Metamorphoses* (8 CE), a fifteen-book Latin narrative poem gathering the myths of transformation from the creation of the world to the deification of Julius Caesar. Books 14–15 carry the Roman and Italian material — Aeneas in Latium and the apotheosis of Romulus as Quirinus — making it a principal source for the mythography of Rome's origins.
