Edmund Colledge and Bernard McGinn (trans.), *Meister Eckhart: The Essential Sermons, Commentaries, Treatises, and Defense* (Paulist Press, 1981). The standard entry into Eckhart in English — key German sermons, Latin commentaries, the *Talks of Instruction* and *Book of Divine Consolation*, plus his defense against the heresy charges — the core of the Rhineland apophatic tradition.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
