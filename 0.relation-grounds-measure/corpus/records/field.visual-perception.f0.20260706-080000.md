**origin** — Hermann von Helmholtz's *Handbuch der physiologischen Optik* (1856–67) — perception as unconscious inference from sensory data.

**codification** — the Gestalt psychologists (Wertheimer, Koffka, Köhler, 1910s–30s) on grouping and organization; David Marr's computational vision (*Vision*, 1982); and the modern Bayesian / predictive-coding account of perception as inference.

**door** — Richard L. Gregory, *Eye and Brain: The Psychology of Seeing* — the standard reference on visual perception and its illusions.

*grade s — origin (Helmholtz), codification (Gestalt; Marr; Bayesian), door (Gregory) present. New field-home for the optical-illusions intake (quarantine, 20260706); homes the perceptual illusions (motion, geometric, depth, brightness, colour, ambiguous, aftereffect, form-completion, impossible-object, and cognitive-visual phenomena).*
