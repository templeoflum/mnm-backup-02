**statement (plain)** — mind-wandering is thought that drifts away from the task at hand to spontaneous, self-generated content. Far from useless, an incubation break spent on an *undemanding* task (which maximizes mind-wandering) improves later creative performance more than a demanding task, rest, or no break — the empirical core of "pull, not push": you cannot force insight, but you can create the loose conditions under which it arrives.

**statement (mechanism)** — associated with the default-mode network [[object.default-mode-network-and-altered-states]], active when attention relaxes from the external world; a candidate substrate of incubation [[object.incubation-effect]] via continued spreading activation and fixation-forgetting.

**basis** — Baird et al. 2012 [[source.baird-2012]] (undemanding-task condition beat alternatives, only for already-seen problems — genuine incubation). *Support* — robust for deliberate mind-wandering and divergent tasks. *Limits* — weaker/mixed for convergent tasks; excessive mind-wandering also costs focus and mood. **[confirmed as attribution]**
