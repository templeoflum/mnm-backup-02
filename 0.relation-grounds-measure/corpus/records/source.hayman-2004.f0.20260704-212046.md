A. P. Hayman, *Sefer Yesira: Edition, Translation and Text-Critical Commentary* (Mohr Siebeck, 2004). **[confirmed]**

Role — the scholarly anchor for the text of [[source.sefer-yetzirah]]: a synoptic edition of the three recensions (modeled on Schäfer's *Synopse zur Hekhalot-Literatur*); links *belimah* to Job 26:7. The dating debate it anchors: most scholars place composition 2nd–6th c. CE; Wasserstrom argues for the 9th c. in an Islamic milieu.
