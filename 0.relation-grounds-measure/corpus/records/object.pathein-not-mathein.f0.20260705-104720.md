**statement (plain)** — initiates "did not learn anything (*ou mathein*) but experienced/suffered (*pathein*) and were put into a certain disposition." The Mysteries worked by inducing an experience and a state of mind, not by transferring propositions; scholarly consensus reports there was likely no transmissible dogmatic "content." The older secret-doctrine view was undone by Lobeck's *Aglaophamus* (1829); Plutarch (*De defectu oraculorum* 22) notes initiates could produce no demonstration of their acquired beliefs.

**basis** — Aristotle, *On Philosophy* fr. 15 (Rose), preserved/paraphrased by Synesius of Cyrene, *Dio* 10; discussed by Nancy A. Evans (2006).
