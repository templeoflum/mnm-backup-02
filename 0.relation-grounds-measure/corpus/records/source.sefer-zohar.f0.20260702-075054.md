Anonymous, *Sefer ha-Zohar* (Castile, late 13th c.). The central work of Kabbalah: a vast Aramaic corpus of mystical midrash on the Torah, framed as the teachings of Shimon bar Yoḥai and his circle, elaborating the sefirot, the divine faces, and the drama of exile and unification; modern scholarship places its composition in the circle of Moses de León.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Access note: the list points to seforimcenter.com Aramaic editions (academic Aramaic; Aramaic menukad + Hebrew trans.); scholarly English is the Pritzker Edition. **[confirmed as attribution]**
