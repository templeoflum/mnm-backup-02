A. A. Long and D. N. Sedley, *The Hellenistic Philosophers*, 2 vols (Cambridge University Press, 1987). The standard sourcebook for Stoicism, Epicureanism, and the skeptical schools: vol. 1 gives the principal sources in translation with philosophical commentary, vol. 2 the Greek and Latin texts with notes and bibliography. Foundational as the reference collection through which Hellenistic philosophy is cited ("LS" numbers); one record covers both volumes.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Hellenistic Philosophy section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
