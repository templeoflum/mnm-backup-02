V. Pedashenko, L. Kushnareva, Y. Khassan Nibal, E. Tulchinskii, K. Kuznetsov, V. Zharchinskii, Y. Maximov, I. Piontkovskaya, "Unveiling Intrinsic Dimension of Texts: from Academic Abstract to Creative Story," arXiv:2511.15210; EACL 2026.

Role — establishes empirically that text intrinsic dimension stratifies by domain/genre rather than being a universal constant. Abstract, verbatim: "ID exhibits robust genre stratification: scientific prose shows low ID (~8), encyclopedic content medium ID (~9), and creative/opinion writing high ID (~10.5) across all models tested." ID is complementary to entropy-based metrics (uncorrelated after length control) and grounded via cross-encoder analysis, linguistic features, and sparse autoencoders; SAE steering shows "scientific" directions reduce ID, narrative/personalization directions increase it. **[confirmed]** (figures from a 2025–26 preprint line — replication pending, per the dive's own caveat)
