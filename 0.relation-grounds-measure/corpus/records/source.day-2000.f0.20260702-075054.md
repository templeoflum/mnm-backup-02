John Day, *Yahweh and the Gods and Goddesses of Canaan* (Sheffield Academic Press, 2000). Systematic deity-by-deity examination — El, Baal, Asherah, Astarte, the sun, moon, and underworld gods — of how Canaanite religion shaped, and was polemicized against in, the Hebrew Bible.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Biblical & Ancient Near East. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
