The **Ramayana** (composed c. 500 BCE – 100 BCE). The Sanskrit epic, traditionally attributed to the poet **Valmiki**, recounting the story of **Rama**, prince of Ayodhya (an avatar of Vishnu): his exile to the forest, the abduction of his wife **Sita** by the demon-king **Ravana** of Lanka, and her rescue with the aid of the monkey **Hanuman** and the monkey army. The second of the two great Sanskrit epics, a principal source, with the Mahabharata, for the classical Hindu mythology of Vishnu and his avatars. **[confirmed as attribution]**
