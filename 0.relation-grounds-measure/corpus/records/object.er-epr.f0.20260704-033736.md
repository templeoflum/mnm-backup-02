**statement (plain)** — take the two strangest things in physics — quantum entanglement (EPR pairs) and wormholes (Einstein–Rosen bridges) — and conjecture they are one thing seen twice: any entangled pair is connected by a (generally Planckian, non-traversable) bridge.

**basis** — proposed for entangled black holes (the thermofield double state's eternal black hole) and conjecturally extended to all entanglement. [[source.maldacena-susskind-2013]] **[confirmed]** (as the conjecture's content) — 2024 frontier: Jiang–Wang–Wu–Yang claim a derivation of the ER bridge from thermofield-double entanglement ([[source.jiang-2024]], preprint weight).
