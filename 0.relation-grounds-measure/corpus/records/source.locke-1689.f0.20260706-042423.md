John Locke, *An Essay Concerning Human Understanding* (1689) — the foundational empiricist account of ideas from sensation and reflection; Book II.xxxii.15 raises the possibility that one person's experience of a violet could be another's experience of a marigold with no detectable difference — the seed of the inverted-spectrum problem. **[confirmed as attribution]**
