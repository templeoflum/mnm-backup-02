**statement (plain)** — The coffer illusion, also known as the "16 Circles" illusion, presents observers with a pattern of rectangular shapes arranged to suggest recessed panels or coffers, as might be found in decorative architectural ceilings. Despite the predominance of rectangular forms in the image, the pattern also contains sixteen complete circles that remain hidden from most observers during casual inspection. The circles are defined by the tangent points where the rectangles meet, creating continuous curved contours that are difficult to detect because they share their orientation and spatial frequency characteristics with the rectangular framework.
