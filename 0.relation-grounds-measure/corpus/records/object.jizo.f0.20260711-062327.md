**statement (plain)** — **Jizō Bosatsu** is the beloved bodhisattva who guards children, travelers, and the dead. He is the Japanese form of the Buddhist bodhisattva **Kṣitigarbha** (Chinese **Dizang**), who vowed to save beings in all realms. He especially protects the souls of deceased children and those suffering in the hell realms, and stands as a stone guardian at roadsides and boundaries.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: children, travelers, the dead, the hell realms, boundaries, protection. Jizō is the Japanese form of Kṣitigarbha (Chinese Dizang); he guards deceased children (who pile stones at the dry riverbed of **Sai-no-Kawara**) and stands as a stone guardian at roadsides and boundaries. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Jizō is documented in Ashkenazi (2003) and this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). He is a Buddhist bodhisattva of non-Japanese origin — the Japanese form of Kṣitigarbha, known in China as Dizang — not a native Shinto kami; his cult of guarding dead children at Sai-no-Kawara and standing as a roadside boundary-guardian belongs to the living Buddhist and folk tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**
