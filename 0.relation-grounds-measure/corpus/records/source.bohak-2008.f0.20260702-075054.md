Gideon Bohak, *Ancient Jewish Magic: A History* (Cambridge University Press, 2008). A historical synthesis of Jewish magical practice from the Second Temple period through late antiquity, working from amulets, magic bowls, Genizah fragments, and literary sources. Foundational as the standard modern history of the field.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
