Martha Warren Beckwith, *Hawaiian Mythology* (Yale University Press, 1940), is the standard comprehensive scholarly reference to Hawaiian myth. It surveys the four great gods (Kāne, Kū, Lono, Kanaloa), the Pele cycle, and the many demigods and heroes, drawing extensively on Hawaiian-language sources and situating them within the wider Polynesian background. It remains the principal scholarly gateway to the Hawaiian pantheon and its Polynesian cognates. **[confirmed as attribution]**
