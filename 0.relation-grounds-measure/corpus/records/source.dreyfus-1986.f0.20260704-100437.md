H. L. Dreyfus & S. E. Dreyfus, *Mind Over Machine* (Free Press, 1986); origin in the 1980 USAF/Berkeley ORC report; restated Rousse & Dreyfus (2021). **[confirmed]**

Role - the five stages novice -> advanced beginner -> competence -> proficiency -> expertise (a sixth "mastery" appears later); expertise is intuitive and non-deliberative ("when things are proceeding normally, experts don't solve problems and don't make decisions; they do what normally works"). Chess illustration: masters distinguish ~50,000 position-types (1986; Rousse-Dreyfus 2021 restate ~100,000; Simon's chunking estimate spans both); the Kaplan lightning-chess-plus-mental-addition experiment. Originated as a critique of rule-based AI; later applied to nursing by Benner. **[candidate as attribution]** on the chess figure - carried as the 50k/100k conflict, not a picked number.
