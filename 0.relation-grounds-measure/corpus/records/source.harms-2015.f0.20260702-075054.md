Daniel Harms, James R. Clark, and Joseph H. Peterson (eds.), *The Book of Oberon: A Sourcebook of Elizabethan Magic* (Llewellyn, 2015). Full edition of a sixteenth-century English magician's working manuscript (Folger V.b.26) — prayers, spirit conjurations including the fairy king Oberon, tools, and images — a rare window onto magic as actually practiced rather than as theorized.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
