**origin** — the social-contract tradition (Hobbes's *Leviathan*, 1651; Locke; Rousseau) and, before it, Plato's *Republic* and Aristotle's *Politics*: on what grounds political authority is legitimate and how goods should be distributed.

**codification** — John Rawls, *A Theory of Justice* (1971) — justice as fairness, derived from a hypothetical choice behind a veil of ignorance; the text that reset twentieth-century political philosophy. [[source.rawls-1971]]

**door** — Will Kymlicka, *Contemporary Political Philosophy: An Introduction* — the standard survey (utilitarianism, liberal egalitarianism, libertarianism, communitarianism, multiculturalism).
