**statement (plain)** — **Osanyin** (Ọ̀sanyìn; **Ossain** in the diaspora) is the **Orisha** of herbs and plant-medicine in Yoruba tradition. Dwelling in the forest, he knows the power of every plant, and all healing and all magical work depend on his leaves, whose secrets he guards. He is depicted with a single eye, a single arm, and a single leg, and with a high, whistling voice.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: herbs, medicine, forest, magic, healing, plant spirits. Keeper of the secret of every leaf; the source of the herbs on which all healing and magic depend; forest-dwelling, depicted one-eyed, one-armed, and one-legged with a high, whistling voice. Diaspora name: **Ossain**. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962) and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; his form and the practice of his herb-craft vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions where he is known as **Ossain**. **[confirmed as attribution]**
