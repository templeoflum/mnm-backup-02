**statement (plain)** — **Romulus** is the legendary founder and first king of Rome, son of the god **Mars** and the Vestal **Rhea Silvia**. In the tradition he and his twin **Remus** were exposed as infants, suckled by a she-wolf (*lupa*), and raised by the shepherd Faustulus. Grown, Romulus founded Rome on the Palatine — the traditional date is 753 BCE — killed Remus in the quarrel over the founding, and reigned as its first king. At the end of his reign he vanished in a storm and was deified as the god **Quirinus**.

**statement (attestation)** — son of Mars and the Vestal Rhea Silvia; twin of Remus; nursed by the she-wolf and reared by Faustulus; founder of the city on the Palatine (traditional 753 BCE); slayer of Remus; first of the seven kings; taken up and worshipped as **Quirinus**. Associated figures: Mars, Rhea Silvia, Remus, Numitor and Amulius of Alba Longa, Faustulus. The she-wolf, the fratricide, and the apotheosis are reported in the tradition and vary across the sources. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in Livy's *Ab Urbe Condita* Book 1, Plutarch's *Life of Romulus*, and Ovid's *Fasti*; this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted fact. The she-wolf nursing, the killing of Remus, and Romulus' deification as Quirinus are attested tradition, variable across sources, and are not to be read as neutral fact. **[confirmed as attribution]**
