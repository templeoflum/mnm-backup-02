Brad Inwood and L. P. Gerson, *Hellenistic Philosophy: Introductory Readings*, 2nd ed. (Hackett, 1997; 1st ed. 1988). The standard classroom anthology of Hellenistic philosophy in translation — Epicurus and the Epicureans, the Stoics, and the skeptics — organized by school with continuous texts rather than fragment-numbers. Foundational as the accessible companion to Long & Sedley's scholarly sourcebook.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Hellenistic Philosophy section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
