Gábor Betegh, *The Derveni Papyrus: Cosmology, Theology and Interpretation* (Cambridge University Press, 2004). A study of the Derveni papyrus — the carbonized fourth-century BCE roll containing an allegorical commentary on an Orphic theogony — with text, translation, and analysis of its cosmology, theology, and interpretive method. Foundational as the standard monograph on the single most important direct witness to early Orphic literature and its ancient reception.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Greek Mysteries. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
