**origin** — the oral traditions of the Polynesian peoples; the **Māori** creation cycle of **Rangi** (sky) and **Papa** (earth) and their children, and the pan-Polynesian deeds of **Māui**, carried in chant and genealogy across the Pacific. **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Sir George Grey's *Polynesian Mythology* (1855) for the Māori cycle, and Martha Beckwith's *Hawaiian Mythology* (1940) for the Hawaiian gods — the two standard collected sources. [[source.grey-1855]] [[source.beckwith-1940]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Katharine Luomala, *Maui-of-a-Thousand-Tricks* (Bishop Museum, 1949) for the pan-Polynesian comparative frame, with Beckwith and Grey. [[source.luomala-1949]]

*grade a — origin (oral Rangi–Papa cycle), codification (Grey / Beckwith), door (Luomala) present. A single-family field (with regional branches) under [[field.comparative-mythology]]. Field-home for the Polynesian pantheon figure intake (20260711). NOTE: regional cognate pairs (Māori Tāne / Hawaiian Kāne; Tangaroa / Kanaloa; Tū / Kū; Rongo / Lono) are recorded as distinct figures with cross-notes, not merged.*
