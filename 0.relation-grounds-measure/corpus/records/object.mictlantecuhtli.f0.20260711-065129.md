**statement (plain)** — **Mictlantecuhtli** ("lord of Mictlan") is the skeletal Aztec/Nahua lord of **Mictlan**, the deepest underworld. A blood-spattered skeleton with a necklace of eyes, he rules the lowest of the nine underworlds with his wife **Mictecacihuatl**. The ordinary dead journey four years through Mictlan's trials to reach him; he guards the ancestral bones that **Quetzalcoatl** stole to make humankind.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: death, the underworld, the dead. Nahuatl *Mictlantecuhtli*, from *Mictlan* (place of the dead) and *tecuhtli* (lord) — "lord of the place of the dead." Consort of Mictecacihuatl; keeper of the bones of past ages retrieved by Quetzalcoatl; lord of the ninth and lowest underworld. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the colonial-era codices (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*), recorded after the conquest; the layers of Mictlan and the trials of the four-year journey vary across the sources. **[confirmed as attribution]**
