**statement (plain)** — Imagine a creature that gets enormously more pleasure from each unit of resource than any ordinary person. Since total utilitarianism maximizes the sum of utility, it must funnel every resource to the monster — everyone else stripped bare to feed its ecstasy.

**statement (framework)** — a *reductio* of classical **utilitarianism**: maximizing the total has no intrinsic commitment to fairness or the *separateness of persons*. Connects to interpersonal utility comparison, prioritarianism, distributive justice, and (now) the moral status of AIs and digital minds. Established; whether it defeats aggregation stays **open**.
