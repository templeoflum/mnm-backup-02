**origin** — G. Frege's principle of compositionality (associated with "Über Sinn und Bedeutung," 1892) — the meaning of a compound is a function of the meanings of its parts and their mode of combination; in tension with his own context principle (1884). [[source.frege-1892]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — R. Montague's model-theoretic semantics (early 1970s) — the syntax is an algebra, the semantics is an algebra, and there is a homomorphism between them; compositionality made a structure-preserving map. [[source.montague-1970]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — the categorial / type-logical grammar line: Ajdukiewicz (1935), Bar-Hillel (1953), J. Lambek's syntactic calculus (1958), Steedman's CCG — syntactic categories as semantic types, combined recursively by a small rule set; Barbara Partee's textbooks are the standard entry. [[source.lambek-1958]] [[source.ajdukiewicz-1935]] **[confirmed]**

*Kin: `generative-grammar` (the syntax it interprets), `argument-structure` (predicate–argument composition), `category-theory` (the algebra/homomorphism machinery).*
