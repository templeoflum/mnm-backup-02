E. Williams, "Argument Structure and Morphology," *The Linguistic Review* 1, 81–114 (1981). **[confirmed as attribution]** (venue/pagination from knowledge; the dive cites author-title-year for the theta-grid notion)

Role — introduces the theta grid: a predicate's argument structure as the list of theta-roles it requires (give = ⟨Agent, Theme, Goal⟩), with external/internal argument distinguished.
