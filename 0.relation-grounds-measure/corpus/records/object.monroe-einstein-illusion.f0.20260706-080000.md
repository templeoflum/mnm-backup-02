**statement (plain)** — The Monroe-Einstein Illusion is a hybrid image that demonstrates distance-dependent face perception through spatial frequency filtering. When viewed up close, the image appears as Albert Einstein, but when viewed from a distance or with blurred vision, it transforms into an image of Marilyn Monroe. This striking illusion is created by combining the high spatial frequency components of one face with the low spatial frequency components of another face in a single composite image.
