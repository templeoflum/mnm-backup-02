Alan F. Segal, *Two Powers in Heaven: Early Rabbinic Reports about Christianity and Gnosticism* (Brill, 1977). Landmark study of the rabbinic polemic against those who held there were "two powers in heaven," reconstructing the early Jewish binitarian currents behind Christology, Gnosticism, and Merkavah speculation.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Apocalypticism, Merkavah and Dead Sea Scrolls. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (reissued by Baylor University Press, 2012). **[confirmed as attribution]**
