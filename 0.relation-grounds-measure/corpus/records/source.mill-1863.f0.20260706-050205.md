John Stuart Mill, *Utilitarianism* (1863) — the systematic defence of the **greatest-happiness principle** (actions are right in proportion as they promote happiness), refining Bentham with the higher/lower pleasures distinction. The canonical statement of classical utilitarianism that the utility-monster and repugnant-conclusion cases target. **[confirmed as attribution]**
