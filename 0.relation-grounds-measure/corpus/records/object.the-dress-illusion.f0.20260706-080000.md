**statement (plain)** — The Dress illusion emerged in February 2015 when a photograph of a striped dress went viral on social media, revealing a striking and persistent individual difference in color perception. Approximately 57% of observers perceive the dress as white with gold stripes, while 30% see it as blue with black stripes, with the remaining observers experiencing intermediate or alternating percepts. The same physical image produces radically different color experiences across individuals, with each observer typically experiencing their percept as stable and compelling.
