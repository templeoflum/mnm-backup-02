M. H. G. Kuijpers, *An Archaeology of Skill* (Routledge, 2018); and "A Sensory Update to the Chaine Operatoire," *J. Archaeological Method and Theory* (2017). **[confirmed]**

Role - Kuijpers' *own verified terms*: craft as "a way of exploring and understanding the material world"; skill as "recognition of and response to material"; "sensate understanding"; craftspeople express the *qualities* rather than *properties* of a material; "perceptive categories" (2017). NOTE: the terms "coarse epistemes" / "perceptive-cognitive enactment" / "attunement with production" are NOT Kuijpers' - they are Crevels' ([[source.crevels-coarse-epistemes]]), drawing on Kuijpers. The corpus records the split honestly.
