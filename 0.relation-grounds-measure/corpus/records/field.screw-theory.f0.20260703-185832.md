**origin** — L. Euler's rotation theorem (1775/76) and G. Mozzi's screw-axis precursor (1763); M. Chasles (1830) — every rigid-body displacement is a screw motion. [[source.euler-1776]] [[source.chasles-1830]] [[source.mozzi-1763]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — R. S. Ball, *A Treatise on the Theory of Screws* (Cambridge UP, 1900) — the definitive classical treatment of twists, wrenches, reciprocal screws, the cylindroid. [[source.ball-1900]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — R. M. Murray, Z. Li, S. S. Sastry, *A Mathematical Introduction to Robotic Manipulation* (CRC, 1994); K. M. Lynch & F. C. Park, *Modern Robotics* (Cambridge UP, 2017). [[source.murray-li-sastry-1994]] [[source.lynch-park-2017]]
