Bentley Layton, *The Gnostic Scriptures* (Doubleday, 1987). Annotated translations of the classic gnostic texts — Nag Hammadi tractates, Valentinian and Thomas material, and heresiological testimonia — with introductions and historical framing; the standard scholarly one-volume gnostic sourcebook.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Gnosticism sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
