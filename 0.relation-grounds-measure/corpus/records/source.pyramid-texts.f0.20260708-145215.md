The **Pyramid Texts** (c. 2400–2300 BCE). The oldest corpus of Egyptian religious and funerary literature: a body of spells (utterances) carved in hieroglyphs on the interior walls of royal pyramids of the late Old Kingdom, from Unas of the Fifth Dynasty onward. The spells provision the dead king for the afterlife and his ascent among the gods — his identification with **Osiris** and his rebirth with the sun god **Ra** — and give the earliest attested form of the solar and Osirian cycles. The foundational source for Egyptian myth, ancestral to the later Coffin Texts and Book of the Dead. **[confirmed as attribution]**
