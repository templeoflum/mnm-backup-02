**statement (plain)** — **Susanoo-no-Mikoto** is the tempestuous storm and sea god, born from **Izanagi**'s nose. His violence drove his sister **Amaterasu** into the Rock Cave and got him cast out of heaven. Exiled to Izumo, he slew the eight-headed, eight-tailed serpent **Yamata-no-Orochi** and drew from its tail the sword **Kusanagi**, which he gave to Amaterasu.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: storms, sea, chaos, heroes. He is the unruly brother of the sun and the dragon-slaying hero of the Izumo cycle. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Susanoo is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant accounts of his banishment and the serpent-slaying; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
