The **Ulster Cycle** (preserved from c. 1100 onward). A body of Old and Middle Irish heroic tales set in the reign of **Conchobar mac Nessa**, king of Ulster, and centered on the hero **Cú Chulainn**. Its central text is the **Táin Bó Cúailnge** ("the Cattle Raid of Cooley"), in which Cú Chulainn single-handedly defends Ulster against the army of **Medb** of Connacht. The tales are preserved in manuscripts such as the **Book of the Dun Cow** (*Lebor na hUidre*, c. 1100) and the Book of Leinster. One of the four great cycles of Irish literary tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**
