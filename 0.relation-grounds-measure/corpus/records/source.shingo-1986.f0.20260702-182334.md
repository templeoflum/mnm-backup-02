Shigeo Shingo, *Zero Quality Control: Source Inspection and the Poka-Yoke System* (Productivity Press, 1986; Japanese original 1985).

Contains — Shingo's exposition of **poka-yoke** ("mistake-proofing") and source inspection: rather than catch defects by later inspection, build the process so the error cannot be made, or is made self-evident at once. A key adjunct to jidoka within the Toyota lineage; Shingo was the industrial engineer who systematized much of the Toyota Production System's shop-floor method.
