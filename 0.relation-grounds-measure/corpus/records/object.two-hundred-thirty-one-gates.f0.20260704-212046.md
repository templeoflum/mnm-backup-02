**statement (plain)** — SY ch. 2: the 22 letters are set "in a circle like a wall with 231 gates," which "oscillates back and forth." 231 = the combinations of 22 letters in pairs without reversal (22×21/2) — the textual basis for letters-as-channels whose combinations generate structure. **[confirmed]** *(status `doctrinal`.)*

**basis** — SY ch. 2; scholarly reconstructions of the pairing sequence (Blumenthal); Eleazar of Worms (12th–13th c.) used a different pairing method — carried as lineage. The combinatorial-language continuation (Lull, Leibniz) is Eco's semiotic line: [[object.ars-combinatoria]] (prose).
