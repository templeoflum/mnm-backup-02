**statement (plain)** — noun phrases, verb phrases, adjective phrases, prepositional phrases all share one blueprint: a head word, an intermediate layer, and a full phrase, with the same slots for specifiers and complements. Swap the head's category and the structure is otherwise identical — a single recurring schema that builds every phrase, and phrases inside phrases without limit.

**statement (formal)** — for a variable X over {N, V, A, P}: X⁰ (head) → X′ (with complement) → XP (maximal projection, with specifier), endocentric (every phrase headed by its own category) and recursive. Descendants: Kayne's antisymmetry (universal spec-head-complement order) and Chomsky's Minimalist bare phrase structure / Merge. **[confirmed]**

**basis** — the strongest classical candidate for "one relational schema at every level" in morphosyntax (distinct, per the signed proposal, from the fractal-parameter line). Chomsky (1970) seeds it; Jackendoff (1977) systematizes. Rests on recursion ([[object.recursion-in-language]]). [[source.chomsky-1970]] [[source.jackendoff-1977]]
