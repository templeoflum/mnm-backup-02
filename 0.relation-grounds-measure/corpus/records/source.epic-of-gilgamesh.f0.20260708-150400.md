The **Epic of Gilgamesh** (standard Babylonian version c. 1200 BCE, from older Sumerian tales). The great Akkadian epic of **Gilgamesh**, king of **Uruk**, and his companion **Enkidu**: their adventures, Enkidu's death, and Gilgamesh's subsequent quest for immortality, which brings him to **Utnapishtim**, survivor of the Flood, who tells the flood story. The standard Babylonian version is traditionally attributed to the scribe **Sin-leqi-unninni**; it draws on older, separate Sumerian tales of the king. The oldest surviving great work of literature and a foundational source for Mesopotamian myth. **[confirmed as attribution]**
