**origin** — René Descartes, *Meditations on First Philosophy* (1641) — the real distinction between mind (*res cogitans*) and body (*res extensa*), which gave the mind–body problem its modern form. [[source.descartes-1641]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Gilbert Ryle, *The Concept of Mind* (1949) — the attack on "the ghost in the machine," the category-mistake diagnosis of Cartesian dualism, and the logical-behaviourist reframing that opened analytic philosophy of mind. [[source.ryle-1949]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Jaegwon Kim, *Philosophy of Mind* (Westview Press) — the standard graduate survey: dualism, behaviourism, the mind–brain identity theory, functionalism, mental causation, consciousness. [[source.kim-2011]]
