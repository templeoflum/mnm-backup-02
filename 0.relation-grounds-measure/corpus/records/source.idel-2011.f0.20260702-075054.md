Moshe Idel, *Kabbalah in Italy, 1280–1510: A Survey* (Yale University Press, 2011). Survey of the distinctively Italian kabbalistic line — Abraham Abulafia's ecstatic kabbalah, Menahem Recanati, Yohanan Alemanno — and its meeting with Renaissance humanism in the circle around Pico, the seedbed of Christian kabbalah.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Renaissance Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
