John A. Wheeler, "The 'Past' and the 'Delayed-Choice' Double-Slit Experiment" (1978) — proposes deferring the wave-or-particle measurement choice until after the photon has passed the slits; "no phenomenon is a phenomenon until it is an observed phenomenon." **[confirmed as attribution]**
