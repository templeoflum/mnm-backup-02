Stafford Beer, *Brain of the Firm* (Allen Lane The Penguin Press, 1972; 2nd ed. Wiley, 1981).

Contains — the founding statement of the **Viable System Model (VSM)** and of management cybernetics: any viable organization comprises five recursively nested subsystems (operations, coordination, control, intelligence, policy) that together give it enough regulatory variety to survive in a complex environment. Applies Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety and the neurophysiology of control to organizations. Extended in Beer's *The Heart of Enterprise* (1979).
