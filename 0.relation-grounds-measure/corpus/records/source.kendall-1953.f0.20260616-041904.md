# source.kendall-1953

Verbatim citation: Kendall, D. G. (1953). "Stochastic Processes Occurring in the Theory of Queues and Their Analysis by the Method of the Imbedded Markov Chain." *The Annals of Mathematical Statistics*, **24**(3), 338-354.

Contains: a rigorous stochastic-process treatment of single-server queues via the imbedded (embedded) Markov chain technique, and the three-factor shorthand A/B/c — interarrival-time distribution / service-time distribution / number of servers — now universally called Kendall's notation.
