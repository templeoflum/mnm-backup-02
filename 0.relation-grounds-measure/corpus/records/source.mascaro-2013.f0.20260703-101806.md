Jennifer S. Mascaro, James K. Rilling, Lobsang Tenzin Negi & Charles L. Raison, "Compassion meditation enhances empathic accuracy and related neural activity," *Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience* 8, no. 1 (2013): 48–55.

Contains — a randomized controlled fMRI study (n = 21): 8 weeks of Cognitively-Based Compassion Training (CBCT) significantly increased scores on the Reading the Mind in the Eyes Test (RMET) and raised activity in the inferior frontal gyrus and dorsomedial prefrontal cortex, versus a health-discussion control. Evidence that contemplative training improves inference of others' mental states, not just the attentional substrate.
