**statement (plain)** — The Reverse Spokes Illusion is a motion perception phenomenon in which the spokes of a rotating wheel appear to reverse their direction of rotation under certain viewing conditions. This illusion is related to temporal aliasing effects that occur when the spatiotemporal characteristics of the stimulus interact with the sampling properties of motion detection mechanisms in the visual system. The reversal typically occurs at specific rotation speeds where the temporal frequency of spoke presentation approaches or exceeds critical values for motion detection.
