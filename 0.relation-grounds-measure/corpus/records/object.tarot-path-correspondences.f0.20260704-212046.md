**statement (plain)** — the modern occult attribution of the 22 Tarot trumps to the 22 Hebrew letters and the Tree's 22 paths: Lévi (1854) first drew the linkage; the Golden Dawn (via Mathers, fed by the cipher manuscripts and the Kircher-derived diagram) fixed a system; **Crowley counterchanged The Emperor and The Star** (paths 15/28) on *Book of the Law* I:57; some French traditions follow a different mapping entirely. Two Tree versions circulate (Malkuth 1-path after Reuchlin vs 3-path), both totaling 22 letter-paths "owing to Kircher's syncretism." *(status `doctrinal`.)*

**basis** — **practitioner-documented systems, not endorsed as historically Kabbalistic by Kabbalah scholars** — entered as documents of the dispute ([[source.levi-1854]], [[source.crowley-1944]]); the related strong claim "the paths, not the nodes, carry the system" is likewise a modern Hermetic-Qabalah emphasis, not a documented medieval thesis (the dive's own flag). Prose-links [[field.western-esotericism]] and [[field.kabbalah]].
