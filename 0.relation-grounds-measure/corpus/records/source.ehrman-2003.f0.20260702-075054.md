Bart D. Ehrman, *Lost Christianities: The Battles for Scripture and the Faiths We Never Knew* (Oxford University Press, 2003). A widely read survey of the varieties of early Christianity — Ebionites, Marcionites, "Gnostics," proto-orthodox — and the forged, suppressed, and rediscovered texts through which their battles were fought. Foundational as the standard accessible entry into early Christian diversity before the canon closed.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Gnosticism section. Popular-audience work rather than a technical monograph. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
