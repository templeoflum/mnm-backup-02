**statement (plain)** — A time traveler takes Beethoven's symphonies to the past and gives them to Beethoven, who publishes them; centuries later the traveler finds them and carries them back. The symphonies exist and are coherent — yet no one ever composed them. The information circles the loop with no origin.

**statement (framework)** — the **ontological / information paradox** of time travel: a self-consistent closed causal loop where an item has no ultimate source. Not a logical contradiction (unlike the grandfather case) but a puzzle about where order/information comes from; permitted in general-relativistic spacetimes with closed timelike curves. Established; **open**.
