**statement (plain)** — **Aphrodite** (Greek *Ἀφροδίτη*) is the Olympian goddess of love, beauty, and sexual desire. Two birth traditions stand side by side: in **Hesiod's** *Theogony* she is born from the white foam (*aphros*) that gathers around the severed genitals of **Ouranos** when Kronos casts them into the sea — hence the ancient gloss of her name as "foam-born" (*Aphrogeneia*); in **Homer** she is the daughter of **Zeus** and the goddess **Dione**. She is the wife of the smith-god **Hephaestus** and the lover of **Ares**. Her power sets the **Judgment of Paris** in motion: promised the most beautiful woman by Paris, she wins the golden apple and grants him **Helen**, setting the Trojan War underway.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: love, beauty, sexual desire, and procreation. Key attributes: the girdle (*kestos himas*) that compels desire, the dove, the myrtle, the rose, the scallop shell; her attendants the **Charites** (Graces) and, in later tradition, **Eros**. Parentage: contested between the Hesiodic foam-birth from Ouranos and the Homeric descent from Zeus and Dione — both are canonical in their respective sources. The derivation of the name from *aphros* ("foam") is an ancient folk-etymology and is not accepted by modern philology; a Near Eastern (Semitic, via Astarte/Ishtar) origin is widely proposed but debated. The Roman *interpretatio* identified her with **Venus**. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Greek mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested in Hesiod's Theogony [[source.hesiod-theogony]] and Homer's epics and the Homeric Hymns [[source.homer]], systematized in Apollodorus' Bibliotheca [[source.apollodorus-bibliotheca]]; the standard reference treatment is Hard [[source.hard-2004]]. The two birth traditions and the Venus *interpretatio* are well established; the etymology of the name is disputed and the *aphros* derivation is ancient folk-etymology, so these should be read as attested tradition, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
