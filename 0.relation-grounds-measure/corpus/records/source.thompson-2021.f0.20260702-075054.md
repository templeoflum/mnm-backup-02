Carolyn Roberts Thompson, *Reading German for Theological Studies: A Grammar and Reader* (Baker Academic, 2021). A text-based grammar and reader for acquiring reading knowledge of German theological scholarship.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Languages section; the list gives the title only, and this Baker Academic 2021 volume is the exact-title match (not Ziefle's *Modern Theological German*, Baker 1997, a different book).
