**statement (plain)** — the grades of initiation as scholars reconstruct them: *myesis* (μύησις, from *myein* "to close" the eyes/lips, connoting secrecy), *telete* (τελετή, from *telos* "goal/completion"), and *epopteia* (ἐποπτεία, "viewing/beholding," the highest grade). The initiate rises from *mystes* (first-timer) to *epoptes* ("one who has seen"). There is genuine scholarly debate on the number and sequence of stages.

**basis** — Simms, "Myesis, Telete, and Mysteria" (GRBS) cautions against equating *telete* with a first-degree ceremony; Pringsheim (1905) argued *myesis* a distinct pre-initiation, which Simms partly resists; Plutarch (*Demetrius* 26) for myesis preceding epopteia.
