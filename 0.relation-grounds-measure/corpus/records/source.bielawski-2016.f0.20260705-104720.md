Krzysztof Bielawski, "Arrheton — Unspeakable in the Greek Mystery Cults" (*Filozofia Chrześcijańska* 13, 2016, pp. 37–52) — catalogues the ancient evidence for *arrheton* and *aporrheton* (Aristotle, Xenophon, Herodotus, Aristophanes, the Derveni Papyrus, early Christian writers), treating Greek religion as an adogmatic, performance- and ritual-centered system.
