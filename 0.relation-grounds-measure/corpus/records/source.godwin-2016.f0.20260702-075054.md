Joscelyn Godwin et al., *Rosicrucian Trilogy: Modern Translations of the Three Founding Documents* (Weiser, 2016). Fresh, annotated English translations of the three texts that launched the Rosicrucian furor — the *Fama Fraternitatis* (1614), the *Confessio Fraternitatis* (1615), and the *Chymical Wedding of Christian Rosenkreutz* (1616) — with the original illustrations.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Rosicrucianism. Access note: the underlying manifestos (Fama 1614, Confessio 1615, Chymical Wedding 1616) are public domain in older translations; this edition is an in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
