Jacob (Jakob) Bernoulli, "Spira mirabilis," *Acta Eruditorum* (1692).

Role — derived the logarithmic spiral from the equiangular property; named it the "marvelous spiral" (motto *Eadem mutata resurgo* — "although changed, I shall arise the same"); noted its invariance under scaling, rotation, inversion, involute, pedal, caustic. **[confirmed as attribution]**
