Malcolm Lambert, *The Cathars* (Blackwell, 1998). Full narrative history of the Cathar movement in the traditional account — origins, doctrine, the Languedoc church, the Albigensian Crusade, and the inquisitorial destruction — a standard scholarly treatment.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Heresy and Inquisition. Represents the traditional account, now contested by deconstructionist scholarship (see Sennis, *Cathars in Question*). Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
