# lighthill-whitham-1955

**Citation (verbatim).** Lighthill, M. J., and Whitham, G. B. (1955). "On Kinematic Waves. II. A Theory of Traffic Flow on Long Crowded Roads." *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London. Series A, Mathematical and Physical Sciences*, 229(1178): 317–345. DOI: 10.1098/rspa.1955.0089.

**Contains.** Part II of the kinematic-waves pair. Applies kinematic-wave theory to estimate how a "hump" (region of increased vehicle concentration) propagates along a crowded main road. Establishes the continuum description of traffic over a long stretch of road, the propagation of kinematic waves at the slope of the flow-density curve, and the formation of shock waves where waves converge.
