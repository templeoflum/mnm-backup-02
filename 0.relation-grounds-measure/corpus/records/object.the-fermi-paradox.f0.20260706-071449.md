**statement (plain)** — The galaxy has hundreds of billions of stars, many with habitable planets, and is ~13 billion years old; a civilization could cross it in a few million years. So intelligent life should have arisen and spread many times over. Yet we see no evidence of any. Fermi: "Where is everybody?"

**statement (framework)** — the tension between the apparent likelihood of ETIs and the observed **Great Silence**. Proposed resolutions: rare-Earth/rare-intelligence (the Great Filter), self-destruction, non-expansion, undetectability, or that we are early. Structures SETI and existential-risk thinking. Established; unresolved (**open**).

**basis** — Fermi's 1950 lunchtime question; formalized in Hart (1975) [[source.hart-1975]]. **[confirmed as attribution]**
