**statement (plain)** — The Logvinenko Illusion demonstrates that perceived lightness depends critically on the spatial structure and configuration of luminance gradients in the visual field, rather than solely on absolute luminance values or simple contrast ratios. In this illusion, target patches of identical luminance embedded in different gradient configurations appear dramatically different in lightness. The effect reveals that the visual system analyzes the spatial derivative structure of the luminance distribution to infer surface reflectance properties and illumination patterns.

**statement (framework)** — category: brightness lightness contrast; perceptual mechanism(s): lightness-computation, spatial-filtering, surface-completion.
