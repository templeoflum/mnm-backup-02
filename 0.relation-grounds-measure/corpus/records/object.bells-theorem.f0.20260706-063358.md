**statement (plain)** — Bell derived an inequality constraining the statistical correlations any *local hidden-variable* theory allows between measurements on distant entangled particles. Quantum mechanics predicts violations of it — and experiments (Aspect 1982; loophole-free 2015) confirm the violations with great precision.

**statement (framework)** — forces abandoning **locality** or **realism** (or exotic escapes: superdeterminism, retrocausality). A genuine mathematical **theorem** (not a thought experiment), turned empirical by Aspect/Clauser/Zeilinger (2022 Nobel); the foundation of quantum cryptography and device-independent protocols. Proven; which pillar to drop stays **open**.
