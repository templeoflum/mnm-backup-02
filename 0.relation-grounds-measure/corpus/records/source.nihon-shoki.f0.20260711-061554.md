The *Nihon Shoki* (*Nihongi*, "Chronicles of Japan," 720 CE), compiled under **Prince Toneri**, is the second-oldest classical Japanese history. It recounts the age of the kami and the imperial line, and characteristically preserves several variant versions of a single myth side by side, making it the primary witness to the traditions that diverge from the *Kojiki*. **[confirmed as attribution]**
