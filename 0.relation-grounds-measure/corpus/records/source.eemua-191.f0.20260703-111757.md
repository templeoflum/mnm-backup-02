EEMUA, *Alarm Systems: A Guide to Design, Management and Procurement*, EEMUA Publication 191 (1st ed. 1999; 2nd 2007; 3rd 2013).

Contains — the benchmark industry guidance on **alarm management** for process control rooms: what an alarm is for (a signal requiring operator action), target alarm rates a human can handle, the pathology of alarm floods and standing/nuisance alarms, prioritization, and the alarm-system lifecycle. Complemented by ANSI/ISA-18.2 ("Management of Alarm Systems for the Process Industries," 2009/2016). The applied door to control-room / dispatch alarm discipline.

Access — EEMUA Publication 191; available for purchase from EEMUA. **[confirmed as attribution]** — the guide and its editions are well-documented; specific clause numbers not cited here.
