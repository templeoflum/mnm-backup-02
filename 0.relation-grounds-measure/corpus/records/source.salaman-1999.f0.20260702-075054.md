Clement Salaman, Dorine van Oyen, and William D. Wharton (trans.), *The Way of Hermes* (Duckworth, 1999). A readable modern English translation of the Corpus Hermeticum [[source.corpus-hermeticum]], paired with Jean-Pierre Mahé's translation of the *Definitions of Hermes Trismegistus to Asclepius* — an Armenian-preserved Hermetic text otherwise hard to access in English.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Hermeticism sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
