W. R. Newman & L. M. Principe, "Alchemy vs. Chemistry: The Etymological Origins of a Historiographic Mistake," *Early Science and Medicine* 3 (1998), 32–65. **[confirmed]**

Role — shows the alchemy/chemistry distinction is an anachronism for the early-modern period and proposes the actors' neutral term **"chymistry"** — the terminological ground of the New Historiography.
