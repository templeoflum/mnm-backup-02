Marco Dorigo and Luca Maria Gambardella, "Ant Colony System: A Cooperative Learning Approach to the Traveling Salesman Problem," *IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation* 1, no. 1 (1997): 53–66.

Contains — **Ant Colony System (ACS)**, a leading refinement of Ant Colony Optimization [[object.ant-colony-optimization]]: a pseudo-random-proportional action rule balancing exploration and exploitation, a *local* pheromone update that diversifies search, and a *global* update reinforcing only the best tour. A canonical ACO variant (alongside MAX–MIN Ant System [[source.stutzle-hoos-2000]]).
