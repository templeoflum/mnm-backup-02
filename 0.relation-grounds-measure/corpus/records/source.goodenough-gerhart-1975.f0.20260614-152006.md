**citation** — John B. Goodenough & Susan L. Gerhart, "Toward a Theory of Test Data Selection," *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering*, Vol. SE-1, No. 2 (June 1975), pp. 156–173. (Also appeared in the Proceedings of the International Conference on Reliable Software, ACM SIGPLAN Notices, 1975.)

**role** — theoretical anchor for the test-case / coverage-criteria keystone. Defines test **reliability** and **validity**, proves that a properly structured (reliable + valid) test can demonstrate absence of errors, and shows by example that exercising all statements / predicates / paths is not by itself sufficient for reliability. Introduces the test-adequacy-criterion framing the field still uses.
