Virgil (Publius Vergilius Maro), the *Aeneid* (c. 29–19 BCE), a Latin epic in twelve books left unfinished at the poet's death. It follows Aeneas' journey from the sack of Troy through his wanderings and the tragic love of Dido at Carthage to the wars in Latium, tracing the mythic foundation of the Roman line. Composed under Augustus, it became the Roman national epic and the central literary source for Aeneas.
