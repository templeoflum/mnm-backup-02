**statement (plain)** — Prisoners chained from birth in a cave see only shadows cast on a wall, and take them for the whole of reality. One is freed, dragged painfully into the sunlight, and at last sees things as they are — culminating in the sun, the Form of the Good. Returning, he is blind in the dark and cannot convince the others.

**statement (framework)** — operates at once as **metaphysics** (Forms vs. appearances), **epistemology** (the ascent from conjecture to understanding), and **politics** (the philosopher's reluctant return to rule). The founding Western image of enlightenment as a turning of the soul; newly resonant with curated information environments. Established; homed here for the knowledge-ascent reading. **open** in interpretation.
