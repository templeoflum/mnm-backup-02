**statement (plain)** — The Ratchet Illusion is a motion perception phenomenon in which a continuously rotating pattern appears to move in discrete, stepwise increments rather than smoothly. This illusory perception of quantized motion occurs despite the physical stimulus undergoing constant angular velocity rotation. The illusion is typically observed with specific spatial patterns, such as radial gratings or circular arrays of elements, that contain strong periodic structure.
