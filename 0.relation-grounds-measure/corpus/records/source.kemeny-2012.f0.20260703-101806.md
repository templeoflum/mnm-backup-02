Margaret E. Kemeny, Carol Foltz, James F. Cavanagh, Margaret Cullen, Janine Giese-Davis, Patricia Jennings, Erika L. Rosenberg, Omri Gillath, Phillip R. Shaver, B. Alan Wallace & Paul Ekman, "Contemplative/emotion training reduces negative emotional behavior and promotes prosocial responses," *Emotion* 12, no. 2 (2012): 338–350.

Contains — the "Cultivating Emotional Balance" RCT: 82 female schoolteachers randomized to an 8-week, 42-hour program combining meditation with Ekman's micro-expression training vs. wait-list. The training **increased recognition of emotions in others** (Micro-Expression Training Tool, METT) and reduced trait negative affect, rumination, depression, and anxiety, with most effects maintained at 5-month follow-up. Direct — if bundled — evidence that contemplative training can improve reading subtle facial signals.

Access — DOI 10.1037/a0026118; PMID 22148989. **[confirmed]** — note the meditation and emotion-recognition drilling were bundled, so the meditation component is not isolable.
