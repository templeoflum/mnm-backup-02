**statement (plain)** — **Hunahpu** and **Xbalanque** are the twin heroes of the K'iche' Maya **Popol Vuh**. Summoned to the underworld **Xibalba**, they survived its houses of trial and defeated the **Lords of Xibalba** through cunning and a feigned, willing death-and-revival, avenging their father and uncle. They then rose into the sky as the sun and the moon.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the underworld, sacrifice and resurrection, the ballgame, cleverness. The paired heroes of the **Popol Vuh**, ballplayers who overcome **Xibalba** and its lords; carried here as a single record for the twin pair. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Maya source is the post-conquest **Popol Vuh** manuscript, recorded in the colonial period; details of the trials and the final apotheosis vary in the telling and should be held loosely. **[confirmed as attribution]**
