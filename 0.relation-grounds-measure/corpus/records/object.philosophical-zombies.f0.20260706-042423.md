**statement (plain)** — Imagine a being atom-for-atom identical to you — same neural firings, same behaviour, same reports of "experience" — but with no inner life at all; "all dark inside." If such a zombie is conceivable (no contradiction), then consciousness is not fixed by the physical facts alone.

**statement (framework)** — the **conceivability argument** against physicalism: conceivability → possibility → the falsity of physicalism, yielding property dualism and the *hard problem*. Physicalist replies attack the conceivability→possibility step (Kripke's a-posteriori necessity, two-dimensional semantics, type-B physicalism). Established as a canonical argument; its soundness stays **open**.
