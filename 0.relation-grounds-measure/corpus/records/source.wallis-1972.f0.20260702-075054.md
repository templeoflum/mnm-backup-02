R. T. Wallis, *Neoplatonism* (Duckworth, 1972). The standard concise survey of the Neoplatonic school from Plotinus [[source.plotinus-enneads]] through Porphyry, Iamblichus, Proclus, and the Athenian and Alexandrian schools, with its influence on later thought; still the usual first scholarly orientation to the tradition.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Neo-Platonism sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
