**origin** — the study of sky phenomena from Aristotle's *Meteorologica* through Descartes' and Newton's optics of the rainbow.

**codification** — the physical optics of scattering, refraction, and diffraction in the atmosphere (Rayleigh scattering; Mie theory), systematized in the twentieth century.

**door** — Marcel Minnaert, *The Nature of Light and Colour in the Open Air* — the classic field guide to atmospheric optical phenomena.

*grade s — origin (Aristotle/Descartes), codification (scattering & refraction physics), door (Minnaert) present. New field-home for the optical-illusions intake (quarantine, 20260706); homes the atmospheric-natural phenomena (halos, mirages, twilight bands, the moon illusion, and kin).*
