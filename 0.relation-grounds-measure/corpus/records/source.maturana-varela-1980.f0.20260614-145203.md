Humberto R. Maturana & Francisco J. Varela, *Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living* (D. Reidel, Dordrecht, 1980; Boston Studies in the Philosophy of Science, vol. 42).

Role — the canonical English-language statement of **autopoiesis**. The volume collects two essays: "Biology of Cognition" (Maturana, 1970) and "Autopoiesis: The Organization of the Living." The term *autopoiesis* was coined in the 1972 Spanish original *De máquinas y seres vivos* (Editorial Universitaria, Santiago de Chile); the 1980 Reidel edition is the standard cited source, with a preface by Stafford Beer.
