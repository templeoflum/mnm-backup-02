M. David Litwa, *Found Christianities: Remaking the World of the Second Century CE* (T&T Clark, 2022). A movement-by-movement survey of second-century Christian groups and teachers — Marcion, Valentinus, Basilides, "Sethians," and many others — treated as found rather than lost, on their own terms after the dismantling of "Gnosticism" as a category. Foundational as the current-generation synthesis that absorbs the Williams-era critique.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Gnosticism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
