J. Ambjørn, J. Jurkiewicz, R. Loll, "The Spectral Dimension of the Universe is Scale Dependent," *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 95, 171301 (2005).

Role — in causal dynamical triangulations (discrete quantum gravity from combinatorial degrees of freedom), the spectral dimension runs: "four-dimensional on large scales" but "appears two-dimensional at short distances" — a "mechanism of dynamical dimensional reduction," leading the authors to conclude "that quantum gravity may be 'self-renormalizing' at the Planck scale." The same 4→2 flow appears in Hořava–Lifshitz gravity (arXiv:0902.3657). **[confirmed]**
