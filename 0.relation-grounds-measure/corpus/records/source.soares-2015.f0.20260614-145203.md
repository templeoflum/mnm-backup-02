Soares, N., Fallenstein, B., Armstrong, S., & Yudkowsky, E., "Corrigibility," in *Artificial Intelligence and Ethics: Papers from the 2015 AAAI Workshop* (WS-15-02), AAAI Press (2015). MIRI technical report companion (2014).

Role: introduces "corrigibility" as a named desideratum — an agent that cooperates with corrective intervention (correction, shutdown, modification) despite default incentives to resist — and analyzes shutdown-button utility functions and the difficulty of avoiding incentives to prevent or cause the button press.

Access — free (intelligence.org/files/Corrigibility.pdf).
