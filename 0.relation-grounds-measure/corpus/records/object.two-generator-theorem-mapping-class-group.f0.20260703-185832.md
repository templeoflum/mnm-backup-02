**statement (plain)** — you do not even need a handful. Two well-chosen mapping classes generate the entire group, and since the group is non-abelian, two is the fewest possible. Korkmaz showed one of the two can be an ordinary single Dehn twist and the other a finite-order (torsion) element.

**statement (formal)** — Mod(Σ_g) is generated by two elements (Wajnryb): one a product of two Dehn twists, the other a product of 2g twists. Korkmaz (2005): the two generators may be taken as a single Dehn twist and a torsion element of order 4g+2; Mod(Σ_g) is also generated by two torsion elements of order 4g+2. Two is minimal (non-abelian). **[confirmed]**

**basis** — part of a large program (Brendle–Farb involutions, Monden torsion, Baykur–Korkmaz commutators, Yıldız, Altunöz–Pamuk–Yildiz 2025 survey) shrinking generating sets of Mod(Σ_g).
