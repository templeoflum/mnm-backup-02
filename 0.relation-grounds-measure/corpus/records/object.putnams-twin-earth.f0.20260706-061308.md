**statement (plain)** — Twin Earth is identical to Earth except its 'water' is a different substance, XYZ, indistinguishable in experience from H2O. An Earthling and their molecule-for-molecule twin are in identical mental states, yet the Earthling's 'water' means H2O and the twin's means XYZ. So meaning outruns the head.

**statement (framework)** — the founding argument for **semantic externalism**: the extension of natural-kind terms ('water', 'gold', 'tiger') is fixed by the actual stuff in the environment, not by internal representations — "meanings ain't in the head." Developed with Kripke's causal theory of reference; reshaped philosophy of language and mind (content externalism). Established; scope **open**.
