Taiichi Ohno, *Toyota Production System: Beyond Large-Scale Production* (Productivity Press, 1988; Japanese original *Toyota seisan hōshiki*, Diamond, 1978).

Contains — the founding first-person account of the Toyota Production System by the engineer who built it. States the two pillars: **just-in-time** (produce only what is needed, when needed, in the amount needed) and **jidoka** (autonomation — automation with a human touch; a machine that stops itself on an abnormality). Frames the whole system as the relentless elimination of waste (*muda*), and traces jidoka's origin to Sakichi Toyoda's self-stopping automatic loom.
