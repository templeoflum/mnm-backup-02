**statement (plain)** — The green ray, also known as the green segment or green beam, is a rare atmospheric optical phenomenon that occurs during sunset or sunrise when a green flash extends into a sustained green ray lasting several seconds. This phenomenon results from atmospheric refraction acting as a prism to separate sunlight into its component wavelengths, with the green wavelength becoming visible when the red and orange portions of the solar disk are obscured below the horizon while the blue and violet components are scattered by the atmosphere. The green ray is significantly rarer and more dramatic than the standard green flash, requiring exceptionally clear atmospheric conditions with minimal turbulence and a distant, sharp horizon such as an ocean view.
