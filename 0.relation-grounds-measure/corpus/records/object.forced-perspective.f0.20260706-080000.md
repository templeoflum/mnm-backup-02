**statement (plain)** — Forced perspective is a photographic and theatrical technique that exploits depth cue conflicts to create illusions of altered object size, distance, or spatial relationships. By carefully positioning objects at different distances from the camera or observer while controlling the viewing geometry, practitioners can make objects appear much larger or smaller than their actual physical dimensions. The technique works by manipulating the relationship between retinal image size and perceived distance, taking advantage of size constancy mechanisms that normally compensate for distance when judging object size.
