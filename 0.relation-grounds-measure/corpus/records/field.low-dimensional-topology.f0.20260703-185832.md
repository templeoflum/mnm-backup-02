**origin** — M. Dehn's study of surface homeomorphisms and the “Dehn twist” (1920s–1938); the mapping class group Mod(Σ_g) = Homeo⁺(Σ_g)/Homeo₀(Σ_g) as isotopy classes of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms. [[source.dehn-1938]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — W. Thurston's geometrization program and the Nielsen–Thurston classification of surface automorphisms (periodic / reducible / pseudo-Anosov); the field consolidated around knots, 3-manifolds, and surface mapping class groups. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — B. Farb & D. Margalit, *A Primer on Mapping Class Groups* (Princeton UP, 2011). [[source.farb-margalit-2011]]
