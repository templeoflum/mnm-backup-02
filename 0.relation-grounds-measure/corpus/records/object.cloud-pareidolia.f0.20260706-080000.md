**statement (plain)** — Cloud pareidolia refers to the perceptual phenomenon in which observers perceive recognizable shapes, objects, or figures in the ambiguous and continuously changing patterns of clouds. Most commonly, people report seeing faces, animals, or familiar objects in cloud formations, despite the stimulus consisting only of water vapor and ice crystals shaped by atmospheric processes. This form of pareidolia represents a particularly dynamic instance of pattern recognition, as clouds constantly shift and transform, allowing for multiple interpretations and encouraging prolonged observation.
