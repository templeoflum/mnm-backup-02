**statement (plain)** — The Wagon Wheel Effect, also known as the Stroboscopic Effect, is a motion perception phenomenon in which a rotating wheel appears to rotate in the wrong direction, rotate more slowly than its actual speed, or appear stationary when viewed at certain rotation rates. The effect is most commonly observed in filmed or televised footage of rotating wheels, where it arises from temporal aliasing due to the discrete frame rate of the recording medium. When the rotation speed is such that the wheel advances nearly one full spoke interval between successive frames, the visual system interprets the nearest match as backward motion.
