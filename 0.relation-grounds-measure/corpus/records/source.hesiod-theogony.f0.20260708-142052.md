Hesiod, *Theogony* (c. 700 BCE). A hexameter poem of just over a thousand lines giving the birth and genealogy of the gods: from Chaos, Gaia, and the primordial powers, through the Titans and the castration of Ouranos, to Kronos, the birth of the Olympians, the Titanomachy, and the establishment of Zeus's sovereignty and the distribution of honours. The foundational text for Greek cosmogony and divine genealogy, transmitted alongside the *Works and Days*. **[confirmed as attribution]**
