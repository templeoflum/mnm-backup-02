Lawrence Fine, *Physician of the Soul, Healer of the Cosmos: Isaac Luria and his Kabbalistic Fellowship* (Stanford University Press, 2003). The standard scholarly study of Isaac Luria as a historical figure: his Safed fellowship, ritual practices, soul-diagnosis, and the social life of Lurianic Kabbalah, rather than doctrine alone.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Safedian Kabbalah. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
