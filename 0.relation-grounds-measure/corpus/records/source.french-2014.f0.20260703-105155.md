Steven French, *The Structure of the World: Metaphysics and Representation* (Oxford University Press, 2014).

Contains — the book-length defense of *eliminativist* ontic structural realism: there are no objects over and above the relational/modal structure of the world; putative objects are dependent on, or dissolved into, structure. Marshals permutation invariance in quantum mechanics, entanglement, and gauge/diffeomorphism invariance as support, and works out how a structure-only metaphysics represents physics. A principal statement of OSR alongside Ladyman & Ross's *Every Thing Must Go*.
