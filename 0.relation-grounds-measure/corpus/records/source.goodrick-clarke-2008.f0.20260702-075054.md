Nicholas Goodrick-Clarke, *The Western Esoteric Traditions: A Historical Introduction* (Oxford University Press, 2008). A chronological survey of Western esotericism from Hermetism and Gnosticism through Renaissance magic, Rosicrucianism, and theosophy to modern occultism, written within the academic framework the field inherited from Faivre [[source.faivre-1994]].

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), General Western Esotericism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
