**statement (plain)** — *multiplicatio* / augmentation: a well-attested late-stage operation of the opus that increases the **potency and quantity** of the Philosophers' Stone (per Ripley, by repeating fermentation / dissolution-coagulation upon the finished Stone), enabling transmutation of ever-larger amounts of base metal; *proiectio* then casts the powder onto molten metal. **[confirmed]** *(status `doctrinal`: the operation's aim is the tradition's tenet; its historical reality as practiced category is established.)*

**basis** — Ripley's definition ("Augmentation... of the Elixir indeed") [[source.ripley-1471]]; Boyle and the 1689 repeal of the Act against Multipliers [[source.principe-1998]]; Starkey's notebooks showing repetition as "quantitative, carefully reasoned laboratory practice" [[source.newman-principe-2002]].

**lineage (flag carried)** — framing the entire opus as a **"recursive loop"/"spiral"** (self-referential metaphysical cycle, individuation) is overwhelmingly Jungian/post-Jungian and New Age writing, NOT the Principe/Newman scholarship — reported, never adopted. The neat tenfold/hundredfold escalation figures come from primary and occult secondary sources (e.g. Manly P. Hall), not the historians. Principe's exact *Secrets of Alchemy* wording on multiplication was not confirmable — the dive's own page-check gap, carried honest.
