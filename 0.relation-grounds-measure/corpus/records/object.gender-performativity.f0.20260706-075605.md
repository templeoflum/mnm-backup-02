**statement (plain)** — Butler argues gender is not something one *is* (a prior, natural identity that causes gendered behavior) but something one *does* — an identity constituted through the repeated, norm-governed performance of gendered acts. The repetition produces the illusion of an abiding gendered self; the 'doer' is constituted in the deed.

**statement (framework)** — a foundational **framework** (not a hypothetical) of gender studies and feminist philosophy: applying the linguistic turn (Austin's performatives, Derrida's iterability) and Foucauldian power to the body and identity, reframing sex/gender/desire as effects of power rather than natural foundations, and locating agency in the possibility of subversive repetition. Established; contested.
