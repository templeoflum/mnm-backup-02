Ellen L. Babinsky (trans.), *Marguerite Porete: The Mirror of Simple Souls* (Paulist Press, 1993). The beguine's dialogue on the soul's annihilation in Love — written c. 1300; Porete was burned as a relapsed heretic in Paris in 1310, and the book circulated anonymously for centuries — a limit-case of apophatic and Free Spirit mysticism.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
