Verbatim citation:

Grassé, P.-P. (1959). La reconstruction du nid et les coordinations interindividuelles chez *Bellicositermes natalensis* et *Cubitermes* sp. La théorie de la stigmergie: Essai d'interprétation du comportement des termites constructeurs. *Insectes Sociaux*, 6(1), 41–80. doi:10.1007/BF02223791

Contains: Grassé's observational study of nest reconstruction in the termites *Bellicositermes natalensis* and *Cubitermes* sp. He coins the term **stigmergie** (from Greek *stigma*, mark/sting, + *ergon*, work) to name the mechanism by which the product of work already done — not the workers themselves — stimulates and directs further work. This resolved the apparent paradox that individual termites appear to work in isolation yet their collective construction is coordinated: each deposit modifies the local environment, and the modified environment triggers the next deposit.
