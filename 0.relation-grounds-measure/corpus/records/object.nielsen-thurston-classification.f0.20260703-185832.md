**statement (plain)** — however tangled a surface self-map is, up to isotopy it falls into one of three clean kinds: periodic (some power is the identity), reducible (it preserves a system of disjoint curves), or pseudo-Anosov (it stretches along one measured foliation and contracts along a transverse one, by a fixed dilatation factor). This trichotomy organizes the whole mapping class group.

**statement (formal)** — each φ ∈ Mod(Σ_g) is periodic, reducible, or pseudo-Anosov; a pseudo-Anosov map fixes a pair of transverse measured foliations, multiplying the measures by λ and 1/λ for a dilatation λ > 1. **[confirmed]**

**basis** — Thurston's classification (announced 1976, *Bull. AMS* 1988), building on Nielsen. Door: Farb–Margalit, *Primer* ch. 13; Fathi–Laudenbach–Poénaru.
