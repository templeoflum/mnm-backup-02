John Dillon, *The Middle Platonists: 80 B.C. to A.D. 220* (Duckworth / Cornell University Press, 1977; revised 1996). The standard survey of Platonism in the period between the Old Academy and Plotinus — Antiochus, Philo, Plutarch, Numenius, the school Platonists — mapping the doctrinal terrain from which Neoplatonism and much Hermetic-Gnostic thought grew. Foundational as the reference work for the philosophical background of the Enneads.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Neo-Platonism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
