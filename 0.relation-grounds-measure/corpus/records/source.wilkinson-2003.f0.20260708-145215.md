Richard H. Wilkinson, *The Complete Gods and Goddesses of Ancient Egypt* (Thames & Hudson, 2003). The standard modern illustrated reference to the ancient Egyptian pantheon: a survey of the gods and goddesses by group and by individual deity, with their iconography, roles, cult centres, and history across the pharaonic period. The common modern door into Egyptian mythology alongside Geraldine Pinch's handbooks. **[confirmed as attribution]**
