Karl Duncker, "On Problem-Solving," *Psychological Monographs* 58, no. 5 (1945), Whole No. 270, i–113 (translation of *Zur Psychologie des produktiven Denkens*, 1935).

Contains — the Gestalt study of productive thinking that introduced **functional fixedness**: the difficulty of seeing that an object can serve a novel function. The famous **candle problem** — mount a candle on a wall given a box of tacks and matches; people fixate on the box as a container and miss its use as a candle shelf. Foundational for the psychology of fixation in problem-solving.
