**statement (plain)** — The Thatcher Illusion, discovered by Peter Thompson in 1980, demonstrates the critical role of configural processing in face perception. When a face is inverted (turned upside down), observers fail to notice grotesque distortions created by locally inverting the eyes and mouth while leaving the face as a whole inverted. However, when the same manipulated face is viewed upright, the distortions become immediately and strikingly apparent, appearing highly abnormal and disturbing.
