James B. Given, *Inquisition and Medieval Society: Power, Discipline, and Resistance in Languedoc* (Cornell University Press, 1997). A study of the medieval inquisition in Languedoc as a technology of power — its documentary techniques, prisons, and punishments, and the ways local communities resisted it — foundational for understanding the institutional side of medieval heresy prosecution.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Medieval Heresy and Inquisition sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
