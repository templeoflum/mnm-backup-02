Geoffrey Hellman, *Mathematics Without Numbers: Towards a Modal-Structural Interpretation* (Oxford University Press, 1989).

Contains — the eliminative, antirealist alternative to Shapiro's ante rem structuralism: mathematics is reinterpreted modally as claims about what *would* hold in *any* structure of the appropriate kind (second-order modal logic), so no abstract structures or positions need exist as objects. The principal foil against which non-eliminative structuralism defines itself.
