Benjamin Baird, Jonathan Smallwood, Michael D. Mrazek, Julia W. Y. Kam, Michael S. Franklin, and Jonathan W. Schooler, "Inspired by Distraction: Mind Wandering Facilitates Creative Incubation," *Psychological Science* 23, no. 10 (2012): 1117–1122.

Contains — an incubation break filled with an *undemanding* task (which maximizes mind-wandering) improved performance on Unusual Uses problems (~14.8% gain) more than a demanding task, rest, or no break — and *only* for problems already encountered, confirming genuine incubation rather than general refreshment. Evidence strongest for deliberate mind-wandering and divergent tasks; convergent-task effects are weaker/mixed.
