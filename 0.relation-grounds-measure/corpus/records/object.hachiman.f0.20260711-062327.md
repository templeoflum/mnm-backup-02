**statement (plain)** — **Hachiman** is the god of war, archery, and divine protection, one of the most widely enshrined deities in Japan. He is venerated as **Hachiman Daibosatsu** ("Great Bodhisattva Hachiman"), a title that fuses kami worship with Buddhist devotion, and is identified with the deified **Emperor Ōjin**. He is the patron of warriors, especially of the **Minamoto** clan, and thus the tutelary deity of the samurai.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: war, archery, divine protection, samurai, the imperial line. Hachiman is a syncretic deity honored under the Buddhist title Hachiman Daibosatsu, identified with the deified Emperor Ōjin and adopted as clan patron by the Minamoto. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Hachiman is documented in Ashkenazi (2003) and this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). He is a Shinto-Buddhist syncretic figure: the title Hachiman Daibosatsu explicitly fuses kami and Buddhist worship, and his cult belongs to the living syncretic tradition rather than to the 8th-century Shinto chronicles. His identification with Emperor Ōjin and his patronage of the Minamoto and the samurai accumulate in post-classical devotion. **[confirmed as attribution]**
