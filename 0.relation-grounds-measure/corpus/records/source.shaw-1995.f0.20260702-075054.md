Gregory Shaw, *Theurgy and the Soul: The Neoplatonism of Iamblichus* (Pennsylvania State University Press, 1995). The standard study of Iamblichus's theurgy — ritual as the soul's participation in the demiurgy of the cosmos — rehabilitating it as serious Platonic philosophy rather than a lapse into magic, against the intellectualist line running from Plotinus through Porphyry. Foundational for understanding ritual's place in the later Platonic tradition and its esoteric afterlives.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Neo-Platonism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
