M. Ausloos, "Generalized Hurst exponent and multifractal function of original and translated texts," *Physical Review E* 86, 031108 (2012). **[confirmed as attribution]** (the dive cites "Ausloos 2012, multifractal Hurst/length-frequency"; venue from knowledge, primary not opened)

Role — the "rudimentary approximation" precursor Alabdulmohsin et al. (2024) improve on: substitutes a word by its frequency/length to build a time series, then measures multifractal Hurst structure. An early quantitative-linguistics door to text self-similarity.
