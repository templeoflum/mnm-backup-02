Joseph H. Peterson (ed.), *The Lesser Key of Solomon: Lemegeton Clavicula Salomonis* (Weiser, 2001). The standard critical edition of the seventeenth-century Solomonic compilation — Goetia, Theurgia-Goetia, Ars Paulina, Ars Almadel, Ars Notoria — edited from the British Library manuscripts rather than from the corrupt occult-revival printings.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. Access note: the older Mathers/Crowley *Goetia* (1904) is public domain; the Peterson edition is an in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
