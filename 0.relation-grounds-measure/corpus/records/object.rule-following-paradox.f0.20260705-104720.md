**statement (plain)** — Kripke reframes Wittgenstein's core (*PI* §201) as a skeptical paradox: "no course of action could be determined by a rule, because every course of action can be brought into accord with the rule." The "quus" example: nothing in past use determines whether one meant *plus* or the deviant *quus*. Kripke's "skeptical solution" (modeled on Hume) grounds meaning in communal agreement / assertibility conditions; the impossibility of private language follows as a corollary.

**basis** — Kripke, *Wittgenstein on Rules and Private Language* (1982, pp. 60, 68). Most commentators accept the paradox as presented but few attribute the skeptical solution to the historical Wittgenstein ("Kripkenstein"). [[source.kripke-1982]] [[source.wittgenstein-1953]] **[confirmed]**
