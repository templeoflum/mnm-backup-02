Edward B. Titchener, *Experimental Psychology: A Manual of Laboratory Practice* (Macmillan, 1901) — the first published appearance of the size-contrast illusion (a circle looks smaller ringed by large circles, larger ringed by small), hence "Titchener circles." Discovered by Hermann Ebbinghaus (c. 1890s), who left no clean primary publication; Titchener popularized it. **[confirmed as attribution]** (Ebbinghaus discovery / Titchener popularization web-verified; exact primary uncertain.)
