Robin Hard, *The Routledge Handbook of Greek Mythology* (Routledge, 2004). A comprehensive reference narrating the myths in their genealogical order with full citation of the ancient sources, based on H. J. Rose's earlier *Handbook of Greek Mythology*. The standard modern English door into the field (alongside Timothy Gantz's *Early Greek Myth*, 1993, for the archaic sources). In print. **[confirmed as attribution]**
