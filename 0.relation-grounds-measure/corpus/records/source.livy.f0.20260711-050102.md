Livy (Titus Livius), *Ab Urbe Condita* ("From the Founding of the City," c. 27–9 BCE), the monumental history of Rome from its legendary origins onward, of which 35 of 142 books survive. Book 1 narrates the foundation legends — Aeneas' arrival in Latium, Romulus and Remus, the she-wolf, and the early kings — and is a principal narrative source for the myths of Rome's beginning.
