Louis Narens, "A General Theory of Ratio Scalability with Remarks About the Measurement-Theoretic Concept of Meaningfulness," *Theory and Decision* 13, no. 1 (1981): 1–70.

Contains — a rigorous development of scale types and of **meaningfulness** as invariance: a statement involving scale values is meaningful iff its truth value is invariant under the admissible transformations of the scales involved. Formalizes and generalizes Stevens's insight that permissible operations/statistics are those preserved under a scale's transformation group; foundational (with Luce) to the invariance/meaningfulness program in representational measurement.
