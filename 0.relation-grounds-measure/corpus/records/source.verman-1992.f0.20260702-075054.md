Mark Verman, *The Books of Contemplation: Medieval Jewish Mystical Sources* (SUNY Press, 1992). Critical texts, translations, and study of the "Circle of Contemplation" (ḥug ha-ʿIyyun) — a shadowy corpus of early thirteenth-century mystical treatises on divine light and emanation that runs parallel to the emergence of theosophic Kabbalah.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
