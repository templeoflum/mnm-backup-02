Arthur Hyman & James J. Walsh (eds.), *Philosophy in the Middle Ages: The Christian, Islamic, and Jewish Traditions* (Harper & Row, 1967; 3rd ed., Hackett, 2010). The standard classroom anthology of medieval philosophy in translation — Augustine, Boethius, Anselm, the falasifa, Maimonides, Aquinas, Scotus, Ockham — treating the three traditions as one conversation.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Philosophy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
