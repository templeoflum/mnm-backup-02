Johann Reuchlin, *De arte cabalistica / On the Art of the Kabbalah* (Hagenau, 1517; English trans. Abaris, 1983). The founding dialogue of Christian Kabbalah — a Christian humanist's systematic appropriation of Jewish kabbalistic doctrine, presented as a three-way conversation among a Kabbalist, a Pythagorean, and a Muslim.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. List caveat: "Trans. Not totally reliable." The 1517 Latin original is public domain; the Goodman translation is an in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
