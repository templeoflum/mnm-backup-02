*Sefer Yetzirah* ("Book of Formation/Creation"), anonymous, compiled between the 2nd and 6th centuries CE (dating contested). Creation through "32 paths of wisdom" — the 10 *sefirot* plus the 22 Hebrew letters — and the 231 gates (letter-pair combinatorics). Earliest manuscripts: 10th-c. Cairo Genizah fragments. Critical edition: A. Peter Hayman, *Sefer Yesira* (Mohr Siebeck, 2004), a synoptic text of three recensions.
