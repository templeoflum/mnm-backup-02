Moshe Idel, *Primeval Evil in Kabbalah: Totality, Perfection, Perfectibility* (KTAV, 2020). Analyzes kabbalistic theories holding that evil precedes good — within the divine realm and in lower reality — tracing this "primeval evil" strand through the tradition, including its Lurianic elaborations.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Safedian Kabbalah. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
