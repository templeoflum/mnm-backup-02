**statement (plain)** — Oscar Reutersvärd's Figures represent a pioneering body of impossible object art created by Swedish artist Oscar Reutersvärd beginning in 1934, predating the more widely known impossible figures of the Penrose family by over two decades. Reutersvärd created his first impossible triangle construction at age 18, initiating a lifelong artistic exploration that ultimately produced over 2,500 impossible figure drawings. His works typically feature geometric constructions built from isometric cube elements arranged to create paradoxical three-dimensional configurations.
