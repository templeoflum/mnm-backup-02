**statement (plain)** — Achilles races a tortoise given a head start. Each time he reaches the tortoise's last position, it has crept a little further; there is always another gap. An infinite sequence of ever-smaller gaps must be crossed, and no single step is the one where he catches up — yet plainly he does.

**statement (framework)** — exposes the gap between observed motion and its analysis into an infinity of subtasks. Aristotle answered with potential vs. actual infinity; calculus with **convergent series** (infinitely many diminishing intervals sum to a finite length); Cantor with completed infinities; the twentieth century with supertasks. Established; whether math dissolves or papers over it stays **open**.
