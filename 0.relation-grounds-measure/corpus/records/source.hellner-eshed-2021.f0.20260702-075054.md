Melila Hellner-Eshed, *Seekers of the Face: Secrets of the Idra Rabba (The Great Assembly) of the Zohar* (Stanford University Press, 2021). A full-length study of the Idra Rabba, the Zohar's dramatic account of the divine faces (partsufim) revealed at the great assembly of Shimon bar Yoḥai's circle — a close reading of the corpus's theological summit.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
