**statement (plain)** — instead of a fixed menu of roles (Agent, Patient, Goal…), Dowty proposes two gradable prototypes. An argument is more Agent-like the more of these it entails: volitional involvement, sentience/perception, causing an event or change of state, movement, independent existence. More Patient-like: undergoes change of state, incremental theme, causally affected, stationary relative to another participant, no independent existence. Roles become degrees of membership, not boxes.

**statement (formal)** — Argument Selection Principle: in a transitive predicate, the argument with the greater number of Proto-Agent entailments is lexicalized as subject; the one with more Proto-Patient entailments as direct object. **[confirmed]**

**basis** — Dowty's desiderata for role theories (Uniqueness, Distinctness, Independence) motivate the move; Levin's 2021 retrospective critically reviews the entailment sets and the selection principle. PropBank's Arg0/Arg1 annotation scheme operationalizes the proto-roles computationally (prose link — [[source.palmer-2005]]). [[source.dowty-1991]]
