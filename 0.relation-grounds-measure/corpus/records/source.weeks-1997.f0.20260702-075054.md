Andrew Weeks, *Paracelsus: Speculative Theory and the Crisis of the Early Reformation* (SUNY Press, 1997). An intellectual study of Paracelsus that reads his medical-alchemical speculation against the religious upheaval of the early Reformation, arguing his thought is inseparable from its theological context. Foundational for placing Paracelsian alchemy and medicine within sixteenth-century religious history rather than treating them as free-standing occultism.
