J. H. Chajes, "Spheres, Sefirot, and the Imaginal Astronomical Discourse of Classical Kabbalah," *HTR* 113.2 (2020), 230–262. **[confirmed]**

Role — analyzes the sefirot as imaged on astronomical sphere-diagram models; notes Cordovero called the inscription of sefirot on parchment an *ilan*. Companion to [[source.chajes-2022]] (*The Kabbalistic Tree*), which argues the ilan form is primarily indebted to the Porphyrian tree and celestial-sphere maps.
