Edmund R. Hunt, Simon Jones, and Sabine Hauert, "Testing the limits of pheromone stigmergy in high-density robot swarms," *Royal Society Open Science* 6, no. 11 (2019): 190225.

Contains — the honest counterweight to stigmergy boosterism. Repellent-pheromone dispersal had only been shown for small swarms (N < 30); testing real Kilobots an order of magnitude larger (N > 300) and simulating to N = 400, the authors find "a simple stigmergic avoidance algorithm becomes first no better, then inferior to, the area coverage of non-interacting random walkers." Earlier termite-style material-deposition robots likewise *worsened* past three or four units as physical interactions became the bottleneck. Evidence that the shared medium can congest — stigmergy is not automatically scalable.
