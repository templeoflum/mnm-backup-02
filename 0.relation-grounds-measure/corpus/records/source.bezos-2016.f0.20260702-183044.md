Jeff Bezos, letter to Amazon shareholders, 2016.

Contains — the widely-cited distinction between **Type 1 decisions** ("one-way doors" — consequential and irreversible, to be made "methodically, carefully, slowly, with great deliberation and consultation") and **Type 2 decisions** ("two-way doors" — changeable, reversible, that "can and should be made quickly by high-judgment individuals or small groups"). Warns that applying the heavyweight one-way-door process to two-way-door decisions produces "slowness, unthoughtful risk aversion, failure to experiment… and consequently diminished invention."

Access — public (Amazon IR; SEC EX-99.1); widely reproduced. **[confirmed as attribution]**
