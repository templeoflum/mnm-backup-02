Alan Charles Kors and Edward Peters (eds.), *Witchcraft in Europe, 400–1700: A Documentary History* (University of Pennsylvania Press, 2nd ed., 2001). A chronological anthology of primary documents — patristic and medieval texts, demonological treatises, trial records, and skeptical responses — with editorial introductions. Foundational as the standard classroom sourcebook for the European witchcraft tradition.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early-Modern Witch Hunts (the list credits "Peters"). Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
