Mohamed Gad-el-Hak, *Flow Control: Passive, Active, and Reactive Flow Management* (Cambridge University Press, 2000).

Contains — the standard synthesis of the engineering craft of directing a flow, organized by the control taxonomy it helped fix: **passive** control (vortex generators, screens, honeycombs, riblets, streamlining — no energy input), **active** control (steady or unsteady suction, blowing, synthetic jets — energy added), and **reactive/feedback** control (sensors + actuators responding to the flow state in real time). Emphasizes control of turbulent flows and coherent structures.
