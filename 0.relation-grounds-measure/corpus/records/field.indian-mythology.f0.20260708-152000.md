**origin** — the **Rigveda** (c. 1500–1200 BCE) — the oldest of the four Vedas, a collection of hymns to the gods, giving the earliest attested form of the Vedic pantheon (Indra, Agni, Varuna, Soma, and the like) and the ritual cosmos ancestral to later Hindu myth. [[source.rigveda]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — the **Mahabharata** and the **Ramayana** (composed c. 400 BCE – 400 CE and c. 500 BCE – 100 BCE respectively) — the two great Sanskrit epics, in which the classical Hindu mythology of Vishnu, Shiva, and their avatars (Rama, Krishna) is given narrative form; the Bhagavad Gita is embedded in the Mahabharata. [[source.mahabharata]] [[source.ramayana]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Wendy Doniger, *Hindu Myths* (Penguin, 1975), with Cornelia Dimmitt & J. A. B. van Buitenen, *Classical Hindu Mythology: A Reader in the Sanskrit Purāṇas* (Temple, 1978) — the standard modern reference translations for the classical Hindu myths.
