Richard Held et al., "The newly sighted fail to match seen with felt," *Nature Neuroscience* 14, no. 5 (2011): 551–553 — tests newly sighted congenitally blind subjects (Project Prakash) within 48 hours of surgery: they cannot immediately transfer shape knowledge from touch to vision (Molyneux's "no"), but cross-modal transfer emerges within days. **[confirmed as attribution]**
