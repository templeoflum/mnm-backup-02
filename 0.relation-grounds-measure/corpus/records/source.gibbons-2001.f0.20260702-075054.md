B. J. Gibbons, *Spirituality and the Occult: From the Renaissance to the Modern Age* (Routledge, 2001). Argues that occult and esoteric currents were not a marginal residue but a persistent strand of Western spirituality, tracing their transmission and transformation from Renaissance Hermetism and theosophy into modern thought.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Theosophy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
