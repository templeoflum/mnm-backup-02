**statement (plain)** — Aristotle held heavier bodies fall faster. Galileo reasons: tie a heavy and a light body together. The light one should slow the heavy one (falling slower); but the combined body is heavier than either, so it should fall faster. Contradiction — so speed cannot depend on weight; all bodies fall alike.

**statement (framework)** — a pure **thought experiment** (no apparatus) refuting Aristotelian dynamics by internal contradiction — a model of a priori reasoning in physics; anticipates the universality of free fall (later the equivalence principle). Established.
