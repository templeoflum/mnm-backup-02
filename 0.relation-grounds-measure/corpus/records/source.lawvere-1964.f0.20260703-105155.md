F. William Lawvere, "An Elementary Theory of the Category of Sets," *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* 52, no. 6 (1964): 1506–1511.

Contains — the origin of **ETCS**: a first-order axiomatization of the category of sets (a well-pointed topos with a natural-numbers object and choice), giving a *structural* foundation for mathematics in which a set's elements have no properties beyond their role and membership is local, not the global ∈ of ZFC. The prototypical structural set theory and an alternative foundation to material set theory. Long version with commentary in *Reprints in Theory and Applications of Categories* 11 (2005).
