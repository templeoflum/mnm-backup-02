Kim J. Vicente & Jens Rasmussen, "Ecological Interface Design: Theoretical Foundations," *IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics* 22, no. 4 (1992): 589–606.

Contains — the framework for **ecological interface design (EID)**: build displays around the *abstraction hierarchy* of the work domain (from physical form up to functional purpose) so that the deep constraints and affordances of the process are directly visible, supporting all three levels of Rasmussen's skill/rule/knowledge behavior — and especially knowledge-based problem-solving during unanticipated events. The principled alternative to interfaces that only show single-sensor readings.
