Masaaki Imai, *Kaizen: The Key to Japan's Competitive Success* (Random House, 1986).

Contains — the work that brought **kaizen** (改善, "change for the better" — continuous, incremental improvement) to Western management as an umbrella philosophy over TQC, JIT, suggestion systems, and standardized work. Frames improvement as everyone's daily responsibility, small step by small step, on the *gemba* (the actual place of work), in contrast to big-leap innovation. Followed by *Gemba Kaizen* (1997).
