Arthur Versluis, *Wisdom's Children: A Christian Esoteric Tradition* (SUNY Press, 1999). A study of the Christian theosophic current that flowed from Jacob Boehme — Gichtel, Pordage, Leade, and the wider Behmenist circles in Germany, England, and America — treating theosophy as a coherent esoteric tradition of its own.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Early Modern Theosophy sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
