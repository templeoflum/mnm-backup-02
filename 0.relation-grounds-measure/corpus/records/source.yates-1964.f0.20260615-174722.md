Frances A. Yates, *Giordano Bruno and the Hermetic Tradition* (Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1964) — places Bruno within a Hermetic tradition of correspondences ("as above, so below") and the *prisca theologia*; field-defining for Renaissance esotericism. The "Yates thesis" — Hermeticism as a stimulus to the Scientific Revolution — is contested by later historians (Vickers, Westman). Extended in *The Art of Memory* (1966).
