Alexander Roob, *Alchemy & Mysticism* (Taschen, 1997). A visual sourcebook of hundreds of alchemical, hermetic, and mystical engravings and emblems from the medieval through early modern periods, organized thematically with brief commentary. Foundational as the standard one-volume image atlas of the alchemical imagination.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Alchemy. Primarily an image compendium, not a scholarly monograph — commentary is light. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
