F. Denti, D. Doimo, A. Laio, A. Mira, "The generalized ratios intrinsic dimension estimator," *Scientific Reports* 12, 20005 (2022).

Role — generalizes TWO-NN's two-neighbor ratio to ratios of higher-order neighbor distances (GRIDE), trading locality for variance; the closed-form Pareto MLE d̂ = N/Σᵢ log μᵢ is properly attributed to this Pareto-MLE derivation, not to Facco et al. (2017). Implemented, with TWO-NN and HIDALGO, in the `intRinsic` R package (Denti 2023, *J. Stat. Software* 106(9)). **[confirmed]**
