Maureen O'Sullivan & Paul Ekman, "The wizards of deception detection," in Pär Anders Granhag & Leif A. Strömwall (eds.), *The Detection of Deception in Forensic Contexts* (Cambridge University Press, 2004), pp. 269–286.

Contains — the Wizards Project: from ~20,000 people tested, roughly 50 "Truth Wizards" (~0.25%) detected deception at ≥80% accuracy (vs. ~50% chance) by reading micro-expressions plus inconsistencies across face, body, and voice — clusters, not single tells. The empirical case for rare, trainable expertise in reading concealed states.
