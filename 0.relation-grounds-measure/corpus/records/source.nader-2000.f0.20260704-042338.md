K. Nader, G. E. Schafe, J. E. LeDoux, "Fear memories require protein synthesis in the amygdala for reconsolidation after retrieval," *Nature* 406:722–726 (2000).

Role — consolidated fear memories, when reactivated, "return to a labile state" requiring de novo protein synthesis (blocked by anisomycin in the lateral/basal amygdala) to re-stabilize — the memory-reconsolidation hypothesis, contradicting the view that consolidated memories are fixed/indelible. **[confirmed]**
