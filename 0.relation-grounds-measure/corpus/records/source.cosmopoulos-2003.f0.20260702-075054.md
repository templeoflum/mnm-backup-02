Michael B. Cosmopoulos (ed.), *Greek Mysteries: The Archaeology and Ritual of Ancient Greek Secret Cults* (Routledge, 2003). A collection of specialist essays on the archaeology and ritual of Greek mystery cults — Eleusis, Samothrace, the Kabeiroi, Dionysiac and Orphic rites — grounding the study of the mysteries in material evidence. Foundational as the archaeological complement to text-based treatments such as Burkert's.
