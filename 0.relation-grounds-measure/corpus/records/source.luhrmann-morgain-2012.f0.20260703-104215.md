T. M. Luhrmann & Rachel Morgain, "Prayer as inner sense cultivation: An attentional learning theory of spiritual experience," *Ethos* 40, no. 4 (2012): 359–389.

Contains — the experimental arm of Luhrmann's cultivation program: Christians randomly assigned to one month of kataphatic (imaginative) prayer vs. Bible study; the kataphatic group afterward reported more unusual sensory experience, more vivid mental imagery, and more "sensory overrides" (hearing/seeing what is not materially present). Argues these practices "change mental experience" and make God feel more real, while explicitly bracketing metaphysics ("to say this is not to say that God is an illusion"). Direct evidence for the **trainable/cultivated** and **framework-licensed** invariants of inward channeling.
