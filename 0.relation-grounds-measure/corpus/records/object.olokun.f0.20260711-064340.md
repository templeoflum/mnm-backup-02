**statement (plain)** — **Olokun** is the **Orisha** of the deep ocean, the reaches where light cannot penetrate, in Yoruba tradition. Olokun is held to be androgynous — sometimes male, sometimes female, sometimes both or neither — and rules the unfathomable deep, its hidden wealth, and its terror. In the diaspora Olokun is especially associated with the ancestors of the Middle Passage, the enslaved who died at sea.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: ocean depths, wealth, secrets, the unconscious. Androgynous ruler of the abyssal sea; keeper of its hidden treasures and its dangers; in the diaspora tied to the ancestors lost in the Middle Passage. Associated with **Yemoja** and the wider domain of the waters. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962) and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; Olokun's gender, form, and relation to other water Orisha vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions. **[confirmed as attribution]**
