Nelson Goodman, *Fact, Fiction, and Forecast* (Harvard UP, 1955) — introduces the predicate **grue** and the **new riddle of induction**: identical evidence confirms incompatible generalizations, so induction presupposes a prior fact about which predicates are *projectible*; Goodman's own answer is the theory of entrenchment. **[confirmed as attribution]**
