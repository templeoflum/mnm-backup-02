J. Jastrow, "Studies from the Laboratory of Experimental Psychology of the University of Wisconsin. II," *The American Journal of Psychology* 4(3) (1892), 381–428.

Contains Jastrow's presentation of the ring-segment / annular-sector size illusion (two identical tapering arches appearing unequal). Note the discovery-vs-publication gap: the object record attributes the illusion to Jastrow **1889** (the year of discovery), but the primary published account is this **1892** paper. **[confirmed]** — the year, venue, volume, issue, and page range agree across the Wikipedia article and the AJP record.
