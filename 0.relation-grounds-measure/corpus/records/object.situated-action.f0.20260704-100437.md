**statement (plain)** - Suchman's claim, from watching office workers fight a photocopier: "however planned, purposeful actions are inevitably situated actions." A plan doesn't run you like a program; it's a resource you draw on while responding, moment to moment, to the actual situation. Her contrast: Trukese navigators fix an objective and adjust course continuously to conditions, where the European tradition forms a plan and adheres to it - and it's the Trukese, ad-hoc mode that models real skilled action.

**basis** - Suchman (1987, *Plans and Situated Actions*; expanded 2007), from her Xerox PARC ethnography; a critique of the planning model in AI. The copier "breakdowns" trace to the machine's inability to read context.
