Ursula K. Le Guin, "The Ones Who Walk Away from Omelas" (1973) — a short story functioning as a moral thought experiment: a radiant city whose happiness depends on one child's permanent misery; some citizens simply walk away. Drawing on Dostoevsky and William James, the sharpest narrative challenge to aggregative ethics. **[confirmed as attribution]**
