John M. Cooper (ed.), *Plato: Complete Works* (Hackett, 1997). The standard one-volume English edition of Plato — all the dialogues and letters, including the spuria and dubia, in modern translations with introductory notes. Foundational as the default scholarly citation edition of Plato in English.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Classical Philosophy section. Access note: public-domain translations of the underlying dialogues (Benjamin Jowett, 19th c.) are freely available online, though dated. **[confirmed as attribution]**
