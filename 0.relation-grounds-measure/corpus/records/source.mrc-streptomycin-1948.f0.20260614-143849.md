Medical Research Council, "Streptomycin Treatment of Pulmonary Tuberculosis," *British Medical Journal* 2 (1948):769–782. Designed by the statistician Austin Bradford Hill.

Role — origin of the modern RCT: treatment allocation by random numbers with concealed assignment and concurrent controls; widely regarded as the first modern randomized controlled trial. Anchor for [[object.randomized-controlled-trial]].

Access — record and documentation via the James Lind Library.
