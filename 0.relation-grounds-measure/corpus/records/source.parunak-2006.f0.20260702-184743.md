H. Van Dyke Parunak, "A Survey of Environments and Mechanisms for Human-Human Stigmergy," in *Environments for Multi-Agent Systems II*, LNCS 3830 (Springer, 2006), 163–186.

Contains — surveys human stigmergy and the engineering of **digital pheromones** (virtual traces with tunable decay, deposited on a shared map, for coordinating unmanned vehicles and multi-agent systems). Offers the *action (ergon) leaves a mark (stigma)* reading of the etymology (complementary to Grassé's *work-as-stimulus* reading). Source of the provocative near-tautology that "it would be more difficult to show a functioning human institution that is *not* stigmergic" — quoted in the literature as the warning that a concept explaining everything risks explaining nothing.
