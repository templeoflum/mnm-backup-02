**statement (plain)** — The Stepping Feet Illusion, discovered by Stuart Anstis in 2001, is a compelling motion illusion in which two bars moving at constant and identical speeds appear to move at different velocities, creating the impression of alternating acceleration and deceleration reminiscent of stepping feet. The illusion is typically created by overlaying two horizontal bars on a background of alternating dark and light vertical stripes, with the bars positioned such that they alternately pass over contrasting background regions. When one bar is on a dark stripe while the other is on a light stripe, they appear to be moving at different speeds, even though both maintain constant velocity.
