Full citation — Y. Zhang, "Bounded gaps between primes," *Annals of Mathematics* (2) 179 (2014), no. 3, 1121–1174. Announced/submitted April 2013; accepted ~3 weeks after submission.

Contains the first proof that liminf_{n→∞} (p_{n+1} − p_n) < 7×10⁷ (70 million) — i.e. infinitely many prime pairs differ by at most 70 million. Method: refinement of Goldston–Pintz–Yıldırım (GPY) plus a stronger (well-distributed-moduli) form of the Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem.

Role — the breakthrough proof source for [[object.bounded-gaps-theorem]].
