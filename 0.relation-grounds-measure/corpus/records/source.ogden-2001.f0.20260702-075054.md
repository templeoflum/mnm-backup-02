Daniel Ogden, *Greek and Roman Necromancy* (Princeton University Press, 2001). A comprehensive study of communication with the dead in Greco-Roman antiquity — oracles of the dead (nekuomanteia), ghost-raising rites, literary necromancies, and the figure of the necromancer. Foundational as the standard scholarly treatment of the subject.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
