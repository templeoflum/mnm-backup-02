**statement (plain)** — A mad scientist keeps your brain in a vat, wired to a computer feeding it exactly the signals of ordinary life. Every perception is simulated; nothing would seem amiss. How do you know this isn't happening to you now? A technologized version of Descartes's evil demon — same experiential evidence, systematically false beliefs.

**statement (framework)** — the standard-bearer for **external-world skepticism**: if the scenario can't be ruled out, we lack knowledge of the external world. Putnam's twist: by **semantic externalism**, an envatted brain's words "brain"/"vat" refer only to simulated things, so it cannot truly think "I am a brain in a vat" — the hypothesis is self-refuting. Critics dispute the scope of externalism and whether it vindicates knowledge or only blocks one formulation. Established; **open**.
