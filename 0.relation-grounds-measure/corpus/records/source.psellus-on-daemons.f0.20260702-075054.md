Michael Psellus (attrib.), *On the Operation of Daemons* (Greek, 11th century; English trans. Marcus Collisson, 1843). Dialogue classifying daemons into six orders and describing their natures and operations; a key Byzantine channel for demonological ideas into later Western magic and demonology. Modern scholarship disputes the attribution to Psellus (often treated as pseudo-Psellus).

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. The 1843 Collisson translation is public domain. **[confirmed as attribution]**
