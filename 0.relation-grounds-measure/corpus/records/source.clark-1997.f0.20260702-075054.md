Stuart Clark, *Thinking with Demons: The Idea of Witchcraft in Early Modern Europe* (Oxford University Press, 1997). A monumental intellectual history showing how witchcraft belief was rational within early-modern frameworks of language, science, history, religion, and politics — demonology as a way of thinking, not an aberration. Foundational as the landmark study of the idea (rather than the prosecution) of witchcraft.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Witch Hunts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
