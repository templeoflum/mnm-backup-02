Carol V. Kaske and John R. Clark (ed., trans.), *Marsilio Ficino: Three Books on Life* (MRTS/Renaissance Society of America, 1989). Critical Latin edition and English translation of Ficino's *De vita libri tres* (1489) — the scholar's health, prolonging life, and the astral magic of *De vita coelitus comparanda* — with introduction and notes; the standard edition.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Renaissance Magic sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
