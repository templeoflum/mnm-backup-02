Emily S. Patterson, Emilie M. Roth, David D. Woods, Renée Chow & José Orlando Gomes, "Handoff strategies in settings with high consequences for failure: lessons for health care operations," *International Journal for Quality in Health Care* 16, no. 2 (2004): 125–132.

Contains — an observational study of shift handover in four high-consequence domains — NASA Johnson Space Center mission control, Canadian nuclear power plants, a US railroad dispatch center, and a Toronto ambulance dispatch center — cataloguing 21 handoff strategies (19 seen in at least one domain). Strategies include face-to-face verbal update with interactive questioning, reading back, updating a written/electronic log continuously, overlapping shifts, and delaying transfer during unstable periods. The cross-domain evidence base for what a robust pass-down actually does.
