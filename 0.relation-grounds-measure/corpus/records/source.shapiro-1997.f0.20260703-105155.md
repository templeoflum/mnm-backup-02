Stewart Shapiro, *Philosophy of Mathematics: Structure and Ontology* (Oxford University Press, 1997).

Contains — the canonical defense of **ante rem** (non-eliminative realist) structuralism: structures exist *sui generis*, ontologically prior to and independent of any system instantiating them; mathematical objects are *places in structures* — "offices rather than officeholders" ("structures are prior to places in the same sense that any organization is prior to the offices that constitute it"). Structures have a dual nature (universals yet named as particulars), and Shapiro gives an axiomatic "structure theory" of which structures exist. Contrasts with eliminative and modal structuralism.
