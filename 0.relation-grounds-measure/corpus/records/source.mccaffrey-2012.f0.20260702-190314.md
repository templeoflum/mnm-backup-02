Tony McCaffrey, "Innovation Relies on the Obscure: A Key to Overcoming the Classic Problem of Functional Fixedness," *Psychological Science* 23, no. 3 (2012): 215–218.

Contains — introduces the **Generic Parts Technique (GPT)**: systematically decompose an object or problem into its constituent parts described *without their conventional function* (a candle's "wick" becomes "string," which affords new uses). Subjects trained on GPT solved on average **67% more** insight problems than a control group. A concrete, evidence-based defixation method.
