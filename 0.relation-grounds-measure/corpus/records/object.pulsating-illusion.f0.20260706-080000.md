**statement (plain)** — The Pulsating Illusion refers to a class of visual phenomena in which static geometric patterns appear to rhythmically expand, contract, or pulsate despite containing no physical motion or luminance modulation. These illusions typically involve radial or concentric configurations with high-contrast edges, spatial frequency gradients, or alternating light-dark regions that create dynamic percepts when viewed steadily or during small eye movements. The pulsating quality often manifests as a breathing or throbbing sensation, with regions appearing to grow and shrink in synchrony or in counter-phase.
