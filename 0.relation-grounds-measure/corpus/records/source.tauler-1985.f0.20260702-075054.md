Maria Shrady (trans.), *Johannes Tauler: Sermons* (Paulist Press, 1985). Selected vernacular sermons of the fourteenth-century Dominican of Strasbourg — Eckhart's most influential heir, preaching the birth of God in the ground of the soul in a more pastoral, practicable register.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
