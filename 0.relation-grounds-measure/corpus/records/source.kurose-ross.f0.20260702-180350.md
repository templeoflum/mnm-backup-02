James F. Kurose and Keith W. Ross, *Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach* (Pearson; 1st ed. 2000, currently 8th ed. 2021).

Contains — the most widely used undergraduate networking textbook. Its transport-layer chapter (ch. 3) is a clean door into TCP's two brakes: flow control (the receive window) and congestion control (slow-start, congestion avoidance, AIMD, fast recovery), including the `min(rwnd, cwnd)` send limit.
