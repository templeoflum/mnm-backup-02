**origin** — the oral tradition of the **Baltic** peoples (Lithuanian and Latvian), preserved above all in the Latvian **dainas** (folk-songs) — a conservative Indo-European mythology close kin to the Slavic. **[confirmed]**

**codification** — the scholarly recovery of Baltic religion: Marija **Gimbutas**, *The Balts* (1963), and A. J. **Greimas**, *Of Gods and Men: Studies in Lithuanian Mythology* (1992). [[source.gimbutas-1963]] [[source.greimas-1992]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Gimbutas, *The Balts* (1963), with the Latvian *dainas* as the primary folk-song corpus. [[source.latvian-dainas]]

*grade a — origin (oral / dainas), codification (Gimbutas / Greimas), door present. A single-tradition field under [[field.comparative-mythology]]. Grounded per the operator's signed word (20260711). NOTE: the ACP mis-filed these four figures under its "Finnish" roster — Baltic is a distinct Indo-European tradition, NOT Finnic (Perkūnas is cognate with Slavic Perun).*
