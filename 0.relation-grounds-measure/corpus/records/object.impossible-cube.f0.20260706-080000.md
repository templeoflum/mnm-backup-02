**statement (plain)** — The Impossible Cube is an impossible object depicting a cube-like structure where edges connect in ways that violate the constraints of three-dimensional Euclidean geometry. The figure typically shows a wire-frame or solid cube where certain edges appear to connect front faces to back faces in contradictory ways, creating depth relationships that cannot be consistently maintained throughout the structure. Unlike the bistable Necker cube, which allows two valid three-dimensional interpretations, the Impossible Cube presents a configuration where local depth cues suggest relationships that are globally inconsistent.
