Franz Brentano, *Psychologie vom empirischen Standpunkt* (*Psychology from an Empirical Standpoint*, 1874; vol. 1 only published that year).

**citation** — Brentano, F. (1874). *Psychologie vom empirischen Standpunkt*. Leipzig: Duncker & Humblot.

**role** — origin of the modern concept of [[object.intentionality]]. Brentano names the "intentional in-existence" (*intentionale Inexistenz*) of an object as the defining mark distinguishing mental phenomena from physical ones: every mental act has content, is directed at an object. Husserl attended Brentano's Vienna lectures (1884–1886) and reinterpreted his "descriptive psychology" as phenomenology. The term has roots in Aristotle and the Scholastic/Thomistic tradition.

**access** — SEP "Franz Brentano"; English trans. by Rancurello, Terrell & McAlister (Routledge, 1973/1995).
