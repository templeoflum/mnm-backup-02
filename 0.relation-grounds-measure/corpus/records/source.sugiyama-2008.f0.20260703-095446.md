Yuki Sugiyama, Minoru Fukui, Macoto Kikuchi, Katsuya Hasebe, Akihiro Nakayama, Katsuhiro Nishinari, Shin-ichi Tadaki & Satoshi Yukawa, "Traffic jams without bottlenecks — experimental evidence for the physical mechanism of the formation of a jam," *New Journal of Physics* 10 (2008): 033001.

Contains — the classic circular-track experiment: vehicles placed equidistantly on a ~230 m ring and instructed to hold a constant speed. Above a critical density, homogeneous flow becomes unstable; a tiny fluctuation grows until a jam cluster forms spontaneously and "propagates backward like a solitary wave." The paper's framing: "a bottleneck is only a trigger and not the essential origin of a traffic jam," and the jam is "a collective phenomenon like 'dynamical' phase transitions."
