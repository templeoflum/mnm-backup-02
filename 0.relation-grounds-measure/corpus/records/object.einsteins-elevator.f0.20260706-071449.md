**statement (plain)** — In a sealed, windowless elevator you feel a force pressing your feet to the floor. Is it gravity, or is the elevator accelerating upward through empty space? Einstein's answer: no local experiment can distinguish them — free fall locally cancels gravity, and acceleration locally mimics it.

**statement (framework)** — the **equivalence principle** — the conceptual cornerstone of general relativity, implying gravity is the geometry of spacetime and predicting light bending and gravitational time dilation. Einstein called the 1907 insight his 'happiest thought.' Established.
