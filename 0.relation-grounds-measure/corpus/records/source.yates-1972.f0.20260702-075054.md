Frances A. Yates, *The Rosicrucian Enlightenment* (Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1972). The classic narrative of the Rosicrucian moment — the manifestos, the Palatinate court of Frederick and Elizabeth, and a proposed Hermetic-alchemical current linking Dee's mission abroad to the origins of the scientific enlightenment.
