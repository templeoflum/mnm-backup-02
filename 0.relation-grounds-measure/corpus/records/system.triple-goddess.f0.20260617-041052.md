**origin** — the modern triple-goddess concept owes most to Robert Graves's *The White Goddess* (1948) and was elaborated in 20th-century Wicca and Goddess spirituality. [[source.graves-1948]]

**door** — Graves, *The White Goddess* (1948), as the modern source.

**settlement applied:** the **canon is the triad** — Maiden, Mother, Crone. The collected set padded this to nine with non-canonical neopagan **aspects** (Enchantress, Amazon, Queen, Lover, Sorceress, Dark Mother). Per the ratified Bin-1 settlement, the **six aspects are dropped**; the three are carried.

**standing** — a modern religious/mythopoetic construct (not an attested ancient triad as such). Held `doctrinal`.
