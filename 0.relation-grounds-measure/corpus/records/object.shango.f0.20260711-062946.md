**statement (plain)** — **Shango** (**Changó** / **Xangô**) is the **Orisha** of thunder, lightning, and fire, and the lord of justice, kingship, and the dance. He was once the fourth **Alafin** (king) of the Oyo empire, deified after his death. He hurls thunderstones and wields the double-headed axe, striking the unjust with lightning.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: thunder, lightning, fire, justice, kingship, dance, virility. Deified fourth **Alafin** (king) of Oyo; wields the double axe (**oshe**); casts down thunderstones; associated with red and white and with virile power; his wives include **Oya**, **Oshun**, and **Obba**. Diaspora names: **Changó** (Santería), **Xangô** (Candomblé). **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962), Bascom (1969), and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; the account of his human kingship and his attributes vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions of Santería and Candomblé. **[confirmed as attribution]**
