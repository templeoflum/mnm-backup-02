The **Mahabharata** (composed c. 400 BCE – 400 CE). The great Sanskrit epic — the longest poem of world literature — recounting the dynastic struggle and the **Kurukshetra war** between the **Pandavas** and their cousins the **Kauravas**. Traditionally attributed to the sage **Vyasa**, it grew by accretion over centuries into its received form. Embedded within it is the **Bhagavad Gita**, the discourse of **Krishna** (avatar of Vishnu) to the warrior **Arjuna** on the eve of battle, one of the central texts of Hindu religious thought. A principal source, with the Ramayana, for the classical Hindu mythology of Vishnu and his avatars. **[confirmed as attribution]**
