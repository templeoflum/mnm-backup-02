Anonymous, *The Cloud of Unknowing* (14th c.). Middle English treatise of practical contemplative instruction in the Dionysian line — God is met not by knowing but by a "sharp dart of longing love" pressed into the cloud of unknowing — the central English apophatic text.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Older editions are public domain — e.g. Evelyn Underhill's 1922 edition is freely available online; the CWS edition (trans. James Walsh, Paulist Press, 1981) is an in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
