**statement (plain)** — **Louhi** is the witch-queen of **Pohjola**, the North, and the chief antagonist of the Kalevala. She sets the heroes their hardest tasks and receives the **Sampo** when Ilmarinen forges it for her. When Väinämöinen and his companions later steal the Sampo back for their own land, she wages war to reclaim it, and in the struggle the Sampo is broken and lost into the sea.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: sorcery, the North, antagonism. She is a shapeshifter and weather-worker, able to raise storms and plagues, and embodies the hostile power of the frozen, distant North against the heroes of Kaleva. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Louhi is attested throughout the Kalevala and treated in modern scholarship ([[source.pentikainen-1999]]); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Kalevala is Elias Lönnrot's nineteenth-century compilation of oral folk poetry, not an ancient text, so her role as the epic's unifying antagonist reflects Lönnrot's narrative arrangement of varied oral material. **[confirmed as attribution]**
