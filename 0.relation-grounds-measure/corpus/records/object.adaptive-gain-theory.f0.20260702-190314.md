**statement (plain)** — the explore/exploit tradeoff has a physiological knob. The locus-coeruleus–norepinephrine (LC-NE) system runs in two modes: **phasic** firing, time-locked to task events, sharpens focus and supports exploiting the current task; a shift to **tonic** firing loosens focus, drives disengagement, and pushes toward searching for alternatives (exploring). The felt "I've squeezed this dry — my mind wants to wander" is this shift; boredom and fatigue may be evolved signals to switch modes.

**statement (framework)** — a neuromodulatory mechanism for the explore/exploit tradeoff [[object.explore-exploit-tradeoff]]; "adaptive gain" because NE adjusts the gain (responsivity) of target neurons to optimize the tradeoff for current utility.

**basis** — Aston-Jones & Cohen 2005 [[source.aston-jones-cohen-2005]], integrating LC electrophysiology with decision theory. *Support* — influential across cognitive neuroscience of attention, effort, and decision-making. *Limits* — the phasic/tonic-to-explore/exploit mapping is a strong model still being tested causally; NE is one of several interacting neuromodulators. **[confirmed]**
