Joseph Naveh and Shaul Shaked, *Amulets and Magic Bowls: Aramaic Incantations of Late Antiquity* (Magnes Press, 1985). A critical edition and translation of Aramaic amulets and Babylonian incantation bowls, with philological commentary. Foundational as a primary scholarly gateway into the epigraphic evidence for Jewish magic in late antiquity.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; specialist academic edition, likely secondhand or library. **[confirmed as attribution]**
