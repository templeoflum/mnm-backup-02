Judith Jarvis Thomson, "A Defense of Abortion," *Philosophy & Public Affairs* 1, no. 1 (1971): 47–66 — grants the fetus a right to life for the sake of argument and, via the violinist case, argues that a right to life is not a right to use another's body without consent. **[confirmed as attribution]**
