John R. Searle, "Minds, Brains, and Programs," *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 3, no. 3 (1980): 417–457 — the target article (with 27 peer commentaries and Searle's reply, in the BBS format) that introduced the **Chinese Room** thought experiment and the distinction between **strong AI** (running the right program is *sufficient* for a mind) and **weak AI** (the computer as a tool for studying the mind). Its thesis: syntax is not sufficient for semantics. **[confirmed]** (venue + pages web-verified.)
