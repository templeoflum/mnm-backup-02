Hans Dieter Betz (ed.), *The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation, Including the Demotic Spells* (University of Chicago Press, 1986). The complete English translation of the Greco-Egyptian magical papyri (PGM) plus the Demotic material — spells, hymns, rituals, and formularies from Roman-era Egypt; the standard sourcebook for ancient magic.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Ancient Magic sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
