**statement (plain)** — Delta Motion is a specific type of apparent motion phenomenon that occurs under particular temporal and spatial parameters, distinguished from other forms of apparent motion by its characteristic perceptual qualities and the stimulus conditions that produce it. While apparent motion in general refers to the perception of movement from sequentially presented static stimuli, delta motion represents a particular regime within the broader parameter space of apparent motion phenomena. The effect is observed when the timing and spacing of stimulus presentations fall within specific ranges that differ from those producing optimal beta motion or other apparent motion variants.
