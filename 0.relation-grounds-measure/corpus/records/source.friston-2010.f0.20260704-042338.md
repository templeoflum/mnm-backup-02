K. Friston, "The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?" *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 11(2):127–138 (2010). Earlier: Friston, Kilner & Harrison, "A free energy principle for the brain," *J. Physiol.-Paris* 100:70–87 (2006).

Role — the FEP: self-organizing biological systems minimize variational free energy (surprise / prediction error / model–world discrepancy); adaptive organisms "are embodied generative models of their environments." "In perception and learning, part of the agent becomes more like the world; and in action, the world becomes more like the agent." **[confirmed]**
