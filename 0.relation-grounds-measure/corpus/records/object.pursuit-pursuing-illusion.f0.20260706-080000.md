**statement (plain)** — The Pursuit-Pursuing Illusion is a perceptual phenomenon in which the motion characteristics of a target object appear distorted during smooth pursuit eye movements. When observers track a moving target with their eyes, the perceived velocity, trajectory, or continuity of the target's motion can differ systematically from its actual physical motion. This illusion arises from the complex interaction between retinal motion signals and extraretinal signals related to eye movement commands.
