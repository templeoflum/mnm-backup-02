Walter L. Wakefield and Austin P. Evans, *Heresies of the High Middle Ages* (Columbia University Press, 1969). Translated primary sources on the heretical movements of medieval Europe — Cathars, Waldensians, and their contemporaries — drawn from polemics, inquisitorial records, and the heretics' own writings, with extensive annotation; the standard documentary collection.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Medieval Heresy sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
