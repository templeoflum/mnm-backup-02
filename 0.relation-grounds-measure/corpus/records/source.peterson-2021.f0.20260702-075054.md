Joseph H. Peterson, *Elucidation of Necromancy: Lucidarium Artis Nigromantice attributed to Peter of Abano* (Ibis Press, 2021). Text, translation, and commentary on the medieval necromantic manual attributed to Peter of Abano, including a new translation of his *Heptameron, or Elements of Magic* — a primary window into the practical necromancy behind the later grimoires.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed]**
