James Ladyman & Don Ross, with David Spurrett and John Collier, *Every Thing Must Go: Metaphysics Naturalized* (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2007). ISBN 9780199276196.

Contains: the most developed statement of ontic structural realism (OSR). Argues metaphysics must be built on current fundamental physics, not a priori intuition — abandoning the picture of the world as self-subsistent individual objects and collision-causation. OSR for fundamental physics is combined with "rainforest realism" (an ontology of *real patterns*, after Dennett) for the special sciences, unifying without reducing. Opens with the polemical claim that much contemporary analytic metaphysics "contributes nothing to human knowledge."

Access: Oxford Academic (academic.oup.com/book/8595); reviewed in Notre Dame Philosophical Reviews; PhilPapers record LADETM.
