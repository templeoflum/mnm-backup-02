**statement (plain)** — **Ereshkigal** is the queen and goddess of the underworld, ruler of the land of the dead. In the **Descent of Inanna/Ishtar** her sister descends through the seven gates to her realm; Ereshkigal has her struck dead and her corpse hung on a hook, and only under pressure from the gods releases her — on the condition that a substitute take her place. In the Akkadian tale **Nergal and Ereshkigal** the god Nergal descends to her, and after their union he becomes her husband and shares the rule of the underworld.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the underworld, the land of the dead, the realm of the dead's queenship. Culture: Sumerian and Akkadian **Ereshkigal**, "Queen of the Great Earth"; her realm is called **Kur** or **Irkalla**. Sister of **Inanna/Ishtar**, whom she kills on her descent and later releases; wife of **Nergal** in the Akkadian narrative. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Mesopotamian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Treated in the standard reference by Black & Green [[source.black-green-1992]]; the primary narrative sources are the **Descent of Inanna/Ishtar** and the Akkadian tale **Nergal and Ereshkigal**. Her queenship of the underworld and kinship with Inanna/Ishtar are established; the descent and marriage narratives are attested tradition, variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
