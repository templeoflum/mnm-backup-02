Rosenthal, R., Hall, J. A., DiMatteo, M. R., Rogers, P. L., & Archer, D., *Sensitivity to Nonverbal Communication: The PONS Test.* Baltimore, MD: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1979 (xxiv, 408 pp.; ISBN 0801821592).

Introduces the Profile of Nonverbal Sensitivity (PONS), a 220-item multi-channel film test of accuracy in decoding affective nonverbal cues (face, body, voice tone, and content-masked speech). Established that nonverbal sensitivity is a measurable individual-difference variable and mapped its correlates. Developed as a research instrument for multi-participant studies, not individual diagnosis.
