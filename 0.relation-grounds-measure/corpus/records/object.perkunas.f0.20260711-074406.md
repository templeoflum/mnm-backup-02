**statement (plain)** — **Perkūnas** (Latvian **Pērkons**) is the thunder-god of Baltic (Lithuanian/Latvian) tradition, the most important Baltic sky-god. He wields an axe or hammer and rides across the heavens in a chariot, striking down demons, the unjust, and his chthonic adversary **Velnias**. His tree is the oak, and he is a god of storms and of justice.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: thunder, lightning, justice, the oak, and war. He is figured with axe or hammer, riding a chariot; his lightning pursues Velnias across the sky. Cognate with Slavic **Perun** and akin to the wider Indo-European thunderer. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Perkūnas is documented in the cited sources ([[source.gimbutas-1963]], [[source.greimas-1992]]); this is documented Baltic tradition (`status: doctrinal`), recovered from oral tradition and folk-song by scholars and folklorists. The Indo-European cognacy with Perun is well attested; the fuller reconstruction of his mythic role rests in part on comparative and structural reconstruction. **[confirmed as attribution]**
