Kurt Rudolph, *Gnosis: The Nature and History of Gnosticism* (T&T Clark / Harper & Row, 1983; German original *Die Gnosis*, 1977). The classic single-volume scholarly synthesis of ancient Gnosticism — sources, mythology, cult and community, history from origins through Manichaeism and the Mandaeans. Foundational as the standard comprehensive treatment written in the wake of the Nag Hammadi finds, and the reference point later category-critiques (Williams) push against.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Gnosticism section. Pre-dates the full category debate; treats "Gnosticism" as a coherent religion. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
