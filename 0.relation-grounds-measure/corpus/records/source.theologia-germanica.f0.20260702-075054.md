Anonymous, *Theologia Germanica* (14th c.). Short German treatise on self-abandonment and deification in the Tauler line, by an unknown Frankfurt priest; Martin Luther edited and published it (partially 1516, fully 1518), declaring he had learned more from it than from any book save the Bible and Augustine. The ESOTERICA list cites the CWS volume *The Theologia Germanica of Martin Luther* (trans. Bengt Hoffman, Paulist Press, 1980).

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Susanna Winkworth's 19th-century translation is public domain and freely available online; the CWS edition is an in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
