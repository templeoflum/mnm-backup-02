Thomas Nagel, "What Is It Like to Be a Bat?" *The Philosophical Review* 83, no. 4 (1974): 435–450 — argues that conscious experience has an essentially subjective character (a point of view) that objective, third-person science structurally cannot capture. Seeded Levine's explanatory gap and Chalmers's hard problem. **[confirmed as attribution]**
