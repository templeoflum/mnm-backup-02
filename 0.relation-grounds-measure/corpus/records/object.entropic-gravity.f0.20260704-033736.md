**statement (plain)** — a polymer coils because entropy pulls it; Verlinde proposes gravity is that kind of force. Matter positions register as information on holographic screens (one bit per Planck area); moving a mass changes the entropy, and the resulting statistical tendency *is* gravity — Newton's law derived, Einstein's equations relativistically.

**basis** — Jacobson (1995) had already derived the Einstein equation as a thermodynamic equation of state from δQ = TdS on local horizons; Verlinde (2010) recast gravity itself as entropic.
