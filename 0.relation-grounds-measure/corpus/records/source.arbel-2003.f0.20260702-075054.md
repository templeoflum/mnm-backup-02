Vita Daphna Arbel, *Beholders of Divine Secrets: Mysticism and Myth in the Hekhalot and Merkavah Literature* (SUNY Press, 2003). Reads the Hekhalot and Merkavah corpus — the early Jewish accounts of ascent through the heavenly palaces to the divine throne — through its mythic patterns and imagery rather than solely its ritual or textual history.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Apocalypticism, Merkavah and Dead Sea Scrolls. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
