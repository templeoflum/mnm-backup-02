Christopher McIntosh, *The Rose Cross and the Age of Reason: Eighteenth-Century Rosicrucianism in Central Europe and its Relationship to the Enlightenment* (Brill, 1992). The first in-depth study of the German Rosicrucian revival and the Gold- und Rosenkreuz order — esoteric orders as a counter-current inside the Age of Reason rather than its opposite, reaching into Prussian court politics.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Rosicrucianism. First published Brill 1992 (Brill's Studies in Intellectual History); SUNY Press reissue 2011. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed]**
