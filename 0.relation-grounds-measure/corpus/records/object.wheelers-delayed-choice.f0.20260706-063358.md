**statement (plain)** — Modify the double slit so the choice — detect which slit (particle) or observe interference (wave) — is made *after* the photon has already passed the apparatus. The photon's behaviour always matches the measurement finally chosen, as though it retroactively 'decides' whether it was wave or particle.

**statement (framework)** — deepens wave–particle duality with a temporal twist suggesting **retrocausality** — but Wheeler's reading is stronger: the photon has *no definite past trajectory* until measurement brings a history into being ("no phenomenon is a phenomenon until observed"). Realized experimentally (Jacques et al. 2007). Established; interpretation **open**.
