R. I. Moore, *The War On Heresy* (Belknap/Harvard, 2012). The leading revisionist account: argues that the "heresies" of medieval Europe were largely constructed by the persecuting apparatus of church and state rather than pre-existing organized movements — the counterweight to the traditional Cathar narrative.
