Peter Schäfer, *Two Gods in Heaven: Jewish Concepts of God in Antiquity* (Princeton University Press, 2020). Traces the persistence of a second divine figure — Son of Man, Metatron, personified Wisdom and Logos — within ancient Judaism itself, arguing binitarian ideas were native to Jewish thought rather than Christian imports; a successor conversation to Segal's *Two Powers in Heaven*.
