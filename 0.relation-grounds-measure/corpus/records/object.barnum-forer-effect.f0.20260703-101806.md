**statement (plain)** — Given a personality description made of statements that are vague and true of almost anyone ("You have a great need for others to like you… at times you are extroverted, at other times reserved"), people rate it as a strikingly accurate portrait of *themselves* — the more so when told it was made just for them and delivered by an authority. Forer's students rated an identical astrology-sourced sketch 4.26/5 for personal accuracy.

**relevance** — the demystifying half of "psychic" reading: much apparent accuracy is the sitter validating generic statements, amplified by confirmation bias (vivid hits remembered, misses forgotten) and cold-reading technique (shotgunning, the rainbow ruse). Sits beside the *genuine* cue-reading half ([[object.clever-hans-effect]]) — both operate at once in a real reading [[source.forer-1949]] **[confirmed]**.
