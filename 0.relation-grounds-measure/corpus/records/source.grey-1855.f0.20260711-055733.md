Sir George Grey, *Polynesian Mythology and Ancient Traditional History of the New Zealand Race* (1855), is the classic collection of Māori myth and tradition gathered from Māori informants during Grey's governorship. It is the standard early source for the Rangi–Papa creation cycle, the separation of sky and earth by Tāne, the deeds of Māui, and the wider Māori pantheon, preserving oral tradition in both Māori and English. Later scholarship builds on and corrects its renderings, but it remains a foundational reference. **[confirmed as attribution]**
