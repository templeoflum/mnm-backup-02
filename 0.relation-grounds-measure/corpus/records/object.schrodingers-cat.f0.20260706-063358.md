**statement (plain)** — A cat is sealed in a box with a radioactive atom, a Geiger counter, and a poison vial. If the atom decays (a random quantum event) the cat is killed. Apply QM consistently and, until the box is opened, the atom — and so the cat — is in a superposition of decayed/not, alive/dead.

**statement (framework)** — Schrödinger's *reductio* of naive superposition scaled to the macroscopic; crystallizes the **measurement problem** — where, how, and why superposition gives way to definite outcomes. Interpretations diverge (collapse, decoherence, many-worlds, Bohm). Established; the measurement problem stays **open**.
