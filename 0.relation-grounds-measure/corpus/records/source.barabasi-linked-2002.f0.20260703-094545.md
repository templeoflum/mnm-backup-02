Albert-László Barabási, *Linked: The New Science of Networks* (Perseus Publishing, 2002).

Contains — the general-audience account of scale-free networks: hubs, the 80/20 concentration of links, preferential attachment ("the rich get richer"), robustness against random failure yet fragility to targeted attack on hubs, and the claim that a common network science spans the web, biology, epidemics, and society. The accessible on-ramp to the 1999 *Science* result.

Access — ISBN 0-7382-0667-9; in print (later editions as *Linked: How Everything Is Connected to Everything Else*). **[confirmed]**
