**statement (plain)** — The Ames Window, also known as the Ames Trapezoid, is a rotating trapezoidal frame that creates the compelling illusion of oscillating back and forth rather than continuously rotating through 360 degrees. Developed by Adelbert Ames Jr. in 1951, this demonstration features a window-like shape with one vertical edge longer than the other, creating a trapezoid that resembles a rectangular window viewed in perspective.
