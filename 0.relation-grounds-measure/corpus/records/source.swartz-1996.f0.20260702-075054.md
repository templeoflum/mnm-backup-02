Michael D. Swartz, *Scholastic Magic: Ritual and Revelation in Early Jewish Mysticism* (Princeton University Press, 1996). Study of the Sar-Torah ("Prince of the Torah") traditions in the Hekhalot literature — rituals for adjuring an angel to grant instant mastery of Torah — situating early Jewish mysticism in the scribal and scholastic culture that produced it.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Apocalypticism, Merkavah and Dead Sea Scrolls. The list spells the author "Schwartz"; the correct spelling is Swartz. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
