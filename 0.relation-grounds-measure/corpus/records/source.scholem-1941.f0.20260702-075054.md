Gershom Scholem, *Major Trends in Jewish Mysticism* (Schocken, 1941). The founding work of the academic study of Jewish mysticism — nine lectures spanning Merkavah mysticism, Hasidism of medieval Germany, Abulafia, the Zohar, Lurianic (Safedian) Kabbalah, Sabbatianism, and Polish Hasidism; the historical backbone that his later encyclopedic *Kabbalah* [[source.scholem-kabbalah-1974]] condenses.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Safedian Kabbalah sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
