Toshiyuki Nakagaki, Hiroyasu Yamada, and Ágota Tóth, "Maze-solving by an amoeboid organism," *Nature* 407, no. 6803 (2000): 470.

Contains — the classic demonstration that the plasmodium of *Physarum polycephalum*, spread through a maze with food at two exits, reorganizes its body into the single shortest tube connecting the food sources — reinforcing the productive route and withdrawing from dead ends. An early anchor for the reinforce-by-use-plus-decay dynamic in a non-neural organism, and the precursor to the later externalized-memory work [[source.reid-2012]].
