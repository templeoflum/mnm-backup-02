The *Popol Vuh* is the K'iche' Maya book of creation and heroic myth, surviving in a 16th-century alphabetic manuscript later transcribed by the Dominican friar **Francisco Ximénez** (c. 1701). It narrates the creation of the world and of humankind from maize, and the deeds of the **Hero Twins** Hunahpú and Xbalanqué against the lords of Xibalba, and is the primary source for Maya creation myth. **[confirmed as attribution]**
