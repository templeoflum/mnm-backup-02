**statement (plain)** — Rotate y=1/x (x≥1) about the x-axis. The resulting infinite trumpet has volume exactly π (finite) but infinite surface area. Intuitively it can be filled with a finite amount of paint, yet its inner surface can never be coated — the 'painter's paradox.'

**statement (framework)** — a **proven** result (finite volume, infinite area) that startled seventeenth-century mathematicians and sharpened the distinction between the actual and potential infinite; the paint paradox dissolves once one notes physical paint has thickness while the mathematical surface does not. Proven (the paradox is intuitive, not mathematical).
