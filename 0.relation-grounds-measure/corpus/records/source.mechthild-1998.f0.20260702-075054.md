Frank Tobin (trans.), *Mechthild of Magdeburg: The Flowing Light of the Godhead* (Paulist Press, 1998). Complete translation of the thirteenth-century beguine's seven-book work mixing vision, dialogue, courtly love-lyric, and prophecy — the first major mystical text composed in German.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
