J. H. Chajes, *The Kabbalistic Tree* (Penn State University Press, 2022). A richly illustrated history of the ilan — the kabbalistic tree diagram — tracing how kabbalists mapped the sefirot and divine structure onto visual scrolls and charts from the medieval period through Safed and beyond; the study of Kabbalah's diagrammatic knowledge.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Safedian Kabbalah. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
