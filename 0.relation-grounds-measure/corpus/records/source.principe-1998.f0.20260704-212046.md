L. M. Principe, *The Aspiring Adept: Robert Boyle and His Alchemical Quest* (Princeton, 1998). **[confirmed]**

Role — documents Boyle's belief in the reality of the Stone/Elixir, his role in the 1689 repeal of the 1404 "Act against Multipliers" (which banned the "multiplication" of metals), and his inverse "Anti-Elixir" tract — multiplication as a live, concrete category, not a metaphysical loop.
