D. L. Schacter, "The seven sins of memory: Insights from psychology and cognitive neuroscience," *American Psychologist* 54(3):182–203 (1999); *The Seven Sins of Memory* (Houghton Mifflin, 2001).

Role — classifies memory failures into seven "sins": transience, absent-mindedness, blocking (omission); misattribution, suggestibility, bias (commission/distortion); and persistence (intrusive memory) — argued to be byproducts of otherwise adaptive features, not design flaws. **[confirmed]**
