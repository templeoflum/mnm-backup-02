**origin** — the anonymous oral poems collected in the **Poetic Edda** (compiled 13th c. from older tradition, chief manuscript the Codex Regius, c. 1270) — the primary attestation of the myths: *Völuspá*, *Hávamál*, *Grímnismál*, and the rest. [[source.poetic-edda]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Snorri Sturluson, the **Prose Edda** (Iceland, c. 1220) — the systematic handbook of the mythology written for skaldic poets, which gives us the connected narrative (the *Gylfaginning*) most later retellings depend on. [[source.prose-edda]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — John Lindow, *Norse Mythology: A Guide to the Gods, Heroes, Rituals, and Beliefs* (ABC-CLIO, 2001; Oxford pbk., 2002) — the standard English reference handbook. [[source.lindow-2001]]

*grade s — origin (Poetic Edda), codification (Prose Edda), door (Lindow) present. A single-tradition field under [[field.comparative-mythology]] (the cross-tradition study above it).
