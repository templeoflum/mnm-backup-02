**statement (plain)** — **Izanami** is the primordial female creator kami and consort of **Izanagi**. With him she bore the islands of Japan and many kami, but died bearing the fire-kami **Kagutsuchi**. When Izanagi followed her into **Yomi** (the underworld) and saw her corrupted body, she was enraged at his flight and vowed to take a thousand lives a day — and so death entered the world.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: creation, death, the underworld, the female principle. She is the mother-goddess of the creation cycle who becomes queen of the dead. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Izanami is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant accounts of her death and the descent to Yomi; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
