**statement (plain)** — The Profile or Portrait Illusion is an ambiguous figure that alternates between appearing as a face viewed from the side (profile view) and a face viewed from the front (portrait or frontal view). This bistable stimulus demonstrates how identical contour information can be organized by the visual system into fundamentally different three-dimensional facial interpretations. The perceptual switch involves a reorganization of which features correspond to which facial landmarks, with lines and curves taking on different anatomical significance depending on the dominant interpretation.

**statement (framework)** — category: ambiguous bistable multistable; perceptual mechanism(s): bistable-competition, figure-ground-segregation, face-processing, top-down-expectation.
