Frank Jackson, "Epiphenomenal Qualia," *The Philosophical Quarterly* 32, no. 127 (1982): 127–136 — the Knowledge Argument: a complete physical knower (Mary) still learns something on first experiencing colour, so not all facts are physical facts. Refined in "What Mary Didn't Know" (*Journal of Philosophy*, 1986). Jackson later recanted and adopted physicalism. **[confirmed as attribution]**
