Joseph Dan (ed.) and Ronald C. Kiener (trans.), *The Early Kabbalah* (Paulist Press, 1986). Translated texts from the earliest kabbalistic circles — Provence and Gerona, the *Iyyun* circle, and the schools preceding the Zohar — with Dan's historical introduction and a preface by Moshe Idel. The list credits "Idel & Dan"; the editorship is Dan's, with Idel contributing the preface.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Early Kabbalah sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
