Bonabeau, E., Dorigo, M., & Theraulaz, G. (1999). *Swarm Intelligence: From Natural to Artificial Systems*. New York: Oxford University Press (Santa Fe Institute Studies in the Sciences of Complexity). ISBN 0-19-513158-4 (hardcover) / 0-19-513159-2 (paperback).

Contains: the foundational textbook of swarm intelligence. It systematizes how mechanisms observed in social insects — recruitment, division of labor, and especially stigmergy — can be transferred to the design of artificial distributed systems and optimization algorithms. Treats ant-colony optimization, ant-based clustering, and collective robotics as engineering instances of stigmergic coordination. Serves as the "door" reference connecting the biological concept to its algorithmic descendants. [confirmed]
