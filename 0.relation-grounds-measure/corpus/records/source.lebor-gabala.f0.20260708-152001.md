The **Lebor Gabála Érenn** ("Book of the Taking of Ireland," compiled 11th century from older material). A medieval Irish prose-and-verse pseudo-history narrating the successive settlings (or "takings") of Ireland by a sequence of peoples — among them the **Tuatha Dé Danann**, "the peoples of the goddess Danu," the divine race, and their displacement by the **Milesians** — framing the Irish gods within a quasi-biblical chronology of invasions. The framing source for the Irish mythological cycle and its pantheon; preserved in several recensions across later manuscripts. **[confirmed as attribution]**
