**statement (plain)** — Omelas is a city of boundless happiness — sustained, everyone knows, by one child kept in permanent misery in a locked room. Free the child and the utopia collapses. Most accept the bargain; a few walk away into the dark. They do not free the child; they merely refuse complicity.

**statement (framework)** — the sharpest **narrative** challenge to aggregative/utilitarian ethics: whether any sum of happiness can justify knowingly torturing one innocent — and whether refusing complicity without acting is morally adequate. Applied to systemic injustice, global inequality, and extractive prosperity. Established; **open**.
