Abraham S. Luchins, "Mechanization in Problem Solving: The Effect of Einstellung," *Psychological Monographs* 54, no. 6 (1942): i–95.

Contains — the classic **water-jar experiments** establishing the **Einstellung (set) effect**: after a series of problems solvable by one method, subjects keep applying that method even when a simpler solution is plainly available — and some fail a problem the learned method cannot solve at all. The founding demonstration that a habitual, once-successful approach mechanizes thought and blocks better alternatives.
