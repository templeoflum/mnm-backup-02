L. M. Principe & W. R. Newman, "Some Problems with the Historiography of Alchemy," in *Secrets of Nature*, ed. W. R. Newman & A. Grafton (MIT Press, 2001), 385–431. **[confirmed]**

Role — the oft-cited statement of the New Historiography: the purely spiritual/psychological interpretation (Atwood, Hitchcock, Waite, Jung) is "simply unsustainable in light of the historical record" — while conceding alchemy and spirituality interpenetrated, as they did in other early-modern sciences. Their critics (the *Annals of Science* 2008 review line) charge the thesis with making alchemy too akin to modern science — the dissent carried on the field record's lineage.
