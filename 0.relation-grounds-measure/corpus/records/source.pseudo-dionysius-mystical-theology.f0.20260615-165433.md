Pseudo-Dionysius the Areopagite, *The Mystical Theology* (*De Mystica Theologia*), c. 485–528 CE — theology becomes "mystical"; the *via negativa* toward the God beyond being and beyond assertion. Draws on Proclus (d. 485); first cited by Severus of Antioch (518–528). Public domain: Rolt (SPCK, 1920) on Internet Archive; Parker on CCEL. Scholarly: Luibhéid & Rorem, *Pseudo-Dionysius: The Complete Works* (Paulist, 1987).
