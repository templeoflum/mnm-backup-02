Contains: a review arguing that creative thought arises from increased cooperation between the **default mode network** (self-generated, associative thought — candidate generation) and the **executive control network** (goal-directed evaluation and constraint). Synthesizes resting-state and task fMRI across divergent thinking and domain-specific creative production (poetry, musical improvisation, visual art).
