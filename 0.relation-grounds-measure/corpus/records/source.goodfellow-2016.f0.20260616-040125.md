Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville, *Deep Learning*, MIT Press, 2016 (Adaptive Computation and Machine Learning series). ISBN 9780262035613.

Comprehensive textbook on deep learning. Chapter 15, "Representation Learning," is the standard door to the field — covers greedy layer-wise unsupervised pretraining, transfer learning and domain adaptation, distributed representations, and what makes a representation good (disentangled factors of variation, the manifold view). Free HTML at deeplearningbook.org.
