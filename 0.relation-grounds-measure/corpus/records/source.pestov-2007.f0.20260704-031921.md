V. Pestov, "An axiomatic approach to intrinsic dimension of a dataset," arXiv:0712.2063 (IJCNN'07 / *Neural Networks* 2008).

Role — axiomatizes what an intrinsic-dimension function should satisfy, tying dimension to concentration-of-measure (the curse of dimensionality) and making ID measure-relative rather than a single manifold invariant — conceptual support for dimension as relational, not assumed. **[confirmed]**
