**statement (plain)** — Perceptual Rivalry refers to the general phenomenon in which incompatible perceptual interpretations compete for conscious awareness, resulting in alternating dominance of mutually exclusive percepts despite constant sensory input. This umbrella category encompasses multiple specific forms of rivalry, including binocular rivalry (different images presented to each eye), ambiguous figures (single images admitting multiple interpretations), and motion rivalry (conflicting motion signals). The hallmark of perceptual rivalry is spontaneous, stochastic alternation between competing percepts, with perception fluctuating even when the physical stimulus remains unchanged.
