**statement (plain)** — Carver & Scheier model goal-directed behavior as a negative-feedback control loop applied to oneself: you hold a reference value (a goal), sense your current state, a comparator registers the discrepancy, and you act to shrink it — the self as its own steersman. Approach goals run discrepancy-reducing loops; avoidance goals run discrepancy-enlarging loops. Goals nest hierarchically (be a certain kind of person → do certain things → make certain movements), and how *fast* the discrepancy is closing feeds a second loop that produces affect.

**basis** — a direct port of cybernetic feedback control ([[object.feedback-loop]]) and Ashby's regulation to human psychology [[source.carver-scheier-1998]] **[confirmed]**. The empirically softer end of the channeling analogy — a model, not a theorem.
