**statement (plain)** — Myers proposed that ordinary waking awareness is only part of the mind: below the *limen* (threshold) lies a broader "subliminal self," a reservoir of consciousness from which both breakdown (automatisms, dissociation) and higher faculty (inspiration, genius, "supernormal" experience) emerge. Mediumistic trance he read as "automatisms" on a spectrum from minor motor movements to full displacement of the primary consciousness — no external spirit required to explain the productivity. James's "transmarginal" or subliminal field is the same idea, the bridge to mainstream psychology.

**relevance** — the architecture behind the "subconscious channel" and the origin of the **threshold/liminality** invariant of inward channeling. The productive-subconscious reading (subliminal generativity rather than spirit) is the through-line to Flournoy's cryptomnesia ([[object.cryptomnesia]]) and Jung's active imagination ([[object.active-imagination]]) [[source.myers-1903]] **[confirmed as attribution]**.
