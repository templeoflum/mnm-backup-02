**statement (plain)** — In Hawaiian tradition **Nāmaka** (**Nā-maka-o-Kaha'i**) is the sea-goddess and elder sister of **Pele**, locked with her in eternal conflict — water against fire. She pursued Pele across the Pacific from **Kahiki**, drowning each island home Pele tried to make, until Pele at last found refuge in the fire-pit of **Kīlauea**, beyond the reach of the sea.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: sea, waves, sea creatures, rivalry. Elder sister and rival of **Pele**; of the Pele clan out of **Kahiki**. Aliases: **Nā-maka-o-Kaha'i** (Hawaiian). Hawaiian tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in Beckwith, *Hawaiian Mythology* (1940) [[source.beckwith-1940]]; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). These myths come through Hawaiian oral tradition collected and recorded in the 19th–20th c.; in some traditions the course of the pursuit and the islands drowned vary across the islands and among tellers. **[confirmed as attribution]**
