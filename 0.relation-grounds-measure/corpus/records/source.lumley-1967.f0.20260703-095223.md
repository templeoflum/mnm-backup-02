John L. Lumley, "The structure of inhomogeneous turbulent flows," in A. M. Yaglom & V. I. Tatarski, eds., *Atmospheric Turbulence and Radio Wave Propagation* (Nauka, Moscow, 1967), pp. 166–178.

Contains — the introduction of **Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD)** to turbulence: an objective, statistics-based method that extracts the most energetic coherent structures of a flow as the eigenfunctions of the two-point velocity correlation. The mathematical means of *defining* coherent structure without appeal to visual judgment; foundational to reduced-order modeling of turbulence.

Access — a Russian-language proceedings chapter, hard to obtain in original form; standardly cited via later POD treatments (Holmes, Lumley & Berkooz, *Turbulence, Coherent Structures, Dynamical Systems and Symmetry*, CUP 1996). **[candidate — exact pagination per the reading-list source; original not opened]**
