Ludimar Hermann, "Eine Erscheinung simultanen Contrastes," *Pflügers Archiv für die gesamte Physiologie* 3 (1870): 13–15 — the ghostly grey blobs seen at the crossings of a white grid on black (**Hermann grid**), long explained by retinal lateral inhibition (the classic account now contested). Brewster noted the effect earlier (1844); Hermann carries the canonical attribution. **[confirmed]** (venue + pages web-verified.)
