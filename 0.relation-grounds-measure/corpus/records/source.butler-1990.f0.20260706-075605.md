Judith Butler, *Gender Trouble: Feminism and the Subversion of Identity* (Routledge, 1990) — argues gender is performative: constituted by the repeated citation of gendered norms rather than expressing a prior, stable identity ("no doer behind the deed"). **[confirmed as attribution]**
