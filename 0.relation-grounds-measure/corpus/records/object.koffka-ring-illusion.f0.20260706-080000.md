**statement (plain)** — The Koffka Ring Illusion consists of a uniform gray ring that spans two regions of different luminance, typically a dark and light background. Despite the ring's physical uniformity, observers perceive a striking discontinuity in its lightness at the boundary between the two backgrounds. The portion of the ring on the dark background appears lighter than the portion on the light background, even though both segments have identical luminance.

**statement (framework)** — category: brightness lightness contrast; perceptual mechanism(s): lightness-computation, figure-ground-segregation, lateral-inhibition.
