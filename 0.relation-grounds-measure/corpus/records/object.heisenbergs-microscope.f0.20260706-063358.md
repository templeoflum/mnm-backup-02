**statement (plain)** — To locate an electron, bounce a photon off it. A short-wavelength photon pins the position but kicks the momentum unpredictably; a long-wavelength one spares the momentum but blurs the position. No arrangement escapes the trade-off: the position–momentum uncertainty product is at least of order ℏ.

**statement (framework)** — Heisenberg's pedagogical device for the **uncertainty principle**, first framed as measurement *disturbance* (as if the electron had definite values). Bohr corrected it: uncertainty is **inherent** — the electron has no simultaneously definite position and momentum. The disturbance-vs-indeterminacy reading still shapes foundations debates. Established; **open**.
