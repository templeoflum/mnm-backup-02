**statement (plain)** — The blotted letters illusion demonstrates the remarkable capacity of the visual system to maintain letter and word recognition despite substantial physical degradation or occlusion of the visual input. When letters are partially obscured by strategically placed blots, smudges, or occlusion patches, observers can typically read the text with surprising ease, perceiving complete letter forms through perceptual completion processes. This phenomenon reveals the interaction between bottom-up visual processing and top-down linguistic and contextual knowledge in reading.
