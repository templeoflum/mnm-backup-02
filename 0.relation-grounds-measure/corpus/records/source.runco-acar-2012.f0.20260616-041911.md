Verbatim citation: Runco, M. A., & Acar, S. (2012). Divergent thinking as an indicator of creative potential. *Creativity Research Journal*, 24(1), 66–75.

Contains: a review of divergent-thinking (DT) testing. Concludes DT tests are reliable and, relative to specified criteria, reasonably valid predictors of creative performance — but indicate *potential*, not guaranteed creative achievement; validity is a matter of degree and criterion. The methodological anchor for treating DT as the standard operationalization of creative capacity.
