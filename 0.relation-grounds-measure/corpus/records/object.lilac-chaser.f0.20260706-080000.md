**statement (plain)** — The Lilac Chaser, created by Jeremy Hinton in 2005, is a striking demonstration that combines several perceptual phenomena including motion perception, afterimages, and perceptual filling-in. The illusion consists of twelve lilac or magenta-colored discs arranged in a circle around a central fixation point. When observers fixate steadily on the central point, the discs sequentially disappear one at a time in a clockwise or counterclockwise pattern, creating a gap that rotates around the circle.
