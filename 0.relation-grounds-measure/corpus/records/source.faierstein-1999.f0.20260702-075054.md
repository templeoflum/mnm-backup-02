Morris M. Faierstein (trans.), *Jewish Mystical Autobiographies: Book of Visions and Book of Secrets* (Paulist Press, 1999). Annotated translations of two first-person mystical diaries — Ḥayyim Vital's *Sefer ha-Ḥezyonot* (Book of Visions), from Luria's chief disciple in Safed, and Isaac Judah Yehiel Safrin of Komarno's *Megillat Setarim* (Book of Secrets) — rare inside views of kabbalistic experience.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Safedian Kabbalah. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
