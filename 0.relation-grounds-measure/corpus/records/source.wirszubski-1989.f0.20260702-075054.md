Chaim Wirszubski, *Pico della Mirandola's Encounter with Jewish Mysticism* (Harvard University Press, 1989). Posthumous study tracing exactly which kabbalistic texts Pico read — largely through Flavius Mithridates' Latin translations — and how he bent them into a Christian kabbalah; the philological foundation for all later work on Pico's kabbalistic theses.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Renaissance Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
