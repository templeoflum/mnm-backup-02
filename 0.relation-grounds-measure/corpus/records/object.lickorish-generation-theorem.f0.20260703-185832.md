**statement (plain)** — the whole (infinite, complicated) mapping class group of a surface can be produced by composing a small fixed handful of Dehn twists. Lickorish showed 3g−1 twists are enough; Humphries pared this to 2g+1 and proved you cannot do it with fewer twists.

**statement (formal)** — Mod(Σ_g) = ⟨T_{c_1}, …, T_{c_k}⟩ for explicit simple closed curves c_i, with k = 3g−1 (Lickorish 1964) and, minimally among twist generators, k = 2g+1 (Humphries 1979, for g > 1). **[confirmed]**

**proof** — Lickorish's constructive generating set via the twist subgroup; Humphries' minimality by a homological/abelianization obstruction. Standard reference: Farb–Margalit ch. 4.
