**statement (plain)** — Lightning kills Davidson in a swamp and by coincidence reassembles nearby molecules into his exact duplicate. Swampman walks out, greets his friends, teaches his classes — physically and behaviourally identical. But he has no causal history: his "memories" formed from nothing, his "words" were never learned from the world.

**statement (framework)** — Davidson's device for **content externalism**: because meaning and belief are partly constituted by causal-historical ties to the world, Swampman's utterance of "water" refers to nothing and his "beliefs" have no content — only the appearance of content. A touchstone for teleosemantics, reference, and (now) whether uploads and LLMs, lacking the right history, mean anything. Established; the verdict stays **open**.
