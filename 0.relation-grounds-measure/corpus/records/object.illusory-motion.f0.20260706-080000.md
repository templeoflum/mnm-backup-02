**statement (plain)** — Illusory Motion refers to a broad class of visual phenomena in which observers perceive motion in stimuli that are objectively static or stationary. These illusions encompass diverse perceptual experiences ranging from subtle drift or shimmer to vivid rotation, expansion, or wave-like propagation, all arising from patterns that contain no physical movement. Illusory motion effects can be induced through various stimulus manipulations including specific arrangements of luminance gradients, color contrasts, spatial frequencies, and orientations.
