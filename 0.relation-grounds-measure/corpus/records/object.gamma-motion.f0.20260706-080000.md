**statement (plain)** — Gamma Motion refers to an illusory expansion or contraction perceived when a visual stimulus undergoes an abrupt change in its presence. Specifically, when a stimulus suddenly appears (onset), observers often report a brief perception of outward expansion or looming, as though the object is growing larger or moving toward the viewer. Conversely, when a stimulus suddenly disappears (offset), observers may perceive a transient inward contraction or receding motion.
