Colm Luibhéid (trans.), *Pseudo-Dionysius: The Complete Works* (Paulist Press, 1987). The standard one-volume English translation of the entire Dionysian corpus — *The Divine Names*, *The Mystical Theology*, *The Celestial Hierarchy*, *The Ecclesiastical Hierarchy*, and the letters — the fountainhead of the Western apophatic tradition.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
