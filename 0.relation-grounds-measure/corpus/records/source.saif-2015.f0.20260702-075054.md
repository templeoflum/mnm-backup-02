Liana Saif, *The Arabic Influences on Early Modern Occult Philosophy* (Palgrave Macmillan, 2015). Traces how Arabic theories of astral causation and occult properties — al-Kindi, Abu Ma'shar, the Picatrix tradition — entered Latin Europe and underwrote the occult philosophy of Ficino, Pico, and Agrippa.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
