Deborah E. Harkness, *John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature* (Cambridge, 1999). The standard scholarly treatment of Dee's angel conversations, reading them not as an aberration but as a coherent natural-philosophical project — cabala, alchemy, and apocalyptic expectation joined in a program to restore knowledge of nature before its end.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
