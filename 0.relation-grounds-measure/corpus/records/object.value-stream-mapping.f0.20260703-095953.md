**statement (plain)** — Value-stream mapping draws the whole sequence of steps — across departments and often across firms — that a product moves through from raw material to customer, and labels each step as adding value, adding no value but currently necessary, or pure waste. Seeing the entire stream exposes where work waits, backtracks, or piles up, so the waste can be removed and the remaining steps made to flow continuously and be pulled by demand.

**relevance** — the SHAPE tool of lean production: make the intended path (value creation) the path of least resistance by removing the seven wastes; the enabling analysis behind just-in-time flow and pull ([[object.just-in-time]], [[object.kanban]]) [[source.womack-jones-1996]] **[confirmed]**. One of the five lean principles (value, value stream, flow, pull, perfection).
