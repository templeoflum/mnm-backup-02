Hilary Putnam, "The Meaning of 'Meaning'" (1975) — argues via Twin Earth that the extension (and hence meaning) of natural-kind terms depends on the external world, not the speaker's mental state: **semantic externalism**, "meanings just ain't in the head." Developed alongside Kripke on rigid designation. **[confirmed as attribution]**
