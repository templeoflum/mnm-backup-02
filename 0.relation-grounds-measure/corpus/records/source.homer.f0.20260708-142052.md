Homer, the *Iliad* and the *Odyssey* (c. 8th c. BCE). The two great hexameter epics of archaic Greece: the *Iliad* on the wrath of Achilles in the tenth year of the Trojan War, the *Odyssey* on the homeward wanderings of Odysseus. They are the earliest and most influential portraits of the Olympian gods in action and of the heroic age, and the wellspring of the later mythological and literary tradition. The name "Homer" attaches to the poems by ancient tradition; their authorship and unity are debated (the Homeric Question). **[confirmed as attribution]**
