(pseudo-)Apollodorus, *Bibliotheca* (1st–2nd c. CE). A prose handbook that gathers Greek mythology into a connected, systematic narrative: the theogony, the descendants of the primordial powers, and the heroic genealogies down to the Trojan War and its aftermath. The most complete surviving ancient summary of the myths. Traditionally ascribed to Apollodorus of Athens (2nd c. BCE) but by a later, anonymous author, hence conventionally "pseudo-Apollodorus." **[confirmed as attribution]**
