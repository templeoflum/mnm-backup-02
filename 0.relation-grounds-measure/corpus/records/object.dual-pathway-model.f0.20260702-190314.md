**statement (plain)** — there is more than one way to be original. The **flexibility** route ranges widely — many categories, broad jumps, novel combinations. The **persistence** route drills deep — systematic, effortful probing within a few categories until something original emerges. Both reach originality; they draw on different moods, motivations, and cognitive styles. People often shift "from a flexible mode… to a systematic, persistent mode," exploring broadly first and then deepening.

**statement (framework)** — the leading process model of creative ideation. Complements the serial-order effect [[object.serial-order-effect]] and the explore/exploit framing [[object.explore-exploit-tradeoff]] (flexibility ≈ explore, persistence ≈ deep exploit).

**basis** — Nijstad et al. 2010 [[source.nijstad-2010]], synthesizing mood/creativity and ideation research. *Support* — well-cited; extended to personality and creativity. *Limits* — the two pathways are analytic idealizations; real ideation interleaves them. **[confirmed as attribution]**
