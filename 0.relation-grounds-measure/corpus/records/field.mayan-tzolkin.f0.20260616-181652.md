**origin** — the *Tzolk'in*, the 260-day ritual count of the Maya (and broader Mesoamerica): 20 day-signs × the numbers 1–13; attested in the codices and inscriptions, fundamental to Maya timekeeping and augury. [[source.tedlock-1982]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — the 20 day-signs (Yucatec names given here) and their auguries; the living tradition documented among the K'iche' daykeepers (*aj q'ij*) by Barbara Tedlock. [[source.tedlock-1982]]

**door** — Tedlock, *Time and the Highland Maya* (1982).

**lineage** — the **count and day-NAMES are ancient and attested**; elaborate day-sign "personalities" are partly ethnographic (living K'iche' practice) and partly modern overlay — carried as a note (themes kept brief). Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
