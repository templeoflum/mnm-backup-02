Brian P. Copenhaver, *Magic and the Dignity of Man: Pico della Mirandola and His Oration in Modern Memory* (Belknap Press of Harvard University Press, 2019). A revisionist study arguing that Pico's famous Oration is a mystical-Kabbalist ascent text rather than a manifesto of human dignity, and tracing how modern memory manufactured the humanist reading; foundational for Renaissance magic and its historiography.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Renaissance Magic sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
