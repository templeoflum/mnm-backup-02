**statement (plain)** — **Saulė** (Latvian **Saule**) is the sun-goddess of Baltic (Lithuanian/Latvian) tradition, the radiant deity who rides a chariot across the sky (or sits in an apple tree). She is a nurturing, maternal figure and the special protector of orphans and the unfortunate. Her solar mythology stands at the center of the Latvian folk-songs.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the sun, fertility, life, orphans, and health. Her mythic household — her daughters, and her contested marriage with the moon **Mēness/Mėnulis**, who is punished for infidelity — forms a major cycle of the Latvian **dainas**. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Saulė is documented in the cited sources ([[source.gimbutas-1963]], [[source.greimas-1992]], [[source.latvian-dainas]]); this is documented Baltic tradition (`status: doctrinal`), preserved above all in the Latvian folk-songs and recorded by scholars and folklorists. The reconstruction of the full solar cycle rests in part on that poetic, fragmentary corpus. **[confirmed as attribution]**
