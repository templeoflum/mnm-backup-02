**statement (plain)** — The Drifting Grating Illusion encompasses a class of systematic misperceptions that occur when viewing moving periodic patterns, particularly sinusoidal or square-wave gratings in motion. These illusions manifest as errors in the perceived direction, speed, or quality of motion compared to the physical properties of the drifting stimulus. Observers may experience biases in drift direction, particularly when gratings are viewed through apertures or combined with other visual features, or may perceive non-uniform motion speeds across different regions of the visual field despite uniform physical drift rates.
