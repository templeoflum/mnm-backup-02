M. E. Chevreul, *De la loi du contraste simultané des couleurs, et de l'assortiment des objets colorés* (Paris: Pitois-Levrault, 1839) — the chemist's law of **simultaneous contrast**: a colour shifts the appearance of an adjacent colour (and its greyscale form, the Chevreul illusion — a staircase of flat bands appears scalloped). Shaped the Impressionists. **[confirmed]** (work/year web-verified.)
