**statement (plain)** — Design society's basic principles from the 'original position', behind a veil that hides your race, sex, class, talents, and even your conception of the good. Not knowing where you'll land, Rawls argues, rational agents choose equal basic liberties and permit inequalities only if they benefit the least-advantaged (the difference principle).

**statement (framework)** — converts justice from altruism into constrained self-interest: what would a rational agent choose who cannot rig the game? The founding device of modern **distributive justice**, using a maximin rule; challenged by libertarians (Nozick), communitarians, utilitarians (Harsanyi), and feminists (Okin). Established; contested (**open**).
