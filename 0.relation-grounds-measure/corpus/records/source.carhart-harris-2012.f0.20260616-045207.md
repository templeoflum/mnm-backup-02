Nutt, "Neural correlates of the psychedelic state as determined by fMRI studies with psilocybin," *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, vol. 109, no. 6, pp. 2138–2143, 2012.

Contains: arterial-spin-labeling and BOLD fMRI before/after intravenous psilocybin in healthy volunteers. Found decreases (no increases) in cerebral blood flow and BOLD signal, concentrated in hub regions (medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate cortex), with reduced resting-state functional connectivity within the default mode network; the magnitude of these decreases correlated with subjective intensity of the psychedelic experience. A key empirical anchor for DMN involvement in drug-induced altered states.
