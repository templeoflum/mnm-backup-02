Robert Sutherland Rattray's *Akan-Ashanti Folk-Tales* (Clarendon Press, 1930) is the classic ethnographic collection of the Anansi (Anansesem) stories of the Akan of Ghana, recorded in Twi with facing English translation. Gathered in the field, it preserves the tales of the spider-trickster Anansi and the sky-god Nyame as told, and remains a foundational record of Akan oral tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**
