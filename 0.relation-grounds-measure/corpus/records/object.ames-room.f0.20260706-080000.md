**statement (plain)** — The Ames Room is a distorted spatial environment designed to create a powerful size illusion when viewed monocularly from a specific vantage point. Constructed by American ophthalmologist Adelbert Ames Jr. in 1946, the room features a trapezoidal floor plan and irregularly shaped walls that appear rectangular when viewed through a peephole positioned at the critical viewing location.
