E. R. Dodds, *The Greeks and the Irrational* (University of California Press, 1951). A landmark study of the non-rational side of Greek culture — shame-culture and guilt-culture, dreams, madness, ecstasy, shamanistic figures, and the reaction against Greek rationalism. Foundational for the study of Dionysiac ecstasy, Orphism, and the religious psychology underlying the mystery cults.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Greek Mysteries. Some framings (e.g. Greek "shamanism") are dated and contested in later scholarship. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
