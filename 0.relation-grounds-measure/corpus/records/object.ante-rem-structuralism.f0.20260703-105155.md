**statement (plain)** — Ante rem ("before the thing") structuralism answers Benacerraf by taking structures as fundamental existents: the natural-number structure exists in its own right, prior to and independent of any system (sets, strokes, beads) that realizes it, and "2" names a *position* in it — an office defined wholly by its relations to the other positions, not an officeholder with an independent nature. "Structures are prior to places in the same sense that any organization is prior to the offices that constitute it."

**contrasts** — against *eliminative* / modal structuralism ([[source.hellman-1989]]), which refuses abstract structures and reinterprets math modally, and against *in re* structuralism, which locates structures only in their instances. The ante rem commitment to positions-as-objects is exactly what exposes it to the identity objection ([[object.identity-of-indiscernibles-structuralism]]) [[source.shapiro-1997]] **[confirmed]**.
