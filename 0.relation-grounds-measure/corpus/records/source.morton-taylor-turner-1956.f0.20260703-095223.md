B. R. Morton, G. I. Taylor & J. S. Turner, "Turbulent gravitational convection from maintained and instantaneous sources," *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London. Series A* 234, no. 1196 (1956): 1–23.

Contains — the **MTT entrainment model** of buoyant plumes and jets, built on the *entrainment assumption*: the rate at which a turbulent plume draws in ambient fluid at any height is proportional to a characteristic velocity there (with self-similar velocity/buoyancy profiles). Yields the classic power-law growth of plume radius and dilution of momentum/buoyancy with height — the physics of how a directed flow maintains coherence while spreading.
