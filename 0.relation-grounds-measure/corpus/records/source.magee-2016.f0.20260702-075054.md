Glenn Alexander Magee (ed.), *The Cambridge Handbook of Western Mysticism and Esotericism* (Cambridge University Press, 2016). A multi-author reference handbook covering Western mysticism and esotericism from antiquity to the present — chapters by specialists on Gnosticism, Hermetism, Neoplatonism, Kabbalah, alchemy, Christian mysticism, and modern occult currents — a one-volume map of the field.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), General Western Esotericism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
