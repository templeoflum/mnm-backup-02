W. Ehrenstein, "Über Abwandlungen der L. Hermannschen Helligkeitserscheinung," *Zeitschrift für Psychologie* 150 (1941), 83–91.

Title translates as "Modifications of the Brightness Phenomenon of L. Hermann" — Ehrenstein presents the effect as a variant of the Hermann grid; the radial-line figure producing an illusory bright central disk. An English translation appeared later (Ehrenstein, W., "Modifications of the Brightness Phenomenon of L. Hermann," 1987). **[confirmed]** — journal, volume (150), and pages (83–91) agree across the Wikipedia article and the aggregated encyclopedia/Scholarpedia references; German title corrected to standard spelling *Helligkeitserscheinung*. Primary not opened.
