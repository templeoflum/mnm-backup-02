Egil Asprem, *Arguing with Angels: Enochian Magic and Modern Occulture* (SUNY Press, 2012). Scholarly reception history of Dee and Kelley's Enochian system — how the angelic material was rediscovered, reinvented, and contested from the Golden Dawn through Crowley to contemporary occulture, with attention to the sociology of ritual-magic legitimacy.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
