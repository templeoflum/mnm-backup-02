Donald Davidson, "Knowing One's Own Mind," *Proceedings and Addresses of the American Philosophical Association* 60, no. 3 (1987): 441–458 — introduces the Swampman to show that, on his causal-historical externalism about mental content, an atom-for-atom duplicate lacking the right history has no genuine thoughts or meanings. **[confirmed as attribution]**
