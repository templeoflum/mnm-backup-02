Yitzhak Frank, *Grammar for Gemara & Targum Onkelos: An Introduction to Aramaic* (Ariel Institute, 2003). A working grammar of Babylonian (Talmudic) and Targumic Aramaic built for readers of the Gemara and Targum Onkelos.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Languages section, with the list's note: "Relies on knowing Hebrew!" Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
