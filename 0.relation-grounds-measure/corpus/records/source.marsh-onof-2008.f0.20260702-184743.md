Leslie Marsh and Christian Onof, "Stigmergic epistemology, stigmergic cognition," *Cognitive Systems Research* 9, no. 1–2 (2008): 136–149.

Contains — argues stigmergy is not merely a coordination mechanism but an epistemic and cognitive one, explicitly joining it to the extended-mind tradition (Clark & Chalmers) [[source.clark-chalmers-1998]]: proposes "social stigmergy as the extra-cranial analog of an artificial neural network providing epistemic structure." A bridge record between the stigmergy cluster and distributed cognition [[object.distributed-cognition]].
