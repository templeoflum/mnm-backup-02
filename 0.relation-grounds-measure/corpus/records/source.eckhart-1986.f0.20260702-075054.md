Bernard McGinn (ed.), *Meister Eckhart: Teacher and Preacher* (Paulist Press, 1986). Companion volume to the 1981 *Essential Sermons* — further Latin biblical commentaries and a wider selection of German sermons, filling out the scholastic and homiletic sides of Eckhart's work.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
