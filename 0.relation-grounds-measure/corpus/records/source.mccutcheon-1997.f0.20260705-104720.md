Russell T. McCutcheon, *Manufacturing Religion: The Discourse on Sui Generis Religion and the Politics of Nostalgia* (Oxford, 1997) — attacks the "sui generis" claim (religion as distinct, unique, self-caused) associated with Eliade and the Chicago school, arguing it renders the object "ahistoric, apolitical, fetishized" ("skyhooks," "politics of nostalgia").
