**statement (plain)** — For an entangled pair, measuring one particle lets you predict the other's property with certainty without disturbing it — so (by EPR's reality criterion) that property was real all along. Assuming locality, QM's failure to include it means QM is incomplete: there must be hidden variables.

**statement (framework)** — the sharpest argument that QM is **incomplete**, resting on **locality + realism**. Bohr replied that the reality criterion is too narrow; Bohm made it testable with spins; Bell (1964) proved no local-realistic theory matches QM. The seed of entanglement science. Established as an argument; its premise (which to keep) **open**.
