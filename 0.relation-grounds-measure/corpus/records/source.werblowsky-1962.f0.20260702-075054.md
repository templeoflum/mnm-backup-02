R. J. Zwi Werblowsky, *Joseph Karo: Lawyer and Mystic* (Oxford University Press, 1962). The classic study of Joseph Karo, author of the Shulḥan Arukh, and his *Maggid Mesharim* — the diary of visitations by a celestial maggid — showing halakhic mastery and mystical audition joined in one Safed figure.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Safedian Kabbalah. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
