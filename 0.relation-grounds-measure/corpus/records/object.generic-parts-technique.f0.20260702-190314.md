**statement (plain)** — to break fixation, strip each part of a problem down to a description that carries *no* hint of its usual function. A candle's "wick" is re-described as "string"; a "prong" as "a shaped piece of metal." Freed of their conventional use, the parts suggest new possibilities. A deliberate, trainable procedure for overcoming functional fixedness [[object.functional-fixedness]].

**statement (method)** — for each component, ask (1) can it be decomposed further? and (2) does my description imply a use? — and if so, re-describe more generically (material, shape, size). Subjects trained on the technique solved ~67% more insight problems than controls.

**basis** — McCaffrey 2012 [[source.mccaffrey-2012]]. *Support* — a rare fixation countermeasure with a measured effect size. *Limits* — tested on well-defined insight puzzles; scaling to open-ended design is less established. **[confirmed as attribution]**
