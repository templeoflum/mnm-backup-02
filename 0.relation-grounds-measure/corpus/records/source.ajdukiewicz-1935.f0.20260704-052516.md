K. Ajdukiewicz, "Die syntaktische Konnexität" ("Syntactic Connexion"), *Studia Philosophica* 1:1–27 (1935). **[confirmed as attribution]** (venue from knowledge; the dive cites Ajdukiewicz 1935 as categorial grammar's origin)

Role — the origin of categorial grammar: assigns expressions fractional category indices that cancel like arithmetic to check well-formedness (a sentence is connected if its indices reduce), the antecedent of Bar-Hillel (1953), Lambek (1958), and Steedman's CCG.
