**statement (plain)** — **Xochiquetzal** ("flower-quetzal / precious flower") is the eternally young and beautiful Aztec/Nahua goddess of flowers, pleasure, and craft. She is patron of lovers, artists, weavers, and courtesans. In one account she was abducted from her husband by **Tezcatlipoca** — in others taken from **Tlaloc** — and the accounts vary.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: flowers, love, beauty, the arts, weaving. Nahuatl *Xochiquetzal*, "flower-quetzal" / "precious flower." Ever-young patroness of lovers, artisans, weavers, and courtesans; the abduction accounts (by **Tezcatlipoca** or from **Tlaloc**) diverge. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the post-conquest colonial records (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*); the abduction narrative varies across accounts and should be hedged, not fixed to one telling. **[confirmed as attribution]**
