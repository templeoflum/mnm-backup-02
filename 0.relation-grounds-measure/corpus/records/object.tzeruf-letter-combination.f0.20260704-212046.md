**statement (plain)** — *tzeruf*: systematic combination of Hebrew letters and divine names, with breathing techniques and visualization, performed by the individual to attain *devekut* / unitive experience as the *summum bonum* — the core technique of the **ecstatic-prophetic** orientation (anthropocentric; symbol "matters very little, if at all"), against the **theosophical-theurgical** orientation where ritual with *kavvanah* acts on the sefirotic structure itself. **[confirmed]** *(status `doctrinal`.)*

**basis** — Idel's phenomenological recovery of the practiced, experiential dimension ([[source.idel-1988]]; [[source.idel-abulafia-1988]]); the Scholem-vs-Idel dispute rides the [[field.kabbalah]] lineage. Idel's own limit honored: "The chance of success in reconstructing the nature of a mystical experience from written texts is close to nil."
