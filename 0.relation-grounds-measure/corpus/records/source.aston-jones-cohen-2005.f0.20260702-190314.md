Gary Aston-Jones and Jonathan D. Cohen, "An Integrative Theory of Locus Coeruleus–Norepinephrine Function: Adaptive Gain and Optimal Performance," *Annual Review of Neuroscience* 28 (2005): 403–450.

Contains — **adaptive-gain theory**: the locus-coeruleus–norepinephrine (LC-NE) system implements the explore/exploit tradeoff [[object.explore-exploit-tradeoff]] at the neuromodulatory level. *Phasic* LC firing (tightly time-locked to task events) supports focused exploitation; a shift to *tonic* LC firing drives task-disengagement and a search for alternatives (exploration). Suggests boredom and mental fatigue may be evolved signals to switch from exploit to explore. A mechanistic anchor for the felt "I've squeezed this dry" shift.
