Ludwig Prandtl, "Über Flüssigkeitsbewegung bei sehr kleiner Reibung" ["On the motion of fluids with very little friction"], *Verhandlungen des III. Internationalen Mathematiker-Kongresses* (Heidelberg, 1904): 484–491.

Contains — the boundary-layer concept: for a fluid of low viscosity, viscous effects are confined to a thin layer adjacent to a solid surface, while the outer flow behaves as effectively inviscid. Resolves d'Alembert's paradox and explains **flow separation** — where the boundary layer detaches, producing wakes, stall, and pressure drag. A ten-minute conference talk that founded modern aerodynamics and the analysis of drag and streamlining.

Access — the canonical modern reference is Schlichting & Gersten, *Boundary-Layer Theory* (8th/9th eds., Springer). Original proceedings short and hard to obtain. **[confirmed as attribution]**
