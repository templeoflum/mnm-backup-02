**statement (plain)** — *Sefer Yetzirah* opens: God created the world through "32 wondrous paths of wisdom" (*netivot*) — the 10 [[object.sefirot]] plus the 22 Hebrew letters. Scholem: the book explains "the hidden network of relationships" between the universe and the paths; the structuralist reading stresses that meaning "arises not through individual units but only in combination." The letters are "the connecting-link between essence and form" — instruments by which the world is produced. **[confirmed]** *(status `doctrinal`.)*

**basis** — SY i–ii; Scholem's discussion; Hayman's critical edition. Scholem flagged the divine-action verbs (engraved, hewed, created) as terminologically ambiguous. Whether SY is cosmological-mystical (Scholem), a grammatical primer (Mordell, rejected by Scholem), *ars poetica* (Liebes), or astrological-magical is genuinely contested — carried as lineage on [[field.kabbalah]].
