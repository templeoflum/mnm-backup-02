**statement (plain)** - a recipe gives the steps but loses the craft - the sensing, improvising, and repair an expert does. This grammar tries to capture that, with seven patterns: granularity shifts (adjustable detail), reflective loops (sense-and-adjust), note-to-self (the tacit "why"), external links, segments (collapsible sections), branches (divergences from the pattern), and revision loops (prototyping, undo/redo/repair). Built on Knight & Stiny's "doing and sensing with stuff to make things"; realized in CraftLink, which turns craft videos into graphs.

**basis** - Batra et al. (2025, CSCW 2026), evaluated with N=7 expert crocheters; anchored in Polanyi, Schon (reflect-in-action), Goodwin (professional vision), Ingold, Pye, Sennett. The residual limit the authors name: embodied "feel" ("I just like feel it in my body") still escapes the grammar.
