**statement (plain)** — Wigner's friend, sealed in a lab, measures a quantum system and sees a definite outcome. But from outside, Wigner must describe the entire lab — friend and result — as a superposition until he himself opens the door. The friend has a definite experience; Wigner's description says she doesn't.

**statement (framework)** — sharpens the measurement problem to *for whom* the wavefunction collapses, and resists the 'too macroscopic' dodge by making the observer a person. Recent extensions (Frauchiger–Renner, Brukner) argue no observer-independent facts. Forces a choice: QM incomplete, consciousness special, or objective outcomes abandoned. Established; **open**.
