Bertram R. Forer, "The fallacy of personal validation: A classroom demonstration of gullibility," *Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology* 44, no. 1 (1949): 118–123.

Contains — the classroom demonstration founding the **Barnum/Forer effect**: 39 students given an *identical* 13-item personality sketch (assembled from an astrology book), each told it was individualized, rated its accuracy a mean of 4.26/5 and accepted an average of 10.23 of the 13 statements as true of them. The mechanism by which vague, universally-applicable statements feel personally validated — the psychology under cold reading. (The label "Barnum effect" was coined by Paul Meehl in 1956.)
