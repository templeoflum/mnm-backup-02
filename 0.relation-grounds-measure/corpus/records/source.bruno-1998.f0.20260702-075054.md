Giordano Bruno, *Cause, Principle and Unity: And Essays on Magic* (Cambridge University Press, 1998). Standard English edition pairing Bruno's central metaphysical dialogue (*De la causa, principio et uno*, 1584) — infinite universe, matter as divine, unity of all — with his essays *On Magic* and *A General Account of Bonding*, giving his magical theory in his own words.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Renaissance Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
