Lin Hong Tan, Bay Chuan Lo & C. Neil Macrae, "Brief mindfulness meditation improves mental state attribution and empathizing," *PLOS ONE* 9, no. 10 (2014): e110510.

Contains — in 72 meditation-naïve participants, even a 5-minute mindfulness induction improved Reading the Mind in the Eyes Test (RMET) performance with a very large effect (reported t(70) = 5.62, p < .001, d = 1.33) versus control. Cited as evidence that self-quieting can rapidly improve mental-state reading — though single-session inductions warrant caution about durability.
