**statement (plain)** — The Munker Illusion demonstrates a powerful assimilation effect in which target patches appear to shift in lightness or color toward the properties of thin overlying stripes, rather than showing the contrast effect predicted by simultaneous contrast principles. When identical gray or colored patches are overlaid with differently colored thin stripes, the patches appear to take on the hue or lightness of the stripes themselves. This effect is opposite to traditional simultaneous contrast, where surrounded regions shift away from their surround.
