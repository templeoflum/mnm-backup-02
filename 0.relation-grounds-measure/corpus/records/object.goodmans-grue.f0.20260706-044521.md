**statement (plain)** — Call a thing *grue* if it is green and observed before time *t*, or blue otherwise. Every emerald seen so far is green — and equally, grue. The evidence for "all emeralds are green" and "all emeralds are grue" is identical, yet they predict differently after *t*. Induction cannot, by itself, pick the right hypothesis.

**statement (framework)** — Goodman's deepening of Hume: even granting induction, we must say *which* predicates are **projectible**. What makes a predicate projectible (Goodman's answer: entrenchment) connects to natural kinds, concept structure, Bayesian priors, and inductive bias in machine learning. Established; the projectibility problem stays **open**.
