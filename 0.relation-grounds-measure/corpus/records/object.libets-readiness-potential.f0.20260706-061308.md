**statement (plain)** — Subjects flex a wrist whenever they feel like it, noting on a clock the instant they became aware of the urge. EEG shows the readiness potential rising ~550 ms before movement, but awareness comes only ~200 ms before — a ~350 ms window in which the brain prepares to act before the person 'decides.'

**statement (framework)** — an **empirical** result (not a hypothetical) taken to threaten conscious free will: if unconscious processes initiate action and 'willing' is a post-hoc narrative, the felt causal role of consciousness is illusory. Heavily contested — the RP as an artifact of averaging, the timing of awareness, and what 'decision' means (Schurger et al.). Empirical; interpretation **open**.
