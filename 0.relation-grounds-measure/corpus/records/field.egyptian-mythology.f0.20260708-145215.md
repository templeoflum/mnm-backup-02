**origin** — the **Pyramid Texts** (Old Kingdom, c. 2400–2300 BCE) — the oldest corpus of Egyptian religious and funerary spells, inscribed in the royal pyramids, giving the earliest attested form of the solar and Osirian cycles and the king's ascent among the gods — continued in the **Coffin Texts** (Middle Kingdom) and the **Book of the Dead** (New Kingdom), the papyrus collection of funerary spells for coming forth by day. [[source.pyramid-texts]] [[source.book-of-the-dead]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — the **Book of the Dead** (New Kingdom, c. 1550 BCE onward) — the standardized New Kingdom collection of funerary spells on papyrus, the reference the afterlife myths and the judgment of the dead are most often drawn from. [[source.book-of-the-dead]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Richard H. Wilkinson, *The Complete Gods and Goddesses of Ancient Egypt* (Thames & Hudson, 2003) — the standard modern illustrated reference (with Geraldine Pinch, *Egyptian Mythology*, for the myths). [[source.wilkinson-2003]]
