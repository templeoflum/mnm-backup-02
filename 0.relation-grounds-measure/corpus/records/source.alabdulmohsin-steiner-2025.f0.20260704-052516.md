I. Alabdulmohsin & A. Steiner, "A Tale of Two Structures: Do LLMs Capture the Fractal Complexity of Language?" arXiv:2502.14924; ICML 2025.

Role — finds natural-language fractal parameters lie in a narrow range while LLM outputs vary widely (usable for synthetic-text detection); Hurst is a strong text-quality predictor; releases GAGLE ("over 240,000 articles generated by various LLMs"), robust across Gemini 1.0 Pro, Mistral-7B, Gemma-2B. States the convention: smaller S = heavier-tailed (stronger self-similar structure); larger H≫0.5 = stronger long-range dependence. **[confirmed]**
