Mark S. Smith, *The Early History of God: Yahweh and the Other Deities in Ancient Israel* (Harper & Row, 1990; 2nd ed. Eerdmans, 2002). Foundational study of how Israelite monotheism developed out of a West Semitic polytheistic matrix — Yahweh's convergence with El and differentiation from Baal, Asherah, and the solar and astral deities.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Biblical & Ancient Near East. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
