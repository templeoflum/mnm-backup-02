**statement (plain)** — **Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto** is the goddess of dawn, mirth, and revelry. When **Amaterasu** hid in the **Ame-no-Iwato** (Heavenly Rock Cave), Uzume's comic, ecstatic dance made the assembled kami roar with laughter, and the curious sun goddess emerged to see the cause, restoring light to the world. She later won over the fierce earthly kami **Sarutahiko**, whom she married.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: dawn, dance, mirth, revelry, shamanism. She is the divine dancer whose revelry restores the sun, and a patroness of performance and shamanic trance. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Ame-no-Uzume is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant details of the Rock Cave dance; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
