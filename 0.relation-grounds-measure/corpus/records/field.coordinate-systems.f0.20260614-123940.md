**origin** — René Descartes, *La Géométrie* (1637, appendix to *Discours de la méthode*) — analytic/coordinate geometry. [[source.descartes-1637]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Felix Klein, *Vergleichende Betrachtungen über neuere geometrische Forschungen* ("Erlangen Program," Erlangen: Deichert, 1872) — a geometry = a transformation group + its invariants. reprinted *Math. Annalen* 43 (1893). [[source.klein-1872]] **[confirmed]**

**through-line** — Klein → general covariance (no privileged chart); Cartan later generalized Klein's homogeneous spaces to Cartan connections.

**door** — Klein's program in English: math.ucr.edu (Baez) and arXiv:0807.3161; *La Géométrie* (Smith–Latham trans.) public-domain on archive.org. [[source.klein-1872]] [[source.descartes-1637]]
