Wendy Wood, *Good Habits, Bad Habits: The Science of Making Positive Changes That Stick* (Farrar, Straus & Giroux, 2019).

Contains — three decades of the author's habit research for a general audience: habits form through repetition in stable contexts; willpower and intention are weak and easily depleted, while friction, cues, and reward-timing in the environment are decisive; the practical program is to redesign context so the wanted behavior becomes the low-friction default and the unwanted one high-friction. Connects to burnout as the self-system's coherence ceiling.
