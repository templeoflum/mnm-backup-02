Mica R. Endsley, "Toward a Theory of Situation Awareness in Dynamic Systems," *Human Factors* 37, no. 1 (1995): 32–64.

Contains — the standard theory of **situation awareness (SA)**: the perception of elements in the environment (Level 1), comprehension of their meaning (Level 2), and projection of their future state (Level 3). Frames attention and working memory as the limiting factors and mental models / goal-directed behavior as the mechanisms that overcome them; analyzes how design, workload, stress, complexity, and automation affect SA, and gives a taxonomy of SA errors. The foundational reference for what an operator must "have" to run a dynamic process.
