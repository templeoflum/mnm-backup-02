**statement (plain)** — **Tlazolteotl** ("filth goddess" / "eater of filth") is the Aztec/Nahua goddess who inspires illicit lust and then absolves it. Penitents could confess grave sins to her priests once in a lifetime, and she "ate" the filth of the transgression. She is associated with steam baths, midwifery, and the earth's regenerative filth.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: sexual desire, sin, purification, confession, midwifery. Nahuatl *Tlazolteotl*, "filth goddess" / "eater of filth." Devourer of transgressions in the once-in-a-lifetime confession; tied to steam baths, midwives, and the regenerative filth of the earth. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the post-conquest colonial records (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*); her attributes and the confession rite are variously reported and should be held loosely. **[confirmed as attribution]**
