Yuval Harari, *Jewish Magic Before the Rise of Kabbalah*, trans. Batya Stein (Wayne State University Press, 2017). A two-part work: a methodological examination of how "magic" is defined and studied, followed by a survey of Jewish magical literature and practice from antiquity to the early medieval period. Foundational alongside Bohak as a pillar of current scholarship on pre-kabbalistic Jewish magic; English translation of the Hebrew original (2010).

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
