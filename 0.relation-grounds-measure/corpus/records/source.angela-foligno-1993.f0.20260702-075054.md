Paul Lachance (trans.), *Angela of Foligno: Complete Works* (Paulist Press, 1993). The *Memorial* — the Umbrian Franciscan tertiary's dictated account of her passage through the steps of penance into darkness beyond vision — together with the *Instructions*; a major witness to Franciscan passion-mysticism shading into the apophatic.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
