**statement (plain)** — Statistical mechanics says any configuration, however improbable, occurs eventually. A fully formed brain with false memories of a life is such a configuration. In an eternal or vast universe, these random 'Boltzmann brains' would vastly outnumber ordinary observers who evolved the long way — so, probabilistically, you should be one.

**statement (framework)** — a **reductio** used to constrain cosmological models: any theory predicting we are typically Boltzmann brains is self-undermining (our observations, including of physics, would be random), so such models are rejected. A live tool in evaluating eternal-inflation and de Sitter cosmologies. Established; **open**.
