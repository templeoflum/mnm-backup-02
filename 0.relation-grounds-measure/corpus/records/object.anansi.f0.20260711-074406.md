**statement (plain)** — **Anansi** (Kwaku Anansi) is the spider-trickster of the **Akan** (Ashanti) of Ghana, the wily figure at the heart of the **Anansesem** ("spider tales"). He won all the world's stories from the sky-god **Nyame** by capturing the creatures Nyame demanded. He embodies wit over strength and a subversive, boundary-crossing intelligence.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: stories, wisdom, trickery, cunning, and weaving. In the great tale he wins the stories by capturing the python, the leopard, the hornets, and the fairy that Nyame set as his price. His tales survived the Middle Passage into the Caribbean and the American South (as **Aunt Nancy** / "Anansi stories"). **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Anansi is documented in the cited sources ([[source.rattray-1930]], [[source.pelton-1980]]); this is documented Akan oral tradition (`status: doctrinal`), recorded in the field by Rattray and studied by Pelton. The diaspora spread of the tales through the slave trade is well attested, though local forms vary and should be read as a broad transmission rather than a single lineage. **[confirmed as attribution]**
