Frank Klaassen, *The Transformations of Magic: Illicit Learned Magic in the Later Middle Ages and Renaissance* (Pennsylvania State University Press, 2013). Traces two streams of illicit learned magic — astrological image magic and ritual magic — through the surviving manuscripts, showing how medieval practice carried into and shaped Renaissance occult philosophy.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
