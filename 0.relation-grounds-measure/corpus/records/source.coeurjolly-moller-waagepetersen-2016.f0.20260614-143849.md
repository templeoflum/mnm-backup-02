J.-F. Coeurjolly, J. Møller, R. Waagepetersen, "A tutorial on Palm distributions for spatial point processes," arXiv:1512.05871v2 (2016); published in *International Statistical Review* 85(3), 2017.

Role — accessible-but-rigorous second source. Gives: the Poisson process by its defining count/independence property and the integral-form characterization (their eq. 1); Campbell's theorem / the higher-order Campbell formula (eq. 6); the reduced Palm distribution via the disintegration of the reduced Campbell measure (eq. 14); the Campbell–Mecke formula (eq. 12). Cites Palm (1943) for the original Palm-distribution idea on the line and Daley & Vere-Jones (2003) for the measure-theoretic disintegration.
