**statement (plain)** — Moiré Pattern Illusions arise when two or more repetitive patterns, such as grids, lines, or dots, are superimposed with a slight offset in angle, spacing, or phase. The interference between these overlapping patterns creates emergent large-scale structures that were not present in either original pattern alone. These emergent structures can include apparent bands, waves, or curved contours that may appear to shimmer, flow, or move when the observer or the patterns shift position.
