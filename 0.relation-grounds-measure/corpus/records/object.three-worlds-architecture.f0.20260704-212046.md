**statement (plain)** — the *Three Books of Occult Philosophy* mirror the Neoplatonic-Kabbalistic cosmos: Book I the **elemental** world (natural magic; occult virtues, sympathies/antipathies), Book II the **celestial** world (astrological/numerical magic; planetary tables of correspondence), Book III the **intellectual** world (ceremonial magic; Christian Kabbalah, angelology, divine names) — "every inferior... governed by its superior, and [receiving] the influence of the vertues thereof," so the magus ascends through correspondences, "a path that leads both upward and downward." **[confirmed]** *(status `doctrinal`.)*

**basis** — Agrippa (1533), drawing on Ficino, Pico, Reuchlin, Trithemius; the structural three-worlds account is uncontroversial and well-attested (Grafton's line: "complex spider webs of influence that passed from high to low and low to high"). [[source.agrippa-1533]] [[source.yates-1964]]
