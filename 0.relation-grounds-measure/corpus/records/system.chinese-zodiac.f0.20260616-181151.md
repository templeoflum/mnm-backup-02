**origin** — the *shēngxiào*, the twelve-animal cycle correlated with the twelve Earthly Branches (*dìzhī*) of the Chinese sexagenary calendar; ancient, folk-calendrical in origin. [[source.eberhard-1986]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — each animal a year-sign with traditional character associations; the cycle repeats every twelve years (and, with the ten Heavenly Stems, every sixty). [[source.eberhard-1986]]

**door** — Eberhard, *A Dictionary of Chinese Symbols* (1986).

**lineage** — folk-astrological character ascription (vs. the calendrical/Earthly-Branch function) carried as a note. Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
