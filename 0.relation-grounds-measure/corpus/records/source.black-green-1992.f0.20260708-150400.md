**Gods, Demons and Symbols of Ancient Mesopotamia** (British Museum Press, 1992), by **Jeremy Black** and **Anthony Green**. The standard illustrated reference dictionary of ancient Mesopotamian religion — the gods, demons, myths, and iconography of Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, and Assyria — with entries drawn from the cuneiform record and illustrated by Tessa Rickards. The modern door into the field. **[confirmed as attribution]**
