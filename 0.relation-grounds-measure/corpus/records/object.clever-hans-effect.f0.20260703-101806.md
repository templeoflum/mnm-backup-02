**statement (plain)** — Named for the horse that appeared to do arithmetic: what Clever Hans actually did was read tiny, involuntary changes in his questioner's posture, breathing, and facial tension, stopping his tapping when the questioner (who knew the answer) unconsciously relaxed. He succeeded only when the questioner knew the answer and was visible. The effect names any case where an apparent ability is really the reading of unintended cues leaked by someone who knows the target.

**two faces** — as a methodological warning it demands double-blind procedure (the origin of the observer-expectancy effect); as a *positive* result it is the cleanest demonstration that subtle nonconscious cue-leakage and cue-reading are real and powerful — the lean core of demystified "psychic" reading. The leaking is an involuntary ideomotor process (expectation → movement; cf. [[object.ideomotor-action]]), read below awareness [[source.pfungst-1907]] **[confirmed]**.
