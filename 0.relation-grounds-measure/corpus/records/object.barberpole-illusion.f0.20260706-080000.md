**statement (plain)** — The Barberpole Illusion is a compelling motion perception phenomenon in which diagonal stripes moving behind a rectangular aperture appear to move along the longer axis of the aperture rather than in their true direction of motion. Named after the traditional rotating barbershop pole that displays this effect, the illusion reveals fundamental principles about how the visual system integrates local motion signals into global motion percepts. When diagonal stripes move at an oblique angle behind a vertically oriented rectangular window, observers perceive predominantly vertical motion rather than the actual diagonal trajectory.
