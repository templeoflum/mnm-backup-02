**origin** — (lineage) predictive-coding roots in Helmholtz's unconscious inference and Rao & Ballard's 1999 cortical predictive coding; the unifying statement is K. Friston, "The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?" *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 11(2):127–138 (2010) — self-organizing systems minimize variational free energy (surprise). [[source.friston-2010]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — the active-inference formulation: perception changes the model to fit the world, action changes the world to fit the model; adaptive organisms are "embodied generative models of their environments." Extended to niche/culture by A. Constant, A. Clark, M. Kirchhoff & K. Friston, "Extended active inference," *Mind & Language* 37(3):373–394 (2020/2022). [[source.constant-2020]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — A. Clark, *Surfing Uncertainty: Prediction, Action, and the Embodied Mind* (Oxford, 2016) is the accessible book-length door; the field overlaps `distributed-cognition` (the extended/embodied program) and `interoception` (interoceptive predictive coding). [[source.clark-2008]]

*Scope: the field entered here at its cognitive/organismic edge — the free-energy principle, active inference, cognitive niche construction. Kin: [[field.distributed-cognition]] holds the extended-mind cluster this program's "extended active inference" reaches into.*
