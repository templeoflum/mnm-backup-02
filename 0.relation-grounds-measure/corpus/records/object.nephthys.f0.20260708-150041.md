**statement (plain)** — **Nephthys** is a funerary goddess and, with her sister **Isis**, one of the two great mourners of the dead. She is the sister of **Isis**, **Osiris**, and **Set**, and the nominal wife of **Set**; yet she sides with Isis, mourning the murdered Osiris and helping to gather and protect his body. As one of the paired mourners she watches over the deceased and the funerary bier, and in some traditions she is the mother of **Anubis**. She belongs, with her siblings, to the Heliopolitan **Ennead**.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: mourning, funerary protection, and the dead. Family: daughter of **Geb** and **Nut**; sister of Isis, Osiris, and Set; nominal wife of Set; in some traditions mother of **Anubis**. She and Isis appear as the two protective mourners flanking the deceased and the coffin, a role central to the funerary tradition from the Pyramid Texts onward. No standard *interpretatio graeca* exists; occasional Greek associations (Aphrodite, Nike) are weak and inconsistent, not an established equation. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Egyptian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested from the Pyramid Texts [[source.pyramid-texts]] onward and present in the Book of the Dead [[source.book-of-the-dead]]; the standard reference treatment is Wilkinson [[source.wilkinson-2003]]. Her pairing with Isis as mourner and protector of the dead is established; her marriage to Set, her partisanship with Isis, and her motherhood of Anubis are attested tradition, variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
