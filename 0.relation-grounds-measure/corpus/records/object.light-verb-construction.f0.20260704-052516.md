**statement (plain)** — in "give a kiss" or Persian *ranj keshidan* ("drag pain" = to suffer), the verb carries little meaning — the noun does the work — yet the pair projects a full argument structure, event boundaries, and aspect, just as a plain verb phrase does. Megerdoomian's reading: the complex predicate is a miniature, self-similar copy of the verb phrase — the same relations at a smaller scale.

**basis** — Folli, Harley & Karimi (2005): the light verb determines agentivity/eventiveness but not fully event structure/telicity — the nonverbal element co-determines it. Wittenberg et al. (2014): ERP shows the light verb is semantically underspecified, the event nominal contributes the meaning and its own argument structure. Megerdoomian (2025, [candidate]): the "self-similar syntax" framing — a complex predicate as "a small-scale recursion of the verb phrase."
