Hilary Putnam, *Reason, Truth and History* (Cambridge UP, 1981), ch. 1 — argues (from semantic externalism, that reference depends on causal connection to the environment) that a lifelong brain in a vat cannot truthfully think "I am a brain in a vat," since its words don't refer to real brains or vats — the scenario is self-refuting. **[confirmed as attribution]**
