Ioannis Marathakis (trans.), *The Magical Treatise of Solomon or Hygromanteia* (Golden Hoard Press, 2011). Translation of the Byzantine Greek Solomonic manuscripts — planetary hours, spirit hierarchies, and ritual instructions — generally regarded as the direct ancestor of the *Key of Solomon* and thus of the whole Solomonic grimoire line.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
