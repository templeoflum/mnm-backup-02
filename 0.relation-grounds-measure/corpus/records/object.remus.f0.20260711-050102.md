**statement (plain)** — **Remus** is the twin brother of **Romulus** in the Roman foundation legend, son of **Mars** and the Vestal **Rhea Silvia**, co-suckled with his brother by the she-wolf. In the contest over where and by whom Rome would be founded — decided by an augury of birds — he was killed. His death, at his brother's hand or in the surrounding fray, sets the fratricidal shadow at the very origin of the city.

**statement (attestation)** — twin of Romulus; son of Mars and Rhea Silvia; co-nursed by the she-wolf and raised by Faustulus. In the augury contest for the founding he loses and is slain — variously by Romulus himself, in the fray, or for leaping contemptuously over the new walls. Associated figures: Romulus, Mars, Rhea Silvia, Faustulus. The death is reported in several incompatible variants across the tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in Livy's *Ab Urbe Condita* Book 1 and Plutarch's *Life of Romulus*; this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted fact. The circumstances of Remus' death vary across the sources — by Romulus, in the fray, or for leaping the walls — and are held as attested tradition rather than settled fact. **[confirmed as attribution]**
