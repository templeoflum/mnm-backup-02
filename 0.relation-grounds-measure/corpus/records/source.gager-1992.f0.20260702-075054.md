John G. Gager (ed.), *Curse Tablets and Binding Spells from the Ancient World* (Oxford University Press, 1992). An annotated collection of Greek and Roman curse tablets (defixiones) and binding spells in English translation, organized by sphere of life (love, litigation, competition, commerce). Foundational as the standard English-language sourcebook for ancient cursing practice.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
