Boaz Huss, *Mystifying Kabbalah: Academic Scholarship, National Theology, and New Age Spirituality* (Oxford University Press, 2020). A critical genealogy of the category "Jewish mysticism" itself — how academic scholarship, Zionist national theology, and New Age spirituality each constructed Kabbalah as "mysticism" — a methodological check on the whole field.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
