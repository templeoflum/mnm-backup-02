**statement (plain)** — Heiligenschein, German for "holy light" or "halo," is an atmospheric optical phenomenon appearing as a bright, diffuse glow surrounding the shadow of the observer's head when cast upon certain surfaces, most commonly dew-covered grass or other vegetation. The effect results from retroreflection, wherein light is preferentially scattered back toward its source by spherical water droplets acting as crude lens systems. Each dewdrop focuses incoming sunlight onto the surface behind it, which then reflects the light back through the droplet, creating a focused return beam directed toward the light source and observer.
