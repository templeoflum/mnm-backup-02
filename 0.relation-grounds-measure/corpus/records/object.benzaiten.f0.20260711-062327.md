**statement (plain)** — **Benzaiten** (Benten) is the goddess of music, the arts, eloquence, and all that flows — water, words, and music alike. She derives from the Hindu river-goddess **Saraswati** and entered Japan through Buddhism, and she is the only female among the **Shichifukujin**, the Seven Lucky Gods. She is associated with water, serpents, and dragons, and is enshrined on islands and by lakes.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: music, arts, eloquence, wisdom, water, snakes and dragons. Benzaiten derives from the Hindu goddess Saraswati, entered Japan via Buddhist scripture (the Sutra of Golden Light), and is the sole female of the Seven Lucky Gods, associated with water, serpents, and dragons. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Benzaiten is documented in Ashkenazi (2003) and this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). She is a Buddhist-syncretic goddess of non-Japanese origin: she derives from the Hindu river-goddess Saraswati and was brought into Japan through Buddhist scripture (the Sutra of Golden Light), not from the native Shinto pantheon. Her place among the Shichifukujin and her water, serpent, and dragon associations belong to the living syncretic tradition rather than the 8th-century chronicles. **[confirmed as attribution]**
