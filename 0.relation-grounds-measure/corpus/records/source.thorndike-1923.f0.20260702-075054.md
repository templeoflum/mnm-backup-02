Lynn Thorndike, *A History of Magic and Experimental Science* (Columbia University Press, 8 vols., 1923–1958). Monumental survey arguing that magic and experimental science grew up together from the Roman Empire through the seventeenth century; still the standard reference corpus for medieval and early modern learned magic.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic & Necromancy. The list says "find online, or on Abebooks, Ebay, library sales"; the early volumes are public domain and available on archive.org. **[confirmed as attribution]**
