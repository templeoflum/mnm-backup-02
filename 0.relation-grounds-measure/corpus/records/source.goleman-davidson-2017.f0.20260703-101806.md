Daniel Goleman & Richard J. Davidson, *Altered Traits: Science Reveals How Meditation Changes Your Mind, Brain, and Body* (Avery, 2017).

Contains — an evidence-weighted synthesis of the contemplative-neuroscience literature by two of its long-standing figures, deliberately separating robust findings from overclaimed ones (they "dutifully report the counter evidence"). Distinguishes the "deep path" (intensive transformation) from the "wide path" (lighter practice for many), and is the standard critical reference for what meditation demonstrably does to attention, emotion, and the brain.
