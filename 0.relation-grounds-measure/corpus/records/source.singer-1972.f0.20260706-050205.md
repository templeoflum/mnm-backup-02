Peter Singer, "Famine, Affluence, and Morality," *Philosophy & Public Affairs* 1, no. 3 (1972): 229–243 — argues from the drowning-child intuition that physical distance is morally irrelevant, so the affluent are strongly obligated to aid the distant poor. The cornerstone of effective altruism. **[confirmed as attribution]**
