Moshe Idel, *The Mystical Experience in Abraham Abulafia* (SUNY Press, 1988). A study of the ecstatic Kabbalah of Abraham Abulafia: letter-combination techniques, breathing and vocalization practices, and the experiences they produced — the experiential-technique wing of Kabbalah that Idel's *Kabbalah: New Perspectives* set against the theosophic mainstream.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
