**statement (plain)** — The "My Wife and My Mother-in-Law" illusion, also known as "Boring's Illusion" or the "Young Girl/Old Woman" figure, is one of the most famous ambiguous images in perceptual psychology. Originally published by William Ely Hill in 1915, the drawing can be perceived as either a young woman looking away or an elderly woman's profile, with identical contours serving different anatomical features in each interpretation. The young woman's chin becomes the old woman's nose, the young woman's ear becomes the old woman's eye, and the choker necklace transforms into a mouth.
