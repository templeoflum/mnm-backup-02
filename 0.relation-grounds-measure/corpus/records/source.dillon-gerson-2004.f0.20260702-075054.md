John Dillon and Lloyd P. Gerson, *Neoplatonic Philosophy: Introductory Readings* (Hackett, 2004). Translated selections from the Neoplatonists — Plotinus [[source.plotinus-enneads]], Porphyry, Iamblichus, Proclus — with introductions and notes; the standard classroom sourcebook for the tradition.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Neo-Platonism sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
