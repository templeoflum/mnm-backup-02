Lewis Carroll, "What the Tortoise Said to Achilles," *Mind* 4, no. 14 (1895): 278–280 — a dialogue in which the Tortoise accepts an argument's premises but demands, as a further premise, the rule licensing the inference — and again, and again — showing that inference cannot be reduced to premises. **[confirmed as attribution]**
