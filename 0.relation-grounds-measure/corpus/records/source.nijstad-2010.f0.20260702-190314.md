Bernard A. Nijstad, Carsten K. W. De Dreu, Eric F. Rietzschel, and Matthijs Baas, "The dual pathway to creativity model: Creative ideation as a function of flexibility and persistence," *European Review of Social Psychology* 21 (2010): 34–77.

Contains — the model that creative originality can be reached by **two** routes: **flexibility** (surveying many categories, broad jumps) and **persistence** (deep, systematic probing within few categories). Describes ideators shifting "from a flexible mode… to a systematic, persistent mode," and specifies the mood/motivation and cognitive conditions favoring each. A leading process model of creative ideation.
