Saul A. Kripke, *Wittgenstein on Rules and Private Language: An Elementary Exposition* (Harvard, 1982) — recasts Wittgenstein's core as a skeptical paradox about rule-following ("quus"), "the most radical and original skeptical problem philosophy has seen" (p. 60), with a Humean "skeptical solution" grounded in communal agreement. Kripke himself doubts the reading is the historical Wittgenstein's ("Kripkenstein").
