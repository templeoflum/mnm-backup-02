**statement (plain)** — **Quetzalcoatl** ("feathered serpent," also read "precious twin") is the Aztec/Nahua god of wind, wisdom, and creation, a culture-hero and priest-king. As a creator he helped make humankind, retrieving the bones of the past ages from **Mictlan**, and gave people maize and the arts; as **Ehecatl** he is the god of wind. In legend he is the priest-king of Tollan (Tula) who was driven out by **Tezcatlipoca** and departed to the east, vowing to return; he is identified with the planet Venus as the morning star.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: wind, wisdom, creation, life, the morning star, priesthood. Nahuatl *Quetzalcoatl*, from *quetzalli* (quetzal-feather / precious) and *coatl* (serpent). His reach is pan-Mesoamerican: the Maya feathered serpent **Kukulkan** is his counterpart. Adversary of Tezcatlipoca. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the colonial-era codices (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*), recorded after the conquest; the Tollan / priest-king narrative and the divine Quetzalcoatl are variously entangled and the accounts diverge. **[confirmed as attribution]**
