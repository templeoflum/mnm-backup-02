**statement (plain)** — **Aganju** (Aganjú) is the **Orisha** of the volcano, the desert, and the wild uncultivated land in Yoruba tradition. He embodies raw earth-force and untamed masculine power, and is invoked as the ferryman who bears people across dangerous waters. In some lineages he is the father of **Shango**, in others his brother.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: volcanoes, wilderness, the desert, rivers (crossing), father energy. Lord of the volcano and the untamed earth; ferryman who carries travelers across the river; kin to **Shango** — father in some lineages, brother in others. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962) and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; his kinship to **Shango** varies by lineage, and his attributes vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions. **[confirmed as attribution]**
