Malcolm Barber, *The Cathars: Dualist Heretics in Languedoc in the High Middle Ages* (Longman, 2000). Scholarly history of Catharism concentrated on Languedoc — social setting, dualist belief and organization, crusade and inquisition — a standard treatment alongside Lambert in the traditional account.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Heresy. Traditional-account framing, contested by the deconstructionist school (see Sennis). Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
