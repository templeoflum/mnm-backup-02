**statement (plain)** — *Decknamen* ("cover-names"): the deliberately obscure code-words of the alchemical corpus (lurid metaphors, beasts, weddings, deaths) that encode concrete substances and bench procedures. Newman's decoding of Eirenaeus Philalethes — metaphor to recipe to successful replication — is the decisive demonstration, and the refutation of the Jungian projection reading. **[confirmed]** *(status `established` — a finding of the historiography, not a tenet.)*

**basis** — [[source.newman-1996]]; Principe recreates decoded recipes at the bench (glass of antimony, the *arbor Dianae*, the Leiden-papyrus "water of sulphur") [[source.principe-2013]]. Rides with [[object.magnum-opus]] (prose).
