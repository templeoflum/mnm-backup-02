Todd R. La Porte and Paula M. Consolini, "Working in Practice But Not in Theory: Theoretical Challenges of 'High-Reliability Organizations'," *Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory* 1, no. 1 (1991): 19–48.

Contains — a foundational statement of the HRO program that names its central puzzle for organizational theory: HROs appear to be **simultaneously centralized and decentralized** — centralized in goals and the disciplined permission to proceed, decentralized in the authority to detect and stop — which standard theory says should not work. This is the theoretical frame behind deference to expertise [[object.deference-to-expertise]] and resolves the apparent paradox in Perrow's account (that a system cannot be both).
