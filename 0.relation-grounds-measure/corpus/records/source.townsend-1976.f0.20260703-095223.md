A. A. Townsend, *The Structure of Turbulent Shear Flow*, 2nd ed. (Cambridge University Press, 1976; 1st ed. 1956).

Contains — the systematic account of organized motion in turbulent shear flows, including the **attached-eddy hypothesis**: wall turbulence as a hierarchy of self-similar eddies attached to the surface, scaling with distance from the wall. A foundational statement that "turbulent" flow is not structureless but contains manipulable, organized eddies.

Access — the attached-eddy hypothesis is most developed in the 1976 second edition. In print / library-held. **[confirmed as attribution]**
