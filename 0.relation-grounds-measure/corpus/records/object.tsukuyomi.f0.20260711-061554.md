**statement (plain)** — **Tsukuyomi-no-Mikoto** is the moon god, born from **Izanagi**'s right eye and ruler of the night. Sent by **Amaterasu** to the food-goddess **Ukemochi**, he killed her in disgust at how she produced food from her body, and Amaterasu turned her face from him forever. This is why the sun and moon are never seen together in the sky.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: moon, night, time. He is the night-ruling counterpart to his sister the sun. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Tsukuyomi is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which variously assign the slaying of the food-goddess to Tsukuyomi or to Susanoo; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
