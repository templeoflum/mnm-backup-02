E. M. Butler, *Ritual Magic* (Cambridge University Press, 1949). Pioneering survey of the Western ritual magic tradition from ancient and Solomonic sources through the medieval grimoires to the Faust legend; a period piece whose scholarship is dated but which remains a much-cited early map of the grimoire corpus.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. Scholarship predates the modern manuscript-based study of the field; read as a classic, not a current authority. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (Penn State reprint). **[confirmed as attribution]**
