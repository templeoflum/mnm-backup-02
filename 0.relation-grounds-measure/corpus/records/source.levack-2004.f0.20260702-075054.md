Brian P. Levack (ed.), *The Witchcraft Sourcebook* (Routledge, 2004). Primary documents on European witchcraft from antiquity through the early modern hunts — demonological treatises, trial records, confessions, skeptical responses — with editorial introductions; the standard documentary reader for the witch hunts.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Early-Modern Witch Hunts sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
