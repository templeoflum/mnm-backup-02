**origin** — C. R. Rao (1945) reads the Fisher information as a Riemannian metric on a parameterized family of distributions — the same paper that carries the Cramér–Rao bound. [[source.rao-1945]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — N. N. Čencov's invariance theorem (Russian 1972; AMS translation 1982): the Fisher metric is the unique (up to scale) Riemannian metric on statistical manifolds invariant under sufficient statistics. [[source.cencov-1982]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — S. Amari, *Information Geometry and Its Applications* (Springer, 2016) — the Fisher–Rao metric, dually-flat structures, divergences. [[source.amari-2016]] **[confirmed as attribution]**
