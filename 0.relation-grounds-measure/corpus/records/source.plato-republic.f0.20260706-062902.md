Plato, *Republic* (c. 380 BCE) — the central dialogue of political and moral philosophy; contains the **allegory of the cave** (Book VII, 514a–520a), the **ring of Gyges** challenge (Book II, 359a–360d), and the **ship of state** analogy (Book VI, 488a–489a), alongside the theory of Forms and the tripartite soul. **[confirmed as attribution]**
