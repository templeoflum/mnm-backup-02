Wouter J. Hanegraaff, *Hermetic Spirituality and the Historical Imagination: Altered States of Knowledge in Late Antiquity* (Cambridge University Press, 2022). A major monograph reinterpreting the ancient Hermetica [[source.corpus-hermeticum]] not as philosophical treatises but as records of a lived spiritual discipline aimed at altered states of knowledge; the author's chief work on Hermeticism after [[source.hanegraaff-2012]].

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Hermeticism sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
