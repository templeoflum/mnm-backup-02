**statement (plain)** — The Checkerboard Bulge Illusion is a geometric distortion effect in which a regular checkerboard pattern with strategically displaced edges appears to bulge outward from the plane, creating a three-dimensional convex appearance despite being presented on a flat surface. The illusion typically involves systematic shifts in the position of individual square edges or boundaries, creating local perturbations in an otherwise regular grid. These displacements are carefully calibrated to produce coherent perception of volumetric expansion, with the center of the pattern appearing to project toward the observer.
