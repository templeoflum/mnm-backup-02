J. H. Chajes, *Between Worlds: Dybbuks, Exorcists, and Early Modern Judaism* (University of Pennsylvania Press, 2003). A historical study of spirit possession (dybbuk) and exorcism in early modern Jewish culture, centered on sixteenth-century Safed, where Lurianic ideas of soul-transmigration made possession legible and exorcism a kabbalistic craft.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Safedian Kabbalah. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
