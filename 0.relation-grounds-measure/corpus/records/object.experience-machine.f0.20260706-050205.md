**statement (plain)** — A machine can give you any experience you want — writing a great novel, falling in love — indistinguishable from reality. Would you plug in for life? Nozick says most refuse: we want to *actually do* things and *be* a certain person and make contact with reality, not merely have the experiences.

**statement (framework)** — the standard objection to **hedonism** and experiential theories of well-being: what we value exceeds felt experience. Experimental philosophers reply that status-quo bias and framing, not deep metaphysics, drive the refusal; VR and algorithmic feeds make the case newly literal. Established as the key anti-hedonist device; its verdict stays **open**.
