**statement (plain)** — Shepard's Tables illusion, created by cognitive scientist Roger N. Shepard in 1990, is a dramatic demonstration of how perspective cues override metric perception of shape. The stimulus consists of two identical parallelograms depicted as tabletops viewed from different angles in three-dimensional space.

**statement (framework)** — category: geometric size length; perceptual mechanism(s): misapplied-size-constancy, depth-cue-conflict.
