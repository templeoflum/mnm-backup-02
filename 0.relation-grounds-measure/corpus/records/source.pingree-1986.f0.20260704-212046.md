D. Pingree, *The Latin Version of the Ghāyat al-Hakīm* (Warburg Institute, 1986). **[confirmed]**

Role — the critical Latin edition of the *Picatrix* (10th–11th-c. Arabic handbook of talismanic/astral magic, compiled per the scholarship by al-Qurtubī from 200+ sources; Castilian then Latin, 13th c.). The scholarly English translation, [[source.attrell-porreca-2019]], is based on it. Couliano traces Ficino's astrological magic partly to this text.
