Immanuel Kant, *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (1785) — grounds morality in the rational will and the **categorical imperative** (act only on maxims you could will as universal law; treat humanity always as an end, never merely as a means). The founding text of deontological ethics. **[confirmed as attribution]**
