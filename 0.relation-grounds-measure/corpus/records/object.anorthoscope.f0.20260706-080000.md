**statement (plain)** — The anorthoscope is a device invented by Joseph Plateau in 1836 that demonstrates the visual system's capacity for temporal integration across sequential glimpses of visual information. The apparatus consists of a distorted or fragmented image mounted on a rotating disc, viewed through a narrow vertical slit. As the disc rotates behind the stationary slit, different portions of the distorted image are revealed in rapid succession.
