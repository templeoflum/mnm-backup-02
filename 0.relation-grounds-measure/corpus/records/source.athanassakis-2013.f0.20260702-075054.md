Apostolos N. Athanassakis and Benjamin M. Wolkow, *The Orphic Hymns* (Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013). Translation of the complete collection of 87 late-antique Orphic hymns to the gods, with introduction and extensive notes on the deities, epithets, and ritual context; a reworking of Athanassakis's earlier 1977 translation. Foundational as the standard scholarly English text of the hymns used in Orphic and mystery-cult ritual.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Greek Mysteries. Access note: an older public-domain translation exists — Thomas Taylor, *The Hymns of Orpheus* (1792), freely available online (e.g. Internet Archive, sacred-texts); Taylor's version is loose and Neoplatonizing. **[confirmed as attribution]**
