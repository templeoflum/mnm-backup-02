**statement (plain)** — Escher's Relativity is a 1953 lithograph by M.C. Escher that depicts an architectural space with three distinct gravitational orientations coexisting impossibly within a single structure. The artwork shows multiple human figures inhabiting a building with interconnected staircases and doorways, where each figure appears to experience gravity in a different direction oriented at right angles to the others.
