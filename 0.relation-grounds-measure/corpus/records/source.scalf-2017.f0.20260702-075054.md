Foy Scalf (ed.), *Book of the Dead: Becoming God in Ancient Egypt* (Oriental Institute of the University of Chicago, 2017). Exhibition catalog and essay collection on the Book of the Dead — its history, production, use, and theology of divinization — by a team of Egyptologists, richly illustrated from the OI collections.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Biblical & Ancient Near East. Oriental Institute (now ISAC, University of Chicago) publications are distributed as free open-access PDFs from the institute's website; this volume is in that program. **[confirmed as attribution]**
