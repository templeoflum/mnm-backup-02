**statement (plain)** — **Enki** (Akkadian **Ea**) is the god of fresh water, wisdom, magic, and craft — the cleverest of the gods, dwelling in the **Apsu**, the freshwater deep beneath the earth. He is the friend and, in the tradition, creator of humankind, and its protector: when the gods resolve to destroy humanity by the Flood, it is Enki/Ea who circumvents the decree and warns the flood hero (**Utnapishtim** in Gilgamesh, **Atrahasis** in the Atrahasis epic) to build a boat and survive. His wisdom and cunning solve the problems of gods and men alike.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: fresh water (the **Apsu**), wisdom, magic, incantation, craft, and creation. Culture: Sumerian **Enki**, Akkadian **Ea**. Chief cult centre: the temple **E-abzu** at **Eridu**, the oldest of the Sumerian cities. He is the friend and creator of humankind and the god who warns **Utnapishtim**/**Atrahasis** of the coming Flood, thus preserving humanity. He forms the great triad with Anu and Enlil. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Mesopotamian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested in the flood narrative of the Epic of Gilgamesh [[source.epic-of-gilgamesh]] and treated in the standard reference by Black & Green [[source.black-green-1992]]. His Eridu cult, dominion over the Apsu, and role as the warner of the flood hero are established; the creation-of-humankind and flood traditions are attested and variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
