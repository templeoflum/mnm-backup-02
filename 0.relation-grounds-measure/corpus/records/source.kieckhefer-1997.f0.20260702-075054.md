Richard Kieckhefer, *Forbidden Rites: A Necromancer's Manual of the Fifteenth Century* (Sutton, 1997). A study and Latin edition of the Munich manual of demonic magic (Clm 849) — conjurations, illusions, and divinatory experiments — with Kieckhefer's analysis of clerical necromancy in late medieval Europe.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Medieval Magic sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
