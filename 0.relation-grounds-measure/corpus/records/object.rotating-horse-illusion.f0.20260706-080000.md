**statement (plain)** — The Rotating Horse Illusion is a bistable motion perception phenomenon in which a silhouette of a horse in rotation appears to spontaneously reverse its direction of rotation. The ambiguity arises because the two-dimensional silhouette projection is compatible with two different three-dimensional interpretations of the horse's spatial orientation and rotation direction. Without depth cues such as shading, occlusion, or binocular disparity, the visual system cannot unambiguously determine whether the horse is rotating clockwise or counterclockwise, leading to perceptual alternations between the two interpretations.
