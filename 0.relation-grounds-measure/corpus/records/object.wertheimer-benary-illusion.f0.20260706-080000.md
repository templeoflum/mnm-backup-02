**statement (plain)** — The Wertheimer-Benary Illusion is a lightness illusion demonstrating that perceived brightness is influenced by Gestalt grouping principles and figure-ground organization rather than simply by local luminance ratios. The classic configuration involves a cross or similar geometric figure with identical gray triangular targets positioned in different locations: one on the main body of the figure and one in a notch or adjacent region. Despite identical luminance and similar surrounding regions, the targets appear different in lightness.

**statement (framework)** — category: brightness lightness contrast; perceptual mechanism(s): lightness-computation, figure-ground-segregation, contour-integration.
