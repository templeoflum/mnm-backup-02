J. J. Oppel, "Ueber geometrisch-optische Täuschungen," *Jahresbericht des physikalischen Vereins zu Frankfurt am Main* (1854–1855), 37–47.

The paper that coined the term *geometrisch-optische Täuschungen* (geometrical-optical illusions) and examined a range of size/orientation distortions, including the filled-space effect later systematized by A. Kundt (hence "Oppel-Kundt illusion"). **[confirmed]** — title, venue, year, and pages (37–47) agree across the PMC translation-and-commentary (Wade, Todorović, Phillips & Lingelbach, 2017) and the Sage *Perception* biographical article on Oppel. Primary (German) not opened; a scholarly English translation exists.
