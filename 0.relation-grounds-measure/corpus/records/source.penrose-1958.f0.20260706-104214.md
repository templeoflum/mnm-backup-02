L. S. Penrose & R. Penrose, "Impossible Objects: A Special Type of Visual Illusion," *British Journal of Psychology* 49, no. 1 (1958): 31–33 — introduces the **Penrose triangle** (tribar) and the **Penrose stairs** (endless staircase), locally consistent but globally impossible figures. A copy sent to Escher inspired his *Ascending and Descending* (1960) and *Waterfall* (1961). **[confirmed]** (venue + pages + DOI web-verified.)
