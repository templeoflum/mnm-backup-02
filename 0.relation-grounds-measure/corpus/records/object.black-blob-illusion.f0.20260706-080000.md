**statement (plain)** — The Black Blob Illusion is a visual phenomenon in which observers experience difficulty perceiving, individuating, or accurately counting black shapes or regions against certain backgrounds. This illusion highlights limitations in the visual system's ability to segment and enumerate low-contrast or homogeneous dark regions, particularly when figure-ground relationships are ambiguous. Unlike positive-contrast objects that benefit from edge detection and lateral inhibition mechanisms, dark regions may fail to generate strong segmentation signals, leading to perceptual grouping failures or underestimation of numerosity.
