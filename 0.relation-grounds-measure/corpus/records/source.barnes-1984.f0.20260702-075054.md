Jonathan Barnes (ed.), *The Complete Works of Aristotle: The Revised Oxford Translation*, 2 vols (Princeton University Press, 1984). The standard complete English Aristotle — the early-20th-century Oxford Translation revised under Barnes, covering the whole corpus including fragments. Foundational as the default scholarly citation edition of Aristotle in English; one record covers both volumes.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Classical Philosophy section. Access note: the underlying early-20th-century Oxford Translation volumes are largely public domain and findable online, though unrevised. **[confirmed as attribution]**
