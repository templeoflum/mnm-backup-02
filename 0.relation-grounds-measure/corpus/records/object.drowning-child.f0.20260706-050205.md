**statement (plain)** — You pass a shallow pond where a child is drowning; wading in ruins your expensive shoes. Obviously you save the child. But children die now, in numbers, of preventable causes you could avert with a modest donation. If distance is morally irrelevant, the obligation is the same — give until giving more would cost you something comparable.

**statement (framework)** — an argument from a near-universal intuition to a conclusion almost no one lives by; the cornerstone of **effective altruism**. The debate centres on the **demandingness objection**, the moral relevance of proximity/identifiability, and individual vs. institutional duty. Established; whether the reasoning binds as Singer claims stays **open**.
