E. Wittenberg, M. Paczynski, H. Wiese, R. Jackendoff, G. R. Kuperberg, "The difference between 'giving a rose' and 'giving a kiss': Sustained neural activity to the light verb construction," *Journal of Memory and Language* 73:31–42 (2014).

Role — cross-linguistic ERP support for the underspecified-light-verb account: LVCs "consist of a semantically underspecified light verb ('give') and an event nominal that contributes most of the meaning and also activates an argument structure of its own ('kiss')." Data favor models where both syntactic and semantic compositionality contribute. **[confirmed]**
