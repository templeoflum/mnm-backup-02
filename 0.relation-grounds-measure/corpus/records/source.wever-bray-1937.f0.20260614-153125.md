Ernest Glen Wever & Charles W. Bray, volley-principle work (c. 1930–37); consolidated in E. G. Wever, *Theory of Hearing* (Wiley, 1949).

Role — the **volley principle**: groups of auditory-nerve fibers fire in alternation so the population phase-locks to frequencies higher than any single fiber's refractory rate can follow, supplying the temporal-coding leg of pitch perception.
