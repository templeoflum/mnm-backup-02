M. C. Baker, *Incorporation: A Theory of Grammatical Function Changing* (University of Chicago Press, 1988); the hypothesis restated in "Thematic Roles and Syntactic Structure," in L. Haegeman (ed.), *Elements of Grammar* (Kluwer, 1997).

Role — states UTAH (1988:46): "Identical thematic relationships between items are represented by identical structural relationships between those items at the level of D-Structure." The 1997 paper adopts a "medium-coarse" version with three role prototypes: agent, theme, goal/path/location. **[confirmed]**
