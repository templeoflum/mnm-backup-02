Frederick Copleston, *A History of Philosophy*, vols. 2–3 (Burns Oates & Washbourne, 1950/1953). The medieval volumes of Copleston's eleven-volume history: vol. 2 runs Augustine to Scotus, vol. 3 Ockham to Suárez — a patient, fair-minded narrative that remains the standard long-form guide to the period. One record covers both volumes.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Philosophy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (modern reprints inexpensive and common secondhand). **[confirmed as attribution]**
