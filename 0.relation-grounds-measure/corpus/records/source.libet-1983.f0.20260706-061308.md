Benjamin Libet et al., "Time of Conscious Intention to Act in Relation to Onset of Cerebral Activity (Readiness-Potential)," *Brain* 106, no. 3 (1983): 623–642 — finds the readiness potential begins ~550 ms before movement but conscious awareness of the urge only ~200 ms before, an unconscious lead used to challenge conscious free will. An empirical result (contested in interpretation). **[confirmed as attribution]**
