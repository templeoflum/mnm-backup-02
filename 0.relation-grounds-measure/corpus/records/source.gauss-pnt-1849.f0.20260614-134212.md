C. F. Gauss, letter to J. F. Encke (24 December 1849), published in *Werke*, vol. 2 — Gauss recollects considering the prime-counting density (∼ 1/ln x, the logarithmic integral Li(x)) "in 1792 or 1793," at age 15–16.

Earliest (unpublished-at-the-time) conjecture of the [[object.prime-number-theorem]]; predates [[source.legendre-1798]] but was not published until the letter. **[confirmed]** for the 1792–93 recollection (multi-source); **[confirmed as attribution]** for the Werke citation.
