Lucy A. Suchman, *Plans and Situated Actions: The Problem of Human-Machine Communication* (Cambridge University Press, 1987; 2nd ed. *Human-Machine Reconfigurations*, 2007).

Contains — the foundational argument in CSCW/HCI that **plans are resources for situated action, not scripts that determine it**: people improvise against a plan in the moment, filling underspecified gaps from the concrete situation. Grounds why stigmergic coordination is needed at all — plans (architectural drawings, specs) are "not assembly manuals," so workers must read the material state and fill in the blanks (invoked directly by Christensen 2013) [[source.christensen-2013]].
