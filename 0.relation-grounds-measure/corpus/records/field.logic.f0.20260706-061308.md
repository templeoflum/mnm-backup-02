**origin** — Aristotle, the *Organon* (esp. *Prior Analytics*, c. 350 BCE) — the syllogistic, the first formal theory of valid inference.

**codification** — Gottlob Frege, *Begriffsschrift* (1879) — modern quantificational logic (a formula language for pure thought); extended by Whitehead & Russell's *Principia Mathematica* and the twentieth-century development of proof theory and model theory. [[source.frege-1879]]

**door** — any standard introduction (e.g. Wilfrid Hodges, *Logic*; or a first-order-logic text). Carroll's regress ([[source.carroll-1895]]) marks the boundary where inference rules cease to be further premises.
