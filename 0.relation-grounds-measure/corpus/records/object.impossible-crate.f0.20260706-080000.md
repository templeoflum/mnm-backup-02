**statement (plain)** — The Impossible Crate is an impossible object derived from the Necker cube geometry that depicts a three-dimensional lattice structure with contradictory depth relationships. The figure typically shows a skeletal framework resembling a shipping crate or open box where vertical and horizontal beams connect in ways that cannot be realized in three-dimensional Euclidean space.
