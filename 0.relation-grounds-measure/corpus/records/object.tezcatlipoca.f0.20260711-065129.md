**statement (plain)** — **Tezcatlipoca** ("smoking mirror") is the Aztec/Nahua god of night, sorcery, and destiny, an omnipotent and capricious lord who tests and torments humankind. He sees all things through his obsidian mirror and is patron of rulers and sorcerers, of war and strife. He is the great adversary of **Quetzalcoatl**, whom he drove from Tollan, and is associated with the jaguar and the night sky.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: night, sorcery, destiny, rulership, discord, the jaguar. Nahuatl *Tezcatlipoca*, from *tezcatl* (mirror) and *poca* (smoking) — the obsidian scrying mirror by which he sees. Adversary of Quetzalcoatl; nagual (animal aspect) the jaguar. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the colonial-era codices (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*), recorded after the conquest; Tezcatlipoca's many aspects and titles vary across the sources. **[confirmed as attribution]**
