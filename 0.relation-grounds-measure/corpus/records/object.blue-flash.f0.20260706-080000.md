**statement (plain)** — The Blue Flash is an exceptionally rare atmospheric optical phenomenon closely related to the more commonly observed green flash, but involving the brief appearance of blue or violet light at the uppermost rim of the sun during sunrise or sunset. This phenomenon occurs under the same fundamental atmospheric conditions as the green flash—requiring atmospheric refraction that disperses sunlight into its component wavelengths—but represents an even more extreme manifestation of the effect. The blue flash appears when atmospheric conditions are extraordinarily clear and stable, allowing the shorter blue wavelengths to reach the observer's eye without being scattered away by atmospheric particles.
