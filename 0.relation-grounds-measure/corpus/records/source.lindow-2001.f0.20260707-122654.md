John Lindow, *Norse Mythology: A Guide to Gods, Heroes, Rituals, and Beliefs* (Oxford University Press, 2001). An A–Z scholarly reference: an introductory survey of the sources and the mythological world, followed by encyclopedic entries on the figures, places, and objects, each with source citations and interpretation. The standard modern English door into the field (alongside Rudolf Simek's *Dictionary of Northern Mythology*). In print. **[confirmed as attribution]**
