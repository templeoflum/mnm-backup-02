Kathleen M. Haig, Staci Sutton & John Whittington, "SBAR: A shared mental model for improving communication between clinicians," *Joint Commission Journal on Quality and Patient Safety* 32, no. 3 (2006): 167–175.

Contains — the influential write-up of **SBAR** (Situation–Background–Assessment–Recommendation), a standardized template for handover and escalation communication, adapted from US Navy nuclear-submarine practice to healthcare. By fixing the order and content of what is conveyed, SBAR builds a shared mental model between the giver and receiver of a handoff and reduces omitted critical information. A concrete instance of standardized handover.
