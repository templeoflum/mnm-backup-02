**statement (plain)** — Lull's *ars combinatoria*: concentric revolving wheels bearing letters standing for divine attributes/notions, whose mechanical recombination was to generate and demonstrate all connections — a finite set of primary elements recombined to map metaphysical relation. Eco reads it (with Kabbalistic letter-combination) as a combinatorial relation-web in the perfect-language quest, feeding forward to Leibniz's symbolic logic. *(status `doctrinal`: Lull's generative claim bracketed; the historical line established.)* **[confirmed]**
