**origin** — control-theory precursor James Clerk Maxwell, "On Governors," *Proc. R. Soc. London* (1868) — first mathematical treatment of feedback; Wiener credits this paper and the shared Greek root (*kybernetes*, steersman). [[source.maxwell-1868]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Norbert Wiener, *Cybernetics: Or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine* (MIT Press / Hermann / Wiley, 1948) — first public use of "cybernetics," synthesizing the Macy Conferences (1946–53; "cybernetics" entered the conference title only at the 7th meeting, March 1950, after the book). [[source.wiener-1948]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — *Cybernetics* (1961 2nd ed.) open access from MIT Press (direct.mit.edu); companion *The Human Use of Human Beings* (1950). [[source.wiener-1948]]
