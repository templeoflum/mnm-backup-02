**statement (plain)** — A billionaire pays a million dollars if, at midnight, you sincerely intend to drink a harmless toxin tomorrow afternoon — verified by a brain scan. You need not actually drink; the money is yours at midnight regardless. Yet knowing you'll have no reason to drink tomorrow, can you sincerely intend to now?

**statement (framework)** — a puzzle about the **rationality of intentions** vs. actions: it is rational to *want* to intend, but a fully rational agent who foresees she won't act seemingly can't sincerely intend. Bears on deterrence, promising, and self-binding (kin to Newcomb's problem). Established; **open**.
