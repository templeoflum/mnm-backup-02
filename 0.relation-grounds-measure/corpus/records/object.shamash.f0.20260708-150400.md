**statement (plain)** — **Shamash** (Sumerian **Utu**) is the sun god and the god of justice, law, and divination. Because the sun crosses the whole sky and sees everything, he is the all-seeing judge who upholds truth and law, protects the traveller, and reveals the future through divination. He is the brother of **Ishtar**/**Inanna**. In the **Epic of Gilgamesh** he is the patron who aids and encourages Gilgamesh and Enkidu, notably in their expedition against **Humbaba** in the Cedar Forest.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the sun, justice, law, truth, and divination. Culture: Sumerian **Utu**, Akkadian **Shamash**. Chief cult centres: the temple **Ebabbar** at **Sippar** and at **Larsa**. He is the all-seeing upholder of law and brother of **Ishtar**/**Inanna**. In Gilgamesh he acts as patron and protector of the heroes, aiding them against Humbaba. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Mesopotamian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested in the Epic of Gilgamesh [[source.epic-of-gilgamesh]] and treated in the standard reference by Black & Green [[source.black-green-1992]]. His Sippar and Larsa cults, solar-justice domain, and kinship with Ishtar/Inanna are established; his narrative role is attested tradition, variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
