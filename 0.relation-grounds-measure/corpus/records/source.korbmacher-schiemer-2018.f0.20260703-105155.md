Johannes Korbmacher & Georg Schiemer, "What Are Structural Properties?," *Philosophia Mathematica* 26, no. 3 (2018): 295–323.

Contains — two formal accounts of what makes a property "structural": the **definability** account (structural = definable purely in terms of a theory's primitive relations) and the **invariance** account (structural = invariant under isomorphism / holding of all structurally similar objects). Their key result: the two do **not** pick out the same class of properties. Connects to the Carnap thesis (structural ⇔ isomorphism-invariant extension) and the Tarski–Sher thesis for logicality (permutation-invariance).
