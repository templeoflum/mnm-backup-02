**origin** — William Moulton Marston's *Emotions of Normal People* (1928) proposed axes of behavior; later authors and the commercial DISC assessments turned it into the four-style instrument. [[source.marston-1928]]

**codification** — modern DISC is a family of commercial assessments built on Marston's quadrant.

**door** — Marston, *Emotions of Normal People* (1928), as the theoretical origin.

**standing** — popular in business/coaching; **Marston himself built no assessment**, and the commercial instruments' validity is contested. Held `doctrinal`.
