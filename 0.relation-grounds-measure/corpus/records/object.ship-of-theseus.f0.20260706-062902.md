**statement (plain)** — Theseus's ship is preserved by replacing each decayed plank until none of the original remains. Is it still the same ship? Hobbes sharpens it: gather the discarded planks and rebuild — now there are two ships. Which is the real Ship of Theseus, the continuously repaired one or the reassembled original?

**statement (framework)** — the founding puzzle of **identity and persistence over time**: is a thing individuated by its matter, its form, spatiotemporal continuity, function, or convention? Every major metaphysics of persistence — hylomorphism, mereological essentialism, four-dimensionalism — is in part a response. Established; **open**.
