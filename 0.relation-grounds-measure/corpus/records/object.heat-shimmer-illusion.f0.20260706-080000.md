**statement (plain)** — The heat shimmer illusion, also known as heat haze or heat wave distortion, is an atmospheric optical phenomenon characterized by shimmering, wavering distortions of distant objects viewed across surfaces heated by solar radiation. This effect results from turbulent mixing of air layers with different temperatures and thus different refractive indices near heated surfaces such as asphalt roads, desert sand, or metal roofs. The turbulent convection creates chaotic, rapidly fluctuating variations in air density, causing light rays traveling through this region to follow constantly changing paths, producing the characteristic undulating, rippling appearance of objects viewed through the disturbed air.
