Aristotle, *Physics* Book II (esp. II.3 on the four causes; II.8 199a on art and nature). **[confirmed]**

Role - the four causes (material, formal, efficient, final), illustrated by the sculptor/statue and builder/house; and the craft analogy: "generally technè in some cases completes what nature cannot bring to a finish, and in others imitates nature" (II.8, 199a15). The house/builder model makes craft the lens on natural production - the source for [[object.four-causes]] and [[object.nature-as-craftsman]].
