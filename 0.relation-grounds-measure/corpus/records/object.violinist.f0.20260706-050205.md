**statement (plain)** — You wake surgically connected to a famous violinist whose life now depends on using your kidneys for nine months; you were kidnapped and never consented. Must you stay connected? Thomson: even his right to life does not give him the right to use your body without consent.

**statement (framework)** — isolates **bodily autonomy** from the personhood question by granting the fetus full personhood *for argument* and denying that the anti-abortion conclusion follows; a landmark in applied ethics on rights, consent, and Good-Samaritan duties. Established; its bearing on the abortion debate stays **open**.
