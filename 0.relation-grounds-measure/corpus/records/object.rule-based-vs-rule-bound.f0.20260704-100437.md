**statement (plain)** - two ways to relate to the rules of a practice. *Rule-bound*: the rules are followed rigidly and mechanically - the recipe as immutable truth, good only for the standard case. *Rule-based*: the rules are a foundation experts amend or break with interpretive freedom, so the practice stays "live, dynamic, and evolving." A novice baker treats the recipe as law; an expert reads it as a starting point and annotates "slap and fold" vs "stretch and fold" by dough hydration.

**basis** - the distinction is Abbott's (*The System of Professions*), applied to craft documentation by Batra et al. (2025), who use it to explain why static step-lists fail to transmit craft.
