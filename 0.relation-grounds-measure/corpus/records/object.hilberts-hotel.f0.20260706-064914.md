**statement (plain)** — A hotel has a room for every natural number, all occupied. A new guest arrives: move each occupant from room n to n+1, freeing room 1. A countably infinite busload arrives: move each occupant to room 2n, freeing all odd rooms. Even infinitely many infinite buses fit, by a pairing trick.

**statement (framework)** — an illustration of **countable infinity**: a proper subset can be put in one-to-one correspondence with the whole (Dedekind-infinity). Makes cardinal arithmetic of ℵ₀ vivid; a staple of set-theory pedagogy. Established.

**basis** — Hilbert's 1924 lectures, popularized in Gamow's *One Two Three... Infinity* (1947) [[source.gamow-1947]]. **[confirmed as attribution]**
