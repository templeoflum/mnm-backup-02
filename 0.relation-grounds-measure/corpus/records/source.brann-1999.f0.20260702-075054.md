Noel L. Brann, *Trithemius and Magical Theology: A Chapter in the Controversy over Occult Studies in Early Modern Europe* (SUNY Press, 1999). Scholarly study of Abbot Johannes Trithemius — teacher-figure behind Agrippa's circle — and his attempt to reconcile angelic magic with Christian theology, set within the early modern debate over the legitimacy of occult studies.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
