E. Verlinde, "On the Origin of Gravity and the Laws of Newton," arXiv:1001.0785; *JHEP* 04 (2011) 029.

Role — proposes gravity is not fundamental but an entropic force arising from changes in information associated with the positions of bodies on holographic screens (one bit per Planck area); Newton's law and, relativistically, the Einstein equations emerge. Builds on 't Hooft's holographic principle and Jacobson's 1995 thermodynamic derivation. **[confirmed]**
