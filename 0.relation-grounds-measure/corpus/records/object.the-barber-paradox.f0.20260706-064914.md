**statement (plain)** — A town's barber shaves all and only those residents who do not shave themselves. Does the barber shave himself? If he does, he shouldn't (he only shaves non-self-shavers); if he doesn't, he must. Contradiction.

**statement (framework)** — Russell's homely illustration of **Russell's paradox**; unlike the set-theoretic version, it is dissolved simply by concluding no such barber can exist (the specification is unsatisfiable). A teaching bridge to self-reference and unrestricted comprehension. Established.
