Steven French & James Ladyman, "Remodelling Structural Realism: Quantum Physics and the Metaphysics of Structure," *Synthese* 136, no. 1 (2003): 31–56. DOI: 10.1023/A:1024156116636.

Contains: an articulation and defence of the *ontic* (metaphysical) form of structural realism. Reconceives objects in structural terms — using quantum statistics and the underdetermination of particle individuality to argue that the realist's best option is to take structure, rather than self-subsistent objects, as fundamental. Replies to objections raised against the earlier epistemic formulations.
