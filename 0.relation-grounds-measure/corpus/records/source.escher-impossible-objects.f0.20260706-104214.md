M. C. Escher's impossible-architecture lithographs: *Relativity* (1953, multiple mutually-inconsistent gravities), *Belvedere* (1958, an impossible cuboid building), *Ascending and Descending* (1960, the endless monks' staircase) and *Waterfall* (1961, a self-feeding watercourse). The last two were inspired by Penrose & Penrose (1958) [[source.penrose-1958]]; *Belvedere* draws on Necker-cube ambiguity. Primary works are the prints; catalogued in Bruno Ernst, *The Magic Mirror of M. C. Escher* (1976). **[confirmed as attribution]** (artwork titles + years documented.)
