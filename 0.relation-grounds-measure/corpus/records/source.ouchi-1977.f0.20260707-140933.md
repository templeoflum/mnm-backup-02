H. Ouchi, *Japanese Optical and Geometrical Art*, Dover Publications, New York, 1977 (Dover Pictorial Archive; ISBN 0-486-23553-X).

A collection of 746 geometric/optical designs. It contains the orthogonally-tiled disk-on-background figure that induces illusory relative motion of the central region under eye movement; the pattern was later isolated, named the "Ouchi illusion," and studied scientifically (notably Spillmann et al., 1993 — a separate source not created here). **[confirmed]** — the book (author, title, publisher, 1977) is verified across multiple booksellers, the Internet Archive scan, and Wolfram MathWorld. Caveat on attribution: Ouchi published the *pattern as art*, not as a described illusion — the illusion-naming is downstream; the object record's "Hajime Ouchi (1977)" attribution points to this book as the pattern's first appearance.
