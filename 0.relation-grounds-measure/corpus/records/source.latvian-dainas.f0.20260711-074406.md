The Latvian *dainas* are the vast corpus of traditional Latvian folk-songs — short, formulaic quatrains transmitted orally over centuries; the foundational collection is Krišjānis Barons's *Latvju dainas* (1894–1915). They are the primary oral source in which the Baltic deities — the sun-goddess Saulė/Saule, the fate-goddess Laima, the moon Mēness — survive, chiefly as figures of the solar mythology and the round of daily and seasonal life. As folk-song rather than doctrinal text, they preserve mythic imagery in fragmentary and poetic form. **[confirmed as attribution]**
