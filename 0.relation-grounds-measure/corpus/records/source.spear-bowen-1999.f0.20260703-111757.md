Steven Spear & H. Kent Bowen, "Decoding the DNA of the Toyota Production System," *Harvard Business Review* 77, no. 5 (Sept–Oct 1999): 96–106.

Contains — the influential distillation of TPS into four tacit rules: (1) all work is highly *specified* as to content, sequence, timing, and outcome; (2) every customer–supplier connection is direct and binary; (3) the pathway for every product/service is simple and direct; (4) improvement is made by the people doing the work, under a teacher, using the scientific method. Resolves the paradox that rigid specification (standardized work) is what *enables* continuous improvement — because a standard makes deviations visible as experiments.
