**statement (plain)** - you don't compute where your hand is; you just know, and reach. Merleau-Ponty calls this the body schema - "neither an idea nor a physiological-physical fact" but "a practical diagram of our relationships with the world," a sub-reflective norm that organizes posture and the perceptual field toward habitual or novel action, context-sensitively. It is how the body is present to itself in doing without being an object of thought.

**basis** - Merleau-Ponty (1945); Cambridge readings (Romdenh-Romluc, Mooney) gloss it as sub-reflective and horizonal. **[confirmed]** Grounds [[object.motor-intentionality]] (the body schema is what makes skilled motor intentionality possible). [[source.merleau-ponty-1945]]
