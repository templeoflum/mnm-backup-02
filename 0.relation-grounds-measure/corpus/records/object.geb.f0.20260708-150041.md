**statement (plain)** — **Geb** is the earth god, his body forming the surface of the earth. He is the son of **Shu** (air) and **Tefnut** (moisture), and the brother-husband of **Nut**, the sky; from their union come **Osiris**, **Isis**, **Set**, and **Nephthys**. He is often depicted reclining beneath the arched Nut, and his laughter was said to cause earthquakes. He belongs to the Heliopolitan **Ennead**.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the earth. Iconography: a reclining god forming the ground beneath the sky-goddess Nut, sometimes shown with green skin or vegetation. Family: son of **Shu** and **Tefnut**; brother-husband of **Nut**; father of **Osiris**, **Isis**, **Set**, and **Nephthys**; a member of the Heliopolitan **Ennead**. His laughter is associated with earthquakes. No standard *interpretatio graeca* is established. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Egyptian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested from the Pyramid Texts [[source.pyramid-texts]] onward and present in the Book of the Dead [[source.book-of-the-dead]]; the standard reference treatment is Wilkinson [[source.wilkinson-2003]]. His identity as the earth and his place in the Ennead are established; his genealogy and the earthquake association are attested tradition, variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
