Simon Goss, Serge Aron, Jean-Louis Deneubourg, and Jacques M. Pasteels, "Self-organized shortcuts in the Argentine ant," *Naturwissenschaften* 76, no. 12 (1989): 579–581.

Contains — the **double-bridge experiment**: given two bridges of different length between nest and food, a colony of Argentine ants (*Linepithema humile*) converges on the shorter path — not by measuring it, but because ants traverse the short branch faster, so pheromone accumulates there faster and recruits more ants (positive feedback). The seminal empirical demonstration that pheromone-trail dynamics alone yield shortest-path selection; the direct antecedent of Ant Colony Optimization [[object.ant-colony-optimization]].
