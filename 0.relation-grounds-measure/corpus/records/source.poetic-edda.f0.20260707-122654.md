The **Poetic Edda** (Elder Edda) — a collection of alliterative Old Norse poems preserved chiefly in the Icelandic *Codex Regius* (c. 1270), the poems themselves drawn from older oral tradition. The mythological poems (*Völuspá*, *Hávamál*, *Grímnismál*, *Vafþrúðnismál*, *Lokasenna*, *Þrymskviða*, and others) are the foundational source for the myths of the Aesir and Vanir; the heroic poems carry the Völsung–Nibelung matter. Anonymous, no single author. Public-domain translations (Bellows 1923, Thorpe) on archive.org; standard modern translation Carolyne Larrington (Oxford World's Classics). **[confirmed as attribution]**
