**statement (plain)** — Lightning strikes both ends of a moving train. A platform observer midway sees the flashes arrive together and calls them simultaneous. The train observer, midway on the train, moves toward the front flash and away from the rear; receiving the front flash first (light speed being equal for all), she calls the front strike earlier.

**statement (framework)** — the demonstration that **simultaneity is relative** — there is no absolute 'now' across space; the ordering of spacelike-separated events depends on the frame. Overturned absolute time and grounded the Lorentz transformation. Established.
