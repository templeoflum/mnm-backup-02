Ronald Hutton, *The Witch: A History of Fear, from Ancient Times to the Present* (Yale University Press, 2017). A long-durée and cross-cultural history of the witch figure, setting the European trials against global ethnography, ancient precedents, and folklore continuities. Foundational as a wide-frame synthesis connecting the early-modern hunts to deeper and broader patterns of witch-fear.
