The **Four Branches of the Mabinogi** (c. 12th–13th c.). The medieval Welsh prose tales — the four linked "branches" (*Pwyll*, *Branwen*, *Manawydan*, *Math*) telling of figures such as **Pwyll**, **Rhiannon**, **Branwen**, **Bendigeidfran** (Brân the Blessed), and **Math** — preserved in the **Red Book of Hergest** and the **White Book of Rhydderch**. The central narrative body of Welsh mythic tradition, drawing on older material. **[confirmed as attribution]**
