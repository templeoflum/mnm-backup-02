**statement (plain)** — The Fraser Spiral Illusion, also known as the Twisted Cord Illusion or False Spiral, is a striking geometric illusion in which a series of concentric circles appears to form an outward-spiraling pattern. Discovered by British psychologist James Fraser in 1908, the illusion is created by overlaying tilted or rotated elements—typically small line segments, arcs, or checkered patterns—onto circular arcs. Despite the underlying structure being perfectly circular, observers reliably perceive a continuous spiral that seems to wind outward from the center.
