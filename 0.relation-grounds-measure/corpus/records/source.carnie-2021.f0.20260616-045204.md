**verbatim citation** — Carnie, Andrew. *Syntax: A Generative Introduction*. 4th ed. Hoboken, NJ: Wiley-Blackwell, 2021.

**what it contains** — the leading introductory textbook on theoretical (generative) syntax. Builds phrase structure, X-bar theory, the lexicon, binding, case, and movement from first principles, with extensive exercises and an accompanying workbook. Presents recursion explicitly: rules that reference their own category (a clause inside a clause, a noun phrase inside a noun phrase) yielding unbounded hierarchical structure from finite rules. Used as the door — the canonical place a newcomer learns the machinery in which recursion-in-language lives.

**access notes** — 4th ed. published April 20, 2021; earlier editions (1st 2002, 2nd 2007, 3rd 2013) are commonly cited. Companion site at andrewcarnie.org.
