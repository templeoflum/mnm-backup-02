Mikael Agricola's verse preface to his Finnish Psalter (*Dauidin Psaltari*, 1551) lists the pagan gods worshipped by the Häme (Tavastians) and the Karelians. Written to condemn the surviving heathen cults, it names deities including Ukko and Tapio among others, and is the earliest documentary catalogue of Finnish deities. Because Agricola was a Reformation bishop compiling the list from a hostile Christian standpoint, its terse entries are the primary sixteenth-century witness to the pre-Christian pantheon. **[confirmed as attribution]**
