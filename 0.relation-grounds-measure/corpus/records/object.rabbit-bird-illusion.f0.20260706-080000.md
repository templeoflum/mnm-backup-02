**statement (plain)** — The Rabbit-Bird Illusion, also known as the "Duck-Rabbit" or "Rabbit-Duck" illusion in its most famous variant, is an ambiguous figure that can be perceived as either a rabbit or a bird depending on how visual features are organized and interpreted. In the classic version, the same contours can represent either a rabbit facing left (with long ears extending backward) or a bird facing right (with the "ears" reinterpreted as a beak). This bistable image demonstrates how identical sensory input can support radically different semantic interpretations based on alternative groupings of visual features.
