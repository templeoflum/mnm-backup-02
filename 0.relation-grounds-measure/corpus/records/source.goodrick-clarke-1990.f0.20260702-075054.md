Nicholas Goodrick-Clarke (ed. and trans.), *Paracelsus: Essential Readings* (Crucible, 1990). An anthology of selections from Paracelsus's medical, alchemical, and theological writings in English translation, with an introductory overview of his life and thought. Foundational as the standard accessible entry point to Paracelsus's own words.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Alchemy. The list gives no editor; verified this session as selected and translated by Nicholas Goodrick-Clarke, first published Crucible 1990 (the 1999 North Atlantic Books printing is a reissue — handle year corrected from 1999 to 1990). Acquisition path: the 1999 reissue is available for controlled lending on the Internet Archive; otherwise an in-print book. **[confirmed]**
