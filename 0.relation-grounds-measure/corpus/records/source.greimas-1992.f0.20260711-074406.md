Algirdas Julien Greimas's *Of Gods and Men: Studies in Lithuanian Mythology* (Indiana University Press, 1992; French original 1979) applies structural-semiotic method to reconstruct the Lithuanian pantheon and its myths from fragmentary attestations and folk sources. Working figure by figure, it recovers deities such as Perkūnas and their narrative roles as a coherent system. Its reconstructions are inferential and method-driven, and should be read as scholarly reconstruction rather than direct attestation. **[confirmed as attribution]**
