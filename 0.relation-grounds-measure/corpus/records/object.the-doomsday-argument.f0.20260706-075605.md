**statement (plain)** — You are roughly the 100-billionth human. If trillions more will be born, your early rank is wildly atypical; if only a few hundred billion will, it's ordinary. Since a typical position is more probable than an atypical one, Bayesian updating on your birth rank favors a smaller total future population — an earlier 'doom.'

**statement (framework)** — a controversial **anthropic / self-locating** probability argument (Carter, Leslie, Gott's Copernican method). Attacked on the reference-class problem, the choice of prior, and self-sampling assumptions (SSA vs. SIA), yet hard to refute by consensus. A cornerstone of observation-selection theory. Established; **open**.
