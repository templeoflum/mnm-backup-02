**statement (plain)** — The Breathing Light Illusion is a dynamic visual phenomenon in which a static luminance pattern appears to pulsate or breathe rhythmically, creating the impression of periodic expansions and contractions in brightness or intensity. Despite the objective stability of the stimulus, observers perceive slow, wave-like modulations that give the appearance of the pattern inhaling and exhaling. This illusory motion typically manifests as periodic changes in perceived brightness, size, or both, with the rhythm of the perceived breathing varying across different stimulus configurations and viewing conditions.
