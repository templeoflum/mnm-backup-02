Mother Columba Hart (trans.), *Hadewijch: The Complete Works* (Paulist Press, 1980). The complete surviving corpus of the thirteenth-century Brabant beguine — visions, letters, poems in stanzas, and poems in couplets — the major monument of Middle Dutch love-mysticism (*minne*).

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
