James P. Allen, *The Ancient Egyptian Pyramid Texts* (Society of Biblical Literature, 2005). Complete modern translation of the Pyramid Texts — the oldest substantial body of religious writing in the world, the Old Kingdom royal spells for the king's ascent among the gods — organized pyramid by pyramid.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Biblical & Ancient Near East. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
