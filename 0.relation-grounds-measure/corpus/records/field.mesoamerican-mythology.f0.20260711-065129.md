**origin** — the religious traditions of Mesoamerica: the **Aztec/Nahua** deities of central Mexico and the **Maya** of the Yucatán and highlands, sharing a culture-area's cosmology — the feathered serpent, the sacred 260-day calendar, human sacrifice, and successive world-ages. **[confirmed]**

**codification** — the colonial-era records: Bernardino de **Sahagún's** *Florentine Codex* (c. 1577) for the Aztec pantheon, and the K'iche' Maya **Popol Vuh** (16th-c. manuscript) for the Maya creation. [[source.sahagun-florentine-codex]] [[source.popol-vuh]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Mary Miller & Karl Taube, *The Gods and Symbols of Ancient Mexico and the Maya* (Thames & Hudson, 1993), the standard modern reference to both pantheons. [[source.miller-taube-1993]]
