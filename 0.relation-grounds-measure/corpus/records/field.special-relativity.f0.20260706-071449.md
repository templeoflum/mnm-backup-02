**origin** — Albert Einstein, "On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies" (1905) — two postulates (the principle of relativity; the constancy of light speed) yield the relativity of simultaneity, time dilation, and length contraction. [[source.einstein-1905]]

**codification** — Hermann Minkowski's spacetime (1908), unifying space and time into a four-dimensional geometry with an invariant interval.

**door** — Taylor & Wheeler, *Spacetime Physics* — the standard first course.
