**statement (plain)** — what holds space together? The conjecture: entanglement. Dial down the entanglement between two halves of the boundary description and the spacetime between the corresponding bulk regions stretches and pinches off — geometry is the macroscopic face of a quantum-information structure.

**basis** — Van Raamsdonk's essay argument runs on the Ryu–Takayanagi formula: as mutual information between boundary regions goes to zero, the minimal-surface areas force the bulk regions apart. [[source.van-raamsdonk-2010]] **[confirmed]** (as the content of the conjecture) — Kin: [[object.er-epr]] identifies entanglement with wormhole connection; [[object.entanglement-renormalization]] gives the constructive tensor-network picture; the 2024 Jiang et al. preprint claims a quantitative realization ([[source.jiang-2024]]).
