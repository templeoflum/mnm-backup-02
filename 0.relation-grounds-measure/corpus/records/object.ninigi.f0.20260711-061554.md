**statement (plain)** — **Ninigi-no-Mikoto** is the grandson of **Amaterasu**, sent down by her in the **tenson kōrin** ("descent of the heavenly grandson") to rule the land. He carried the three imperial regalia — the mirror, the sword **Kusanagi**, and the jewel — and married **Konohanasakuya-hime**. Through him descends the Japanese imperial line.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: divine descent, sacred kingship, agriculture, the imperial line. He is the heavenly grandson whose descent founds the earthly rule of the imperial house. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Ninigi is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant accounts of the descent and the regalia; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
