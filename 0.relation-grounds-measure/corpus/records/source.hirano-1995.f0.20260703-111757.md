Hiroyuki Hirano, *5 Pillars of the Visual Workplace: The Sourcebook for 5S Implementation* (Productivity Press, 1995).

Contains — the canonical implementation guide to **5S**, the five workplace-organization disciplines: *seiri* (sort — remove the unneeded), *seiton* (set in order — a place for everything), *seiso* (shine — clean and inspect), *seiketsu* (standardize — make the first three routine), and *shitsuke* (sustain — hold the discipline). Frames 5S as the foundation of the visual workplace and of the wider TPS disciplines, including close-of-shift order.
