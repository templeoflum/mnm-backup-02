Richard Kieckhefer, *Magic in the Middle Ages* (Cambridge University Press, 1989). The standard survey of magic in medieval Europe — the common tradition of charms and remedies, learned astral and image magic, necromancy in the clerical underworld, and the church's shifting responses — framing magic as a crossroads of religion, science, and popular culture.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Medieval Magic & Necromancy sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
