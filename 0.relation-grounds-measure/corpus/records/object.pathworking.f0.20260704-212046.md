**statement (plain)** — modern occult *pathworking*: visualizing oneself traveling the 22 paths between sefirot on the [[object.tree-of-life]], engaging each path's Hebrew letter, Tarot trump, and astrological/elemental attribution. **A late-19th/20th-century development (Golden Dawn line), not medieval Kabbalah** — the medieval scholarship documents contemplative use, ascent motifs (Heikhalot/Merkavah), and Chajes's mimetic *tikkun*, which is the scholarly anchor for "the Tree is used, not merely viewed." *(status `doctrinal`.)*

**basis** — rigorous treatment falls to Western-esotericism studies (Hanegraaff, Faivre), not Kabbalah philology; the dive's own gap-flag carried honest: citable scholarly description of pathworking specifically is thin, the detailed accounts are practitioner sources. **[confirmed as attribution]** for the practice's detail; the modern-not-medieval distinction **[confirmed]**.
