**statement (plain)** — Imagine an intellect that knows, at one instant, the exact position and momentum of every particle in the universe, and all the forces. For it, past and future would be equally present to its eyes: it could compute everything. Nothing would be uncertain.

**statement (framework)** — the sharpest statement of **causal determinism** in classical physics. Challenged by thermodynamic irreversibility, chaos (sensitive dependence makes prediction infeasible in practice), and quantum indeterminacy (which undercuts it in principle); also by the computational impossibility of the demon itself. Established as an image; determinism's truth stays **open**.

**basis** — Laplace's *Essai philosophique sur les probabilités* (1814) [[source.laplace-1814]]. **[confirmed as attribution]**
