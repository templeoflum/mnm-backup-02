Ruth Majercik, *The Chaldean Oracles: Text, Translation, and Commentary* (Brill, 1989). Greek text with facing English translation of the fragmentary Chaldean Oracles — the second-century hexameter revelations attributed to Julian the Theurgist — plus introduction and fragment-by-fragment commentary covering their theology and their use in Neoplatonic theurgy. Foundational as the standard English scholarly edition of a text central to late-antique theurgy and to later Western esoteric currents.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Greek Mysteries. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (Brill; later reprinted by Prometheus Trust). **[confirmed as attribution]**
