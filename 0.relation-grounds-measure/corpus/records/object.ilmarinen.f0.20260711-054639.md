**statement (plain)** — **Ilmarinen** is the divine smith of the Kalevala, the eternal hammerer who forged the dome of the sky itself. He is the maker of magical artifacts, and above all of the **Sampo**, the mill of endless prosperity that grinds out grain, salt, and gold. He forges the Sampo for the North to win the hand of the **Maiden of Pohjola**.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: smithcraft, sky-forging, craft. His name connects to *ilma* (air, weather), suggesting an older link to sky and air — an etymological reading that is proposed rather than certain. His marriage to the Maiden of Pohjola ended in her death, after which he attempted to forge a bride of gold and silver. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Ilmarinen is attested throughout the Kalevala and treated in modern scholarship ([[source.pentikainen-1999]]); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The proposed connection of his name to *ilma* is a scholarly etymology and should be held as such. The Kalevala is Elias Lönnrot's nineteenth-century compilation of oral folk poetry, not an ancient text. **[confirmed as attribution]**
