Leonhard Euler, "Recherches sur une nouvelle espèce de quarrés magiques" (Enestrøm E530), published 1782 (read/written 1779).

Role — combinatorial origin of [[object.latin-square]]: Euler studied squares in which symbols are arranged so each appears once per row and column (and the Graeco-Latin / orthogonal case), the source of the "Latin square" terminology. Predates by ~140 years its use in [[field.experimental-design]] by Fisher.
