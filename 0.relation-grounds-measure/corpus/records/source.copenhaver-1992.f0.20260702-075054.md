Brian P. Copenhaver, *Hermetica: The Greek Corpus Hermeticum and the Latin Asclepius in a New English Translation, with Notes and Introduction* (Cambridge University Press, 1992). The standard modern scholarly English translation of the philosophical Hermetica, with a substantial introduction and dense notes drawing on Festugière and Nock-Festugière. Foundational as the default citation text for the Corpus Hermeticum in English.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Hermeticism section. Access note: an older public-domain translation of the Corpus Hermeticum exists — G. R. S. Mead, *Thrice-Greatest Hermes* (1906) — freely available online, though dated. **[confirmed as attribution]**
