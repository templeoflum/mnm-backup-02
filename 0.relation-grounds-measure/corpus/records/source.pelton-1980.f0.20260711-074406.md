Robert D. Pelton's *The Trickster in West Africa: A Study of Mythic Irony and Sacred Delight* (University of California Press, 1980) is the standard scholarly study of the West African trickster, treating Anansi of the Akan alongside other regional figures. It reads the trickster's cunning and boundary-crossing as sacred play, and situates the Anansi tales within the wider mythology of the region. **[confirmed as attribution]**
