Marvin Meyer and Richard Smith (eds.), *Ancient Christian Magic: Coptic Texts of Ritual Power* (HarperSanFrancisco, 1994). A translated anthology of Coptic spells, amulets, curses, and healing rites from Christian Egypt, framed as "texts of ritual power." Foundational as the standard English sourcebook for Christian magical practice in late antiquity.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (Princeton reissue). **[confirmed as attribution]**
