Frank Tobin (trans.), *Henry Suso: The Exemplar, with Two German Sermons* (Paulist Press, 1989). The fourteenth-century Dominican's own edition of his works — the *Life of the Servant*, *Little Book of Eternal Wisdom*, *Little Book of Truth*, and letters — Eckhart's disciple, joining Rhineland apophaticism to intensely devotional passion-piety.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
