**origin** — the oral tradition of the **Akan** (Ashanti) of Ghana: the sky-god **Nyame** and the spider-trickster **Anansi**, whose stories (**Anansesem**) are the great vehicle of Akan wit and wisdom. **[confirmed]**

**codification** — R. S. **Rattray**, *Akan-Ashanti Folk-Tales* (1930), the classic recorded collection of the Anansi tales in Twi and English. [[source.rattray-1930]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Robert D. Pelton, *The Trickster in West Africa* (1980), the standard scholarly study of Anansi and the West African trickster. [[source.pelton-1980]]

*grade b — origin + codification (Rattray), door (Pelton) present; a single-figure field for now (Anansi), extensible by a fuller Akan pass. Under [[field.comparative-mythology]]. Grounded per the operator's signed word (20260711). NOTE: the ACP mis-filed Anansi under its "African"/Yoruba-Orisha roster — the Akan are a distinct people from the Yoruba.*
