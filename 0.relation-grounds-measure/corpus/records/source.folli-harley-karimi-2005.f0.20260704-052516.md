R. Folli, H. Harley, S. Karimi, "Determinants of event type in Persian complex predicates," *Lingua* 115(10):1365–1401 (2005).

Role — the peer-reviewed base for light-verb argument structure: the light verb and nonverbal element jointly determine syntactic properties and event structure; constructionalist (Hale–Keyser) over projectionist. The LV determines agentivity/eventiveness but "fails to completely determine its event structure and telicity" — the same LV yields different event types depending on the NV element. **[confirmed]**
