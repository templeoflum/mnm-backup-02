The **Enuma Elish** ("When on high," c. late 2nd millennium BCE). The Babylonian creation epic, written in Akkadian on seven clay tablets. It recounts the birth of the gods from the primordial waters of **Apsu** (fresh water) and **Tiamat** (salt sea), the conflict of the young gods with the old, and the rise of **Marduk**, champion of Babylon, who defeats and slays Tiamat and creates the ordered world — heaven and earth — from her divided body, and is exalted as king of the gods. Recited in the Babylonian New Year (*akitu*) festival. A foundational source for Mesopotamian creation myth. **[confirmed as attribution]**
