**statement (plain)** — **Tammuz** is the shepherd god of fertility and vegetation and the consort of **Inanna/Ishtar**. In the **Descent of Inanna** the goddess returns from the underworld under demon escort and, finding Tammuz enthroned and unmourning in her absence, hands him over as her substitute to take her place below. His sister **Geshtinanna** offers to share his fate, so that Tammuz spends half the year in the underworld and half above — the dying and returning of vegetation. His descent is mourned in seasonal rites of lamentation.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: shepherds, fertility, vegetation, the seasonal death and return of plant life. Culture: Sumerian **Dumuzi**, Akkadian **Tammuz**. Consort of **Inanna/Ishtar**; in the **Descent of Inanna/Ishtar** he is given as her substitute to the underworld, alternating half the year with his sister **Geshtinanna**. The Epic of Gilgamesh cites Ishtar's mistreatment of Tammuz among the ruined lovers she is reproached for. His death is marked by rites of mourning. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Mesopotamian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Treated in the standard reference by Black & Green [[source.black-green-1992]]; the primary narrative source is the **Descent of Inanna/Ishtar**, and the Epic of Gilgamesh [[source.epic-of-gilgamesh]] refers to Ishtar's treatment of him. His domains and the substitution myth are attested tradition, variable across Sumerian and Akkadian versions, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
