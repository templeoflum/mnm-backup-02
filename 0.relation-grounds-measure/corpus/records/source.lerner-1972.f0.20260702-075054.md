Robert E. Lerner, *The Heresy of the Free Spirit in the Later Middle Ages* (University of California Press, 1972). The classic study showing that the "Free Spirit" was less an organized antinomian sect than a construct of inquisitorial imagination projected onto beguines and mystics — Marguerite Porete is a central case.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Heresy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
