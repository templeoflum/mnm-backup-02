**statement (plain)** — **Ukko** is the supreme sky-god of Finnish tradition, lord of thunder, weather, and the harvest. His name means "old man," and he is the divine grandfather who strikes lightning across the sky. His union with the earth-goddess **Akka** brings the fertilizing rain that ripens the crops, and he is invoked for good weather and a full harvest.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: sky, thunder, rain, harvest, and the oak. He is figured wielding a hammer, axe, or sword to make lightning, and grew more prominent as the chief god of the pantheon over time. Cognate: Uku (Estonian). **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Ukko is attested in Mikael Agricola's 1551 list of pagan gods, throughout the Kalevala, and in modern scholarship ([[source.pentikainen-1999]]); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Kalevala itself is Elias Lönnrot's nineteenth-century compilation of oral folk poetry, not an ancient text, so its portrait of Ukko is a compiled and edited one drawn from much older runo-song. **[confirmed as attribution]**
