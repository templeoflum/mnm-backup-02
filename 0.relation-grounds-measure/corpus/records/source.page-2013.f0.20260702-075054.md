Sophie Page, *Magic in the Cloister: Pious Motives, Illicit Interests, and Occult Approaches to the Medieval Universe* (Pennsylvania State University Press, 2013). Study of the magical manuscripts collected by the monks of St. Augustine's Abbey, Canterbury, showing how monastic readers fit works of image magic, ritual magic, and natural magic into a pious medieval cosmology.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
