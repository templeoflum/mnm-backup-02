Julio Fernando Peres, Alexander Moreira-Almeida, Leonardo Caixeta, Frederico Leão & Andrew Newberg, "Neuroimaging during trance state: A contribution to the study of dissociation," *PLoS ONE* 7, no. 11 (2012): e49360.

Contains — SPECT of ten Brazilian Spiritist psychographers (five experienced, 15–47 years of automatic writing; five less expert) during trance vs. non-trance writing. Experienced mediums showed **lower** activity in the left culmen, left hippocampus, left inferior occipital gyrus, left anterior cingulate, right superior temporal gyrus, and right precentral gyrus during psychography — while the psychographed content scored **higher** in complexity than their control writing. The authors argue relaxation cannot explain underactivation of areas tied to the cognitive processing being performed — the empirical core of the "productive subconscious in a distinct state" claim.

Access — DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0049360; open access. **[confirmed]** — a preliminary study (n=10); treat effect sizes/regions as provisional.
