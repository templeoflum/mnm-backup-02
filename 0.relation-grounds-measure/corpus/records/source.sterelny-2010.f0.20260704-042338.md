K. Sterelny, "Minds: extended or scaffolded?" *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences* 9(4):465–481 (2010); and "Social intelligence, human intelligence and niche construction," *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B* 362(1480):719–730 (2007).

Role — the "scaffolded mind": rather than parts of mind literally extending into the world, cognition is supported (scaffolded) by engineered, epistemically-structured environments built and inherited across generations — niche construction as the mechanism. **[confirmed]**
