**statement (plain)** — Jones votes for A on his own. Unknown to him, a device would have forced him to vote A had he wavered — but it never fired. He could not have done otherwise, yet acted freely. Most judge him responsible, which contradicts the Principle of Alternate Possibilities (responsibility requires the ability to do otherwise).

**statement (framework)** — **Frankfurt cases** are the central tool for **compatibilism**: responsibility depends on acting from the right will, not on alternate possibilities. Frankfurt's positive view is a hierarchy of first- and second-order desires (the willing vs. unwilling addict). Established; whether the cases truly refute PAP stays **open**.
