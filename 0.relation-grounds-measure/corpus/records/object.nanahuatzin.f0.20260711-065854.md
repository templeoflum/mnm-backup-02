**statement (plain)** — **Nanahuatzin** ("the pimply / pustuled one") is the Aztec/Nahua god who, at the creation of the Fifth Sun at **Teotihuacan**, leapt without hesitation into the fire while the wealthy **Tecuciztecatl** shrank back. Lowly and disease-covered, he rose as the sun **Tonatiuh**, and the hesitant Tecuciztecatl became the moon.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: sacrifice, the sun, humility, transformation. Nahuatl *Nanahuatzin*, "the pimply / pustuled one." His self-sacrifice at **Teotihuacan** kindles the Fifth Sun; he becomes **Tonatiuh**, and **Tecuciztecatl** the moon. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the post-conquest colonial records (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*); the creation-of-the-sun narrative survives in several recensions that diverge in detail and should be held loosely. **[confirmed as attribution]**
