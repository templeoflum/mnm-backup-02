Brian P. Copenhaver & Charles B. Schmitt, *Renaissance Philosophy* (Oxford University Press, 1992). The standard one-volume survey of philosophy from Petrarch to Bruno — humanism, revived Platonism and Aristotelianism, and the occult philosophy of Ficino, Pico, and their heirs — placing Renaissance magic inside the period's mainstream thought.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Renaissance Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
