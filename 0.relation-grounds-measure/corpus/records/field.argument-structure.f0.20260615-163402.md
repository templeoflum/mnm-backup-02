**origin (thematic relations)** — Jeffrey S. Gruber, *Studies in Lexical Relations* (MIT PhD diss., 1965) — the first "theme" and a system of thematic relations. [[source.gruber-1965]] **[confirmed]**

**origin (parallel "case")** — Charles J. Fillmore, "The Case for Case," in *Universals in Linguistic Theory* (eds. Bach & Harms; Holt, Rinehart and Winston, 1968), 1–88. [[source.fillmore-1968]] **[confirmed]**

**systematizer** — Ray Jackendoff, *Semantic Interpretation in Generative Grammar* (MIT, 1972) — popularizes theta-roles. [[source.jackendoff-1972]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Beth Levin, *English Verb Classes and Alternations* (Chicago, 1993) — 3,000+ verbs sorted by meaning / syntactic behavior. [[source.levin-1993]] **[confirmed]**

**lineage** — the single "originator" of thematic roles is shared: Gruber (originated 1965) · Jackendoff (systematized 1972) · Fillmore (parallel case tradition 1968); ancient precursor Pāṇini's *kārakas*. *(carried as lineage, not flattened — contested.)*

**door** — Levin (1993); Van Valin's introductions; Goldberg, *Constructions* (1995).
