**statement (plain)** — a universe with gravity in a negatively-curved box can be described, completely and equivalently, by a quantum theory *without* gravity living on the box's wall, one dimension down. Space with gravity inside = information on the boundary.

**statement (formal)** — string/supergravity theory on AdS_{d+2} is dual to a conformal field theory on its conformal boundary; canonical instance: Type IIB string theory on AdS₅ × S⁵ ↔ N=4 supersymmetric SU(N) Yang–Mills in 3+1D (Maldacena 1997). **[confirmed]**

**basis** — unproven as a mathematical statement (hence `open`), but supported by an enormous body of consistency checks; the most-cited result in high-energy physics (15,000+ citations by 2019, INSPIRE-HEP).
