A. Abbott, *The System of Professions* (University of Chicago Press; the rule-based/rule-bound distinction as cited by Batra et al. 2025). **[confirmed as attribution]** (cited via the (De)composing-Craft paper; edition year per the dive's citation)

Role - the distinction the craft grammar rests on: *rule-bound* = rigid mechanical adherence to explicit rules (recipes, the standard case); *rule-based* = experts use rules as a flexible foundation that can be amended or broken with interpretive freedom - the difference between craft that is "rote, mechanical and static" and craft that is "live, dynamic, and evolving."
