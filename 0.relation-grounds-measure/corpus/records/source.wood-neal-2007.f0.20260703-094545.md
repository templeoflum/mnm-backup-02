Wendy Wood & David T. Neal, "A new look at habits and the habit–goal interface," *Psychological Review* 114, no. 4 (2007): 843–863.

Contains — the influential reframing of habit as **context–response associations** learned through repetition: once formed, habits are triggered directly by recurring environmental cues, run automatically, and interface with (and often override) goal systems. Distinguishes habitual from goal-directed control and explains why habits persist even when goals change.
