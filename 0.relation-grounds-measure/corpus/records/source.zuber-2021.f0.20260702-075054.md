Mike A. Zuber, *Spiritual Alchemy: From Jacob Boehme to Mary Anne Atwood* (Oxford University Press, 2021). A history of the "spiritual alchemy" current — alchemy read as inner regeneration rather than laboratory work — tracing it from Boehme's theosophy through Behmenist and pietist circles to its nineteenth-century culmination in Atwood. Foundational for distinguishing the spiritual-alchemy lineage from laboratory chymistry and for correcting the assumption that spiritual readings are a purely modern occultist invention.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Alchemy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
