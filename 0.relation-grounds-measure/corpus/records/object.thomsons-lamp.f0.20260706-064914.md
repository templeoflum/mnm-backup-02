**statement (plain)** — A lamp is on at t=0, off at 1/2 min, on at 3/4, off at 7/8, toggling at each halved interval. By t=1 minute, infinitely many switches have occurred. Is the lamp on or off? The series of states has no last term, so neither answer follows from the sequence.

**statement (framework)** — Thomson's argument that a **supertask** (completing infinitely many acts in finite time) is incoherent — the final state is undetermined by the process. Benacerraf replied the sequence simply doesn't fix the t=1 state; the debate feeds into Zeno, the continuum, and the physics of supertasks. Established; **open**.
