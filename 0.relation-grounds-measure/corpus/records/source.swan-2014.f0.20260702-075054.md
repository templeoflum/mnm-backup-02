Laura Swan, *The Wisdom of the Beguines: The Forgotten Story of a Medieval Women's Movement* (BlueBridge, 2014). Accessible survey of the beguines — lay religious women of the Low Countries and Rhineland who lived outside cloister and vow — covering their communities, spirituality, and suppression; useful orienting context for the beguine mystics.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Popular-level history rather than a scholarly monograph. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
