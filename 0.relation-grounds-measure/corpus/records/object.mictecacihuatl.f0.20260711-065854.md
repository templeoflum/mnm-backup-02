**statement (plain)** — **Mictecacihuatl** ("lady of the dead") is the Aztec/Nahua skeletal queen of the underworld. With her husband **Mictlantecuhtli** she rules the lowest realm of **Mictlan** and keeps the bones of past generations. She presided over the Aztec festivals of the dead that, in colonial syncretism, fed into the modern **Día de los Muertos** — a post-conquest development rather than an unbroken identity.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: death, the underworld, the bones of the dead. Nahuatl *Mictecacihuatl*, "lady of the dead." Consort of **Mictlantecuhtli**, with whom she shares rule over the depths of Mictlan. Presider over the old festivals of the dead. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the post-conquest colonial records (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*); the continuity from her festivals to the modern Día de los Muertos is a syncretic reading and should be hedged, not asserted as an unbroken line. **[confirmed as attribution]**
