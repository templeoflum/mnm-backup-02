Lyndy Abraham, *A Dictionary of Alchemical Imagery* (Cambridge University Press, 1998). An alphabetical reference decoding the symbolic vocabulary of alchemy — the green lion, the chemical wedding, the peacock's tail — with definitions grounded in citations from primary alchemical texts and English literature. Foundational as the standard desk reference for reading alchemical emblem and allegory.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Alchemy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
