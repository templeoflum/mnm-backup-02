**statement (plain)** — **Amun**, "the Hidden One," is the creator god of **Thebes**. Originally a local deity, he rises to supremacy in the **New Kingdom** and merges with the sun god **Ra** as **Amun-Ra**, king of the gods and patron of the **pharaohs**. His hidden, unseen nature and his kingship over the pantheon make him the pre-eminent state god of the New Kingdom, with his great cult centre at **Karnak** in Thebes.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: creation, hiddenness, kingship over the gods, and (as Amun-Ra) the sun; patron of the pharaohs. Great cult centre: **Karnak** at **Thebes**. His name means "the Hidden One"; he rises to supremacy in the New Kingdom and fuses with **Ra** as **Amun-Ra**. The Greeks by *interpretatio graeca* equated him with **Zeus** (as **Zeus-Ammon**). **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Egyptian mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested from the Pyramid Texts [[source.pyramid-texts]] onward and present in the Book of the Dead [[source.book-of-the-dead]]; the standard reference treatment is Wilkinson [[source.wilkinson-2003]]. The Karnak cult, the New Kingdom rise to supremacy, and the Zeus-Ammon *interpretatio graeca* are established; the fusion with Ra and his creator role are attested tradition, variable across sources, and should be read as such, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
