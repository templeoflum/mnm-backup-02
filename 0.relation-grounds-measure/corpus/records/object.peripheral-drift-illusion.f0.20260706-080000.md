**statement (plain)** — The Peripheral Drift Illusion produces a vivid perception of continuous rotation or translation in completely static patterns when viewed in peripheral vision. The effect is maximized by repeating asymmetric luminance profiles, typically consisting of sequential color or brightness steps arranged in sawtooth or staircase configurations along circular or spiral trajectories. When observers fixate near but not directly on these patterns, the configurations appear to rotate smoothly and persistently, with the direction of apparent motion determined by the polarity of the luminance gradient.
