Jennifer M. Rampling, *The Experimental Fire: Inventing English Alchemy, 1300–1700* (University of Chicago Press, 2020). Traces four centuries of English alchemical practice from George Ripley's medieval tradition through its early modern readers, showing how practitioners continually reinterpreted older recipes against bench experience. Foundational as the standard account of a national alchemical tradition and of how alchemical reading and practice interlock.
