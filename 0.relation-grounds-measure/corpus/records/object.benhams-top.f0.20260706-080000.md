**statement (plain)** — Benham's Top is a chromatic illusion in which a rotating disk patterned with black and white elements produces vivid subjective color percepts despite containing no chromatic information in the stimulus itself. The classic design features a half-black, half-white disk with concentric arcs drawn in the white region. When rotated at specific angular velocities (typically 5-10 Hz), observers report seeing faint bands of red, green, blue, or yellow that vary with rotation speed and direction.

**statement (framework)** — category: color; perceptual mechanism(s): photoreceptor-adaptation, neural-adaptation, color-constancy-processing.
