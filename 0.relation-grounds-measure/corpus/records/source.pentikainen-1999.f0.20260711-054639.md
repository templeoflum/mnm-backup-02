Juha Pentikäinen's *Kalevala Mythology* is the standard modern scholarly reference to Finnish mythology and the Kalevala tradition. It appeared in a revised and expanded English edition from Indiana University Press in 1999 (translated and edited by Ritva Poom), following the Finnish original of 1987. Pentikäinen sets the Kalevala within the living runo-song and shamanic traditions of the Finnic peoples and treats Lönnrot's role as compiler and editor critically. **[confirmed as attribution]**
