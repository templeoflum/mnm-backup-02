**statement (plain)** — memory is not a recording you play back; it is a reconstruction you build each time, from stored fragments, shaped by your schemas — your organized expectations about how things go. Bartlett's subjects, retelling an unfamiliar folk tale ("War of the Ghosts"), progressively reshaped it toward their own cultural expectations. The distortions are not noise; they are the mechanism.

**basis** — Bartlett (1932), serial-reproduction studies; schema "not merely something that works the organism, but something with which the organism can work" (p. 206). The foundation for the systematic-distortion view ([[object.seven-sins-of-memory]]) and, at the trace level, for reconsolidation ([[object.memory-reconsolidation]]).
