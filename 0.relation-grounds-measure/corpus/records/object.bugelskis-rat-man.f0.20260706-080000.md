**statement (plain)** — Bugelski's Rat-Man is an ambiguous figure that can be perceived as either a rat or a man's face wearing glasses, depending on the observer's perceptual set and prior context. Created by B.R. Bugelski and Daphne Alampay in 1961, this stimulus became a classic demonstration of how top-down expectations and priming influence perceptual interpretation of ambiguous visual information.

**statement (framework)** — category: ambiguous bistable multistable; perceptual mechanism(s): top-down-expectation, figure-ground-segregation.
