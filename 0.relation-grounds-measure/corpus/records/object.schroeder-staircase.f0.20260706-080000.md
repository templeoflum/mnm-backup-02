**statement (plain)** — The Schroeder Staircase, first described by German scientist Heinrich G.F. Schroeder in 1858, is an ambiguous figure depicting a staircase that spontaneously reverses its apparent spatial orientation during viewing. The drawing can be perceived either as stairs viewed from above, ascending from lower left to upper right, or as stairs viewed from below, descending into the picture plane.

**statement (framework)** — category: ambiguous bistable multistable; perceptual mechanism(s): bistable-competition, depth-cue-conflict, top-down-expectation, figure-ground-segregation.
