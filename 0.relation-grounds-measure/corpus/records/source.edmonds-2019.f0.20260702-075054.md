Radcliffe G. Edmonds III, *Drawing Down the Moon: Magic in the Ancient Greco-Roman World* (Princeton University Press, 2019). A systematic scholarly study of magic in Greco-Roman antiquity — curses, love charms, healing, divination, astrology, alchemy, and theurgy — analyzing "magic" as a discourse of non-normative ritual practice rather than a fixed category.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Ancient Magic sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
