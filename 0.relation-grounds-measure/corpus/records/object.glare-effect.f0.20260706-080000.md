**statement (plain)** — The Glare Effect, also known as the Luminosity Illusion, occurs when a bright region surrounded by darkness appears to glow or emit light rather than simply reflecting it. This phenomenon represents a failure or extension of the visual system's ability to distinguish between luminous (self-emitting) and illuminated (light-reflecting) surfaces. In typical viewing conditions, the visual system is remarkably adept at making this distinction, a capacity critical for understanding material properties and light source locations in the environment.
