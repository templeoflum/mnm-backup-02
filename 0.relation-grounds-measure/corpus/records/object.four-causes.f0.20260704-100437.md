**statement (plain)** - to fully explain a made thing, Aristotle says you answer four "why" questions: what it's made of (material), what shape/definition it has (formal), what brought it about (efficient - the maker), and what it's for (final). A bronze statue: the bronze, the figure it depicts, the sculptor, and the purpose it serves. Craft is the clearest case, which is why the craftsman becomes the model for explaining nature.

**basis** - *Physics* II.3 uses the sculptor/statue and builder/house to set out the four causes; Aquinas's commentary notes the builder knows both the form of the house and its matter (bricks, wood). The material and formal causes belong to one natural science.
