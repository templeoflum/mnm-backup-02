Arthur Versluis, *Magic and Mysticism: An Introduction to Western Esoteric Traditions* (Rowman & Littlefield, 2007). A concise textbook introduction to Western esotericism organized around the poles of magic and mysticism, running from antiquity through the Middle Ages and Renaissance to modern currents.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), General Western Esotericism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
