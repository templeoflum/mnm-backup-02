Hugh Bowden, *Mystery Cults of the Ancient World* (Princeton University Press, 2010). An illustrated scholarly survey of the ancient mystery cults — Eleusis, Samothrace, Dionysos, Cybele, Isis, Mithras — emphasizing what initiates experienced over doctrinal reconstruction; a more recent complement to Burkert [[source.burkert-1987]].

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Ancient Greek Mysteries sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
