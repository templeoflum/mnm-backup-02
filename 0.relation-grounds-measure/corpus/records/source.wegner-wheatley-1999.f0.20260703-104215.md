Daniel M. Wegner & Thalia Wheatley, "Apparent mental causation: Sources of the experience of will," *American Psychologist* 54, no. 7 (1999): 480–492.

Contains — the model that the *experience* of consciously willing an act is an inference, constructed when a thought precedes the act with the right timing (**priority**), matches it (**consistency**), and appears to be its only cause (**exclusivity**). Because the feeling of will is inferred rather than read off true causation, it can be present without causation and absent when causation is real — the cognitive engine of felt involuntariness in automatic writing, Ouija, and trance channeling (developed in Wegner's 2002 book, [[source.wegner-2002]]).
