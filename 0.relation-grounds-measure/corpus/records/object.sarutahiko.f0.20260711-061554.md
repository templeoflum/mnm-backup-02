**statement (plain)** — **Sarutahiko-Ōkami** is a powerful earthly kami (kunitsukami) who stood radiant at the crossroads of heaven and earth. When the heavenly host descended, he met them and guided **Ninigi** down to the land. He married **Ame-no-Uzume**, whose line thereafter took his name.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: crossroads, guidance, earthly kami, martial arts, travel. He is the leader of the earthly kami and the guide of the heavenly descent. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Sarutahiko is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant details of the meeting at the crossroads; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
