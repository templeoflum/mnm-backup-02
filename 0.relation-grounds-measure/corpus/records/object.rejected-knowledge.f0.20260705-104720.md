**statement (plain)** — after the Enlightenment, Western esotericism was conceptualized as a "waste-basket category" for domains of knowledge rejected by disenchanted science (alchemy, astrology, magic, divination, the occult), defined in sharp contrast to biblical monotheism and Greek rationality — "the Other by which academics define their identity." Hanegraaff adopts Assmann's distinction between *history* ("what happened") and *mnemohistory* ("what a culture imagines has happened"): esotericism became "a casualty of mnemohistory."

**basis** — Hanegraaff, *Esotericism and the Academy* (2012; "waste-basket" ~p. 254, p. 375). [[source.hanegraaff-2012]] **[confirmed]**
