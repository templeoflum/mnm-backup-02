**statement (plain)** — Chromostereopsis is a depth illusion in which objects of different colors, presented at the same physical distance and viewed binocularly, appear to be located at different depths. The effect is most pronounced for red and blue stimuli, with red typically appearing closer than blue for most observers, though a minority experience the opposite. The phenomenon arises primarily from longitudinal chromatic aberration in the eye's optics, which causes different wavelengths to focus at slightly different distances behind the cornea.
