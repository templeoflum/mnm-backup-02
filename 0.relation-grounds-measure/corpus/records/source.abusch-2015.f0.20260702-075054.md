Tzvi Abusch, *The Witchcraft Series Maqlû* (SBL Press, 2015). An edition with facing English translation of Maqlû ("Burning"), the longest Mesopotamian anti-witchcraft ritual series — a night-long ceremony of incantations and figurine-burning against witches. Foundational as the accessible standard translation of the core Akkadian witchcraft text, by its principal modern editor.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Ancient Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (SBL paperbacks are inexpensive). **[confirmed as attribution]**
