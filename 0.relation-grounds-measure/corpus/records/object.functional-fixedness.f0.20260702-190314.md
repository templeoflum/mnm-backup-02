**statement (plain)** — once we know what a thing is *for*, it becomes hard to see it as anything else. Given a candle, a box of tacks, and matches, and asked to fix the candle to a wall, people struggle to see the tack *box* (a container) as a shelf to be tacked up — its usual function blocks the novel one. A basic and cross-culturally robust form of fixation in problem-solving.

**statement (framework)** — one of the fixation family (with the Einstellung effect [[object.einstellung-effect]] and design fixation [[object.design-fixation]]); a countermeasure is the Generic Parts Technique [[object.generic-parts-technique]], which strips objects to function-free descriptions.

**basis** — Duncker's Gestalt study of productive thinking [[source.duncker-1945]]. *Support* — replicated widely, including in populations with little exposure to manufactured tools. *Limits* — degree varies with framing and hints; not an absolute blindness but a strong bias. **[confirmed]**
