Wouter J. Hanegraaff, *Western Esotericism: A Guide for the Perplexed* (Bloomsbury, 2013). A short, accessible overview of the academic study of Western esotericism — its history, main currents, worldviews, practices, and historiography — by the Amsterdam chair who anchored the field; the companion primer to his monograph *Esotericism and the Academy* [[source.hanegraaff-2012]].

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Start Here section — the list's single entry door ("read this cover to cover"). Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
