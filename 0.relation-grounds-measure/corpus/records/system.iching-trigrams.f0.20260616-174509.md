**origin** — ancient Chinese divination; the eight trigrams (bāguà) attributed in legend to Fú Xī, foundational to the *Yìjīng* (I Ching, *Book of Changes*); two arrangements — Earlier Heaven (Fú Xī) and Later Heaven (King Wén). [[source.wilhelm-baynes-1950]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — Richard Wilhelm's German translation, rendered to English by Cary F. Baynes, *The I Ching or Book of Changes* (Bollingen/Princeton, 1950) — the standard Western text, giving each trigram its image, attribute, family member, and compass direction.

**door** — Wilhelm/Baynes, *The I Ching or Book of Changes* (1950).

**lineage** — divinatory/cosmological tradition; the two trigram arrangements carried as lineage. Pairs of trigrams compose the 64 hexagrams ([[system.iching-hexagrams]] — to ground). Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
