Plato, *Theaetetus* (c. 369 BCE) — Socrates and Theaetetus test three accounts of knowledge (perception; true belief; true belief plus a *logos*), each found wanting; the dialogue frames the justified-true-belief question that epistemology has pursued ever since. **[confirmed as attribution]**
