**statement (plain)** — A teletransporter scans you atom by atom, destroys the original, and builds a perfect replica on Mars with all your memories and sense of self. Is it you? In the branching variant the original survives — now two beings each claim to be you. No answer avoids absurdity.

**statement (framework)** — Parfit's route to **reductionism** about personal identity: identity is not what matters in survival — only psychological continuity and connectedness ('Relation R'), which can hold in degrees and can branch. The self is a bundle, not a deep fact. Urgent now for uploading, copies, and cryonics. Established; **open**.
