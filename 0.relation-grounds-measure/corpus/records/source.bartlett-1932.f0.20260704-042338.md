F. C. Bartlett, *Remembering: A Study in Experimental and Social Psychology* (Cambridge University Press, 1932).

Role — establishes that remembering is reconstruction governed by schemas ("effort after meaning"), via the "War of the Ghosts" serial-reproduction studies; schema is "not merely something that works the organism, but something with which the organism can work" (p. 206). The origin of the reconstructive view of memory. **[confirmed]**
