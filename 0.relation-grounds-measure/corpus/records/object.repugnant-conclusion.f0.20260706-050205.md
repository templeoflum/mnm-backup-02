**statement (plain)** — Total utilitarianism ranks a world better if it has more total well-being. So for any world of deeply happy people, there is a 'better' world with a vastly larger population each of whose lives is barely worth living — a huge sea of near-misery outranking a small paradise. Parfit called this repugnant.

**statement (framework)** — the central problem of **population ethics**. Its force is that every alternative (average, critical-level, person-affecting, variable-value views) yields its own severe counterintuitive result — formalized in Arrhenius's impossibility theorems: no axiology satisfies a few modest conditions at once. Established; **open**, no consensus resolution.
