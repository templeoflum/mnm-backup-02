Marija Gimbutas's *The Balts* (Thames & Hudson, 1963) is the standard English-language introduction to the archaeology, culture, and pre-Christian religion of the Baltic peoples (Lithuanians, Latvians, and their prehistoric kin). It surveys the material record alongside the surviving mythology, treating the thunder-god Perkūnas, the fate-goddess Laima, and the sun-goddess Saulė as part of a conservative Indo-European tradition close to the Slavic. It remains a foundational reference for the recovery of Baltic religion. **[confirmed as attribution]**
