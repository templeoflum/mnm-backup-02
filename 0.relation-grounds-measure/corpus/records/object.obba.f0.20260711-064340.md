**statement (plain)** — **Obba** (Ọba) is the **Orisha** of marriage and domestic devotion in Yoruba tradition, and the senior wife of **Shango**. In the standard tradition she was deceived by the jealous **Oshun** into cutting off her own ear to put in Shango's soup as a love-charm; when she was found out she was rejected and withdrew in grief. She is associated with lakes and rivers and honored as the patron of devoted and wronged women.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: marriage, domestic life, lakes, devotion, sacrifice, betrayed women. Senior wife of **Shango**; tricked by **Oshun** into disfiguring herself and then cast off; patron of faithful and betrayed women; associated with the lake and river waters. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962) and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; the ear-cutting tale is the standard tradition but is variously told, and her attributes vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions. **[confirmed as attribution]**
