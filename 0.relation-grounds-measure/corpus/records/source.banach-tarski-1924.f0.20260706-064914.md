Stefan Banach & Alfred Tarski, "Sur la décomposition des ensembles de points en parties respectivement congruentes" (1924) — proves that a solid ball can be split into finitely many (non-measurable) pieces and reassembled by rigid motions into two balls of the original size; a consequence of the axiom of choice. **[confirmed as attribution]**
