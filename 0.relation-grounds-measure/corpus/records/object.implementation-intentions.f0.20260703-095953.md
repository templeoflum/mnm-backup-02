**statement (plain)** — An implementation intention is a specific plan that links an anticipated situation to a response: "When X happens, I will do Y." Unlike a bare goal ("I want to exercise more"), it decides in advance *where, when, and how*, so that the encounter with the cue triggers the action automatically — the person builds the banks before the water comes. Meta-analyses find a medium-to-large effect on goal attainment, especially where getting started, remembering, or resisting distraction is the obstacle.

**relevance** — the self-directed analog of **passive** flow control and pre-channeling (SHAPE for the self): control is delegated to the environment ahead of time, sparing willpower at the decision point. Pairs with cybernetic self-regulation ([[object.cybernetic-self-regulation]]) for the sensing/correcting side (HOLD) [[source.gollwitzer-1999]] **[confirmed]**.
