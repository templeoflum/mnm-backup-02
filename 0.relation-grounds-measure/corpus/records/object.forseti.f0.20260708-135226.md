**statement (plain)** — **Forseti** (Old Norse *Forseti*, "the presider" or "chairman") is the god of justice, reconciliation, and mediation. Snorri names him in *Gylfaginning* as the son of **Baldr** and **Nanna**. His hall is **Glitnir**, described in the Poetic Edda in *Grímnismál* as pillared with gold and roofed with silver, where he dwells the day long and settles all disputes; Snorri repeats that all who come to him with legal quarrels leave reconciled, so that he is the best of judges among gods and men. The name itself carries the sense of one who presides over an assembly, and he is closely tied to the settling of law-cases.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: justice, law, reconciliation, and mediation of disputes. Key relations: son of **Baldr** and **Nanna**; lord of the hall **Glitnir**. His hall Glitnir and his office of settling all quarrels appear in the Poetic Edda in *Grímnismál*; Snorri's *Gylfaginning* supplies his parentage from Baldr and Nanna and names him the best of judges who reconciles all who bring cases before him. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — a figure of Norse mythic tradition, documented as *held* (`status: doctrinal`), not asserted. Attested across the Poetic Edda [[source.poetic-edda]] and systematized in Snorri's Prose Edda [[source.prose-edda]]; the standard reference treatment is Lindow [[source.lindow-2001]]. His parentage from Baldr and Nanna comes chiefly from Snorri's *Gylfaginning* and should be read as attested tradition through his 13th-c. Christian framing, not as neutral report. **[confirmed as attribution]**
