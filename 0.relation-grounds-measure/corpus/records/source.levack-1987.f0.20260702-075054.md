Brian P. Levack, *The Witch-Hunt in Early Modern Europe* (Longman, 1987). The standard one-volume synthesis on the European witch-hunts — intellectual and legal foundations, social dynamics, regional patterns, chronology, and decline — the usual scholarly entry point to the subject, kept current through revised editions.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Early-Modern Witch Hunts sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
