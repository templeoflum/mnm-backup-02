Katharine Luomala, *Maui-of-a-Thousand-Tricks: His Oceanic and European Biographers* (Bernice P. Bishop Museum Bulletin 198, 1949), is the standard comparative study of the pan-Polynesian Māui traditions. It gathers and compares the Māui narratives across the Pacific — island-fishing, snaring the sun, winning fire, and the fatal quest for immortality — and traces how these traditions were collected and interpreted by successive Oceanic and European recorders. It is the principal comparative frame for the trickster culture-hero across Polynesia. **[confirmed as attribution]**
