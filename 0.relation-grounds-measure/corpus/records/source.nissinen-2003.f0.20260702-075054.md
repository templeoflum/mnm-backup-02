Martti Nissinen, *Prophets and Prophecy in the Ancient Near East* (Society of Biblical Literature, 2003). The standard sourcebook of ancient Near Eastern prophetic texts outside the Bible — Mari letters, Neo-Assyrian oracles, and West Semitic inscriptions — in translation, placing biblical prophecy in its wider institutional context.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Biblical & Ancient Near East. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
