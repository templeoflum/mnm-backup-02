**statement (plain)** — Escher's Waterfall is a 1961 lithograph by M.C. Escher depicting a perpetual motion machine based on the impossible geometry of the Penrose triangle. The artwork shows an aqueduct system where water appears to flow downhill along a zigzagging channel, ultimately cascading down a waterfall that feeds back into the starting point of the same channel.
