John J. Collins, *The Apocalyptic Imagination: An Introduction to Jewish Apocalyptic Literature* (Crossroad, 1984). The standard scholarly introduction to Jewish apocalypticism — genre definition, Enochic literature, Daniel, Qumran, and the apocalyptic worldview — foundational for reading Merkavah and Dead Sea Scrolls material in context.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Apocalypticism, Merkavah and Dead Sea Scrolls sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
