The *Kojiki* ("Records of Ancient Matters," 712 CE), compiled by **Ō no Yasumaro**, is the oldest extant Japanese chronicle and the foundational text of Shinto mythology. It records the creation of the islands, the primordial pair **Izanagi** and **Izanami**, and the birth of **Amaterasu**, **Susanoo**, and **Tsukuyomi**, along with the Izumo cycle and the descent of the imperial line. **[confirmed as attribution]**
