Plutarch, *Life of Romulus* (from the *Parallel Lives*, c. 96 CE), the biography that pairs the Roman Romulus with the Greek Theseus. Written in Greek, it gathers and weighs the variant traditions and is a principal narrative source for the founding legend — the she-wolf, the fratricide, and Romulus' disappearance and deification.
