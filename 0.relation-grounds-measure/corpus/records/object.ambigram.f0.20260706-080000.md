**statement (plain)** — An ambigram is a typographic design that maintains readability when viewed from multiple orientations or perspectives, such as when rotated 180 degrees, reflected in a mirror, or viewed from different angles. This calligraphic art form exploits the perceptual system's ability to recognize letterforms even when they deviate substantially from canonical representations, relying on top-down processing and contextual expectations to resolve ambiguous visual information into coherent text. The creation of ambigrams requires careful manipulation of positive and negative space, symmetry principles, and stroke patterns to ensure that the same visual elements can be interpreted as different letters depending on viewing orientation.
