**statement (plain)** — **Amaterasu-Ōmikami** is the supreme sun goddess, ruler of **Takamagahara** (the High Plain of Heaven) and ancestress of the imperial line, born from **Izanagi**'s left eye. After the outrages of her brother **Susanoo** she withdrew into the **Ame-no-Iwato** (Heavenly Rock Cave), plunging the world into darkness, and was drawn out only by the ecstatic dance of **Ame-no-Uzume**. Her grandson **Ninigi** descended from heaven to rule, founding the imperial line.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: sun, light, sovereignty, weaving, sacred agriculture. She is the head of the heavenly kami and the divine ancestress of the emperors. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Amaterasu is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant accounts of the Rock Cave and her birth; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
