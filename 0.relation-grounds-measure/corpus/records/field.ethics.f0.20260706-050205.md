**origin** — Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* (c. 340 BCE) — the *eudaimonia* / virtue tradition: the good life as activity of the soul in accordance with virtue, cultivated as a mean between extremes.

**codification** — the two great modern systems: Immanuel Kant, *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (1785) — the categorical imperative and duty [[source.kant-1785]]; and John Stuart Mill, *Utilitarianism* (1863) — the greatest-happiness principle [[source.mill-1863]]. Most modern moral thought experiments (trolley, utility monster, Omelas) are stress tests between these deontological and consequentialist poles.

**door** — James Rachels & Stuart Rachels, *The Elements of Moral Philosophy* — the standard survey of the traditions and their problems.
