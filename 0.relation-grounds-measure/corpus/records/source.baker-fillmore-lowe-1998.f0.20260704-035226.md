C. F. Baker, C. J. Fillmore, J. B. Lowe, "The Berkeley FrameNet Project," COLING-ACL 1998. **[confirmed as attribution]** (the dive cites FrameNet as a project, UC Berkeley 1997–present, continuing into the 2020s under Baker and Petruck; this is its canonical project paper, detail from knowledge)

Role — the computational descendant of frame semantics: a lexical database of frames with frame elements, annotated example sentences — one of the two resources (with PropBank) that made semantic-role labeling a benchmark task. (Handle carries all three authors — the corpus's `baker-1988` is Mark C. Baker, a different linguist.)
