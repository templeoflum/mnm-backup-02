Bruce G. Blair, "Keeping Presidents in the Nuclear Dark, Episode 1: The Case of the Missing Permissive Action Links," Center for Defense Information, February 2004.

Contains — Blair, a Minuteman ICBM launch control officer 1970–74, reports that the Permissive Action Link enabling codes for the Minuteman force were set to **00000000**, and that launch checklists instructed crews to verify the enable panel read all zeros; he lobbied for years and the codes were "activated" in 1977. (The Air Force stated to Congress in 2014 that eight zeros "has never been used to enable" a Minuteman ICBM.) Cited as a real-world illustration that a two-person/two-key procedure is only as strong as the commitment to actually following it.

Access — Blair archive at Princeton SGS; widely reported. Citation web-corroborated 20260702. **[confirmed as attribution]**
