The *Kalevala* is the national epic of Finland, compiled by Elias Lönnrot from Karelian and Finnish oral folk poetry (runo-song) that he collected on field expeditions. It was published first as the Old Kalevala (1835) and then as the expanded New Kalevala (1849), the latter running to fifty runos (cantos). It is the central literary source for Finnish mythology, gathering the deeds of Väinämöinen, Ilmarinen, Lemminkäinen, and others into a connected narrative. It is a nineteenth-century compilation of much older oral material, arranged and edited by Lönnrot, not itself an ancient text. **[confirmed as attribution]**
