**origin** — the oral **runo-song** tradition of the Finnic peoples (Finland and Karelia); the earliest documentary trace of the pagan gods is **Mikael Agricola's** 1551 list. [[source.agricola-1551]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — **Elias Lönnrot's** *Kalevala* (1835 Old, 1849 New), the national epic compiled from collected oral folk poetry into a connected narrative. [[source.kalevala]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Juha Pentikäinen, *Kalevala Mythology* (Indiana University Press, 1999), the standard modern scholarly reference. [[source.pentikainen-1999]]

*grade a — origin (runo-song / Agricola), codification (Lönnrot's Kalevala), door (Pentikäinen) present. A single-tradition field under [[field.comparative-mythology]]. Field-home for the Finnish pantheon figure intake (20260711); homes Ukko, Väinämöinen, Ilmarinen, Lemminkäinen, and the forest and underworld figures. NOTE: the ACP "Finnish" roster also carried four BALTIC figures (Perkūnas, Velnias, Laima, Saulė) — a distinct tradition, held out of this field for separate disposition.*
