**statement (plain)** — **Huitzilopochtli** ("hummingbird of the south," or "of the left") is the tribal war- and sun-god of the Mexica (Aztecs). Born fully armed from the earth-mother **Coatlicue**, he defeated his sister **Coyolxauhqui** and the star-brothers who attacked her. As patron of the Mexica he guided their migration from Aztlan to the eagle-and-cactus sign where they founded **Tenochtitlan**; he was the chief god of the Templo Mayor, fed by human sacrifice to keep the sun moving.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: war, the sun, sacrifice, the Mexica nation. Nahuatl *Huitzilopochtli*, from *huitzilin* (hummingbird) and *opochtli* (left / south). Son of Coatlicue; adversary of Coyolxauhqui and the Centzon Huitznahua; patron deity of the Mexica migration and the Templo Mayor. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the colonial-era codices (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*), recorded after the conquest; the migration legend and the Coatepec birth-battle vary across the sources. **[confirmed as attribution]**
