**statement (plain)** — Fire particles one at a time at a barrier with two slits. Each lands as a point, but over many arrivals an *interference* pattern builds up — as if each particle went through both slits and interfered with itself. Place a which-path detector and the interference vanishes; remove it and interference returns.

**statement (framework)** — an **empirical** experiment (not a hypothetical) that Feynman called the 'only mystery' of QM: **wave–particle duality** and the dependence of outcomes on which-path information available *in principle*. Realized with electrons, atoms, and large molecules. Empirical; its interpretation stays **open**.

**basis** — Young's 1801 light experiment; the quantum treatment in Feynman Lectures Vol. III (1965) [[source.feynman-1965]]. **[confirmed as attribution]**
