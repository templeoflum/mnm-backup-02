**statement (plain)** — **Orunmila** (Ọ̀rúnmìlà) is the **Orisha** of wisdom, knowledge, and divination in Yoruba tradition. He was present when **Olodumare** ordered the cosmos, and is held to be the only witness to all destinies at creation. Unlike most Orisha he does not possess his devotees; instead he speaks through the **Ifá** divination system, whose signs are read and interpreted by his priests, the **babalawo**.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: divination, wisdom, destiny, the **Ifá** system, knowledge, healing. Witness to every destiny at creation; patron of the **Ifá** oracle, spoken through rather than embodied; served by the **babalawo** (fathers of secrets) who interpret the *odu*. Closely linked to **Eshu**, who carries the offerings, and to **Olodumare**, whose order he witnessed. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962), Bascom (1969), and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; details of his role and of the divination practice vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions. **[confirmed as attribution]**
