Pierre Grimal, *The Dictionary of Classical Mythology* (Blackwell, 1986), the English edition of the French original *Dictionnaire de la mythologie grecque et romaine* (PUF, 1951). A standard modern scholarly reference covering both Greek and Roman myth, cross-referenced to the ancient sources; the everyday door into the tradition for figures and their variants.
