**statement (plain)** — **Konohanasakuya-hime** is the blossom princess who married **Ninigi**. When he doubted that her swiftly-conceived children were his, she set fire to the birthing hut and bore them unharmed within the flames, proving their divine parentage. She embodies ephemeral beauty (the cherry blossom) and is enshrined as the goddess of **Mount Fuji**.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: cherry blossoms, Mount Fuji, ephemeral beauty, childbirth, fire. She is the blossom-princess of the imperial descent and the volcano-goddess of Fuji. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Konohanasakuya-hime is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which give variant accounts of the burning-hut birth; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
