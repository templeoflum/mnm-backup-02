**statement (plain)** — **Ix Chel** is the Maya goddess of two faces. As the youthful **Ix Chel** she is goddess of the moon, love, and fertility; as the aged **Chak Chel** ("great rainbow" / red crone) she is a fearsome figure who sends floods and destruction. She is patroness of weaving, childbirth, and healing.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the moon, love, fertility, weaving, childbirth, medicine, floods. Young **Ix Chel** of the moon and fertility; aged **Chak Chel** ("great rainbow" / red crone) who pours down floods; patroness of weaving, childbirth, and medicine. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited source; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Maya record rests chiefly on post-conquest colonial sources and later scholarship; the identification of the young Ix Chel with the aged Chak Chel is a scholarly reading and should be hedged, not asserted as a single fixed figure. **[confirmed as attribution]**
