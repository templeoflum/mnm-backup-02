**statement (plain)** — The Luminance Gradient Illusion refers to a class of perceptual phenomena in which physically uniform surfaces appear to contain luminance gradients, or conversely, surfaces containing actual gradients appear more uniform than their physical properties would suggest. These effects arise when target surfaces are positioned adjacent to or surrounded by regions containing luminance gradients in specific spatial configurations. The illusion demonstrates that the visual system's interpretation of shading and illumination patterns can induce complementary or assimilating gradient percepts in neighboring regions.
