Gottlob Frege, *Begriffsschrift, eine der arithmetischen nachgebildete Formelsprache des reinen Denkens* (1879) — introduces quantifiers and bound variables, function-argument analysis of predication, and a formal system of proof: the foundation of modern predicate logic. **[confirmed as attribution]**
