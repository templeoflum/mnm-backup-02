**statement (plain)** — Assume the universe is infinite, eternal, and uniformly filled with stars. Then every line of sight eventually meets a star's surface, so the whole sky should be as bright as a star. Yet the night sky is dark. Why?

**statement (framework)** — the dark sky is evidence that the universe is **not** infinite-eternal-static: it has a finite age (so light from very distant stars hasn't arrived) and expands (redshifting distant light). An empirical paradox whose resolution helped establish Big-Bang cosmology. Established.
