**statement (plain)** - a smith judges the steel's temperature by its colour, a potter reads the clay through the hands - knowing the material in action, without a scientific account of it. Craft is a trained attunement between maker and material achieved through hands, eyes, and tools: a "recognition of and response to material" that grasps its *qualities*, not just measurable properties.

**basis** - Kuijpers (2018) supplies "perceptive categories" and the qualities-not-properties framing ([[source.kuijpers-2018]]); Crevels' "Coarse epistemes" essay develops the hypothesis that craftsmanship is a "perceptive-cognitive enactment within the making process, a form of attunement with production" ([[source.crevels-coarse-epistemes]]). **[confirmed as attribution]** *Attribution split recorded honestly per the dive's verified flag: attunement/coarse-epistemes = Crevels; perceptive-categories = Kuijpers.* Kin (prose): [[object.tacit-knowing]], [[object.indwelling]], [[object.motor-intentionality]].
