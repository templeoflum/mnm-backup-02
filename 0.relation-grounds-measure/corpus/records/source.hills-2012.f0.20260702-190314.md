Thomas T. Hills, Michael N. Jones, and Peter M. Todd, "Optimal Foraging in Semantic Memory: Is There a Global Depletion Effect?" *Psychological Review* 119, no. 2 (2012): 431–440.

Contains — when people retrieve items from memory (e.g. naming as many animals as possible) they move in clusters — exploiting a semantic "patch" (pets, then farm animals, then African animals) and switching patches when the current one depletes, following the same optimal patch-leaving policy (marginal-value theorem [[object.marginal-value-theorem]]) that governs animals foraging in space. The core evidence for **area-restricted search** in internal cognitive search; complements the broader synthesis in [[source.hills-2015]].
