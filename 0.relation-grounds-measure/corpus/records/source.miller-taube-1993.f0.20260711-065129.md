Mary **Miller** & Karl **Taube**, *The Gods and Symbols of Ancient Mexico and the Maya: An Illustrated Dictionary of Mesoamerican Religion* (Thames & Hudson, 1993), is the standard modern English reference to the Aztec and Maya pantheons. Organized as an illustrated dictionary, it collates the deities, symbols, and ritual concepts of Mesoamerican religion for both the Aztec/Nahua and the Maya traditions. **[confirmed as attribution]**
