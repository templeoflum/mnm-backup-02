Descartes' *Meditations on First Philosophy* (Latin, 1641) — six meditations moving from methodic doubt to the *cogito*, the existence of God, and the **real distinction** between the thinking thing (*res cogitans*) and extended body (*res extensa*). The source of substance dualism and the modern framing of the mind–body problem. Standard English in *The Philosophical Writings of Descartes*, trans. Cottingham, Stoothoff & Murdoch (Cambridge UP, 1984). **[confirmed as attribution]**
