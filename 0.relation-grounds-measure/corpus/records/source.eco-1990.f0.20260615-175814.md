Umberto Eco, *The Limits of Interpretation* (Indiana University Press, 1990) — the chapter "Unlimited Semiosis and Drift" (from his 1989 lecture) names *hermetic semiosis* / *hermetic drift* (everything resembles everything; interpretation without brakes) and bounds it by the Augustinian coherence criterion and *intentio operis* vs *intentio lectoris*. See also *Interpretation and Overinterpretation* (Cambridge, 1992).
