Georg Dehn (ed.), *The Book of Abramelin: A New Translation*, trans. Steven Guth (Ibis, 2006). Modern edition of the Abramelin operation — the extended kabbalistic-magical retreat for attaining the Holy Guardian Angel — translated from the earlier and more complete German manuscripts, restoring a fourth book and correct word-square material absent from the French line.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. Access note: the Mathers 1897 translation (from an incomplete French ms.) is public domain; the Dehn/Guth edition is an in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
