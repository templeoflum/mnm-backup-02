**statement (plain)** — Use a ruler to measure a table and you assume the ruler is true; the reading is about the table. But if you doubt the ruler, the reading is really about the ruler. The less reliable the instrument, the more its output reflects the instrument, not the world.

**statement (framework)** — an **epistemology-of-measurement** heuristic: every measurement is a relation between two entities, and doubt about the apparatus (cognitive, institutional, technological) transfers to the result. Bears on evaluating experts, metrics, models, Goodhart's law, the replication crisis, and AI-evaluating-AI. Named for Wittgensteinian themes though it is Taleb's formulation. Established as a heuristic; not a formal result (**open** in scope).
