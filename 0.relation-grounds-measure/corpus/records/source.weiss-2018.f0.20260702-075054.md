Tzahi Weiss, *"Sefer Yeṣirah" and Its Contexts: Other Jewish Voices* (University of Pennsylvania Press, 2018). A scholarly study of Sefer Yetzirah's origins, dating, and cultural milieu, arguing for a Syriac-Christian-adjacent seventh-century context and reframing the text's place in Jewish intellectual history.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
