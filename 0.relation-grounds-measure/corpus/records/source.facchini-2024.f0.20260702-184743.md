Giulio Facchini, Alann Rathery, Stéphane Douady, David Sillam-Dussès, and Andrea Perna, "Substrate evaporation drives collective construction in termites," *eLife* 12 (2024): RP86843.

Contains — studying *Coptotermes gestroi*, argues that substrate curvature and water evaporation — not necessarily a building pheromone — drive collective construction, and states plainly that "several years since Grassé's early observations, there is still not a consensus on the exact nature of the stigmergic stimuli involved in regulating termite construction." The current empirical caution on the founding example of stigmergy [[object.stigmergy]]: the textbook "pure sematectonic" termite case is contested and may be partly physics, partly marker-based.
