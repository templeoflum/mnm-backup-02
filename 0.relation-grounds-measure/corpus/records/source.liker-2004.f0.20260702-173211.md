Jeffrey K. Liker, *The Toyota Way: 14 Management Principles from the World's Greatest Manufacturer* (McGraw-Hill, 2004).

Contains — the widely-read synthesis of the Toyota Production System as 14 principles across four categories (Philosophy, Process, People/Partners, Problem Solving). Principle 5 — "build a culture of stopping to fix problems, to get quality right the first time" — is the jidoka/andon principle. Documents the **NUMMI** case (the 1984 Toyota–GM joint venture in Fremont, CA), where roughly the same workforce previously rated the worst at GM reached Toyota-level defect rates within a year after the system and its supporting management culture were installed.
