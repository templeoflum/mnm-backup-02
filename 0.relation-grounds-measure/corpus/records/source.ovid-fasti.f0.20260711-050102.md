Ovid (Publius Ovidius Naso), the *Fasti* (c. 8 CE), an elegiac poem on the Roman religious calendar treated month by month; of a projected twelve books, six survive (January–June). It is a primary source for the Roman gods, their rites and festivals, and the aetiologies behind the calendar — Janus, who answers the poet, opens Book 1.
