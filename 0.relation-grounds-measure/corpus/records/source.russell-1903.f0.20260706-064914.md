Bertrand Russell, *The Principles of Mathematics* (1903) — publishes the paradox of the set of all sets that are not members of themselves (found 1901, sent to Frege 1902), which demolished naive set theory; the barber is Russell's own popular illustration. **[confirmed as attribution]**
