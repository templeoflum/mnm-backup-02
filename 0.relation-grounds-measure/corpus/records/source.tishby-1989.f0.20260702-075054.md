Isaiah Tishby, *The Wisdom of the Zohar: An Anthology of Texts* (Littman Library / Oxford University Press, 1989; English trans. David Goldstein, 3 vols.; Hebrew original 1949/1961). A thematically organized anthology of Zohar passages with extensive introductions to each topic — Godhead, creation, soul, evil, worship — long the standard systematic entry into Zoharic theology.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
