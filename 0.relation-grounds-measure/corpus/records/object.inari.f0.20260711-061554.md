**statement (plain)** — **Inari-Ōkami** is the kami of rice, foxes, fertility, and worldly prosperity, one of the most widely worshipped deities in Japan. The white **kitsune** (foxes) serve as messengers and guardians, and Inari is honored at tens of thousands of shrines, the head shrine standing at **Fushimi**. The deity is variously depicted as male, female, or androgynous.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: rice, foxes, fertility, industry, prosperity, swordsmiths. Inari is a widely-worshipped kami whose developed cult is post-classical. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Inari is a Shinto kami whose developed cult is post-classical; the deity is documented in modern scholarship ([[source.ashkenazi-2003]]) and this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). Later tradition variously depicts Inari as male, female, or androgynous, and the folk cult accumulates well beyond the 8th-century chronicles. **[confirmed as attribution]**
