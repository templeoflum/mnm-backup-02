Baba Ifa Karade's *The Handbook of Yoruba Religious Concepts* (Samuel Weiser, 1994) is a widely-used modern introduction to the **Orisha** and Yoruba spiritual practice, including the diaspora traditions of **Santería** and **Candomblé**. Written as a practical primer for a contemporary readership, it surveys the major Orisha, their attributes, and the concepts of **ashé** and Ifá, and is a common entry point to the tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**
