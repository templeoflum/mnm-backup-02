Paul Ekman & Wallace V. Friesen, "Nonverbal leakage and clues to deception," *Psychiatry* 32, no. 1 (1969): 88–106.

Contains — the founding statement of **nonverbal leakage**: when people conceal or falsify emotion, true affect escapes through less-controllable channels (and in very brief facial displays — micro-expressions), giving leakage cues and deception clues. Introduces the leakage/deception-clue distinction and the differential controllability of face vs. body channels that later grounds Ekman's micro-expression and FACS program.
