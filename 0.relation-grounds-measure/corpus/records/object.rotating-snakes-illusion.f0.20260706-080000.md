**statement (plain)** — The Rotating Snakes Illusion, created by Akiyoshi Kitaoka in 2003, is a striking illusory motion phenomenon in which static circular patterns composed of specific luminance sequences produce strong percepts of continuous rotation. The stimulus consists of concentric rings containing repeating sequences of colored or grayscale elements arranged in a particular order, typically progressing from dark to light. When viewed in peripheral vision or during small eye movements such as microsaccades, the circular patterns appear to rotate spontaneously and continuously, despite being completely static.
