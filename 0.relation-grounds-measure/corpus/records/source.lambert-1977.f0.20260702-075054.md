Malcolm Lambert, *Medieval Heresy: Popular Movements from the Gregorian Reform to the Reformation* (Edward Arnold, 1977; 3rd ed. Blackwell, 2002). The standard one-volume survey of Western medieval heresy — Waldensians, Cathars, Free Spirit, Lollards, Hussites — from the eleventh century to the eve of the Reformation.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Heresy; the list recommends the current 3rd edition (2002). Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
