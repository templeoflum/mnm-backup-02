**statement (plain)** — a robot arm's end-effector pose is computed by multiplying, one joint at a time, the exponential of each joint's screw twist scaled by its joint value. Each factor is a one-parameter screw motion (infinitesimal Chasles); their product is the full configuration. It turns the geometry of screws into a clean matrix recipe.

**statement (formal)** — for a serial chain with joint screws S_i (twists in 𝔰𝔢(3)) and joint values θ_i, the end-effector configuration is T(θ) = e^{[S_1]θ_1} e^{[S_2]θ_2} ⋯ e^{[S_n]θ_n} · M, where M is the home configuration and [S_i] the 4×4 twist matrix. **[confirmed]**

**basis** — Brockett (1983); SE(3) is generated by one-parameter subgroups of screw motions and is exponential. Doors: Murray–Li–Sastry (1994); Lynch–Park (2017).
