Stanton J. Linden (ed.), *The Alchemy Reader: From Hermes Trismegistus to Isaac Newton* (Cambridge University Press, 2003). An anthology of primary alchemical writings spanning antiquity to the seventeenth century — the Emerald Tablet, Zosimos, Geber, Paracelsus, Maier, Newton — with editorial introductions; the standard one-volume alchemical sourcebook.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Alchemy sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
