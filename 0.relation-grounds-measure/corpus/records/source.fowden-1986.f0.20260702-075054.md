Garth Fowden, *The Egyptian Hermes: A Historical Approach to the Late Pagan Mind* (Cambridge University Press, 1986). The standard historical study situating the Hermetica — technical and philosophical together — in Greco-Roman Egypt, arguing for their social and religious milieu and for reading them as a "way of Hermes" rather than a detached philosophy. Foundational as the work that re-Egyptianized Hermetic studies after Festugière's Hellenizing reading.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Hermeticism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
