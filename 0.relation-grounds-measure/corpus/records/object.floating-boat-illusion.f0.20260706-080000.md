**statement (plain)** — The Floating Boat Illusion is a superior mirage phenomenon in which ships, boats, or other objects on or near the water surface appear to be hovering or floating in the air above the actual water surface, sometimes with a visible gap of empty space between the vessel and the water below. This illusion occurs when a layer of cooler air lies beneath a layer of warmer air near the water surface, creating a temperature inversion that causes light rays from distant objects to bend downward as they travel through the density gradient. As a result, light from the lower portions of a distant vessel is refracted downward and fails to reach the observer's eye, while light from the upper portions is bent less severely and successfully reaches the observer, creating the appearance that the vessel is elevated above the water with its lower sections invisible or obscured.
