**statement (plain)** — **Olodumare** (also **Olorun**, "owner of the sky") is the supreme being of the Yoruba tradition, the transcendent creator and source of all **ashé** (divine energy). He is a remote high god who is not worshipped with shrines or sacrifice as the **Orisha** are, but from whom all ashé flows and to whom all destiny returns. The ordering of the world is delegated to the Orisha, his emanations, who mediate between him and humankind.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: supreme being, creation, destiny, cosmic order. Named also **Olorun** ("owner of the sky") and **Olofin**; conceived as spirit, without a physical cult of shrines and offerings, standing above and behind the pantheon of Orisha. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962), Bascom (1969), and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; the exact relation of Olodumare to the Orisha and the names used vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions of Santería and Candomblé. **[confirmed as attribution]**
