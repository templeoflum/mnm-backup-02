J. Sutton, "Exograms and Interdisciplinarity: History, the Extended Mind, and the Civilizing Process," in R. Menary (ed.), *The Extended Mind* (MIT Press, 2010), 189–225.

Role — names the "two compatible but distinct movements or waves" of extended-mind theory and states the second-wave complementarity principle: external vehicles can be "radically unlike" internal ones yet contribute jointly. Develops the exogram/engram contrast (from Donald 1991) within that frame. **[confirmed]**
