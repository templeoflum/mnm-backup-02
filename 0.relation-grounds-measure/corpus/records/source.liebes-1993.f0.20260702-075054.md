Yehuda Liebes, *Studies in the Zohar* (SUNY Press, 1993). Landmark essays including "How the Zohar Was Written" — arguing for group composition rather than Moses de León alone — plus studies of the Zohar's messianism and its mythic reading of rabbinic sources; a hinge text in modern Zohar scholarship.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
