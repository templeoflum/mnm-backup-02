**statement (plain)** — You travel back and kill your grandfather before your parent is conceived. Then you are never born, so you never travel back, so he lives, so you are born, so you travel back and kill him — a loop with no stable resolution.

**statement (framework)** — the canonical objection to changing the past. Lewis's resolution: time travel is consistent, but the past cannot be *changed* — the traveler simply never succeeds (something always intervenes); consistency is required, not free rewriting. Related: Novikov self-consistency, branching-worlds readings. Established; **open**.
