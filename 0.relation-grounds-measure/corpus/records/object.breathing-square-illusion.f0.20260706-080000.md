**statement (plain)** — The Breathing Square Illusion is a motion illusion in which a square shape embedded in a static pattern appears to expand and contract rhythmically, as if breathing or pulsating. Despite the complete absence of physical change in the stimulus, observers perceive periodic variations in the size of the square, with alternating phases of apparent growth and shrinkage creating a dynamic, oscillatory impression. The illusory breathing motion typically occurs at a slow, regular rhythm and can persist for extended viewing periods, though the effect may vary in strength depending on attention, fixation stability, and individual differences.
