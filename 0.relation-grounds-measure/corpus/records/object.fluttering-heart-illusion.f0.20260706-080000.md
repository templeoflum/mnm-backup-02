**statement (plain)** — The Fluttering Heart Illusion is a motion phenomenon in which an isoluminant colored shape, typically rendered in a heart configuration, appears to flutter, jitter, or oscillate when presented against a dark background as the observer's eyes move. The illusion is characterized by an apparent instability or trembling motion of the colored form, despite the fact that the stimulus itself remains completely stationary on the display. The effect is particularly pronounced when the colored shape is equiluminant or nearly equiluminant with the background, meaning that the two regions differ primarily in chromatic content rather than luminance.
