Christiano, P., Shlegeris, B., & Amodei, D., "Supervising strong learners by amplifying weak experts," arXiv:1810.08575 (2018).

Role: introduces **Iterated Amplification (IDA)** — a scalable-oversight training strategy that builds a training signal for hard problems by recursively decomposing them into easier subproblems a human can supervise, then amplifying. Related to expert iteration but uses no external reward function. A core proposed answer to the scalable-oversight problem posed in [[source.amodei-2016]].
