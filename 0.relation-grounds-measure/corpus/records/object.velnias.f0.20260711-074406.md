**statement (plain)** — **Velnias** (Latvian **Velns**) is the chthonic god of Baltic (Lithuanian/Latvian) tradition — originally a god of the dead and protector of herds, associated with wealth, music, and cunning. He is the counterpart and adversary of the thunder-god **Perkūnas**, who pursues him with lightning. Under Christianization his name was reduced to the ordinary word for the devil.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the underworld, cattle, wealth, music, and trickery. He is the opponent whom Perkūnas chases across the sky; in his older form he protects herds and holds wealth and cunning. His later identity as "the devil" is a Christian-era overlay, not the original figure. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Velnias is documented in the cited sources ([[source.gimbutas-1963]], [[source.greimas-1992]]); this is documented Baltic tradition (`status: doctrinal`), recovered from oral tradition and folk-song by scholars. His reduction to the Christian devil should be hedged as a Christian-era demonization rather than the original chthonic god, and the reconstruction of his pre-Christian role rests in part on scholarly inference. **[confirmed as attribution]**
