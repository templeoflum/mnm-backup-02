**statement (plain)** — Primrose's Field Illusion is a motion illusion in which static patterns containing field-like spatial arrangements produce compelling percepts of illusory drift, flow, or directional movement. The phenomenon typically involves arrays of small oriented or patterned elements distributed across a surface in ways that suggest vector fields or flow patterns, despite the absence of any physical motion. When viewed peripherally or during small eye movements, these configurations generate sustained impressions of coordinated movement along specific trajectories determined by the local orientation and spacing of the elements.
