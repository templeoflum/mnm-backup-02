Verbatim citation: Y. Gao, Y. Xiong, X. Gao, K. Jia, J. Pan, Y. Bi, Y. Dai, J. Sun, M. Wang, and H. Wang, "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey," arXiv:2312.10997, 2024.

Contains: a comprehensive review of RAG for LLMs, motivated by hallucination, outdated parametric knowledge, and untraceable reasoning. Defines the progression Naive RAG → Advanced RAG → Modular RAG and the tripartite RAG foundation (retrieval, generation, augmentation), with sections on retriever/generator improvements, evaluation, and open problems. Widely cited as the field's reference survey; serves as the corpus "door."
