**statement (plain)** — The Silhouette Illusion, also known as the "Spinning Dancer," is a bistable apparent motion display created by Japanese web designer Nobuyuki Kayahara in 2003. The animation shows the silhouette of a pirouetting dancer that can be perceived as rotating either clockwise or counterclockwise, with spontaneous reversals occurring during sustained viewing. The ambiguity arises because the silhouette lacks depth cues such as shading, texture, and binocular disparity—all information that would normally specify whether a limb is in front of or behind the body.
