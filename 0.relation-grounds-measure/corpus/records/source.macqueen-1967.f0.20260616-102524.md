**citation (verbatim)** — J. B. MacQueen, "Some Methods for Classification and Analysis of Multivariate Observations," in *Proceedings of the Fifth Berkeley Symposium on Mathematical Statistics and Probability*, vol. 1 (University of California Press, 1967), pp. 281–297.

**what it contains** — introduces and names the **k-means** process: partitioning an N-dimensional population into k sets to obtain reasonably efficient (low within-class variance) partitions from a sample. MacQueen's version is *sequential* — it updates the cluster means as each observation arrives — distinct from the batch (Lloyd) iteration usually taught today. The paper studies the asymptotic behaviour of the procedure.
