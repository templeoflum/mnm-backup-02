**statement (plain)** — Illusion knitting, also known as shadow knitting, is a textile technique that produces knitted fabric containing hidden images or patterns that become visible only when the fabric is viewed from an oblique angle, appearing as uniform texture when viewed perpendicularly. The technique relies on alternating rows of different stitch types that create varying degrees of relief or texture in the fabric surface. When viewed straight-on, these textural variations are subtle and blend together, but when viewed at an acute angle, the raised stitches cast shadows or catch light differentially, revealing the concealed design.
