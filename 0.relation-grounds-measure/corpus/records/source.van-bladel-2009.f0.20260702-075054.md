Kevin van Bladel, *The Arabic Hermes: From Pagan Sage to Prophet of Science* (Oxford University Press, 2009). Traces the transmission and transformation of Hermes Trismegistus into the Arabic-Islamic world — via Sasanian and Ṣābian intermediaries — where he became an ancient prophet and patron of the sciences. Foundational as the standard account of the Hermetic tradition's eastward channel, the missing middle between antiquity and the Latin revival.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Hermeticism section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
