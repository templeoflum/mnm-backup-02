Hasok Chang, *Inventing Temperature: Measurement and Scientific Progress* (Oxford University Press, 2004).

Contains — the foundational history-and-philosophy treatment of the **coordination problem**: how scientists established and validated thermometric standards without circular reliance on the very instruments in question (mercury vs. alcohol expand differently — which is "the" temperature?). Chang's answer is **epistemic iteration** — successive stages of knowledge each built on and correcting the previous, progressing within a continuous tradition rather than resting on a self-evident foundation. The central case for how measurement standards bootstrap.
