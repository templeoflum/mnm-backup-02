**statement (plain)** — **Laima** (from Lithuanian *laimė*, "luck/happiness") is the goddess of fate and fortune in Baltic (Lithuanian/Latvian) tradition. She appears at every birth to decree the child's destiny, and she is the patron of women, marriage, and childbirth. In the folk-songs she weaves the thread of a life's fate.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: fate, luck, childbirth, weaving, and women. She pronounces the newborn's destiny and presides over women's lives; she is often linked with the sun-goddess **Saulė**, and her thread-weaving of destiny is a recurring image in the dainas. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Laima is documented in the cited sources ([[source.gimbutas-1963]], [[source.greimas-1992]], [[source.latvian-dainas]]); this is documented Baltic tradition (`status: doctrinal`), preserved chiefly in the Latvian folk-songs and recorded by scholars and folklorists. The systematic reconstruction of her role rests in part on comparative and structural reading of that oral corpus. **[confirmed as attribution]**
