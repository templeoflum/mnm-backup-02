Ira Robinson (trans.), *Moses Cordovero's Introduction to Kabbalah: An Annotated Translation of His Or Ne'erav* (Yeshiva University Press / KTAV, 1994). Cordovero's own primer defending and introducing the study of Kabbalah — the pre-Lurianic Safed systematizer's gateway text — in annotated English translation.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Safedian Kabbalah. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
