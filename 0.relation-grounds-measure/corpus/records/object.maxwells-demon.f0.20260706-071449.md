**statement (plain)** — A demon controls a trapdoor between two gas chambers, letting fast molecules pass one way and slow the other. It builds a hot side and a cold side without doing macroscopic work — apparently decreasing entropy and violating the second law.

**statement (framework)** — the puzzle that tied **entropy to information**. The resolution (Szilard 1929; Landauer 1961; Bennett 1982): the demon must *measure* and *store* which-molecule information, and **erasing** that memory dissipates at least kT·ln2 of entropy per bit — saving the second law and founding the physics of information. Established.
