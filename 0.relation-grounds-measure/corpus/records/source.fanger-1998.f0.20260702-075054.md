Claire Fanger (ed.), *Conjuring Spirits: Texts and Traditions of Medieval Ritual Magic* (Pennsylvania State University Press, 1998). Foundational essay collection on medieval ritual magic manuscripts — the Sworn Book of Honorius, the Ars Notoria, John of Morigny's visions, necromantic handbooks — that helped establish the modern academic study of the field.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
