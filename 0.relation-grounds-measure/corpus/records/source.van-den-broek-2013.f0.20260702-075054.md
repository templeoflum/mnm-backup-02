Roelof van den Broek, *Gnostic Religion in Antiquity* (Cambridge University Press, 2013). A survey of ancient gnostic religion by one of the senior Dutch scholars of the field — sources (Nag Hammadi and beyond), mythology, practice, and the debate over "Gnosticism" as a category — treating gnosis as a religious phenomenon in its own right rather than a Christian heresy.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Secondary Texts and Gnosticism sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
