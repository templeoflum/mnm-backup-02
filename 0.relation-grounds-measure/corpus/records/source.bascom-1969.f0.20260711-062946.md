William Bascom's *Ifa Divination: Communication Between Gods and Men in West Africa* (Indiana University Press, 1969) is the standard study of the Yoruba **Ifá** divination system, describing the *odu* corpus, the diviner (*babalawo*), and the ritual practice by which the **Orisha** are consulted. Based on Bascom's own fieldwork in Nigeria, it documents the mechanics and the verses of Ifá in detail, and remains the reference work on the tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**
