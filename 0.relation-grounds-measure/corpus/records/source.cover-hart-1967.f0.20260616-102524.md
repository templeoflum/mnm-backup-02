**citation (verbatim)** — T. M. Cover and P. E. Hart, "Nearest Neighbor Pattern Classification," *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*, vol. 13, no. 1 (January 1967), pp. 21–27. DOI 10.1109/TIT.1967.1053964.

**what it contains** — the foundational analysis of the nearest-neighbor (NN) decision rule. Establishes that, in the large-sample limit, the single-nearest-neighbor classification error rate R is bounded above by R*(2 − R*M/(M−1)) where R* is the Bayes error and M the number of classes — i.e. asymptotic NN error never exceeds twice the optimal Bayes error, despite the rule using no model and no training. Treats the k-NN generalization and the consistency of the rule.
