*Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra* (BPHS), attributed to the sage Parāśara — the most cited foundational text of Jyotiṣa (Vedic astrology).

Source for the rāśi (signs), grahas (planets), bhāvas (houses), and the framework of Vedic horoscopy. **[confirmed as attribution]** *(traditional attribution; the extant compilation is medieval. Cited for the rāśi system.)*
