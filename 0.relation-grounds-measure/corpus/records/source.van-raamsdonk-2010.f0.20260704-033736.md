M. Van Raamsdonk, "Building up spacetime with quantum entanglement," *Gen. Rel. Grav.* 42, 2323–2329 (2010); arXiv:1005.3035 (Gravity Research Foundation essay).

Role — argues that reducing entanglement between two boundary regions pulls the corresponding bulk regions apart (tears spacetime): classical connectivity of space is carried by quantum entanglement of the boundary state. Follow-up pedagogy: "Lectures on Gravity and Entanglement," TASI 2015. **[confirmed]**
