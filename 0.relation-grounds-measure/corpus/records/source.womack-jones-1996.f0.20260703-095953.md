James P. Womack & Daniel T. Jones, *Lean Thinking: Banish Waste and Create Wealth in Your Corporation* (Simon & Schuster, 1996; 2nd ed. Free Press, 2003).

Contains — the book that named and generalized "lean production" from the Toyota Production System into five principles: specify **value** (from the customer's view), identify the **value stream** (and remove its waste), make value **flow** without interruption, let the customer **pull**, and pursue **perfection**. Popularized value-stream mapping and pull scheduling as general management practice beyond automotive manufacturing.

Access — ISBN 0-684-81035-2 (1st ed.); in print. Follows the authors' *The Machine That Changed the World* (1990). **[confirmed]**
