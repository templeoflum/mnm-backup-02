David G. Jansson and Steven M. Smith, "Design Fixation," *Design Studies* 12, no. 1 (1991): 3–11.

Contains — the founding study of **design fixation**: across four studies, expert and novice engineers shown a deliberately flawed example design reused its features — even flaws that had been explicitly pointed out — far more than a no-example control. Fixation was measured by counting recurrences of the example's key features. Establishes that exposure to an example anchors and constrains subsequent design, a core result for creativity and engineering education.
