**statement (plain)** — **Oshun** (**Ochún** / **Oxum**) is the **Orisha** of fresh water, love, and beauty, goddess of the river that bears her name. She is the Orisha of sweetness, prosperity, and divination, often named the youngest and most beloved of the Orisha. A diviner and healer, her sweetness can turn to flood when she is scorned.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: fresh water, love, beauty, fertility, prosperity, diplomacy, divination. Orisha of the river Oshun; associated with honey, gold, and brass, and with the colours yellow and amber; a diviner and healer and a wife of **Shango**. Diaspora names: **Ochún** (Santería), **Oxum** (Candomblé). **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — documented in Idowu (1962), Bascom (1969), and Karade (1994); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). It is an oral tradition carried in the **Ifá** corpus (the *odu*) and recorded by scholars; her attributes and her marriages vary between Yorubaland and the diaspora traditions of Santería and Candomblé. **[confirmed as attribution]**
