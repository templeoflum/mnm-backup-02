**statement (plain)** — animals don't just adapt to a fixed world; they remake it, and their descendants inherit the remade version. For humans the remaking is largely *epistemic* — we build tools, symbols, and structured environments that carry cognitive work, and each generation inherits and extends them. Mind is scaffolded by an inherited cognitive niche as much as by the brain.

**basis** — the "cognitive niche" originates with Tooby & DeVore (1987, hominid strategic modeling) and is developed by Pinker (2010); the general evolutionary mechanism is Odling-Smee-Laland-Feldman niche construction (2003, with ecological inheritance); Sterelny (2010) recasts the extended mind as the "scaffolded mind" built on this loop. Active-inference formulation: Constant et al. (2018, variational niche construction).
