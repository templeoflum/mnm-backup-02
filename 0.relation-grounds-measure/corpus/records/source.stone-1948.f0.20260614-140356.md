Full citation — M. H. Stone, "The Generalized Weierstrass Approximation Theorem," *Mathematics Magazine* **21**(4) (1948), 167–184, and **21**(5) (1948), 237–254 (two parts).

Stone's accessible re-exposition with the simplified proof of the theorem he first proved in his 1937 *Trans. AMS* paper. This is the source most often cited for the clean statement and proof. Reprinted in R. C. Buck (ed.), *Studies in Modern Analysis* (MAA, 1962).
