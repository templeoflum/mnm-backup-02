Nicholas Campion, *A History of Western Astrology Volume II: The Medieval and Modern Worlds* (Continuum, 2009). Continues the history from the early medieval transmission (Arabic and Latin) through the Renaissance, the Enlightenment decline, and astrology's 19th–20th-century revivals into New Age culture. Foundational as the standard modern academic survey of medieval and modern Western astrology, completing the two-volume set.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Astrology section. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
