Erika Bourguignon, *Possession* (Chandler & Sharp, 1976).

Contains — the monograph developing her cross-cultural survey: of the societies studied, 77% held possession beliefs, and "possession trance is significantly linked to female participation and occurs more in societies with complex social roles and hierarchies." Extends the trance vs. possession-trance distinction and argues the near-universality of institutionalized altered states is "a matter of major importance, not merely a bit of anthropological esoterica." Companion to her 488-society survey ([[source.bourguignon-1973]]).
