**statement (plain)** — *correspondence* holds that every level of reality mirrors every other through non-causal links — "as above, so below" — binding macrocosm (planets, cosmos) and microcosm (the human body, metals, plants, hours). It is the first of Faivre's four intrinsic characteristics of esotericism: a "theater of mirrors," everything in nature a sign to be read.

**basis** — the *Corpus Hermeticum* and its Renaissance synthesis (Ficino, Pico, Agrippa's three worlds); Yates's account. [[source.corpus-hermeticum]] [[source.yates-1964]] **[confirmed]** *(status `doctrinal`.)*
