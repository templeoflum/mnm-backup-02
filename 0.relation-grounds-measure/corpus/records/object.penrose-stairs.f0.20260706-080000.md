**statement (plain)** — The Penrose Stairs, also known as the Impossible Staircase, is an impossible object created by geneticist Lionel Penrose and his mathematician son Roger Penrose in 1959. The figure depicts a staircase that forms a closed loop while maintaining a consistent upward or downward incline throughout its circuit, creating a paradoxical structure where one could ascend or descend indefinitely without ever changing elevation. The illusion exploits depth cue conflicts in two-dimensional representations of three-dimensional space, where local depth relationships appear geometrically consistent but global integration reveals violations of Euclidean geometry.
