Antonio Sennis (ed.), *Cathars in Question* (York Medieval Press, 2016). Conference volume staging the central current debate in heresy studies — traditionalists versus deconstructionists on whether an organized dualist Cathar church existed at all — with essays from both camps.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Heresy; list note: "A Must Read w/ traditional account." Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
