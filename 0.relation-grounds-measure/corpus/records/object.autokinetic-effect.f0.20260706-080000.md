**statement (plain)** — The Autokinetic Effect is a striking illusion of motion in which a small, stationary point of light viewed in complete darkness appears to move spontaneously and erratically. Despite the objective stability of the light source, observers consistently report perceiving the point drifting, wandering, or jumping in various directions, with individual reports showing considerable variability in the perceived trajectory, speed, and extent of movement. The effect emerges after several seconds of fixation and can persist for extended viewing periods, with the illusory motion patterns changing unpredictably over time.
