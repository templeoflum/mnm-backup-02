U.S. Air Force Instruction (AFI) 91-104, *Nuclear Surety Tamper Control and Detection Programs* — the standing directive codifying the **two-person concept** for U.S. nuclear weapons: no single individual may have the knowledge, ability, or opportunity to perform an unauthorized act on a nuclear weapon; two authorized persons, each capable of detecting an incorrect or unauthorized procedure, are required.

Contains — the formal rule behind the Minuteman dual-key launch (two crews independently validate the order; two keys turned simultaneously in slots too far apart for one person). The asymmetry: either operator may unilaterally *refuse*, but launch requires both.
