W. R. Newman, "'Decknamen or Pseudochemical Language'? Eirenaeus Philalethes and Carl Jung," *Revue d'histoire des sciences* 49 (1996), 159–188. **[confirmed]**

Role — decodes Philalethes's (George Starkey's) lurid metaphors as *Decknamen* for concrete chemical procedures, then replicates them; concludes the analysis "shows that Jung was entirely wrong" and "casts serious doubt on the Jungian interpretation of alchemy as a whole."
