**statement (plain)** — Situation awareness is the operator's up-to-date internal picture of what is going on, in three levels: **Level 1** perceive the relevant elements (readings, states, actors); **Level 2** comprehend what they mean together (this pattern = that condition); **Level 3** project what will happen next (where it is heading). Attention and working memory limit how much SA one can hold; mental models and clear goals let an expert build and maintain it efficiently.

**relevance** — the thing a control-room operator must carry and a handover must transfer intact: a pass-down that conveys only Level-1 data without the Level-2/3 understanding hands over a degraded picture. Alarm floods, high workload, poor displays, and automation surprises all erode SA — which is why alarm management ([[object.alarm-management]]) and good displays ([[object.ecological-interface-design]]) exist. The measurable target the interface and the handover both serve [[source.endsley-1995]] **[confirmed]**.
