**origin** — the Bohr–Einstein debates (from the 1927 Solvay Conference) over whether quantum mechanics is complete: the founding dispute of the field.

**codification** — John von Neumann's *Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics* (1932) formalized measurement and collapse; the EPR argument (1935) [[source.epr-1935]] and Bell's theorem (1964) [[source.bell-1964]] turned interpretation into a question with empirical teeth (locality vs. realism).

**door** — David Albert, *Quantum Mechanics and Experience* — the standard entry to the measurement problem and the main interpretations (Copenhagen, many-worlds, Bohmian, GRW).

*grade s — origin (Bohr–Einstein), codification (von Neumann; EPR; Bell), door (Albert) present. New field-home for the thought-experiments intake (quarantine, 20260706); homes the quantum-foundations cluster (Schrödinger's cat, EPR, Bell, Wigner's friend, the microscope, delayed choice, double slit, quantum eraser).*
