Andrew Weeks, *Boehme: An Intellectual Biography of the Seventeenth-Century Philosopher and Mystic* (SUNY Press, 1991). The standard English intellectual biography of Jacob Boehme, the Görlitz shoemaker-theosopher — situates his visionary dialectic of divine self-revelation in its Lutheran, Paracelsian, and civic context, work by work.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Theosophy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
