**statement (plain)** — **Mielikki** is the mistress of the forest and wife of **Tapio**. She is associated with healing and the care of wounded animals, keeper of the forest's medicines, and she tends the creatures of the wild. Hunters propitiate her along with Tapio to be granted game from the woodland.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: forest, healing, game. Her name may relate to *mieli* ("mind, pleasure") or be read as "the pleasant one," and has been connected to honey — readings that reflect her gentler, benevolent nature but are not settled etymologies. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Mielikki is attested in the Kalevala and treated in modern scholarship ([[source.pentikainen-1999]]); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). Proposed meanings of her name are scholarly and contested and should be held as such. The Kalevala is Elias Lönnrot's nineteenth-century compilation of oral folk poetry, not an ancient text. **[confirmed as attribution]**
