E. B. Dynkin, work on the explicit form of the BCH series (1947).

Role — gave the first actual explicit formula for the BCH series with all numerical coefficients (the "Dynkin form"); hence the formula is sometimes called Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff–Dynkin (BCHD). Separately, Dynkin introduced Dynkin diagrams (also 1947), the modern combinatorial encoding of root systems used in the Killing–Cartan classification.
