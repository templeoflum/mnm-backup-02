Edward Peters, *The Magician, the Witch, and the Law* (University of Pennsylvania Press, 1978). Traces how the figure of the learned magician was assimilated to heresy and sorcery in medieval law and theology — the legal-intellectual groundwork that made the later witch prosecutions possible.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Medieval Heresy. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
