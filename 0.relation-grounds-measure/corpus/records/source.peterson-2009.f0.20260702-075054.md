Joseph H. Peterson (ed.), *Arbatel: Concerning the Magic of the Ancients* (Ibis, 2009). Critical Latin-English edition of the anonymous 1575 Basel grimoire — notable among early modern magical texts for its pious, aphoristic tone and its system of the seven Olympic planetary spirits; a key influence on later ritual magic.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Modern Magic. Access note: the underlying Latin *Arbatel* (1575) is public domain (an edition circulates via Peterson's Esoteric Archives); the Ibis edition is an in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
