Full citation — W. M'F. Orr, "The stability or instability of the steady motions of a perfect liquid and of a viscous liquid," *Proceedings of the Royal Irish Academy. Section A: Mathematical and Physical Sciences*, vol. 27 (1907), pp. 9–68 (Part I) and pp. 69–138 (Part II).

Contains — a systematic linear stability analysis of viscous parallel flows, deriving the fourth-order eigenvalue equation for two-dimensional disturbances that — together with Sommerfeld's 1908 derivation — became known as the **Orr–Sommerfeld equation**.
