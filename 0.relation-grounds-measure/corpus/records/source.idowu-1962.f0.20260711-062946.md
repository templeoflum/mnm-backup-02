E. Bọlaji Idowu's *Olódùmarè: God in Yoruba Belief* (Longmans, 1962) is the classic academic study of Yoruba religion, centred on the supreme being **Olodumare** and the **Orisha** who emanate from and serve under him. Written by a Yoruba theologian and scholar, it argues for the coherence of Yoruba belief as a system with Olodumare at its head, and became a foundational reference for the study of the tradition. **[confirmed as attribution]**
