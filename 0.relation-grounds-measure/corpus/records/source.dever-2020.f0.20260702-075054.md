William G. Dever, *Has Archaeology Buried the Bible?* (Eerdmans, 2020). A leading Syro-Palestinian archaeologist's accessible synthesis of what a century of excavation actually shows about ancient Israel — where the biblical account holds, where it doesn't, and what a historically grounded Israelite religion looked like on the ground.
