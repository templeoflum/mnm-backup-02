Menachem Kellner, *Maimonides' Confrontation with Mysticism* (Littman Library of Jewish Civilization, 2006). Argues that Maimonides' rationalist program was a deliberate confrontation with the proto-kabbalistic, "enchanted" worldview of his day — holiness, Hebrew, Israel, and angels as institutional rather than ontological — a key counterpoint for understanding what Kabbalah asserted.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Early Kabbalah & Foundational Texts. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
