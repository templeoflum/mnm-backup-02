**verbatim citation** — Pinker, Steven, and Ray Jackendoff. "The Faculty of Language: What's Special about It?" *Cognition* 95, no. 2 (2005): 201–236.

**what it contains** — a direct critique of the Hauser–Chomsky–Fitch (2002) "recursion-only" hypothesis for FLN. Argues (1) much of grammar is not recursive yet is language-specific — phonology, morphology, case, agreement, word properties; (2) recursion is found outside language (e.g., visual cognition), so it cannot be the sole human/linguistic novelty; (3) language is better understood as a complex adaptation for communication that evolved piecemeal. Pairs with Fitch, Hauser & Chomsky's reply (Cognition 97, 2005) in a documented exchange.
