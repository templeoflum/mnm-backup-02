Charles S. Carver & Michael F. Scheier, *On the Self-Regulation of Behavior* (Cambridge University Press, 1998).

Contains — the mature statement of the authors' **control-theory model of self-regulation**: behavior is a negative-feedback loop (current state → comparator against a reference/goal → output that reduces the discrepancy). Distinguishes discrepancy-reducing (approach) from discrepancy-enlarging (avoidance) loops, organizes goals hierarchically, derives affect from a second-order (rate-of-progress) loop, and treats disengagement, persistence, and dynamic-systems shifts among goals. Wiener/Ashby feedback applied to the self.
