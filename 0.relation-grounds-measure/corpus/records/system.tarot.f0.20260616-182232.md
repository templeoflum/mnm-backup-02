**origin** — tarot cards appear in 15th-c. Italy as a card game (*tarocchi*); their **esoteric/divinatory** use was developed in 18th–19th-c. France (Court de Gébelin, Etteilla) and the occult revival (Éliphas Lévi, the Golden Dawn). [[source.waite-1911]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — the **Rider-Waite-Smith** deck (A. E. Waite & Pamela Colman Smith, 1909) and Waite's *Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1911) became the standard modern reference — 22 Major Arcana + 56 Minor Arcana (Wands/Cups/Swords/Pentacles, Ace–10 + Page/Knight/Queen/King). [[source.waite-1911]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — Waite, *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1911).

**lineage** — game-origin vs. esoteric-overlay (the divinatory meanings are early-modern, not medieval) — carried as a note; meanings here follow the RWS tradition. Members link to [[system.classical-elements]] (suit elements) and [[field.kabbalah]] (the Golden Dawn correspondences). Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
