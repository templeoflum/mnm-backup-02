**statement (plain)** — Kopfermann Cubes are a series of line drawings systematically created by Hertha Kopfermann in 1930 to investigate the conditions under which two-dimensional patterns are perceived as three-dimensional objects versus flat configurations. Kopfermann varied specific geometric properties such as angles, line lengths, and orientations to create a range of cube-like figures that differed in their tendency to evoke three-dimensional versus two-dimensional interpretations. Her research demonstrated that small changes in the stimulus configuration could dramatically alter whether observers perceive a drawing as representing a three-dimensional cube or merely as a flat pattern of lines in the picture plane.
