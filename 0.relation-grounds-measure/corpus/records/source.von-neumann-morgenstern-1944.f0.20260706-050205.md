John von Neumann & Oskar Morgenstern, *Theory of Games and Economic Behavior* (Princeton UP, 1944) — founds game theory and proves the **expected-utility theorem**: an agent whose preferences over lotteries satisfy a few axioms acts as if maximizing expected utility. The axiomatic backbone of decision theory. **[confirmed as attribution]**
