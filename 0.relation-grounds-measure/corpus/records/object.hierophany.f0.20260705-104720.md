**statement (plain)** — a *hierophany* is the manifestation of the sacred; for Eliade "the manifestation of the sacred ontologically founds the world." For *homo religiosus* space and time are non-homogeneous; the sacred is "an absolute reality, opposed to the nonreality of the vast surrounding expanse." A hierophany reveals a fixed point — a center — out of the formless flux, orienting the world.
