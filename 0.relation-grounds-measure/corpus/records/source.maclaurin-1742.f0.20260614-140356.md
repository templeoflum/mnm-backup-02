Full citation — C. Maclaurin, *A Treatise of Fluxions*, 2 vols., Edinburgh, 1742.

Contains Maclaurin's independent derivation of the summation formula now bearing his and Euler's names, used to evaluate integrals and sums. Discovered around the same time as Euler's (mid-1730s) and published in 1742; the two derivations were independent.

Role — origin (Maclaurin side) of the Euler–Maclaurin summation formula. Euler's prior publication is E25 (1738) [[source.euler-1738]].

Access — historical scans; widely catalogued.
