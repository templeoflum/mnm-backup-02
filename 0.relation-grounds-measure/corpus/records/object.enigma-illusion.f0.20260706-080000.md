**statement (plain)** — The Enigma Illusion, created by artist Isia Leviant in 1984, produces a compelling perception of rotary or swirling motion in a completely static image. The illusion consists of concentric rings or circular contours positioned over a background pattern of radiating lines that alternate in color or luminance. When observers view this configuration, they typically report seeing continuous circular motion within the rings, as though the pattern is rotating or flowing despite the complete absence of physical movement.
