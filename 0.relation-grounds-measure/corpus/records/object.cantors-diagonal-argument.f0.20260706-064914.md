**statement (plain)** — Suppose the infinite binary sequences (or reals) could be listed against the naturals. Build a new sequence differing from the n-th listed one in its n-th digit. This diagonal sequence differs from every entry, so it was not on the list — contradiction. No such list exists: the reals are uncountable.

**statement (framework)** — a **proven theorem** founding the hierarchy of infinities (there are strictly more reals than naturals). The diagonal method itself recurs across mathematics and logic — in Gödel's incompleteness, Turing's halting problem, and the theory of computability. Proven.
