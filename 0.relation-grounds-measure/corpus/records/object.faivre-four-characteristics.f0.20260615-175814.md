**statement (plain)** — Faivre defines esotericism as a "form of thought" by four *intrinsic* characteristics, whose simultaneous presence is necessary and sufficient: (1) **correspondences**, (2) **living nature**, (3) **imagination & mediations**, (4) **experience of transmutation** — plus two *secondary* traits (praxis of concordance, transmission). The standard typological definition of the academic field.

**basis** — Faivre, *Access to Western Esotericism* (1994). [[source.faivre-1994]] **[confirmed]** *(Hanegraaff later recasts the field on an empirical-historical, anti-essentialist basis — see [[field.western-esotericism]].)*
