**statement (plain)** — The Face on Mars refers to a mesa formation in the Cydonia region of Mars that appeared to resemble a humanoid face in images captured by NASA's Viking 1 orbiter on July 25, 1976. The original photograph, taken at a resolution of approximately 43 meters per pixel under specific illumination conditions, showed what appeared to be two eyes, a nose, and a mouth arranged in face-like configuration. This image became one of the most famous examples of pareidolia in the space age, generating substantial public interest and speculation about artificial structures on Mars.
