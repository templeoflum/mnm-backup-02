Michael Ashkenazi's *Handbook of Japanese Mythology* (ABC-CLIO, 2003) is the standard modern English reference to Japanese myth. It surveys the Shinto kami, the folk deities, and the Buddhist-syncretic figures of the living tradition, covering both the classical Kojiki–Nihon Shoki cycle and the deities of later popular worship. **[confirmed as attribution]**
