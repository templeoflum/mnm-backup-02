**statement (plain)** — The Scintillating Grid Illusion is a dynamic brightness illusion in which white dots located at the intersections of gray lines on a dark background appear to flicker or scintillate between bright and dark as observers move their gaze across the pattern. The illusory dark spots appear transiently at intersections in peripheral vision, but disappear when directly fixated. Discovered by Elke Lingelbach in 1994, this illusion represents a compelling variant of the classical Hermann Grid illusion, with the addition of white dots at intersections producing a dramatically enhanced dynamic effect.
