John Leslie, *The End of the World: The Science and Ethics of Human Extinction* (Routledge, 1996) — the extended formalization of the Doomsday argument (originating with Brandon Carter, c. 1983; independently Gott 1993): treating one's birth rank as a random sample updates the expected total human population downward. **[confirmed as attribution]**
