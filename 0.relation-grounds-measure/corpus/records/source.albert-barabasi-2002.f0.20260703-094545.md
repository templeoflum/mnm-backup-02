Réka Albert & Albert-László Barabási, "Statistical mechanics of complex networks," *Reviews of Modern Physics* 74, no. 1 (2002): 47–97.

Contains — the field-defining review: empirical degree distributions across domains, the small-world and clustering properties, the Barabási–Albert model and its variants, and a statistical-mechanics framework for the topology and evolution of real networks. The standard reference that turned a 1999 result into a research programme.
