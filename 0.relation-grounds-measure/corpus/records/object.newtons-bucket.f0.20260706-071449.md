**statement (plain)** — A bucket of water hangs on a twisted rope; released, it spins. At first the bucket turns but the water is still and flat. Later the water spins with the bucket and its surface becomes concave — yet now water and bucket are at rest relative to each other. So the concavity (true rotation) can't be motion relative to the bucket; Newton says it's relative to absolute space.

**statement (framework)** — Newton's argument for **absolute space and time**; challenged by Leibniz and Mach (rotation relative to the distant masses of the universe — 'Mach's principle'), a debate that fed into general relativity. The touchstone of the absolute-vs-relational dispute. Established; **open**.
