Ven Te Chow, *Open-Channel Hydraulics* (McGraw-Hill, 1959).

Contains — the canonical treatment of flow with a free surface in channels: uniform and gradually/rapidly varied flow, the Chézy (1770s) and Manning (1889) resistance formulas, specific energy, critical/subcritical/supercritical regimes, hydraulic jumps, and the design of banks, beds, weirs, and sluices as the resistance landscape that shapes the flow. The direct hydraulic lineage of the word "channel."
