**statement (plain)** — The Dynamic Luminance-Gradient Effect is a temporal variant of classical luminance gradient illusions in which smoothly varying changes in luminance gradients over time produce enhanced or novel lightness perception effects. While static luminance gradients have long been known to influence lightness perception through mechanisms related to spatial filtering and edge detection, the dynamic version introduces temporal modulation that can amplify illusory effects or produce qualitatively different perceptual experiences. In typical demonstrations, regions that gradually transition from containing one gradient profile to another can produce enhanced simultaneous contrast effects, apparent motion of lightness boundaries, or bistable perceptual states where the same physical stimulus yields alternating interpretations.
