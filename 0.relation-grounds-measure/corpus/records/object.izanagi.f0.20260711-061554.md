**statement (plain)** — **Izanagi** is the primordial male creator kami. With his consort **Izanami** he stirred the ocean with the jeweled spear (Ame-no-nuboko) and, as the brine dripped from its tip, formed the first island; together they bore the islands of Japan and many kami. After Izanami died bearing fire and he failed to bring her back from **Yomi** (the land of the dead), his cleansing bath (misogi) produced the greatest kami: **Amaterasu** from his left eye, **Tsukuyomi** from his right eye, and **Susanoo** from his nose.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: creation, purification, life, the male principle. He is the father-god of the creation cycle and the source, through his purification, of the sun, moon, and storm kami. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — Izanagi is attested in the *Kojiki* (712) and the *Nihon Shoki* (720); this is documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The classical myths are codified in the two 8th-century chronicles, which diverge on details of the creation and the descent to Yomi; the accounts are variant between Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. **[confirmed as attribution]**
