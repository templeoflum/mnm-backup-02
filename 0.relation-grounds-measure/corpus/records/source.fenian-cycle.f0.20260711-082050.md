The **Fenian Cycle** (the *Fiannaíocht*, also the **Ossianic Cycle**). The body of Irish tales of **Fionn mac Cumhaill** and his roving warrior-band the **Fianna**, set in the reign of **Cormac mac Airt**. Its central compilation is **Acallam na Senórach** ("Colloquy of the Ancients," c. 1200), a frame-tale in which the aged Fianna warriors **Oisín** and **Caílte** recount the deeds of Fionn to St. Patrick. The cycle includes the Salmon of Knowledge, the elopement of **Diarmuid and Gráinne**, and the later Ossianic ballads. One of the four great cycles of Irish literary tradition, distinct from the **Ulster Cycle**. **[confirmed as attribution]**
