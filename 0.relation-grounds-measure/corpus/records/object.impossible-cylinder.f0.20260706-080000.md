**statement (plain)** — The Impossible Cylinder is an impossible object that depicts a cylindrical structure with contradictory cross-sections that cannot coexist in three-dimensional space. The figure typically shows a cylinder or tube where one end appears circular when viewed from a particular orientation while the other end appears rectangular or otherwise non-circular, despite the cylinder maintaining apparently straight and continuous sides connecting these incompatible endpoints. This creates a paradoxical percept where the same three-dimensional object appears to smoothly transition from one geometric form to an incompatible geometric form without any visible distortion or tapering of its lateral surfaces.
