J. J. Sledge, "The ESOTERICA Library" (April 2024) — a curated, deliberately non-exhaustive list of ~160 foundational texts for the study of Western esotericism up to ~1650: one entry door, foundational primary and secondary tiers, eighteen sub-domain sections, and a language-tools section. Selection criteria stated: foundational, in print, in English, affordable, not overly specialized. Methodology discussed at https://youtu.be/1-S95SMkBVw.
