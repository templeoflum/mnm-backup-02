Plutarch, *Life of Theseus* §23 (c. 75 CE) — reports that the Athenians preserved Theseus's ship by replacing decayed planks, and that philosophers disputed whether it remained the same ship: the classical statement of the identity-over-time puzzle. Hobbes (*De Corpore*, 1655) later added the reassembly variant. **[confirmed as attribution]**
