**statement (plain)** — The Möbius Strip represents a unique class of perceptual and cognitive illusions arising from topological properties that violate ordinary spatial intuitions. Named after mathematician August Ferdinand Möbius, this single-sided, non-orientable surface challenges fundamental assumptions about inside versus outside, front versus back, and continuous orientation in three-dimensional space. When observers trace a path along the surface of a Möbius strip, they return to their starting point having traversed what appears to be both sides of the surface without crossing an edge, creating a profound violation of expected spatial relationships.
