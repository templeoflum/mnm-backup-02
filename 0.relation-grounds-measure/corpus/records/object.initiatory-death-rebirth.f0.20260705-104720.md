**statement (plain)** — initiation is a change of existential/ontological status enacted as symbolic death and rebirth. "Death" is a temporary return to Chaos, "the end of a mode of being — the mode of ignorance and of the child's irresponsibility"; "initiatory death provides the clean slate on which will be written the successive revelations… the formation of a new man." Initiatory spaces (huts, caves, chambers) are "both the grave and the womb." "It is only in initiation that death is given a positive value."

**basis** — Eliade, *Rites and Symbols of Initiation* (1958). [[source.eliade-1958]] **[confirmed on attribution; contested — see [[object.sui-generis-critique]].]**
