Bernard McGinn (ed.), *Meister Eckhart and the Beguine Mystics: Hadewijch of Brabant, Mechthild of Magdeburg, and Marguerite Porete* (Continuum, 1994). Collected scholarly essays arguing that Eckhart's apophatic mysticism developed in dialogue with the vernacular theology of the beguines — the key study connecting the two currents.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Western Christian Mysticism. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
