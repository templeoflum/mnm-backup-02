The **Book of the Dead**, the Egyptian *Book of Coming Forth by Day* (c. 1550 BCE onward). The New Kingdom collection of funerary spells written on papyrus and placed with the dead, developed out of the earlier Pyramid Texts and Coffin Texts. Its spells guide the deceased through the underworld and the judgment of the dead — including the weighing of the heart against the feather of **Maat** before **Osiris** (spell 125) — to secure a favourable afterlife. The standard source for the New Kingdom afterlife myths, its papyri varying in selection and arrangement of spells. **[confirmed as attribution]**
