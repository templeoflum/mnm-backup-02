William R. Newman, *Newton the Alchemist: Science, Enigma, and the Quest for Nature's "Secret Fire"* (Princeton University Press, 2018). A reconstruction of Isaac Newton's decades of alchemical experimentation from his laboratory notebooks and manuscripts, arguing his chymistry was rigorous experimental science rather than mystical aberration. Foundational for the modern rehabilitation of alchemy within the history of science.
