J. Tooby & I. DeVore, "The reconstruction of hominid evolution through strategic modeling," in W. G. Kinzey (ed.), *The Evolution of Human Behavior: Primate Models* (SUNY Press, 1987).

Role — coins the "cognitive niche": the "core of our zoological distinctiveness" as "conceptually abstracting from a situation a model of what manipulations are necessary to achieve proximate goals that correlate with fitness."
