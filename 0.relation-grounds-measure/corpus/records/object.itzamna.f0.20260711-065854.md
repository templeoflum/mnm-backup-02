**statement (plain)** — **Itzamná** is the aged supreme god of the Maya, lord of the sky and of day. He is credited with giving humankind **writing** and the **calendar**, and is patron of scribes, priests, and healers. He is often shown as a toothless old man with a large nose.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: creation, the heavens, the day, writing, the calendar, healing. Supreme creator of the Maya; giver of **writing** and the **calendar**; patron of scribes, priests, and healers, depicted as an aged, large-nosed figure. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited source; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Maya record rests chiefly on post-conquest colonial sources and later scholarship; his attributes are variously reported and should be held loosely. **[confirmed as attribution]**
