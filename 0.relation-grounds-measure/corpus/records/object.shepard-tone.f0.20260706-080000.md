**statement (plain)** — The Shepard tone is an auditory illusion of a tone that appears to ascend or descend continuously in pitch without ever getting higher or lower, creating the impression of an infinite staircase in pitch space. Created by Roger Shepard in 1964, the illusion is produced by superimposing sine wave components separated by octaves, with each component's amplitude controlled by a bell-shaped spectral envelope that remains fixed as the frequencies glide upward or downward. As higher frequency components fade out at the top of the envelope, lower components simultaneously fade in at the bottom, creating the perceptual experience of continuous directional change despite the cyclic physical structure.
