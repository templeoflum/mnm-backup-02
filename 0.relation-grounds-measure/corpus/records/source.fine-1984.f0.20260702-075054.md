Lawrence Fine, *Safed Spirituality: Rules of Mystical Piety, The Beginning of Wisdom* (Paulist Press, 1984). Translations of devotional texts from the sixteenth-century kabbalistic community of Safed — the hanhagot (rules of mystical piety) of Cordovero, Luria's circle, and others, plus selections from Elijah de Vidas's *Reshit Hokhmah* (*The Beginning of Wisdom*) — with Fine's introduction.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Foundational Primary Texts and Safedian Kabbalah sections. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book. **[confirmed as attribution]**
