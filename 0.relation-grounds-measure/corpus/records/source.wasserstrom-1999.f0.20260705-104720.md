Steven M. Wasserstrom, *Religion after Religion: Gershom Scholem, Mircea Eliade, and Henry Corbin at Eranos* (Princeton, 1999) — argues the three Eranos thinkers de-emphasized law, ritual, and social history and extolled the symbolism of mystical experience, a program he reads as crypto-theological. (Wasserstrom is himself criticized in turn — e.g. Benavides — so the critique is two-sided.)
