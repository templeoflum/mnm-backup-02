**statement (plain)** — Horsemaning is a photographic technique and internet meme in which two people position themselves to create the visual illusion of a single decapitated body, with one person showing only their headless torso while a second person positions their head to appear separated from the body. The name references the Headless Horseman of folklore and became popular as a revival of a 1920s photography fad. This phenomenon exploits several principles of visual perception, including the visual system's tendency to group spatially proximate elements into unified objects, the limited depth information available in two-dimensional photographs, and top-down expectations about human body configuration that can be violated through careful staging.
