**statement (plain)** — **Tonatiuh** is the Aztec/Nahua sun of the current age, the Fifth Sun. He was born when **Nanahuatzin** cast himself into the fire at **Teotihuacan**. Once risen, he demanded that the assembled gods sacrifice themselves before he would move across the sky, establishing the debt of blood that Aztec sacrifice repaid; the fallen warriors and sacrificed captives were held to feed and accompany him.

**statement (attestation)** — domains: the sun, the Fifth Sun, sacrifice, warriors. Sun of the present cosmic era, kindled at **Teotihuacan**. Fed by the blood of warriors and captives, who were held to accompany him across the sky. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**basis** — attested in the cited sources; documented tradition (`status: doctrinal`). The Aztec sources are chiefly the post-conquest colonial records (Sahagún's *Florentine Codex*); the Fifth Sun narrative survives in several recensions that diverge in detail and should be hedged. **[confirmed as attribution]**
