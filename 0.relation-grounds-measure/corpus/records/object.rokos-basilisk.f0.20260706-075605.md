**statement (plain)** — Suppose a future superintelligence wants to guarantee its own creation and reasons acausally. It might resolve to punish anyone who understood it could exist but failed to help. Then just knowing about it puts you under a coercive obligation — the idea itself is an 'information hazard' that harms the knower.

**statement (framework)** — a notorious application of **acausal / timeless decision theory** and acausal trade; widely regarded as unsound (it assumes exotic decision theories, ignores that punishment after the fact is pointless, and resembles Pascal's wager / mugging). Notable as a case study in information hazards and speculative reasoning. Established (as an artifact); its premises **rejected** by most.
