**statement (plain)** — an analytical typology of claimed knowledge: **reason** (logos) is both communicable and verifiable/falsifiable; **faith** (pistis) is communicable but not verifiable/falsifiable; **gnosis** is "a superior type of knowledge that is neither communicable nor verifiable/falsifiable" — the structurally key claim for the transmission problem. Offered as a typology, NOT a description of actual historical currents (against the older Quispel approach).

**basis** — Hanegraaff, "Reason, Faith, and Gnosis" (2008), citing *Corpus Hermeticum* IX,10: gnosis is "a gift from God… that can no longer be communicated but only beheld directly by some faculty beyond the senses and reason." [[source.hanegraaff-2008]] **[confirmed]**
