**origin** — Hesiod's **Theogony** (c. 700 BCE) — the foundational genealogy and cosmogony of the gods, from Chaos through the Titans to the sovereignty of Zeus — together with **Homer's** *Iliad* and *Odyssey*, the epics that give the Olympians their characters and the heroic world its shape. [[source.hesiod-theogony]] [[source.homer]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — (pseudo-)Apollodorus, the **Bibliotheca** (1st–2nd c. CE) — the systematic mythographic handbook that gathers the genealogies and myths into a connected narrative, the ancient reference most later summaries depend on. [[source.apollodorus-bibliotheca]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Robin Hard, *The Routledge Handbook of Greek Mythology* (2004) — the standard modern English reference (with Timothy Gantz, *Early Greek Myth*, 1993, for the archaic sources). [[source.hard-2004]]
