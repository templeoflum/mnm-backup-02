**statement (plain)** — being shown an example design narrows, rather than seeds, the search: designers reproduce the example's features — including flaws that were explicitly pointed out — far more than designers given no example. The example becomes an anchor the mind cannot easily leave. A form of fixation specific to generative design work.

**statement (framework)** — the design-domain member of the fixation family [[object.functional-fixedness]] · [[object.einstellung-effect]]; countered by defixating instructions (told *what to avoid*, not just warned of general risk), incubation, and reflecting on one's own past fixation.

**basis** — Jansson & Smith's four studies [[source.jansson-smith-1991]], fixation measured by recurrence of the example's key features vs. a no-example control. *Support* — heavily replicated in engineering-design and creativity research. *Limits* — examples can also *help* (analogical transfer) — the effect depends on how examples are framed and timed. **[confirmed as attribution]**

**sources** — [[source.jansson-smith-1991]] (the founding study). **[confirmed as attribution]**
