**statement (plain)** — A solid ball can be partitioned into as few as five disjoint pieces which, moved only by rotations and translations (no stretching), reassemble into two solid balls each the size of the original. One sphere becomes two.

**statement (framework)** — a **proven theorem** — the pieces are non-measurable sets, constructible only via the **axiom of choice**; 'volume' is not preserved because the pieces have no well-defined volume. Often cited as a reason to doubt (or accept the strangeness of) the axiom of choice. Proven.
