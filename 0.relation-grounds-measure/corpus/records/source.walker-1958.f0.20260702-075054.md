D. P. Walker, *Spiritual and Demonic Magic: From Ficino to Campanella* (Warburg Institute, 1958). Classic Warburg study of Renaissance magical theory — Ficino's astral-spiritual magic and its defenders, critics, and demonic shadow through Agrippa, Bruno, and Campanella; the companion pillar to Yates's account of the Hermetic tradition.

Recommended in the ESOTERICA library list (J. J. Sledge, April 2024), Renaissance Magic. Acquisition path unresearched; in-print book (Penn State reprint). **[confirmed as attribution]**
