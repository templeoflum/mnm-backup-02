M. E. Houle, "Local intrinsic dimensionality I: an extreme-value-theoretic foundation for similarity applications," SISAP 2017.

Role — grounds LID in extreme-value theory: the local dimension at a point is the rate parameter governing how the probability measure of a neighborhood scales with its radius, defined per-point rather than per-dataset. The theoretical anchor of the LID line (estimation: Amsaleg et al., KDD 2015 / DMKD 2018). **[confirmed]**
