Donella H. Meadows, *Thinking in Systems: A Primer*, ed. Diana Wright (Chelsea Green, 2008; Meadows d. 2001).

Contains — the widely used cross-domain introduction to systems thinking: stocks and flows, balancing and reinforcing feedback loops, delays, resilience, and system traps. Its signature contribution is the ranked list of **leverage points** — "places to intervene in a system," from weak (adjusting parameters) to strong (changing goals, paradigms, and the power to transcend paradigms). The vocabulary of where and how to intervene in a flow system.

Access — ISBN 978-1-60358-055-7; in print. The leverage-points ranking derives from Meadows's 1999 Sustainability Institute essay. **[confirmed]**
