Full citation — J. Stirling, *Methodus Differentialis: sive Tractatus de Summatione et Interpolatione Serierum Infinitarum*, London, 1730.

Contains the result that the undetermined constant in de Moivre's factorial asymptotic equals √(2π), completing n! ∼ √(2πn)(n/e)^n, together with an asymptotic series in 1/n. Published within months of de Moivre's *Miscellanea Analytica* (1730) [[source.demoivre-1730]]; the two worked in close contact.

Role — origin (the constant √(2π)) of Stirling's approximation; the formula is named after Stirling though the leading asymptotic is de Moivre's.

Access — historical scans; English translation by I. Tweddle (Springer, 2003).
