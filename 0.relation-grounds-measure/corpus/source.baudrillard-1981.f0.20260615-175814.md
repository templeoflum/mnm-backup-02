Jean Baudrillard, *Simulacra and Simulation* (*Simulacres et simulation*, Galilée, 1981; English trans. Sheila Faria Glaser, University of Michigan Press, 1994) — the *simulacrum* (a copy without an original) and the "precession of simulacra"; the sign detached from any referent. A key source for why borrowed symbolic systems hollow out or "go dead."
