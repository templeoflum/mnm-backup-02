P. Billingsley, *Probability and Measure* (Wiley, 1st ed. 1979; subsequent eds. 1986, 1995, 2012).

Standard graduate text developing measure and integration as the foundation of probability — the bridge from Lebesgue measure to Kolmogorov's measure-theoretic axiomatization (1933). A door into the probability side of measure theory. **[confirmed]**
