Varela, F. J. (1996). Neurophenomenology: A Methodological Remedy for the Hard Problem. *Journal of Consciousness Studies*, 3(4), 330–349.

Contains — proposes *neurophenomenology*: a research program marrying cognitive neuroscience with a disciplined (phenomenological) approach to lived experience, in which first-person accounts and third-person neural data mutually constrain one another. Responds to Chalmers's hard problem of consciousness; the methodological keystone for first-person methods in contemplative science.
