Full citation — C. F. Gauss, *Mathematisches Tagebuch* entry of 25 October 1800 (the limiting digit/remainder law); and Gauss to P.-S. Laplace, letter of 30 January 1812, in *Carl Friedrich Gauss Werke*, vol. X.1 (ed. Königliche Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften, Göttingen).

Role — earliest statement of the continued-fraction distribution result for [[object.gauss-kuzmin-theorem]]. Gauss recorded the limiting law in his diary in 1800 and, in the 1812 letter, asked Laplace to estimate the rate of convergence — the question ("Gauss's problem") that went unsolved for over a century until Kuzmin (1928).

Access — Gauss *Werke* digitized at the Göttingen digitization centre / archive.org. The 1800 diary date and the 30 Jan 1812 Laplace-letter date are reported consistently by Encyclopedia of Mathematics, Wikipedia, and Wolfram MathWorld. Distinct from [[source.gauss-1801]] (Disquisitiones Arithmeticae), which does not contain this result. **[confirmed as attribution]**
