**citation** — Konrad Z. Lorenz, "The Companion in the Bird's World," *The Auk*, vol. 54, no. 3 (1 July 1937), pp. 245–273.

**role** — the English-language condensation of the 1935 "Kumpan" monograph ([[source.lorenz-1935]]); the principal English source through which the concepts of the releaser, the innate releasing schema, and *Prägung* (imprinting) reached the anglophone behavioral-science literature.

**access** — open / public via the AOU/Auk archive (Oxford Academic; klha.at PDF; USF digitalcommons).
