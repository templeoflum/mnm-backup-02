# klein-2008

**Citation (verbatim):** Klein, G. (2008). Naturalistic decision making. *Human Factors, 50*(3), 456-460. https://doi.org/10.1518/001872008X288385

**Contains:** A short retrospective review, written for the journal's 50th-anniversary set, of the NDM research approach: its emergence in the 1980s to study how people make decisions in real-world settings, its defining task features (time pressure, uncertainty, ill-defined goals, high stakes, team and organizational constraints), its methods (cognitive task analysis, the critical decision method), and its central finding that experience lets people rapidly categorize situations and act. Serves as a concise entry door to the field's models and contributions.
