H. Lebesgue, *Intégrale, longueur, aire* — doctoral thesis, Sorbonne, 1902 (under Borel); published in *Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata* (3) **7** (1902), 231–359.

Extends Borel's notion of measurable sets and defines the Lebesgue integral, handling pathological functions beyond the reach of the Riemann integral. The codifying work of measure theory.
