**citation** — Sol LeWitt, "Paragraphs on Conceptual Art," *Artforum* 5(10), June 1967, pp. 79–83.

**role** — pre-computational anchor for the keystone concept [[object.process-as-artwork]]: "the idea becomes a machine that makes the art," and "all of the planning and decisions are made beforehand and the execution is a perfunctory affair." The artist authors a system; its running produces the work.

**access** — essay widely anthologised; full text reproduced in conceptual-art readers and online (Wikiquote, museum archives).

**confidence** — **[confirmed]** (essay, title, and Artforum 1967 venue confirmed by multiple secondary sources; the catch-phrase is sometimes dated to 1965 — carried as lineage on the object).
