Robert L. Goldstone, "Perceptual Learning," *Annual Review of Psychology*, 49, 585–612 (1998). DOI 10.1146/annurev.psych.49.1.585. PMID 9496632.

Defines perceptual learning as relatively long-lasting changes to a perceptual system that improve its ability to respond to the environment, and organizes the literature around four mechanisms: **attention weighting**, **imprinting** (developing receptors/detectors for stimuli), **differentiation**, and **unitization**. The standard entry point for the modern field.

Available via Annual Reviews (paywalled); widely circulated PDF.
