Pickands III, J. (1975). "Statistical Inference Using Extreme Order Statistics." *The Annals of Statistics* 3(1):119–131. doi:10.1214/aos/1176343003.

Role — co-origin of the peaks-over-threshold (POT) approach: shows that excesses over a high threshold are asymptotically generalized Pareto. Half of the Pickands–Balkema–de Haan theorem; distinct from Balkema & de Haan (1974).
