**origin** — Aušra Augustinavičiūtė ('Augusta') in 1970s Lithuania, combining Jung's *Psychological Types* with Antoni Kępiński's theory of information metabolism. [[source.augustinaviciute-socionics]]

**codification** — sixteen types, each a four-letter code and a pseudonym (Don Quixote, Hamlet, Napoleon…); the model adds intertype-relation theory.

**door** — the works of A. Augustinavičiūtė (foundational socionics texts).

**standing** — **not recognized in mainstream Western academic psychology**; an active tradition mainly in the post-Soviet sphere. Held `doctrinal`. Distinct from MBTI despite the shared four-letter look and Jungian root.
