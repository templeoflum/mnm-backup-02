**citation** — J.-L. Lagrange, *Méchanique analitique* (Paris, 1788; 2nd ed. *Mécanique analytique*, 1811). The multiplier appears in the statics section, applying virtual velocities to constrained mechanical systems; the 2nd edition (1811) stresses the multipliers' importance for constrained optimization.

**role** — origin of the method of Lagrange multipliers for equality-constrained optimization; the direct ancestor of the [[object.kkt-conditions]] (which generalize it to inequalities) and of the Lagrangian in [[object.convexity-and-lagrange-duality]]. Predecessor idea: virtual velocities, from Johann Bernoulli's 1715 letter to Varignon.

**access** — historical text; method is standard in every multivariable-calculus and optimization course. Secondary: MacTutor; Wikipedia "Lagrange multiplier."

**[confirmed]** — Lagrange's authorship of the multiplier method is uncontested.
