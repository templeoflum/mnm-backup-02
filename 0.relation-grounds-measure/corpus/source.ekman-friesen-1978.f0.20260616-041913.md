Ekman, P., & Friesen, W. V., *Facial Action Coding System: A Technique for the Measurement of Facial Movement.* Palo Alto, CA: Consulting Psychologists Press, 1978.

The original published manual for FACS — an anatomically based, comprehensive system for categorizing visible facial movement by underlying muscle action into Action Units (AUs). Self-instructional; designed for certification by testing. (Lineage: adapts the muscle-action taxonomy of anatomist Carl-Herman Hjortsjö.) A later 2002 revision adds Joseph C. Hager as co-author.

Access: monograph/standard, not open-access; widely cited as the canonical FACS reference. Citation detail confirmed via secondary indexes; primary manual not opened.
