**statement (plain)** — within a creator's records, keep them in the order the creator actually used. Don't re-sort the folders alphabetically or by topic; the order itself carries information about how the creator worked and why the records exist.

**statement** — original order (l'ordre primitif; respect de l'ordre intérieur) is the principle that records within a [[object.fonds]] are maintained in "the organization and sequence of records established by the creator of the records" ([[source.saa-dictionary]]). It is the internal limb of [[object.provenance]]: respect des fonds separates creators from one another, original order preserves the arrangement inside each creator's holdings. **[confirmed]**

**basis** — the creator's own arrangement encodes context — the functions, transactions, and working logic that produced the records. Preserving it keeps that contextual evidence and makes the records usable as an organic whole; re-sorting by an external scheme erases it. The principle is normative but not absolute: archivists may reconstruct or document order where it is lost, and digital, virtual, and multimedia records strain a single physical "order." **[confirmed]**

**lineage** — the internal complement to respect des fonds (de Wailly, 1841). Given its canonical statement in the Dutch Manual ([[source.muller-feith-fruin-1898]], 1898), whose Rule 16 — held by the authors as the most important — directs that arrangement "must be based on the original organization of the archival collection, which in the main corresponds to the organization of the administrative body that produced it." Carried forward by Jenkinson ([[source.jenkinson-1922]]) and Schellenberg ([[source.schellenberg-1956]]). **[confirmed]**
