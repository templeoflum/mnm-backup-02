Full citation — A. de Polignac, "Recherches nouvelles sur les nombres premiers," *Comptes rendus hebdomadaires des séances de l'Académie des sciences*, Paris, t. 29 (1849).

Contains the general conjecture that for every natural number k there are infinitely many primes p such that p + 2k is also prime. The case k = 1 (gap 2) is the twin prime conjecture; the general statement is Polignac's conjecture.

Role — the standard attribution source for [[object.twin-prime-conjecture]]. The conjecture is often called simply "the twin prime conjecture" with no single named originator; de Polignac (1849) is the earliest documented printed statement of the general form.
