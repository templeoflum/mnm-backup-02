L. R. Ford, Jr. and D. R. Fulkerson, "Maximal Flow through a Network," *Canadian Journal of Mathematics* 8 (1956): 399–404.

Contains: the formulation of the maximum-flow problem in a directed network with arc capacities; the max-flow min-cut theorem (the maximum flow value equals the minimum cut capacity); and the labeling / augmenting-path method that finds a maximum flow.

Access notes: published by the Canadian Mathematical Society, available via Cambridge Core. DOI 10.4153/CJM-1956-045-5.
