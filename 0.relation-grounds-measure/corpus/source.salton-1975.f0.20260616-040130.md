Verbatim: Salton, G., Wong, A., and Yang, C. S. (1975). "A Vector Space Model for Automatic Indexing." *Communications of the ACM* 18(11):613–620. DOI 10.1145/361219.361220.

Contains: the formulation of documents and queries as vectors in a space whose axes are index terms; the use of space-density (term discrimination) arguments to choose an indexing vocabulary; the basis for cosine similarity ranking that became standard IR practice. Often cited (with later Salton works) as the founding statement of the vector space model.
