Pierre Janet, *L'automatisme psychologique: Essai de psychologie expérimentale sur les formes inférieures de l'activité humaine* (Paris: Félix Alcan, 1889) — Janet's doctoral thesis (Sorbonne).

Contains: the founding clinical formulation of **psychological automatism** — that mental life has lower, partly autonomous forms of activity — and of **dissociation** (Janet's term *désagrégation psychologique*, "psychological disaggregation"): under a narrowing of the *field of consciousness* (often from trauma or a weak "psychological tension"), fixed ideas and systems of behavior split off from central awareness and run automatically. Built from clinical work with hysteria and hypnotic / somnambulistic states (later at the Salpêtrière under Charcot). Distinguishes **total automatism** (catalepsy, somnambulism) from **partial automatism** (subconscious acts, automatic writing, suggestion).

Access: French original on Internet Archive (archive.org/details/lautomatismepsyc00jane) and Classiques UQAM; HathiTrust catalog 000660264.
