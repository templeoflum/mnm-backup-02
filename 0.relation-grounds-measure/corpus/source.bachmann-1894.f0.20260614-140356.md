Full citation — P. Bachmann, *Die analytische Zahlentheorie* (= *Zahlentheorie*, Zweiter Teil), B. G. Teubner, Leipzig, 1894.

Role — the **first appearance of the O symbol** ("big-O"). Bachmann chose the letter O for *Ordnung* (order of magnitude / order of approximation). This is the origin of what later became the Bachmann–Landau notation.

Access — historical; scans available via archive.org / GDZ Göttingen. **[confirmed as attribution]**
