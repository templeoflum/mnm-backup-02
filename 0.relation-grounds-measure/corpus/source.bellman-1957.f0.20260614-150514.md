Richard E. Bellman, *Dynamic Programming*, Princeton University Press, 1957.

**citation** — the first book on dynamic programming; gives the method its name and states the principle of optimality. Bellman developed the theory at RAND from the early 1950s; see also his earlier paper "The Theory of Dynamic Programming," *Bull. Amer. Math. Soc.* 60(6) (1954), 503–515.

**role** — origin / attribution for [[object.dynamic-programming]].

**access** — Princeton UP; 2010 Princeton Landmarks in Mathematics reissue.
