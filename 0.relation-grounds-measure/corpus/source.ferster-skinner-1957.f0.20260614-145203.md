C. B. Ferster & B. F. Skinner, *Schedules of Reinforcement* (Appleton-Century-Crofts, 1957).

The systematic empirical study of reinforcement schedules — fixed/variable × ratio/interval and their compounds — representing over 70,000 hours of research primarily with pigeons. Establishes that the *schedule* (the rule relating responses/time to reinforcement) is itself a determinant of behavior, producing characteristic, reproducible response-rate patterns. (Distinct from Skinner's 1938 *The Behavior of Organisms* and 1953 *Science and Human Behavior*.)

Access — reissued by the B. F. Skinner Foundation (print and PDF).
