Full citation — J. Plücker, "Über ein neues Coordinatensystem," *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (Crelle's Journal) 5 (1830), pp. 1–36.

Role — co-origin: gives a theory of homogeneous coordinates, possibly independent of [[source.mobius-1827]]. Plücker took the step of treating homogeneous coordinates algebraically (without a fixed mass/triangle interpretation), which advanced the full algebraization of projective geometry; he later used them to resolve the duality paradox. (Lines in projective 3-space later carry "Plücker coordinates," a related but distinct construction.)

Lineage note — homogeneous coordinates are commonly said to have been found independently four times: Bobillier, Plücker, Feuerbach, and Möbius; Möbius (1827) carries the priority of first publication. Some sources date Plücker's version to 1829.

Access — Crelle vol. 5 is public domain (GDZ / digitized Crelle archives).
