Verbatim citation:

Theraulaz, G., & Bonabeau, E. (1999). A Brief History of Stigmergy. *Artificial Life*, 5(2), 97–116. doi:10.1162/106454699568700

Contains: a historical review tracing the concept from Grassé (1959) through later work in ethology and self-organization, and its codification for the modern study of collective behavior and artificial life. Articulates the distinction between **quantitative stigmergy** (traces differing in degree/intensity, where a stronger trace elicits more of the same response) and **qualitative stigmergy** (discrete stimuli differing in kind, where a different configuration triggers a different action). Frames stigmergy as a general mechanism linking individual behavior to emergent collective structure. [confirmed]
