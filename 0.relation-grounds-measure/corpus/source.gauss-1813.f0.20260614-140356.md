Full citation — C. F. Gauss, "Disquisitiones generales circa seriem infinitam 1 + (αβ/1·γ)x + (α(α+1)β(β+1)/1·2·γ(γ+1))x² + …" (Pars Prior), *Commentationes Societatis Regiae Scientiarum Gottingensis recentiores*, vol. 2 (1813). Presented to the Göttingen Royal Society in 1812 (an announcement appeared in *Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen*, Feb. 1812); the full text was printed in 1813. Often cited as "Gauss 1812."

Contains the first systematic study of the hypergeometric series ₂F₁(a,b;c;z), including its convergence, the contiguous relations (fifteen relations among neighboring functions), the continued-fraction expansion of a ratio of two such series, and the summation value at z=1 (Gauss's summation theorem). Gauss introduced the notation F(a,b,c,x).

Role — codification/origin of the theory of the Gauss hypergeometric function.

Access — Gauss, *Werke* vol. 3; English translation on Wikisource.
