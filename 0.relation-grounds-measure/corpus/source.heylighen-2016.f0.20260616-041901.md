Verbatim citation:

Heylighen, F. (2016). Stigmergy as a universal coordination mechanism I: Definition and components. *Cognitive Systems Research*, 38, 4–13. doi:10.1016/j.cogsys.2015.12.002

Heylighen, F. (2016). Stigmergy as a universal coordination mechanism II: Varieties and evolution. *Cognitive Systems Research*, 38, 50–59. doi:10.1016/j.cogsys.2015.12.007

Contains: a two-part synthesis defining stigmergy generally as indirect coordination through traces left in a shared medium, and decomposing it into components (agent, action, medium, trace/mark, condition). Part II catalogs varieties along axes including quantitative vs. qualitative and sematectonic vs. sign-based (marker-based), and argues the same mechanism underlies coordination in insect colonies, human collaboration (e.g., Wikipedia, open-source software, markets), and artificial multi-agent systems.
