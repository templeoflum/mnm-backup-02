Philip J. Kellman, Christine M. Massey & Ji Y. Son, "Perceptual Learning Modules in Mathematics: Enhancing Students' Pattern Recognition, Structure Extraction, and Fluency," *Topics in Cognitive Science*, 2(2), 285–305 (2010). DOI 10.1111/j.1756-8765.2009.01053.x. PMID 25163790.

Translates perceptual learning theory into instruction. Describes perceptual learning modules (PLMs) — structured sets of short classification/mapping trials with feedback — and reports three classroom studies in middle- and high-school mathematics, showing improvements in students' ability to recognize patterns, extract structure across representations, and gain fluency.
