**citation** — F. J. Dyson, "The Threefold Way. Algebraic Structure of Symmetry Groups and Ensembles in Quantum Mechanics," *Journal of Mathematical Physics* **3**(6):1199–1215 (1962). DOI 10.1063/1.1703863.

**role** — establishes the symmetry classification (real / complex / quaternionic, via an anti-unitary time-reversal operator and the Frobenius theorem) that gives the Dyson index β = 1, 2, 4 for GOE / GUE / GSE; foundational for [[object.gaussian-ensembles]]. Dyson published a series of 1962 papers in J. Math. Phys. introducing these ensembles.
