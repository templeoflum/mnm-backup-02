Verbatim: van Rijsbergen, C. J. (1979). *Information Retrieval*, 2nd ed. London: Butterworths. 208 pp.

Contains: the foundational textbook treatment of retrieval evaluation — precision and recall, the precision-recall tradeoff, and the E/F effectiveness measures combining them — plus automatic classification, the cluster hypothesis, and probabilistic retrieval. The canonical door before Manning–Raghavan–Schütze (2008).

Access: out of print; full text freely available online (author's mirror at the University of Glasgow / Klagenfurt mirror). Citation and pagination verified via JASIS review (1979) and Library Review. **[confirmed]**
