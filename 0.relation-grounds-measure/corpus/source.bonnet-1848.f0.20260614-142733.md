Bonnet, Pierre Ossian (1848) — published the integral relation between total geodesic curvature of a bounding curve, total Gaussian curvature, and the turning of the boundary, for a region homeomorphic to a disc (the local Gauss–Bonnet formula).

Role — codification / co-eponym: the "Bonnet" of the Gauss–Bonnet theorem. Gauss (1827, [[source.gauss-1827]]) had the geodesic-triangle angle-excess result; Bonnet generalized it to bounded regions in 1848. The global closed-surface form (curvature integral = 2π·Euler characteristic) is later still.

Access — citation only (via Encyclopedia of Mathematics "Gauss–Bonnet theorem" and Wikipedia, which give "published by O. Bonnet [1848] in a special form, for surfaces homeomorphic to a disc").
