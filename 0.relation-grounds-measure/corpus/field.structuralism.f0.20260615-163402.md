**origin** — Ferdinand de Saussure, *Cours de linguistique générale* (Payot, 1916, posthumous; compiled by Bally & Sechehaye from 1906–11 lecture notes) — langue/parole, signifier/signified, language as a system of differences. [[source.saussure-1916]] **[confirmed]** *(lineage flag: Saussure did not write the Cours; fidelity to his views is debated.)*

**codification** — Claude Lévi-Strauss, *Anthropologie structurale* (Plon, 1958) — the structural method carried from language to kinship and myth. [[source.levi-strauss-1958]] **[confirmed]** *(distinction: linguistic origin vs anthropological extension.)*

**door** — *Course in General Linguistics* (Baskin trans. 1959; Harris trans. 1983). [[source.saussure-1916]]
