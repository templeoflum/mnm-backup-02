**statement (plain)** — Type Eight, "The Challenger": self-confident, decisive, willful, confrontational. Center: **gut/body** (the anger triad). Basic fear: being harmed, controlled, or violated by others. Basic desire: to protect themselves, to be in control of their own life and destiny.

**basis** — Riso & Hudson's systematization of the Ichazo/Naranjo type. [[source.riso-hudson-1999]] *(status `doctrinal`: the tradition's typology, recorded as held, not asserted.)*
