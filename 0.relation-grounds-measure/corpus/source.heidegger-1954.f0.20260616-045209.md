Heidegger, Martin. "Die Frage nach der Technik." In *Vorträge und Aufsätze* (Pfullingen: Günther Neske, 1954). Derived from the Bremen lecture "Das Ge-stell" (Dec. 1, 1949), part of the cycle *Einblick in das was ist*. English: "The Question Concerning Technology," trans. William Lovitt, in *The Question Concerning Technology and Other Essays* (New York: Harper & Row, 1977), pp. 3–35.

Contains — the argument that the essence of technology is "nothing technological": technology is a mode of *aletheia* (revealing/unconcealment). Modern technology reveals by *herausfordern* (challenging-forth), gathering all beings — nature, human — as *Bestand* (standing-reserve), orderable resource. The name for this gathering mode of revealing is *Gestell* (enframing). The danger is that enframing crowds out other modes of revealing, including *poiesis* (bringing-forth, on the model of *techne* and *physis*); "but where danger is, grows the saving power also" (Hölderlin).
