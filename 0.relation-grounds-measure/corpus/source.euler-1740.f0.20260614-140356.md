Full citation — L. Euler, "De summis serierum reciprocarum" (E41), *Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae* 7 (1734/35), pp. 123–134; read in the St. Petersburg Academy on 5 December 1735, printed 1740.

Contains Euler's first solution of the Basel problem: Σ 1/n² = π²/6, obtained by factoring sin(x)/x as an infinite product over its roots and matching the x² coefficient. The same paper gives the values of ζ(2n) for further even n. The volume's title page carries 1734/1735 (presentation) and 1740 (printing); carry the date as "1740 (presented 5 Dec 1735)."

Role — origin: the founding source for ζ(2)=π²/6. The product-of-roots step was not fully rigorous; Euler returned to the problem with more careful arguments (1741, 1743, 1755).

Access — Euler Archive (eulerarchive.maa.org, E41), Pacific Euler Archive scans.
