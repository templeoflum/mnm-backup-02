M. Zorn, "A remark on method in transfinite algebra," *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society* **41**(10): 667–670 (1935).

Gave the maximal-principle for arbitrary chains, proposed it as an axiom replacing the well-ordering theorem, and exhibited algebraic applications. The name "Zorn's lemma" is due to Tukey (1940).
