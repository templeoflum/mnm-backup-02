B. B. Mandelbrot, *Les objets fractals : forme, hasard et dimension*. Flammarion, Paris, 1975. (English: *Fractals: Form, Chance, and Dimension*, W. H. Freeman, 1977.)

Contains — the first published use of the term "fractal" (coined 1975), drawn from the Latin *fractus*; an early gathering of the irregular/self-similar objects later expanded in *The Fractal Geometry of Nature* (1982). The coinage attribution rests on this work.
