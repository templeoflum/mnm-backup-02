Full citation — Pappus of Alexandria, *Synagoge* (*Collection / Mathematicae Collectiones*), Book VII; written c. 320–340 CE. Standard critical edition: F. Hultsch, *Pappi Alexandrini Collectionis quae supersunt*, 3 vols. (Berlin, 1876–78).

Role — contains the hexagon theorem now bearing Pappus's name (Book VII, propositions in the chapters numbered ~133–143 in the Hultsch edition), the earliest known instance of the collinearity result that is foundational to projective geometry. Also contains an early implicit appearance of the cross-ratio (anharmonic ratio).

Access — Greek text public domain; Hultsch edition and English translations (e.g. Jones, *Pappus of Alexandria: Book 7 of the Collection*, 1986) available.
