C. R. Rao, "Information and the accuracy attainable in the estimation of statistical parameters," *Bulletin of the Calcutta Mathematical Society* 37 (1945):81–91.

Role — origin (shared): contains both the finite-sample variance lower bound (the "Cramér–Rao" inequality) and the conditional-expectation improvement on a sufficient statistic (the "Rao–Blackwell" step). Written when Rao was 24, teaching estimation at Calcutta University.

Access — reprinted in *Breakthroughs in Statistics* vol. I (Kotz & Johnson, eds., Springer 1992).
