H. L. Montgomery and R. C. Vaughan, "The large sieve," *Mathematika* 20(2) (1973): 119–134.

Establishes the clean, essentially sharp constant in the analytic [[object.large-sieve-inequality]]: for δ-spaced points the bound is (N + δ⁻¹) Σ|aₙ|² (Selberg obtained the same constant independently by a different argument). Distinct from [[source.montgomery-vaughan-2007]], their *Multiplicative Number Theory I* textbook.
