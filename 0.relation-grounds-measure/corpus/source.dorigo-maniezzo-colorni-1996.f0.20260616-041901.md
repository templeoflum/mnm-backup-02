Verbatim citation:

Dorigo, M., Maniezzo, V., & Colorni, A. (1996). Ant System: Optimization by a Colony of Cooperating Agents. *IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics — Part B (Cybernetics)*, 26(1), 29–41. doi:10.1109/3477.484436

Contains: the canonical archival publication of the Ant System. Defines a colony of simple agents ("ants") that construct candidate solutions and deposit pheromone proportional to solution quality; subsequent ants probabilistically prefer high-pheromone components, with evaporation preventing premature convergence. Demonstrated on the traveling salesman, quadratic assignment, and job-shop scheduling problems. The pheromone trail is the shared environment — a computational realization of stigmergy. [confirmed]
