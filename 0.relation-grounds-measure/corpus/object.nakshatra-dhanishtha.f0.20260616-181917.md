**statement (plain)** — Dhaniṣṭhā: the twenty-third of the 27 nakṣatras (lunar mansions). Symbol: **drum**. Deity: **the Vasus**. Planetary lord (Vimśottarī): **Mars**. Theme: rhythm, wealth, music, vibrant abundance.
