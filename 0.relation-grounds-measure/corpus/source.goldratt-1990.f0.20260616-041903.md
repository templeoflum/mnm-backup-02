Verbatim citation: Goldratt, Eliyahu M. *What Is This Thing Called Theory of Constraints and How Should It Be Implemented?* Great Barrington, MA (Croton-on-Hudson, NY): North River Press, 1990.

Contains: Goldratt's explicit codification of the Theory of Constraints as a named management methodology. Sets out the five focusing steps (identify the constraint, exploit it, subordinate everything else to that decision, elevate the constraint, and if the constraint is broken go back to step one) as a process of ongoing improvement (POOGI), and discusses the process of change, effect-cause-effect reasoning, and the construction of simple solutions to complex problems.
