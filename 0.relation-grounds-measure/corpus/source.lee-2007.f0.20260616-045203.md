John A. Lee, Michel Verleysen, *Nonlinear Dimensionality Reduction*, Springer, New York, 2007 (Information Science and Statistics series). ISBN 9780387393506.

The standard textbook door to manifold learning / nonlinear dimensionality reduction. Treats the topic from a unifying point of view: characteristics of an analysis method, estimation of intrinsic dimension, distance-preserving methods (MDS, Sammon mapping, Isomap), topology/neighborhood-preserving methods (LLE, Laplacian eigenmaps, SOM), and their respective strengths and shortcomings. Intended for statisticians, computer scientists, and data analysts with a basic statistics/learning background.
