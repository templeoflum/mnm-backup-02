**citation (verbatim)** — Newberg, Andrew B., Nancy A. Wintering, Donna Morgan, and Mark R. Waldman. "The measurement of regional cerebral blood flow during glossolalia: a preliminary SPECT study." *Psychiatry Research: Neuroimaging* 148, no. 1 (2006): 67–71. doi:10.1016/j.pscychresns.2006.07.001.

**what it contains** — The first functional neuroimaging investigation of glossolalia. Single-photon emission computed tomography (SPECT) measured regional cerebral blood flow (CBF, a correlate of neural activity) in five experienced practitioners during glossolalia, compared against a religious singing baseline in English (matched except that it uses actual language). The frontal lobes, parietal lobes, and left caudate were most affected; the study reports decreased frontal activity during glossolalia relative to the singing state. Described as preliminary given the small sample.
