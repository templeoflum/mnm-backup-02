Sir John Woodroffe (Arthur Avalon), *The Serpent Power: The Secrets of Tantric and Shaktic Yoga* (Ganesh & Co., 1919) — translation and commentary on Pūrṇānanda's *Ṣaṭ-cakra-nirūpaṇa* (1577) and the *Pādukā-pañcaka*.

The standard scholarly source for the classical seven-chakra system — the bījas, elements, lotus petals, and presiding deities of each center. **[confirmed as attribution]** *(primary not opened; cited for the chakra schema it transmitted.)*
