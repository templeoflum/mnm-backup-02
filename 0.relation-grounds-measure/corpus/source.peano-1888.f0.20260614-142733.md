Peano, *Calcolo geometrico secondo l'Ausdehnungslehre di H. Grassmann, preceduto dalle operazioni della logica deduttiva* (Torino, 1888).

Role — first modern axiomatic definition of a vector space ("linear system") over a field, given in Chapter IX ("Transformations of a linear system"); the eight axioms in essentially their present form, building on and clarifying Grassmann's Ausdehnungslehre.

Access — historical, Italian.
