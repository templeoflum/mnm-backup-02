D. M. Green & J. A. Swets, *Signal Detection Theory and Psychophysics* (New York: Wiley, 1966).

Role — door / canonical text bringing signal detection theory into psychophysics: separates a subject's intrinsic sensitivity (d′) from response bias / decision criterion (c) via the receiver operating characteristic (ROC).

Lineage — the theory itself originated earlier in radar/electrical engineering and statistical decision theory: Peterson, Birdsall & Fox (1954) on the theoretical side; W. P. Tanner & J. A. Swets (1954, *Psychological Review*) carried it into perception. Green & Swets is the consolidating 1966 monograph.

Note — distinct from [[source.stevens-1946]] (scales) and [[source.stevens-1957]] (power law); SDT is a separate strand.
