Leland Wilkinson, *The Grammar of Graphics* (Springer, 1999; 2nd ed. 2005).

Role: codification of data visualization — proposes that virtually all statistical charts can be generated from a small set of orthogonal constructs (variables, algebra, scales, statistics, geometry, coordinates, aesthetics) rather than enumerated as a fixed taxonomy of chart types. Direct ancestor of ggplot2, Tableau/VizQL (via Polaris), and Vega-Lite.

Note: distinct from James H. Wilkinson (numerical analysis, [[source.wilkinson-1965]], [[source.wilkinson-1963]]) — same surname, different person and field.
