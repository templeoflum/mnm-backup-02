Full citation — S. N. Bernstein, "Démonstration du théorème de Weierstrass fondée sur le calcul des probabilités," *Communications de la Société mathématique de Kharkow* (2) **13** (1912/13), 1–2.

Contains the first explicitly **constructive** proof of the Weierstrass approximation theorem: for f ∈ C[0,1], the Bernstein polynomials Bₙ(f)(x) = Σₖ₌₀ⁿ f(k/n) C(n,k) xᵏ(1−x)ⁿ⁻ᵏ converge uniformly to f. The argument rests on elementary probability (the weak law of large numbers / Bernoulli trials), giving explicit approximants rather than an existence statement.

Access — the proof is reproduced in essentially every approximation-theory text (e.g. Cheney; Trefethen ATAP). **[confirmed]**
