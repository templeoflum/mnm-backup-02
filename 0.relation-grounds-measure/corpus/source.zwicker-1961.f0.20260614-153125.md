Eberhard Zwicker, "Subdivision of the Audible Frequency Range into Critical Bands (Frequenzgruppen)," *Journal of the Acoustical Society of America* 33(2) (1961):248.

Role — proposes the **Bark scale**: one Bark = the width of one critical band; the scale runs 1–24 over the first 24 critical bands of hearing. Zwicker and colleagues (Germany, 1950s–60s) refined the critical-band concept Fletcher originated, using loudness summation, masking, and pitch.
