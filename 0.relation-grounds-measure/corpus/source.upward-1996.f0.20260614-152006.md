Upward, Frank. "Structuring the Records Continuum — Part One: Postcustodial Principles and Properties." *Archives & Manuscripts* 24, no. 2 (1996): 268–285. Part Two ("Structuration Theory and Recordkeeping") followed in 1997.

Role — founding published statement of the records continuum model, developed at Monash University (with Sue McKemmish, Livia Iacovino), drawing on Ian Maclean's Australian "continuum of records administration," Peter Scott's series system, and Anthony Giddens's structuration theory.

Access — published by the Australian Society of Archivists (publications.archivists.org.au); abstract open, full text via institutional access.
