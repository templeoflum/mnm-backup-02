# klein-calderwood-macgregor-1989

**Citation (verbatim):** Klein, G. A., Calderwood, R., & MacGregor, D. (1989). Critical decision method for eliciting knowledge. *IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, 19*(3), 462-472. https://doi.org/10.1109/21.31053

**Contains:** Describes the Critical Decision Method (CDM), a structured retrospective interview technique for modeling tasks in naturalistic environments marked by high time pressure, high information content, and changing conditions. CDM is a variant of Flanagan's (1954) critical incident technique, extended with cognitive probes that elicit the basis for perceptual and conceptual discriminations, typicality judgments, expectancies, goals, and critical cues. Reported applications include eliciting domain knowledge from urban and wildland fireground commanders, tank platoon leaders, structural and design engineers, paramedics, and computer programmers. The paper is a foundational data-collection method of the NDM program and the source of much of the fireground-commander evidence behind the recognition-primed decision model.
