Full citation — J.-L. Lagrange, "Additions au mémoire sur la résolution des équations numériques" and related memoirs on the resolution of equations by continued fractions, *Mémoires de l'Académie royale des sciences et belles-lettres de Berlin* (read 1768–1769, published 1770; reprinted in *Œuvres de Lagrange*, vol. 2).

Role — origin / proof source for [[object.lagrange-continued-fraction-theorem]]: contains Lagrange's proof that the regular continued fraction of a real number is eventually periodic if (and only if) the number is a quadratic irrational. The converse ("only if" — periodic ⟹ quadratic) was already due to Euler (1737/1744); Lagrange supplied the harder "if" direction, so the full statement is sometimes called the Euler–Lagrange theorem.

Access — *Œuvres de Lagrange* (ed. Serret, Gauthier-Villars, 1867–1892) digitized at the Bibliothèque nationale de France / archive.org; secondary accounts in MacTutor and Encyclopedia of Mathematics.
