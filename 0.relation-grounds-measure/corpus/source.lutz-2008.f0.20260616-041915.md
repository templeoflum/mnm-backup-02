Lutz, A., Slagter, H. A., Dunne, J. D., & Davidson, R. J. (2008). Attention regulation and monitoring in meditation. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 12(4), 163–169. doi:10.1016/j.tics.2008.01.005

Contains — proposes the influential two-style taxonomy: *focused attention* (FA) meditation, voluntarily sustaining attention on a chosen object; and *open monitoring* (OM) meditation, non-reactive moment-to-moment monitoring of experience. Frames meditation as a family of attentional and emotional regulatory training regimes, and surveys candidate neural mechanisms.
