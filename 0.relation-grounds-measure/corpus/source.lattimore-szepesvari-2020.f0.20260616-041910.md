Tor Lattimore and Csaba Szepesvári, *Bandit Algorithms*, Cambridge University Press, 2020. ISBN 9781108486828 (hardback); 9781108571401 (ebook).

**citation** — rigorous, comprehensive treatment of the multi-armed bandit across stochastic, adversarial, and Bayesian settings: regret lower bounds, UCB and Thompson sampling, and the Gittins-index theory for discounted bandits. Standard graduate-level door into the algorithmic side of the explore–exploit problem.
