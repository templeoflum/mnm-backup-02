Full citation — M.-A. Parseval des Chênes, "Mémoire sur les séries et sur l'intégration complète d'une équation aux différences partielles linéaires du second ordre, à coefficients constants," presented to the Académie des Sciences (Paris) on **5 April 1799**, published 1806 in *Mémoires présentés … par divers savans*, sér. 1, t. 1.

Role — the origin of **Parseval's identity**: the sum of squares of the (Fourier) coefficients equals the integral of the square of the function. Parseval stated it as a result about series, before Fourier's systematic theory; it was later recognized as the energy identity for the [[object.fourier-series]] and generalized by Plancherel to the Fourier transform.

Access — historical memoir, hard to source directly; statement and the 1799-presented / 1806-published dates confirmed via Wikipedia "Parseval's theorem" and standard histories.
