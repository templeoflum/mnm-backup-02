Full citation — Michel Chasles, *Aperçu historique sur l'origine et le développement des méthodes en géométrie* (Brussels: M. Hayez, 1837).

Role — gave an independent proof of the projective invariance of the cross-ratio and coined the French term *rapport anharmonique* ("anharmonic ratio"). Chasles arrived at the cross-ratio through his study of Euclid's lost works and knew Pappus had the idea. One of three near-simultaneous independent invariance proofs: Möbius (1827), Steiner (1832), Chasles (1837).
