Gilbert Ryle, *The Concept of Mind* (Hutchinson, London, 1949) — ch. 2 draws the distinction between *knowing how* (intelligent capacity) and *knowing that* (propositional knowledge), attacking the "intellectualist legend" by a regress argument. Developed earlier in Ryle's 1945–46 Aristotelian Society paper "Knowing How and Knowing That."

Lineage source for tacit knowledge: the knowing-how/knowing-that distinction that Polanyi's tacit knowing builds on.

Access — widely reprinted (Routledge / Penguin / Univ. Chicago Press 2009 with Daniel Dennett intro); full scans available online.
