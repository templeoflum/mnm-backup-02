Full citation — Emmy Noether, "Abstrakter Aufbau der Idealtheorie in algebraischen Zahl- und Funktionenkörpern," *Mathematische Annalen*, vol. 96 (1927), pp. 26–61.

Role — codification source for [[object.isomorphism-theorems]]: Noether formulated the isomorphism theorems in general form for homomorphisms (of modules / groups-with-operators) and gave a uniform method of deriving them from the fundamental homomorphism theorem, applicable across groups, rings, and modules — hence "Noether's isomorphism theorems." Noether herself noted the results appear "in a somewhat more special conception" already in Dedekind.
