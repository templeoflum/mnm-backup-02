Malcolm Gladwell, *Outliers: The Story of Success* (New York: Little, Brown and Company, 2008).

Role — popularization, not origin. Outliers coined the "10,000-hour rule" as "the magic number of greatness," drawing on Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer (1993). Ericsson himself called it "a popularized but simplistic view of our work" — the 10,000 figure was an *average* among the best violinists studied (some far fewer, some over 25,000 hours), not a threshold that automatically confers mastery, and Gladwell dropped the *deliberate* qualifier on the practice. Carry as popularization distinct from the source.

Access — widely available (ISBN 978-0316017923).
