Role — origin of the **closure-operator (Kuratowski) axioms** defining a [[object.topological-space]], equivalent to the open-set axioms.

**Same-name trap, noted** — distinct from `source.kuratowski-1922` (the "élimination des nombres transfinis" maximal-principle paper, *Fund. Math.* 3: 76–108, basis of the Kuratowski–Zorn lemma). Two different Kuratowski papers in the same 1922 volume.
