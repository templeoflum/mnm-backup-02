Full citation — B. Riemann, *Grundlagen für eine allgemeine Theorie der Functionen einer veränderlichen complexen Grösse*, Inauguraldissertation, Göttingen, 1851.

Role — founds the geometric / conformal-mapping theory of functions of a complex variable; treats the Cauchy–Riemann equations as defining holomorphy and introduces Riemann surfaces. One of the two namesakes of the CR equations.

Access — historical text; in Riemann's *Gesammelte mathematische Werke*. Attribution reported in Encyclopedia of Mathematics and Wikipedia. **[confirmed as attribution]**
