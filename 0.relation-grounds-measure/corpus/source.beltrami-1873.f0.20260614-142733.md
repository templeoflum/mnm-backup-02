Beltrami, "Sulle funzioni bilineari," *Giornale di Matematiche* 11 (1873), 98–106.

Role — first appearance of the singular value decomposition: Beltrami showed that a real bilinear form can be reduced to diagonal form by independent orthogonal substitutions on the two sets of variables, the diagonal entries being the singular values. Jordan reached the same result independently (1874), Sylvester again for real square matrices (1889), and Eckart–Young extended it to rectangular/complex matrices (1936).

Access — historical, Italian; see Stewart, "On the early history of the singular value decomposition," *SIAM Review* 35 (1993).
