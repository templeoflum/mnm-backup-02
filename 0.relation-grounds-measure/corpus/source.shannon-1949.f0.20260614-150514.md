C. E. Shannon, "Communication in the Presence of Noise," *Proc. IRE* 37(1) (Jan 1949), 10–21. Manuscript received by the Institute July 23, 1940; publication delayed by World War II; presented to the IRE New York Section (1947) and the IRE National Convention (March 1948).

Contains the sampling theorem with a clean statement and proof, and uses it to derive the channel-capacity formula C = W log(1 + S/N). Shannon explicitly credits the prior work of Whittaker, Nyquist, and Gabor.

Distinct from Shannon's *1948* information-theory paper [[source.shannon-1948]] and from the 1949 Shannon & Weaver book [[source.shannon-weaver-1949]]. This is the paper that carries the sampling theorem.
