**citation (verbatim)** — A. Bejan and S. Lorente, *Design with Constructal Theory*, John Wiley & Sons, Hoboken, NJ (2008). ISBN 978-0-471-99816-7.

**what it contains** — the comprehensive treatment of the constructal law as a method for generating flow-system architecture: the general statement (configurations evolve to ease the currents through them), worked derivations across heat and fluid flow, and the extension of the argument to tree networks in nature, technology, and (contestedly) social and economic organization. The standard reference and entry point for the theory.
