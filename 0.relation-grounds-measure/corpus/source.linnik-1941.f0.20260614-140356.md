Yu. V. Linnik, "The large sieve" ("Velikoe reshето"), *Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR (C. R. Acad. Sci. URSS)* 30 (1941): 292–294.

The paper that originates the large sieve, introduced to study the distribution of quadratic non-residues (showing large values of the least quadratic non-residue mod p are rare). "Large" because it can exclude many residue classes mod p, unlike combinatorial sieves that exclude few. Source for the lineage of [[object.large-sieve-inequality]].
