S. Banach, *Théorie des opérations linéaires*, Monografje Matematyczne 1 (Warsaw, 1932) — French edition of the 1931 Polish *Teorja operacyj*.

Role — the founding monograph of [[field.functional-analysis]] as a unified discipline: the first systematic treatment of complete normed linear spaces (now **Banach spaces**) and the general theory of linear operators between them, synthesizing the earlier strands of Fréchet, Hilbert, and Riesz. Carries the closed graph / open mapping / Hahn–Banach circle of results into one framework.

Access — historical, French; English translation *Theory of Linear Operations* (North-Holland, 1987). Attribution corroborated by Britannica, Wikipedia ("Stefan Banach", "Functional analysis"), and the *Mathematical Gazette* review. **[confirmed]**
