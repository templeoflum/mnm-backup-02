The study of how mental activity can split off from central awareness — running automatically, feeling un-willed, or appearing to issue from another agent. It treats automatic writing, table-turning, trance, possession, and mediumistic "communication" as *studied phenomena*: products of dissociated cognition, ideomotor movement, subliminal memory, and the inferential construction of the will-experience. The mediumship claims are bracketed; the science is of the mind that produces them.

**origin** — Pierre Janet, *L'automatisme psychologique* (1889) — the founding clinical theory of dissociation (*désagrégation*) and psychological automatism. [[source.janet-1889]] **[confirmed]**

**codification / synthesis** — F. W. H. Myers, *Human Personality and Its Survival of Bodily Death* (1903) — the subliminal-self synthesis unifying automatism, hypnosis, and survival research; Théodore Flournoy, *From India to the Planet Mars* (1900) — the model case study, introducing cryptomnesia as explanation. [[source.myers-1903]] [[source.flournoy-1900]] **[confirmed]**

**door / mature statement** — Ernest R. Hilgard, *Divided Consciousness* (1977) — neodissociation theory, the modern experimental frame; Daniel M. Wegner, *The Illusion of Conscious Will* (2002) — the cognitive-science reframing of automatism as apparent mental causation; Zoltán Dienes & Josef Perner (2007) — cold control, the metacognitive theory of hypnotic response. William B. Carpenter (1852) supplies the founding physiological mechanism, the ideo-motor principle. [[source.hilgard-1977]] [[source.wegner-2002]] [[source.dienes-perner-2007]] [[source.carpenter-1852]] **[confirmed]**

**lineage** — two interpretive registers run through the field: the *psychological* program (dissociation, automatism, cryptomnesia, ideomotor action — empirical) and the *survival / spiritualist* register (the tenet that some automatisms evidence discarnate agency — doctrinal, bracketed). Myers held both; Janet, Flournoy, Carpenter, Hilgard, Wegner, and Dienes work the psychological register and decline the spirit hypothesis.

*grade [s] — origin (Janet) + codification (Myers / Flournoy) + door (Hilgard / Wegner / Dienes–Perner).*
