**statement (plain)** — Úr (ᚒ), the third letter of Aicme Ailme. Tree/plant: **heather / earth**. The grave and the land, passion, deep rest.

**basis** — the Ogham tract and word-oghams per McManus. [[source.mcmanus-1991]] *(status `doctrinal`: the tradition's system, held not asserted; tree associations partly traditional kennings.)*
