Verbatim citation: M. E. Raichle, A. M. MacLeod, A. Z. Snyder, W. J. Powers, D. A. Gusnard, and G. L. Shulman, "A default mode of brain function," *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, vol. 98, no. 2, pp. 676–682, 2001.

Contains: the foundational characterization of a baseline or "default" mode of brain function. Using PET measures of the oxygen extraction fraction (uniform across the awake resting brain) and consistent task-induced deactivations, the paper argues for an organized default mode of activity that is attenuated during specific goal-directed behavior — the empirical seed of the default-mode-network (DMN) concept later central to altered-states neuroscience.
