# library.index — the shelf register — f0 — 20260702-073445

**The catalog of the library proper — what sits on the shelves at `library/texts/`.** Third
index at the front desk, beside `corpus.index` (the records) and `raw.index` (the feedstock).
One line per shelved item: `collection/filename · what it is · how it entered (vet ref)`.

**Git rule (operator settled, 20260702):** the shelf *contents* are NOT tracked —
`library/texts/` is gitignored; source texts are replaceable and don't belong in repo
history. **This register IS the tracked record of the shelves** — a text is only ON the
shelf when its line is here. Losing the folder loses bytes, never the knowledge of what
was held.

**Intake rule:** nothing lands on the shelves except through intake (keeper's trade,
operator's eye) — quarantine and `library/raw/` do not leak in by move. Each shelving
names its vet.
