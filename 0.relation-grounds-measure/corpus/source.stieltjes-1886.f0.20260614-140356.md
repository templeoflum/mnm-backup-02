Full citation — T. J. Stieltjes, "Recherches sur quelques séries semi-convergentes," *Annales scientifiques de l'École normale supérieure*, 3e série, **3** (1886), 201–258.

Contains an independent, rigorous treatment of "semi-convergent" (asymptotic) series, published the same year as Poincaré's *Acta Math.* paper. The two are universally credited as the **simultaneous, independent** founders of the theory of asymptotic expansions.
