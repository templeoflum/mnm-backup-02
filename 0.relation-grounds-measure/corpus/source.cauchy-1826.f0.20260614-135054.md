Full citation — A.-L. Cauchy, "Sur un nouveau genre de calcul analogue au calcul infinitésimal," in *Exercices de Mathématiques*, vol. 1, Paris, 1826; the calculus of residues was developed further across the *Exercices* (1826–1829).

Role — introduces the residue concept and the word *résidu* (1826); the origin of the calculus of residues that underlies the residue theorem.

Access — historical text; in Cauchy's *Œuvres complètes*. Attribution reported in the MacTutor biography of Cauchy (St Andrews), Wikipedia ("Residue theorem"), and arXiv:1701.04887. **[confirmed as attribution]**
