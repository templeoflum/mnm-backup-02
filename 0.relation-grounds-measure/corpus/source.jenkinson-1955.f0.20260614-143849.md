Jenkinson, A. F. (1955). "The Frequency Distribution of the Annual Maximum (or Minimum) Values of Meteorological Elements." *Quarterly Journal of the Royal Meteorological Society* 81(348):158–171. doi:10.1002/qj.49708134804.

Role — introduced the single three-parameter form (location, scale, shape) merging the Gumbel, Fréchet and reversed-Weibull types into one family; the generalized extreme value (GEV) distribution is commonly attributed to this paper.
