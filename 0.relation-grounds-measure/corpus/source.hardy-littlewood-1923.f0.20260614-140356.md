Full citation — G. H. Hardy and J. E. Littlewood, "Some problems of 'Partitio numerorum'; III: On the expression of a number as a sum of primes," *Acta Mathematica* 44 (1923), 1–70 (DOI 10.1007/BF02403921).

Contains the first Hardy–Littlewood conjecture: the quantitative (circle-method heuristic) asymptotic for the number of prime k-tuples. For twin primes it predicts π₂(x) ∼ 2C₂ · x/(ln x)², where the twin-prime constant 2C₂ = 2∏_{p>2}(1 − 1/(p−1)²) ≈ 1.3203.

Role — the quantitative-conjecture source for [[object.twin-prime-conjecture]] (a strengthening that implies infinitude). Itself open.
