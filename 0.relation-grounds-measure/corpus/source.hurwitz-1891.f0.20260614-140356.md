Full citation — A. Hurwitz, "Ueber die angenäherte Darstellung der Irrationalzahlen durch rationale Brüche," *Mathematische Annalen* 39, no. 2 (1891), 279–284.

Contains Hurwitz's theorem: for every irrational ξ there are infinitely many coprime p/q with |ξ − p/q| < 1/(√5·q²), and √5 is the best (largest) possible constant — it fails for any A > √5, with the golden ratio as the extremal case. Formal-statement source for [[object.hurwitz-approximation-theorem]].

Note — distinct from the unrelated [[source.hurwitz-1897]] (Hurwitz's measure / random-matrix precursor). This is the Diophantine-approximation paper.
