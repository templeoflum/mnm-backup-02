Alexander Schrijver, *Combinatorial Optimization: Polyhedra and Efficiency*, Springer-Verlag, 2003 (Algorithms and Combinatorics, vol. 24; three volumes A, B, C; ~1880 pp.).

Contains: an in-depth survey of polyhedral combinatorics and efficient algorithms, including maximum flow, minimum cut, the max-flow min-cut theorem, residual networks, and the augmenting-path / blocking-flow / push-relabel families, with extensive historical notes and references on the founding results.

Access notes: ISBN 3-540-44389-4. Standard scholarly reference for the field's history and proofs.
