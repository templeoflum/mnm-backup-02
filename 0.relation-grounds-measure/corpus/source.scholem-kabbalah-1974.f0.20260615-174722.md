Gershom Scholem, *Kabbalah* (Keter, 1974; assembled from his Encyclopaedia Judaica entries). With *Major Trends in Jewish Mysticism* (1941) and *Origins of the Kabbalah* (1962), founded the modern academic study of Kabbalah; reads the sefirot as a closed, mutually-defined emanative structure ("their end is in their beginning").
