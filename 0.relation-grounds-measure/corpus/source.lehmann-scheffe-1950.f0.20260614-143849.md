E. L. Lehmann & H. Scheffé, "Completeness, Similar Regions, and Unbiased Estimation — Part I," *Sankhyā* 10(4) (1950):305–340.

Role — introduces completeness; with a complete sufficient statistic, Rao–Blackwellization yields the unique uniformly minimum-variance unbiased estimator (the Lehmann–Scheffé theorem). This paper coined the name "Rao–Blackwell theorem."
