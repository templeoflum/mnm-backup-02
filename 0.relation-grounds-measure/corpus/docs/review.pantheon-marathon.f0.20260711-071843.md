## the twelve

**Norse (21)** · Aesir/Vanir, straight ACP roster. spot: *odin* — the one-eyed Allfather of war, wisdom, poetry, and the dead who hung himself on Yggdrasil for the runes · *loki* — the shape-shifting trickster, father of monsters, bound until Ragnarök.

**Greek (45)** · Olympians/Titans/heroes/monsters. spot: *zeus* — sky-king who overthrew the Titans and wields the thunderbolt · *medusa* — the gorgon whose gaze turns to stone, slain by Perseus.

**Egyptian (13)** · Ra + the ACP's 12. spot: *osiris* — murdered god of the dead and resurrection, lord of the afterlife · *thoth* — ibis-headed god of writing, wisdom, and the moon.

**Mesopotamian (12)** · Sumer/Akkad/Babylon, distinct tradition (no interpretatio). spot: *marduk* — Babylon's champion who slew Tiamat and ordered the cosmos · *ereshkigal* — queen of the underworld Kur. *(held out: kur, nibiru — a place + an epithet, not figures.)*

**Indian (6)** · Vedic/Hindu. spot: *shiva* — the destroyer-transformer, lord of yogis and the dance · *devi* — the great Goddess, divine feminine energy (shakti).

**Celtic (6)** · Irish Tuatha Dé Danann. spot: *lugh* — the many-skilled god who leads the gods against the Fomorians · *morrigan* — the triple war-and-fate goddess, the crow over battle.

**Roman (5)** · CURATED DISTINCT-ONLY. the ACP roster was all Dii Consentes (Jupiter=Zeus … Hercules=Heracles) — already under Greek, so recording it would duplicate. instead I grounded the genuinely-Roman figures with NO Greek counterpart: *janus* (the two-faced god of doorways/beginnings, no Greek equivalent) · *romulus* (founder of Rome, deified as Quirinus) · remus · aeneas · quirinus.

**Finnish (11)** · Kalevala. spot: *vainamoinen* — the primordial sage born old, greatest singer, maker of the kantele · *louhi* — the witch-queen of Pohjola. the Kalevala flagged throughout as Lönnrot's 19c compilation, not an ancient text. *(CONFLATION CAUGHT: the ACP "Finnish" roster also held 4 BALTIC figures — a different language family — held out → your paperwork.)*

**Polynesian (17)** · one family, regional branches. Māori Rangi–Papa cycle + pan-Polynesian Māui + Hawaiian gods. cognate pairs (Tāne/Kāne, Tangaroa/Kanaloa, Tū/Kū, Rongo/Lono) recorded DISTINCT with cross-notes, not merged. spot: *maui* — the trickster who fished up islands and snared the sun · *pele* — the Hawaiian volcano goddess of Kīlauea.

**Japanese (17)** · Shinto + Buddhist-syncretic (shinbutsu-shūgō). 11 Shinto kami + 6 Buddhist. PROVENANCE HONESTY: the foreign imports marked non-native — *benzaiten* ← Hindu Saraswati, *jizo* ← the bodhisattva Kṣitigarbha, *enma* ← Yama. spot: *amaterasu* — the sun goddess, ancestress of the imperial line · *susanoo* — the storm god who slew the eight-headed serpent.

**Yoruba (16)** · named for the real tradition, NOT "African" (a continent is not a field). Orisha + diaspora names (Santería/Candomblé). *eshu*'s equation with the Christian devil explicitly REJECTED as colonial misID. spot: *shango* — thunder Orisha, a deified king of Oyo · *oshun* — Orisha of the river, love, and sweetness. *(CONFLATION CAUGHT: Anansi (Akan/Ghana) held out → your paperwork.)*

**Mesoamerican (17)** · a real culture-area (unlike "African"), but spans DISTINCT Aztec + Maya civilizations, each marked. 14 Aztec/Nahua + 3 Maya. spot: *quetzalcoatl* — the Feathered Serpent, wind/wisdom/creation · *hero-twins* — the Maya Hunahpu & Xbalanque who beat the lords of Xibalba. *(the check caught a dup — sahagun-florentine-codex already existed settled; reused, not duplicated.)*
