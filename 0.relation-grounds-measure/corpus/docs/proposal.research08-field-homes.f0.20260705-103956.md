## the flags (your judgment)

**FLAG 1 — new field `philosophy-of-language`.** Target 2C.2 grounds the
private-language argument — Wittgenstein *PI* §§243–315 (the diarist "S" §258,
the beetle-in-a-box §293), Kripke's rule-following/quus skepticism and its
"skeptical solution" — plus Russell's acquaintance/description (2C.1). No home
fits: `tacit-knowledge` is the "we can know more than we can tell" field, and
Wittgenstein's conclusion is the opposite claim (there is no private object to
tell — it "drops out of consideration"); forcing it there distorts both.
**Default: create `field.philosophy-of-language`** [mind canon; origin
russell-1912, codification wittgenstein-1953, lineage kripke-1982; objects
**knowledge-by-acquaintance-description**, **private-language-argument**,
**rule-following-paradox**]. Peer to `formal-semantics` (r05, the compositional/
Montague side) — this is the private-meaning/mind side, not a split of it.
*Alternative: route the three objects into `tacit-knowledge` + `epistemology`
(no epistemology field exists yet) — honest but buries a canonical field and
mis-homes the anti-private-object argument. Keeper's read: new field.*
*Note the seed is lopsided (private-language angle, not reference/Frege/Grice) —
carried openly, narrowness is not the invention tell.*

**FLAG 2 — new field `history-of-religions`.** Target 2C.3 grounds Eliade
(*The Sacred and the Profane*, *Rites and Symbols of Initiation*) — hierophany,
*homo religiosus*, initiation as a change of existential/ontological status,
death-and-rebirth — AND the post-1970s critique that made him the named
contested figure: J. Z. Smith (constructed/local place over universal
cosmology), McCutcheon (the "sui generis religion" critique), Wasserstrom
(the Eranos crypto-theology reading). No home: `comparative-mythology` is the
Müller/Dumézil/Parry–Lord *oral-tradition* lineage (distinct); `phenomenology`
is Husserlian (distinct). **Default: create `field.history-of-religions`** [mind
canon; origin eliade-1957/1958, codification/fault-line smith-jz-1987 +
mccutcheon-1997 + wasserstrom-1999 — the Eliade-vs-critics debate carried as
lineage exactly as `western-esotericism` carries Faivre-vs-Hanegraaff; objects
**hierophany**, **initiatory-death-rebirth**, **sui-generis-critique**].
*Alternatives on the NAME: `phenomenology-of-religion` (sharper on Eliade's
own method) · `critical-religious-studies` (centers the critique). Keeper's
read: `history-of-religions` — the Chicago-school umbrella that holds both
poles without taking a side.*

**FLAG 3 — gnosis: route, don't add a field (default).** The reason/faith/gnosis
