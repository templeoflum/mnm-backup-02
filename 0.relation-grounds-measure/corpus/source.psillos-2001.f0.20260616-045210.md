Stathis Psillos, "Is Structural Realism Possible?" *Philosophy of Science* 68, no. 3 (2001): S13–S24 (Proceedings of the 2000 Biennial PSA meeting). DOI: 10.1086/392894.

Contains: a sustained objection to structural realism. Distinguishes the "upward path" (empiricist premises → realism, after Russell/Maxwell) and the "downward path" (realist premises → weaker realism, after Worrall/French/Ladyman) and criticizes the metaphysical presuppositions of both. Core charge: the *structure vs nature* distinction that ESR relies on cannot be sustained, and structure-only commitment reduces to uninterpreted equations that cannot deliver predictions — so SR collapses toward either standard realism or empiricism.
