A. Tychonoff, "Über einen Funktionenraum," *Mathematische Annalen* **111** (1935): 762–766.

Role — earliest known published proof of the **general** [[object.tychonoff-theorem]] (arbitrary products of compact spaces). Tychonoff first proved the special case (powers of [0,1]) in **1930** ("Über die topologische Erweiterung von Räumen," *Math. Ann.* 102); the 1935 paper states the general theorem and defines the product topology at this generality.
