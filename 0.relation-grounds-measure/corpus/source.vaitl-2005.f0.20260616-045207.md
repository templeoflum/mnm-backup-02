Wackermann, and T. Weiss, "Psychobiology of Altered States of Consciousness," *Psychological Bulletin*, vol. 131, no. 1, pp. 98–127, 2005.

Contains: a review (product of the Altered States of Consciousness Consortium) of ASC arising spontaneously, evoked by physical/physiological stimulation, induced by psychological means, and caused by disease. A phenomenological analysis characterizes ASC along four dimensions — activation, awareness span, self-awareness, and sensory dynamics — and the psychological and neurobiological underpinnings of each induction route are surveyed. Serves as the field's standard survey "door."
