---
name: ontic-structural-realism
type: field
essence: the position in philosophy of science that structure / relations are ontologically fundamental — what mature science gets right is structure, and on the ontic reading structure is all there is
canon: measuring
grade: s
state: settled

**origin** — J. Worrall, "Structural Realism: The Best of Both Worlds?" *Dialectica* 43, no. 1–2 (1989): 99–124 — revives the structuralist line (crediting Poincaré) to reconcile the no-miracles argument with the pessimistic meta-induction: what survives theory change is *form/structure, not content*. [[source.worrall-1989]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — J. Ladyman, "What Is Structural Realism?" *Stud. Hist. Phil. Sci. Part A* 29, no. 3 (1998): 409–424 — splits the position into *epistemic* (we know only structure) and *ontic* (structure is all there is) structural realism, and argues for the ontic reading. [[source.ladyman-1998]] **[confirmed]** — extended in S. French & J. Ladyman, "Remodelling Structural Realism," *Synthese* 136 (2003): 31–56 [[source.french-ladyman-2003]] **[confirmed]**, and in J. Ladyman & D. Ross (with D. Spurrett & J. Collier), *Every Thing Must Go: Metaphysics Naturalized* (Oxford UP, 2007) — OSR for fundamental physics plus "rainforest realism" for the special sciences. [[source.ladyman-ross-2007]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — J. Ladyman, "Structural Realism," *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (first publ. 2007; rev.) — the standard survey of ESR/OSR, the motivating arguments, and the objections. [[source.sep-structural-realism]] **[confirmed]**

**lineage (contested)** — a live, recognized position, not a settled truth. The deep split is *epistemic vs ontic* SR [[source.ladyman-1998]]. Standing objections carried as lineage: the **Newman objection** (Newman 1928, against Russell's structuralism; revived by Demopoulos & Friedman) — pure structure plus cardinality is trivially satisfiable, so structure alone fixes nothing beyond cardinality [[source.newman-1928]]; and **Psillos's critique** (2001) — the structure/nature distinction cannot be sustained and SR threatens to collapse into either standard realism or empiricism [[source.psillos-2001]]. The eliminativist OSR slogan "relations without relata" is itself disputed. Recorded as a position held and argued, status established. **[confirmed]**

*grade s — origin (Worrall 1989) + codification (Ladyman 1998 → French & Ladyman 2003 → Ladyman & Ross 2007) + door (SEP). Contested: epistemic/ontic split and the Newman + Psillos objections carried as lineage.*
