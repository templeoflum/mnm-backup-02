David Wood, Heather Wood & David Middleton, "An experimental evaluation of four face-to-face teaching strategies," *International Journal of Behavioral Development* 1(2) (1978):131–147.

Role: origin of the **contingency** principle — support whose degree of control rises after a learner's failure and falls after success. The study taught 3–4-year-olds a construction task under four strategies; the contingent approach produced the most active, efficient learners, able to perform most of the task alone afterward.

Access — abstract via SAGE journals (journals.sagepub.com); widely cited in secondary scaffolding reviews.
