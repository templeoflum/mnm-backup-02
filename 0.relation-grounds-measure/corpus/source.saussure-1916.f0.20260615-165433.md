Ferdinand de Saussure, *Cours de linguistique générale* (Payot, 1916) — posthumous, compiled by Charles Bally & Albert Sechehaye from students' notes on Saussure's 1906–11 Geneva lectures. langue/parole, signifier/signified, language as a system of differences; founds structuralism and (as "semiology") one root of semiotics. English: Baskin trans. (1959); Harris trans. (1983). *(Saussure did not write it; fidelity to his views is debated.)*
