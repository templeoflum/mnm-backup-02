K. Kuratowski, "Une méthode d'élimination des nombres transfinis des raisonnements mathématiques," *Fundamenta Mathematicae* **3**: 76–108 (1922).

Proved a version of the maximal principle (sets ordered by inclusion, closed under unions of well-ordered chains) thirteen years before Zorn — hence "Kuratowski–Zorn lemma."
