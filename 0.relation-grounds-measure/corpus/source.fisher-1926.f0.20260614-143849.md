R. A. Fisher, "The Arrangement of Field Experiments," *Journal of the Ministry of Agriculture of Great Britain* 33 (1926):503–513.

Role — origin of the factorial-design argument: Fisher contends that complex designs varying several factors together are more efficient than asking "Nature ... one question at a time," and lays out randomized blocking for field trials. A foundational paper for [[object.factorial-design]].
