Full citation — K. F. Roth, "Rational approximations to algebraic numbers," *Mathematika* 2 (1955), 1–20; corrigendum, *Mathematika* 2 (1955), 168.

Contains the final form of the Thue–Siegel–Roth theorem: for an irrational algebraic number α and any ε > 0, |α − p/q| < 1/q^(2+ε) has only finitely many coprime solutions — the approximation exponent of an algebraic number is exactly 2. This is the formal-statement source for [[object.roth-theorem]].

Roth was awarded the Fields Medal in 1958 largely for this result.

Access — *Mathematika* (LMS / Cambridge); widely cited; statement reproduced in Encyclopedia of Mathematics ("Thue-Siegel-Roth theorem") and on Wikipedia. **[confirmed as attribution]**
