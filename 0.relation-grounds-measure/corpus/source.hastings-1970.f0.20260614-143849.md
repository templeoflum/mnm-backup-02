W. K. Hastings, "Monte Carlo Sampling Methods Using Markov Chains and Their Applications," *Biometrika* 57, no. 1 (1970):97–109.

Role — generalizes the 1953 Metropolis algorithm from symmetric to general (asymmetric) proposal distributions, adding the proposal-ratio correction g(x|x')/g(x'|x); brings MCMC to the broader statistics community. Distinct from Metropolis et al. (1953). Also contains the first mention of what became the Metropolis-within-Gibbs scheme.

Access — Biometrika / Oxford Academic; widely reprinted.
