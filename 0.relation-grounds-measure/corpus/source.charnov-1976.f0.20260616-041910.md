Eric L. Charnov, "Optimal foraging, the marginal value theorem," *Theoretical Population Biology*, vol. 9, no. 2 (1976), pp. 129–136.

**citation** — derives, for a forager exploiting depleting patches in a habitat, the optimal patch-residence time: leave a patch when its instantaneous (marginal) intake rate drops to the long-term average rate achievable across the habitat. Recognised as a Citation Classic.

**role** — origin / attribution for [[object.marginal-value-theorem]]; foraging basis for [[object.explore-exploit-tradeoff]].
