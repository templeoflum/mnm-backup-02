Harvey Fletcher, "Auditory Patterns," *Reviews of Modern Physics* 12(1) (Jan 1940):47–65.

Role — refines and names the **critical band** concept Fletcher first broached in his 1933 loudness work; introduces the masking-band experiments behind the critical-bandwidth idea (audibility of a tone limited by noise within its band).

Access — journal article (APS, Reviews of Modern Physics). Not opened directly here; the attribution is corroborated across two independent secondary sources (Wikipedia "Critical band"; ERB/critical-band literature). **[confirmed as attribution]**
