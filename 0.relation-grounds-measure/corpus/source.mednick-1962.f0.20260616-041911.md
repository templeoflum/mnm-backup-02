Verbatim citation: Mednick, S. A. (1962). The associative basis of the creative process. *Psychological Review*, 69(3), 220–232.

Contains: defines creative thinking as the forming of associative elements into new combinations that are useful, and proposes that creative individuals have "flatter" associative hierarchies — more, and more remote, associations available to a stimulus. Theoretical basis of the **Remote Associates Test (RAT)**, in which a solver finds a single word linking three otherwise unrelated cue words.
