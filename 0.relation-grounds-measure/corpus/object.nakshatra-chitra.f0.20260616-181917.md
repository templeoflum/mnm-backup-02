**statement (plain)** — Citrā: the fourteenth of the 27 nakṣatras (lunar mansions). Symbol: **bright jewel / pearl**. Deity: **Tvaṣṭṛ (Viśvakarman)**. Planetary lord (Vimśottarī): **Mars**. Theme: brilliance, craftsmanship, dazzling form.
