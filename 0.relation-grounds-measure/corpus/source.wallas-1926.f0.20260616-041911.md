Verbatim citation: Wallas, G. (1926). *The Art of Thought*. London: Jonathan Cape.

Contains: an early stage model of creative/insightful thought, articulating four phases — **preparation** (defining and working the problem), **incubation** (setting it aside for unconscious processing), **illumination** (the sudden appearance of the idea, the "aha"), and **verification** (testing and elaborating the idea). Source of the field's vocabulary for the creative *process* (as distinct from the later ability/psychometric tradition).
