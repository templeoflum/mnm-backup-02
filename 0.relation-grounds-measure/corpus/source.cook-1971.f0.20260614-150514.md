Stephen A. Cook, "The Complexity of Theorem-Proving Procedures," in *Proceedings of the Third Annual ACM Symposium on Theory of Computing* (STOC '71), ACM, 1971, pp. 151–158.

**citation** — the paper that introduces NP-completeness and proves that the propositional satisfiability problem (SAT / tautology problem) is complete for NP under polynomial-time reducibility. The Western half of the Cook–Levin theorem.

**role** — origin / attribution for [[object.np-completeness-cook-levin-theorem]] and the precise statement of [[object.p-vs-np]].

**access** — ACM Digital Library (DOI 10.1145/800157.805047); widely reprinted.
