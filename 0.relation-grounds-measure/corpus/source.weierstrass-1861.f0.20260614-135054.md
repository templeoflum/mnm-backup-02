Full citation — K. Weierstrass, Berlin lecture course on the differential calculus (c. 1861), known through students' lecture notes (notably H. A. Schwarz's notes); Weierstrass published little himself, so the ε–δ formulation is transmitted through his lectures of the 1860s.

Contains the (ε, δ) definition of limit and continuity "in the form it is usually written today," together with the lim notation. This is the formal-definition source — distinct from `source.weierstrass-1885` (the approximation theorem) and `source.bolzano-1817` / `source.cauchy-1821` (the precursor limit ideas).

Access — primary lecture notes are archival; the attribution and 1861 date are standard in the history-of-analysis literature. **[confirmed as attribution]**
