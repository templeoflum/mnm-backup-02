**statement (plain)** — a *simulacrum* is a copy with no original — a sign that no longer refers to any reality, only to other signs. Baudrillard's "precession of simulacra" describes signs that precede and replace the real. The concept supplies a vocabulary for why a borrowed symbolic system can hollow out and "go dead": cut from its referent, it circulates as pure self-referential form.
