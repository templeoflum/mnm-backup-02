Cauchy, "Sur l'équation à l'aide de laquelle on détermine les inégalités séculaires des mouvements des planètes," *Exercices de mathématiques* 4 (1829).

Role — the finite-dimensional spectral theorem: Cauchy proved that the eigenvalues (roots of the secular/characteristic equation) of a real symmetric matrix are real and that such a matrix can be diagonalized by an orthogonal change of variables. This generalized earlier principal-axis results of Euler and Lagrange and the work on quadratic forms.

Access — historical, French; widely cited via histories of the spectral theorem.
