**codification** — H. Lebesgue, thesis *Intégrale, longueur, aire* (1902) — measure and the Lebesgue integral; abstract outer-measure axiomatization C. Carathéodory (1914). [[source.lebesgue-1902]] [[source.caratheodory-1914]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Folland, *Real Analysis: Modern Techniques and Their Applications* (2nd ed., 1999); Billingsley, *Probability and Measure* for the probability bridge (Kolmogorov 1933 axiomatization). [[source.folland-1999]] [[source.billingsley-1979]] [[source.kolmogorov-1933]]
