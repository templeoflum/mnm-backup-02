Full citation — A. N. Kolmogorov, "Sur la loi forte des grands nombres," *Comptes Rendus de l'Académie des Sciences*, Paris, **191** (1930), 910–912.

Role — Kolmogorov's general **strong law of large numbers**. The 1930 paper gives the sufficient condition for independent variables with finite variance: if Σ Var(Xₙ)/n² < ∞, then Sₙ/n − E(Sₙ/n) → 0 almost surely. In his 1933 *Grundbegriffe* he established, for i.i.d. variables, that existence of a finite mean is **necessary and sufficient** for almost-sure convergence of the sample mean to that mean — the definitive form of the strong law.

Access — historical; Comptes Rendus digitized at gallica.bnf.fr. Attribution corroborated by Encyclopedia of Mathematics ("Strong law of large numbers") and standard texts. **[confirmed]**
