**statement (plain)** — Pūrva Phālgunī: the eleventh of the 27 nakṣatras (lunar mansions). Symbol: **front legs of a bed**. Deity: **Bhaga**. Planetary lord (Vimśottarī): **Venus**. Theme: pleasure, rest, union, enjoyment.
