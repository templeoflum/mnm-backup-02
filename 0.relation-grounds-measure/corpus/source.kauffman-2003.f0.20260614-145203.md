Louis H. Kauffman, "Eigenforms — Objects as Tokens for Eigenbehaviors," *Cybernetics and Human Knowing*, 10(3-4), 2003, pp. 73-90.

Role — develops and formalizes von Foerster's eigenform concept: an eigenform is a fixed point of a transformation, J = F(J), so that an "object" is the stability that recursion settles into. Door into the mathematics (infinite nesting, fixed points) behind "objects are tokens for eigenbehaviors."
