**citation** — Leonard Bereska and Efstratios Gavves. "Mechanistic Interpretability for AI Safety — A Review." arXiv:2404.14082, 2024. Published in Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR). Authors at the University of Amsterdam.

**contains** — a structured review of mechanistic interpretability framed as reverse-engineering neural networks into human-understandable algorithms and concepts. Establishes foundational concepts (features encoding knowledge in activations; hypotheses about how features are represented and computed, including superposition), surveys causal methods for dissecting model behavior (probing, activation patching, circuit analysis), and assesses relevance to AI safety (understanding, control, alignment) alongside challenges of scalability and automation.
