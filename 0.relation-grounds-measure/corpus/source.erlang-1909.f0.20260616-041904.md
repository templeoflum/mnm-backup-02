# source.erlang-1909

Verbatim citation: Erlang, A. K. (1909). "The Theory of Probabilities and Telephone Conversations." *Nyt Tidsskrift for Matematik*, B, **20**, 33-39. (Originally Danish: "Sandsynlighedsregning og Telefonsamtaler.")

Contains: the first application of probability theory to telephone traffic. Shows that the number of calls arriving in a fixed interval, under random demand, follows the Poisson distribution. The founding paper of teletraffic engineering and of queueing theory as a quantitative discipline. [confirmed]

Access notes: primary in Danish; the title and venue are widely reproduced in secondary literature (St Andrews MacTutor / IFORS OR Hall of Fame biographies of Erlang). Primary not opened here; bibliographic detail confirmed against multiple independent secondary sources. [confirmed as attribution]
