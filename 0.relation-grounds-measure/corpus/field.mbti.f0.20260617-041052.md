**origin** — Katharine Cook Briggs & Isabel Briggs Myers, operationalizing C. G. Jung's *Psychological Types* (1921) into a self-report instrument. [[source.myers-mbti-manual-1998]]

**codification** — the MBTI Manual standardizes the four dichotomies and the sixteen four-letter types.

**door** — Myers, McCaulley, Quenk & Hammer, *MBTI Manual* (3rd ed., 1998).

**standing** — extremely widely used but **contested in academic psychology** (questionable test-retest reliability, dichotomized scales, weak predictive validity). Held `doctrinal` — recorded as the instrument defines it, not asserted as validated. **Note:** the popular nicknames ('The Architect' etc.) are *16Personalities/NERIS* branding, **not** Myers-Briggs canon — the canonical member is the four-letter code.
