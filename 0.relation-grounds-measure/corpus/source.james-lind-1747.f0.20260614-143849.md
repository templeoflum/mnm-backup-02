James Lind, controlled comparison of treatments for scurvy conducted aboard HMS Salisbury in 1747; reported in *A Treatise of the Scurvy* (Edinburgh, 1753).

Role — precursor to [[object.randomized-controlled-trial]]: a controlled comparison (concurrent treatment groups) containing many elements of a controlled trial, but without randomization. Commonly cited as an early landmark in fair comparative testing.

Access — historical record via the James Lind Library.
