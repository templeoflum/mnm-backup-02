J. F. Schouten, "The residue, a new component in subjective sound analysis," *Proc. Koninklijke Nederlandse Akademie van Wetenschappen* 43 (1940):356–365; with "The perception of subjective tones," *idem* 41 (1938):1086–1093, and "The perception of pitch," *Philips Technical Review* 5 (1940):286.

Role — names the **residue** and argues the pitch of a complex tone tracks the periodicity of the waveform rather than a present spectral fundamental; confirms Seebeck's observation that the fundamental partial is dispensable.

Access — older proceedings; not opened directly. Years/titles corroborated across two independent secondary sources (pitch-perception review PDFs; virtual-pitch references). **[confirmed as attribution]**
