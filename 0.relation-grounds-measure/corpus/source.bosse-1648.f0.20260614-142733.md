Full citation — Abraham Bosse, *Manière universelle de Mr Desargues, pour pratiquer la perspective par petit-pied, comme le géométral* (Paris: Pierre Des-Hayes, 1648). Bosse (1602/04–1676) was Desargues's friend and pupil.

Role — first **printed** appearance of what is now called Desargues's theorem (two triangles in perspective from a point are in perspective from a line, and conversely). Girard Desargues never published the theorem himself; it survives because Bosse included it as an appendix to this practical perspective manual.

Access — early-modern imprint; digitized scans in the public domain (e.g. via Gallica / archive.org).
