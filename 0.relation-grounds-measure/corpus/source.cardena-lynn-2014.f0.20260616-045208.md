**citation (verbatim)** — Cardeña, Etzel, Steven Jay Lynn, and Stanley Krippner, eds. *Varieties of Anomalous Experience: Examining the Scientific Evidence*. 2nd ed. Washington, DC: American Psychological Association, 2014. (1st ed. 2000.)

**what it contains** — An edited scientific survey reviewing the empirical evidence on anomalous and altered-state experiences — including dissociation, hallucination, mystical, out-of-body, and near-death experiences. It provides the standard methodological and evidential frame within which glossolalia is studied as a describable human experience rather than adjudicated as a religious claim. Serves as the field's door: an authoritative reference orienting a reader to the scientific literature on anomalous experience.
