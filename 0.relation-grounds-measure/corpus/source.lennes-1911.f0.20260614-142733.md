N. J. Lennes, "Curves in non-metrical analysis situs with an application in the calculus of variations," *American Journal of Mathematics* **33** (1911): 287–326.

Role — one of the independent early-20th-century origins of the modern partition definition of [[object.connectedness]] (no split into two disjoint nonempty open / separated sets). Hausdorff later acknowledged Lennes's priority, hence the "Lennes-Hausdorff definition."
