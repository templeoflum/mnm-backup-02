Verbatim citation: Goldratt, Eliyahu M., and Jeff Cox. *The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement*. Croton-on-Hudson, NY: North River Press, 1984.

Contains: a management-oriented novel in which plant manager Alex Rogo, guided by the physicist Jonah, learns to identify and manage the bottleneck (constraint) governing his factory's output. Introduces the core Theory of Constraints insight — a system's output is governed by its weakest link — and the operational measures throughput, inventory, and operating expense that became throughput accounting. The drum-buffer-rope scheduling logic is present in narrative form here (it is named only later, in *The Race*). [confirmed]

Access notes: in print through North River Press; revised editions issued 1986, 1992, 2004 (3rd revised), and a 30th-anniversary edition in 2014. Widely held in business-school libraries; reported sales exceed several million copies across 27+ languages. [confirmed]
