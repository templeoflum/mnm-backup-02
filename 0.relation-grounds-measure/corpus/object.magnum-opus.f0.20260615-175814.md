**statement (plain)** — the *magnum opus* ("great work") is the staged process toward the Philosophers' Stone and the transmutation of base metal into gold (*chrysopoeia*): the colour sequence *nigredo* (blackening) → *albedo* (whitening) → [*citrinitas*] → *rubedo* (reddening), worked through laboratory operations (*solutio, calcinatio, coniunctio, sublimatio, coagulatio*).

**basis** — the Greco-Egyptian corpus (Zosimos, Maria the Jewess). Principe stresses the operations were *real bench procedures* (he recreates them); the transmutation aim is the doctrinal claim. [[source.principe-2013]] **[confirmed]** *(status `doctrinal`: the transmutation aim recorded as the tradition's tenet; the chemistry itself is historically attested.)*
