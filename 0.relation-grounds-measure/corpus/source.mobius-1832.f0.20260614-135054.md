Full citation — A. F. Möbius, "Über eine besondere Art von Umkehrung der Reihen," *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (Crelle's Journal) 9 (1832), 105–123.

Contains the first systematic study of the function now called the Möbius function μ(n) and of the inversion relation that bears his name ("a special kind of reversal of series"). The number-theoretic μ and its inversion formula trace to this paper; the precursor μ-coefficients appear implicitly in earlier work (Euler), but Möbius is the standard attribution.
