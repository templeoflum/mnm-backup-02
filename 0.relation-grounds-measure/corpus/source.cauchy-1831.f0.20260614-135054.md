Full citation — A.-L. Cauchy, memoir of 1831 (the Turin "calcul des limites" memoir), in which the Cauchy integral formula and related estimates are developed; reproduced in Cauchy's *Œuvres complètes*.

Role — the integral formula f(z) = (1/2πi)∮ f(ζ)/(ζ−z) dζ is commonly dated to 1831. (The Encyclopedia of Mathematics traces the underlying integral to Cauchy's 1823 *Résumé des leçons … sur le calcul infinitésimal*; carried here as an 1823–1831 lineage.)
