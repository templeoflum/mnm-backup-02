Full citation — P. L. Chebyshev (Tchebychef), "Mémoire sur les nombres premiers," presented 1850, published 1852; also in *Journal de mathématiques pures et appliquées* (Liouville's journal), sér. 1, t. 17 (1852), 366–390.

Contains the first proof of Bertrand's postulate, obtained by deducing it from effective (explicit, quantitative) upper and lower bounds for the Chebyshev functions / prime-counting function π(x). This is the proof source for [[object.bertrands-postulate]] — hence the alternate name "Bertrand–Chebyshev theorem."

Note — distinct from the existing `source.chebyshev-1850s` record, which covers Chebyshev's best-uniform-approximation work (approximation theory), a different line of his output.

Access — historical; widely cited in analytic-number-theory texts. **[confirmed]**
