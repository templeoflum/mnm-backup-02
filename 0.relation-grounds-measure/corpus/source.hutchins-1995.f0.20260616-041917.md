Verbatim citation: Hutchins, Edwin. *Cognition in the Wild*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press (Bradford Books), 1995. ISBN 9780262082310 (hardcover); 9780262581462 (paperback). ~381 pp.

Contains: an extended ethnographic and computational analysis of navigation aboard U.S. Navy ships. Develops the distributed cognition (DCog) framework — the proper unit of cognitive analysis is the functional system composed of individuals, artifacts (charts, alidades, fix tools), and their coordination, not the bounded individual. Cognition is recast as the propagation and transformation of representational state across internal and external media. Treats culture and social organization as cognitive architecture. **[confirmed]**
