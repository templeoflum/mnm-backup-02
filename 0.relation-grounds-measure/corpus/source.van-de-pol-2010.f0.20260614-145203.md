Janneke van de Pol, Monique Volman & Jos Beishuizen, "Scaffolding in Teacher–Student Interaction: A Decade of Research," *Educational Psychology Review* 22(3) (2010):271–296.

Role: the contemporary synthesis defining scaffolding by three key characteristics — (a) contingency (adaptive, responsive support), (b) fading (gradual withdrawal of support), and (c) transfer of responsibility (handing the task/learning back to the student via contingent fading). Standard reference for the modern operationalization of scaffolding.

Access — Springer (paywalled article page); record and abstract via ERIC EJ924182 and the VU Amsterdam research repository.
