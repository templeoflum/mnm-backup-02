R. Duncan Luce & John W. Tukey, "Simultaneous conjoint measurement: a new type of fundamental measurement," *Journal of Mathematical Psychology* 1, no. 1 (Jan. 1964):1–27. doi:10.1016/0022-2496(64)90015-X.

Role — founding (algebraic) exposition of additive conjoint measurement: shows fundamental measurement need not require a concatenation operation; two attributes are scaled jointly from the order on their combinations, yielding interval scales with a common unit. Distinct from the KLST 1971 treatise (which codifies and generalizes it). Independently anticipated topologically by Debreu (1960); features anticipated by Hölder (1901).

Access — *J. Math. Psychol.* (Elsevier); widely reprinted/cited.
