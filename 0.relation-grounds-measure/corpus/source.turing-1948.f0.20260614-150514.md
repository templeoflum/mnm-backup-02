Alan M. Turing, "Rounding-Off Errors in Matrix Processes," *Quart. J. Mech. Appl. Math.* 1(1) (1948):287–308.

Role: formulates Gaussian elimination as the matrix factorization A = LU (the "unsymmetric Choleski" / triangular resolution), and introduces the term **condition number** of a matrix (κ = ‖A‖‖A⁻¹‖, plus his M- and N-condition variants). Note: distinct from [[source.turing-1936]] (the computability paper) — same author, different work; do not collapse.

Confidence — Higham (2002) and the Encyclopedia/secondary literature credit Turing with coining "condition number" and the LU formulation here; the conditioning idea is independently in [[source.vonneumann-goldstine-1947]] (1947). **[confirmed]**
