# source.gross-harris

Verbatim citation: Gross, D., Shortle, J. F., Thompson, J. M., & Harris, C. M. (2008). *Fundamentals of Queueing Theory* (4th ed.). Hoboken, NJ: John Wiley & Sons (Wiley Series in Probability and Statistics). First edition 1974 (Gross & Harris); 5th ed. 2018.

Contains: a comprehensive textbook treatment of the field — Markovian queues (M/M/1, M/M/c), Kendall's notation, Little's law, the Erlang loss and delay formulae, networks of queues, and bounds and approximations. Long regarded as a standard reference and course text. Serves as the corpus "door" into queueing theory. [confirmed]

Access notes: widely held; ISBN 978-0-471-79127-0 (4th ed.). Edition history confirmed against the Wiley catalog and multiple booksellers. [confirmed]
