Full citation — Qin Jiushao (秦九韶), *Shushu Jiuzhang* (數書九章, "Mathematical Treatise in Nine Sections"), 1247.

Role — first systematic general method for [[object.chinese-remainder-theorem]]: the *Dayan qiuyi shu* (大衍求一術, "Great Extension method for finding unity"), a step-by-step algorithm for systems of linear congruences, including cases where the moduli are not pairwise coprime.

Access — translations and analysis in U. Libbrecht, *Chinese Mathematics in the Thirteenth Century* (1973); summarized across Wikipedia / Britannica.
