Full citation — J. Maynard, "Small gaps between primes," *Annals of Mathematics* (2) 181 (2015), no. 1, 383–413. arXiv:1311.4600 (19 Nov 2013).

Contains a new multidimensional ("GPY-Maynard-Tao") sieve giving liminf_{n→∞} (p_{n+1} − p_n) ≤ 600 unconditionally — a far simpler proof than Zhang's and a much smaller bound. Also proves liminf_{n→∞} (p_{n+m} − p_n) < ∞ for every fixed m, and ≤ 12 (twin), ≤ 600 (m=2) under the Elliott–Halberstam conjecture. Terence Tao found the method independently and contemporaneously.

Role — the simplification/strengthening proof source for [[object.bounded-gaps-theorem]]; basis of the later Polymath8b bound 246.
