# source.little-1961

Verbatim citation: Little, J. D. C. (1961). "A Proof for the Queuing Formula: L = λW." *Operations Research*, **9**(3), 383-387.

Contains: a general proof that the mean number of units in a queueing system equals the mean arrival rate times the mean time a unit spends in the system, L = λW. The result is shown under the conditions that the three means are finite, the relevant processes strictly stationary, and the arrival process metrically transitive with nonzero mean; it does not depend on the arrival-time or service-time distributions, the number of servers, or the queueing discipline. [confirmed]
