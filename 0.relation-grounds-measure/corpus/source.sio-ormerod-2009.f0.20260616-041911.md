Verbatim citation: Sio, U. N., & Ormerod, T. C. (2009). Does incubation enhance problem solving? A meta-analytic review. *Psychological Bulletin*, 135(1), 94–120.

Contains: a meta-analysis of empirical incubation studies. Finds a positive incubation effect — a delay (an interpolated period away from a problem) improves later solution rates — with divergent-thinking tasks benefiting more than linguistic or visual insight tasks, and longer preparation periods and low-cognitive-demand incubation activities yielding larger effects. The quantitative anchor for Wallas's "incubation" stage.
