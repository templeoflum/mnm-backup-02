Verbatim citation: Clark, Andy. *Supersizing the Mind: Embodiment, Action, and Cognitive Extension*. New York: Oxford University Press, 2008. ISBN 9780195333213 (hardcover); 9780199773688 (paperback). ~320 pp.

Contains: an extended defense of the extended mind thesis first stated in Clark & Chalmers (1998), set within a survey of situated, embodied, and distributed cognition research. Rejects the "brainbound" model in favor of the extended model; addresses the constancy/coupling objections (e.g. the Adams & Aizawa "mark of the cognitive" critique) and develops cognitive extension via scaffolding and dynamical coupling. **[confirmed]**

Access notes: OUP catalogue (global.oup.com/academic/product/supersizing-the-mind-9780195333213); reviews in *Mind* and Notre Dame Philosophical Reviews; indexed on PhilPapers. **[confirmed]**
