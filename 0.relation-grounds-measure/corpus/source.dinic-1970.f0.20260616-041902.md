E. A. Dinic, "Algorithm for Solution of a Problem of Maximum Flow in a Network with Power Estimation," *Soviet Mathematics Doklady* 11 (1970): 1277–1280 (translated from the Russian *Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR*).

Contains: the level (layered) graph and blocking-flow construction; by augmenting a blocking flow in the layered residual network at each phase, the algorithm achieves O(V^2 E) maximum-flow time, predating and outperforming the Edmonds-Karp bound on dense graphs. Author later romanized as Yefim Dinitz; the method is variously cited as "Dinic's" / "Dinitz'" algorithm.

Access notes: original Russian Doklady 1970; widely cited via the English Soviet Math. Doklady translation.
