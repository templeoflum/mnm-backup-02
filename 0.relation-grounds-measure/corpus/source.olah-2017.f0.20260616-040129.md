**citation** — Chris Olah, Alexander Mordvintsev, and Ludwig Schubert. "Feature Visualization." Distill, 2017. https://distill.pub/2017/feature-visualization/ (DOI 10.23915/distill.00007). Authors affiliated with Google Brain / Google Research at publication.

**contains** — surveys feature visualization: answering "what is a network, or part of a network, looking for?" by optimizing an input image to maximize a chosen activation (neuron, channel, layer, logit), with regularization/preconditioning to produce interpretable rather than adversarial images. Positions feature visualization and attribution as the two major threads of interpretability research and is a direct precursor to the circuits program.
