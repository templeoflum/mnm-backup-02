**citation** — Konrad Z. Lorenz, "Der Kumpan in der Umwelt des Vogels. Der Artgenosse als auslösendes Moment sozialer Verhaltensweisen," *Journal für Ornithologie*, vol. 83 (1935), pp. 137–215 and 289–413.

**role** — origin (scientific ethology): the founding monograph of classical ethology. Introduces the "Kumpan" (companion) concept — that a young bird acquires a functionally distinct social companion for each social activity — and sets out the releaser / sign-stimulus / innate-releasing-schema framework. The locus classicus for Lorenz's account of *Prägung* (imprinting) in nidifugous birds.

**access** — original German in *Journal für Ornithologie* 83. English condensation: see [[source.lorenz-1937]] ("The Companion in the Bird's World," *The Auk* 54(3):245–273). Lorenz/Tinbergen/von Frisch shared the 1973 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
