*dual lineage*

**origin (scientific ethology)** — Lorenz & Tinbergen as co-founders; Tinbergen's four questions in "On Aims and Methods of Ethology," *Zeitschrift für Tierpsychologie* (1963). [[source.tinbergen-1963]] **[confirmed]**

**codification (ethology)** — Niko Tinbergen, *The Study of Instinct* (Oxford/Clarendon, 1951) — foundational first textbook. Lorenz/Tinbergen/von Frisch shared the 1973 Nobel. [[source.tinbergen-1951]] **[confirmed]**

**origin (horsemanship craft)** — ancient: Xenophon, *On Horsemanship* (*Peri Hippikēs*, c. 4th c. BCE), earliest surviving treatise. modern "natural horsemanship": Tom Dorrance, *True Unity* (1987); Ray Hunt, *Think Harmony with Horses*; Bill Dorrance, *True Horsemanship Through Feel*. [[source.xenophon-horsemanship]] [[source.dorrance-1987]] **[confirmed]**
