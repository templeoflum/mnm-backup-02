**citation** — Chris Olah, Nick Cammarata, Ludwig Schubert, Gabriel Goh, Michael Petrov, and Shan Carter. "Zoom In: An Introduction to Circuits." Distill, 2020. https://distill.pub/2020/circuits/zoom-in/ (DOI 10.23915/distill.00024.001). Authors affiliated with OpenAI at publication.

**contains** — the foundational circuits manifesto. Three claims, framed as speculative hypotheses: (1) **Features** — features are the fundamental unit of neural networks and correspond to directions in activation space; (2) **Circuits** — features are connected by weights forming circuits, meaningful algorithms readable in the weights; (3) **Universality** — analogous features and circuits recur across models and tasks. Worked examples include curve detectors and high-low frequency detectors in vision models (InceptionV1).
