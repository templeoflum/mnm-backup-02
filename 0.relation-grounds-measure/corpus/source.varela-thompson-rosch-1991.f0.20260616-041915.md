Varela, F. J., Thompson, E., & Rosch, E. (1991). *The Embodied Mind: Cognitive Science and Human Experience*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

Contains — introduces the *enactive* approach to cognition, in which mind, body, and world are co-determined; pioneers a sustained dialogue between cognitive science, Western phenomenology, and Buddhist meditative psychology (mindfulness/awareness). A founding text for contemplative science and embodied/enactive cognition.

Access — MIT Press; revised edition January 2017 (original text unchanged, two new introductions, foreword by Jon Kabat-Zinn). Widely cited. **[confirmed]**
