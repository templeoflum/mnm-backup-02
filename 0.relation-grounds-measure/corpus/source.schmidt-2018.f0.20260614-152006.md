Johannes F. K. Schmidt, "Niklas Luhmann's Card Index: The Fabrication of Serendipity," *Sociologica* 12, no. 1 (2018): 53–60. DOI 10.6092/issn.1971-8853/8350.

Role — primary archival scholarship on Luhmann's Zettelkasten. Schmidt is scientific coordinator of the Bielefeld long-term project "Niklas Luhmann — Theorie als Passion" (2015–2030), which digitized the ~90,000-card archive. This is the documented core that separates verified practice from later community interpretation of "Luhmann technique."

Access — open access at sociologica.unibo.it (article view 8350); companion digital archive at niklas-luhmann-archiv.de. **[confirmed]**
