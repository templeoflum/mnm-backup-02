**origin** — the alchemical corpus of Greco-Egyptian antiquity: Zosimos of Panopolis (3rd–4th c. CE), transmitter of Maria the Jewess's apparatus; the staged *magnum opus* — the colour sequence *nigredo → albedo → citrinitas → rubedo* and the laboratory operations (*solutio, calcinatio, coniunctio, sublimatio, coagulatio*). [[source.principe-2013]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Lawrence Principe & William Newman's revisionist "chymistry" scholarship recovers alchemy as genuine laboratory practice (chrysopoeia, *Decknamen* cover-names); Principe, *The Secrets of Alchemy* (Chicago, 2013), recreates the recipes at the bench. [[source.principe-2013]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Principe, *The Secrets of Alchemy* (2013).

**lineage** — **Principe/Newman vs. Jung**: laboratory chymistry vs. the psycho-spiritual allegory reading (Jung, *Psychology and Alchemy*, 1944) — carried as lineage; the Jungian reading recorded as the contested position, not the consensus. [[source.jung-1944]]
