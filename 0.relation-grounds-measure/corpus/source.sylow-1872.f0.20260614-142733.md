Full citation — P. Ludwig M. Sylow, "Théorèmes sur les groupes de substitutions," *Mathematische Annalen*, vol. 5 (1872), pp. 584–594. A treatise of about ten pages.

Role — origin / proof source for [[object.sylow-theorems]]: this single short paper states and proves all three results — existence of a subgroup of order pⁿ (the highest power of p dividing |G|), conjugacy of all such subgroups, and the count nₚ ≡ 1 (mod p). Sylow worked in the language of permutation (substitution) groups; the abstract-group restatement came later.
