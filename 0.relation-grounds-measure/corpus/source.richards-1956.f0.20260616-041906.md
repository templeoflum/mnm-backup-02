# richards-1956

**Citation (verbatim).** Richards, P. I. (1956). "Shock Waves on the Highway." *Operations Research*, 4(1): 42–51. DOI: 10.1287/opre.4.1.42.

**Contains.** Develops a simple continuum theory of traffic flow by replacing individual vehicles with a continuous "fluid" density and applying an empirical speed-density relation. Introduces a "graph-shearing" construction for following traffic-wave development and the frequent appearance of shock waves. Complements Lighthill & Whitham (1955), together forming the LWR model.
