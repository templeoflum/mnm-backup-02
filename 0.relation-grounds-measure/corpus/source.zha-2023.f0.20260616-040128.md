Daochen Zha, Zaid Pervaiz Bhat, Kwei-Herng Lai, Fan Yang, Zhimeng Jiang, Shaochen Zhong, and Xia Hu, "Data-centric Artificial Intelligence: A Survey," *ACM Computing Surveys* (2023). arXiv:2303.10158; DOI: 10.1145/3711118. Companion resource list at github.com/daochenzha/data-centric-AI.

Organizes data-centric AI into three goals — training-data development, inference-data development, and data maintenance — and surveys representative methods (collection, labeling, cleaning, reduction, augmentation, deduplication, quality assurance) and open challenges. Serves as an entry point and taxonomy for the field, motivating the shift of attention from model design to data quality and quantity.
