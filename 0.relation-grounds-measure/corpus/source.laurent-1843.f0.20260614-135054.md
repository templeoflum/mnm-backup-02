Full citation — P. A. Laurent, memoir on the expansion of functions in doubly-infinite power series, submitted to the Académie des Sciences for the Grand Prix in 1843; reported on by Cauchy in the *Comptes Rendus* and published after Laurent's death.

Role — first publication of the Laurent series (expansion valid on an annulus, with positive and negative powers). Laurent has publication priority; the result bears his name.

Access — historical text; Cauchy's report in *Comptes Rendus* 1843. Attribution reported in Wikipedia ("Laurent series," "Pierre Alphonse Laurent"), MacTutor (St Andrews), and arXiv:2606.06542. **[confirmed as attribution]**
