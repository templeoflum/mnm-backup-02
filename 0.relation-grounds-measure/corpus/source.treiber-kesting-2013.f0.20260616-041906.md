# treiber-kesting-2013

**Citation (verbatim).** Treiber, M., and Kesting, A. (2013). *Traffic Flow Dynamics: Data, Models and Simulation*. Berlin Heidelberg: Springer-Verlag. ISBN 978-3-642-32459-8 (print); 978-3-642-32460-4 (eBook). DOI: 10.1007/978-3-642-32460-4. Translated from the German by Christian Thiemann.

**Contains.** Comprehensive textbook covering empirical traffic data, fundamental diagrams, macroscopic (continuum/LWR and higher-order) models, microscopic car-following models, traffic instabilities, simulation, and traffic-state estimation. Serves as the standard "door" survey into the field.

**Access.** Published by Springer (link.springer.com, DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-32460-4); companion site traffic-flow-dynamics.org. Reviewed in *Physics Today* 67(3): 54. Bibliographic detail cross-checked across Springer and the publisher/author site [confirmed].
