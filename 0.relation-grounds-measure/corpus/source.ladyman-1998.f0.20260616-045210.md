James Ladyman, "What Is Structural Realism?" *Studies in History and Philosophy of Science Part A* 29, no. 3 (1998): 409–424. DOI: 10.1016/S0039-3681(98)80129-5.

Contains: the now-standard taxonomy. **Epistemic structural realism (ESR)** — there are unobservable objects with properties and relations, but we can know only the *structure* (the relations), not the intrinsic natures. **Ontic structural realism (OSR)** — we can know only structure because *structure is all there is*; the world just is structure. Ladyman argues the ESR reading is unstable and the ontic reading is the one a realist should take in light of modern physics (quantum statistics, particle (non)individuality, spacetime).
