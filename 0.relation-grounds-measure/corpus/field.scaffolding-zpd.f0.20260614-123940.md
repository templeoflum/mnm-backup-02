**origin (ZPD)** — Lev Vygotsky, zone of proximal development; early 1930s (d. 1934), to English via *Mind in Society* (Harvard, 1978, posthumous). [[source.vygotsky-1978]] **[confirmed]**

**origin (term "scaffolding")** — David Wood, Jerome S. Bruner & Gail Ross, "The Role of Tutoring in Problem Solving," *J. Child Psychology and Psychiatry* 17(2) (1976):89–100. [[source.wood-bruner-ross-1976]] **[confirmed]** *(distinction: Vygotsky never used "scaffolding"; the term is Wood-Bruner-Ross's, applied to the ZPD later.)*

**door** — *Mind in Society*; the 1976 paper circulates free as PDF. [[source.vygotsky-1978]]

*grade [t] — origin + door.*
