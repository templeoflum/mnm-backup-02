A. Tarski, "A lattice-theoretical fixpoint theorem and its applications," *Pacific Journal of Mathematics* **5**(2): 285–309 (1955).

The general form of the Knaster–Tarski theorem: a monotone map on a complete lattice has least and greatest fixed points, and the fixed-point set is itself a complete lattice. Distinct from the 1928 power-set special case (Knaster & Tarski).
