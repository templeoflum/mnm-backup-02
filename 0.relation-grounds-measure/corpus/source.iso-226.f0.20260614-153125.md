ISO 226, *Acoustics — Normal equal-loudness-level contours*. Editions: 1987 (based on Robinson & Dadson 1956), 2003 (re-determined from pooled international data), 2023 (latest revision).

Role — the modern standard defining equal-loudness contours and the **phon**. The generic term "equal-loudness contours" is now preferred; the Fletcher–Munson curves are a historical subset. Notably the 2003 re-determination found the original Fletcher–Munson (1933) curves in better low-frequency agreement than the Robinson–Dadson (1956) curves the 1987 standard rested on.
