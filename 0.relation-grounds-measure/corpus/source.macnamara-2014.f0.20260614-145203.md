Brooke N. Macnamara, David Z. Hambrick & Frederick L. Oswald, "Deliberate Practice and Performance in Music, Games, Sports, Education, and Professions: A Meta-Analysis," *Psychological Science* 25(8) (2014):1608–1618.

Role — critique / boundary. Meta-analysis finding deliberate practice explains ~12% of variance in performance overall, varying sharply by domain: 26% games, 21% music, 18% sports, 4% education, <1% professions. Conclusion: deliberate practice "is important, but not as important as has been argued." Companion to the Macnamara & Maitra (2019) replication of the 1993 violinist study (RSOS 6:190327), which found 26% variance vs. the original's 48%.

Access — *Psychological Science* (DOI 10.1177/0956797614535810); preprints widely circulated.
