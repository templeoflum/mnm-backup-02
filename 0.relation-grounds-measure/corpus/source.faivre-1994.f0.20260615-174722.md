Antoine Faivre, *Access to Western Esotericism* (SUNY, 1994; trans. of *Accès de l'ésotérisme occidental*) — defines esotericism by four intrinsic characteristics (correspondences, living nature, imagination/mediations, experience of transmutation) plus two secondary (praxis of concordance, transmission). The founding typological definition of the academic field.
