Eckart & Young, "The approximation of one matrix by another of lower rank," *Psychometrika* 1 (1936), 211–218.

Role — first proof of the singular value decomposition for general rectangular (and complex) matrices, and the **Eckart–Young theorem**: the truncated SVD gives the best rank-k approximation to a matrix in both the spectral (2-)norm and the Frobenius norm. They presented it as a generalization of the principal-axis transformation for Hermitian matrices.

Access — historical, English; *Psychometrika*.
