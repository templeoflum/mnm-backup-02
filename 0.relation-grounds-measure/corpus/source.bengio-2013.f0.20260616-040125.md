Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville, Pascal Vincent, "Representation Learning: A Review and New Perspectives," *IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence* 35(8) (August 2013), 1798–1828. DOI: 10.1109/TPAMI.2013.50.

Surveys unsupervised feature learning and deep learning (probabilistic models, autoencoders, manifold learning, deep nets) and argues a unifying program: the success of ML hinges on data representation, and a good representation disentangles the underlying explanatory factors of variation. Articulates the priors that guide representation learning — smoothness, the manifold hypothesis, depth/compositionality, distributed representation, disentanglement.

Available via IEEE Xplore / ACM DL (DOI above); preprint widely mirrored (arXiv:1206.5538).
