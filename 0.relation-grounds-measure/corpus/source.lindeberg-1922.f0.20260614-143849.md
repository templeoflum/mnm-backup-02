Full citation — J. W. Lindeberg, "Eine neue Herleitung des Exponentialgesetzes in der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung," *Mathematische Zeitschrift* **15** (1922), 211–225.

Role — introduces the **Lindeberg condition**, a sufficient condition for the central limit theorem for sums of independent variables that is more general than Lyapunov's, together with the elementary "**Lindeberg method**" (replacement / swapping argument) of proof avoiding characteristic functions. For i.i.d. variables with finite variance the condition holds automatically, giving the classical **Lindeberg–Lévy CLT**. Feller (1935) later showed the Lindeberg condition is essentially necessary (Lindeberg–Feller theorem).
