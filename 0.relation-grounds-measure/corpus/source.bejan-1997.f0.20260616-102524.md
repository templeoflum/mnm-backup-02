**citation (verbatim)** — A. Bejan, "Constructal-theory network of conducting paths for cooling a heat generating volume," *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer*, vol. 40, no. 4 (1997), pp. 799–816. DOI 10.1016/0017-9310(96)00175-5. (Received 1996.)

**what it contains** — the origin of constructal theory. Solves how to collect and channel to one point the heat generated throughout a low-conductivity volume, given a fixed amount of high-conductivity material for the channels. Building from the smallest element toward larger assemblies and optimizing shape and channel width at each scale yields a **tree-shaped network** in which every geometric detail is fixed by theory. Bejan generalizes this into the constructal principle of flow-system evolution toward easier access.
