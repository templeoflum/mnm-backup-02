Lutz, A., Dunne, J. D., & Davidson, R. J. (2007). Meditation and the neuroscience of consciousness: an introduction. In P. D. Zelazo, M. Moscovitch, & E. Thompson (Eds.), *The Cambridge Handbook of Consciousness* (ch. 19). Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

Contains — reviews early neuroscientific findings on meditation and argues for a more precise understanding of meditation as a scientific explanandum (the need to specify *which* practice is being studied). A foundational framing chapter for the neuroscience program of contemplative science.
