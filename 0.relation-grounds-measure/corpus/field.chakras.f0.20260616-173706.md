**origin** — the tantric/yogic subtle-body tradition of medieval India; the classical seven-chakra system is set out in the *Ṣaṭ-cakra-nirūpaṇa* (1577) of Pūrṇānanda. [[source.woodroffe-1919]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — Sir John Woodroffe (Arthur Avalon), *The Serpent Power* (1919), translated the *Ṣaṭ-cakra-nirūpaṇa* and the *Pādukā-pañcaka* and introduced the seven-chakra model to the West (the basis for most later popular systems). [[source.woodroffe-1919]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — Woodroffe, *The Serpent Power* (1919).

**lineage** — the classical tantric account (chakras as ritual/meditative loci with bījas and deities) vs. the modern Western psychologized/"rainbow" model; carried as lineage. Held as the tradition's account (members `status: doctrinal`), the subtle anatomy not asserted as physiological fact.
