Martin Davis, *Computability and Unsolvability* (McGraw-Hill, 1958; Dover reprint 1982).

Role: the first textbook treatment of computability and unsolvability; the term "the halting problem" first appears in print in this book (Davis had used it in lectures from ~1952). Carries the modern self-referential framing of the result that Turing's 1936 paper made possible but did not itself name.

Access — Dover reprint in print; widely held. **[confirmed]**
