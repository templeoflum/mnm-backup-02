S. Geman & D. Geman, "Stochastic Relaxation, Gibbs Distributions, and the Bayesian Restoration of Images," *IEEE Trans. Pattern Anal. Mach. Intell.* PAMI-6, no. 6 (1984):721–741.

Role — introduces and names the Gibbs sampler (sampling each variable from its full conditional), in the setting of Bayesian image restoration over a Markov random field; also gives a convergence proof for simulated annealing. Distinct from Hastings (1970) and from Metropolis et al. (1953). NOTE the two authors are brothers Stuart and Donald Geman — a single paper, not two.
