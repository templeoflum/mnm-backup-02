James Ladyman, "Structural Realism," *The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, ed. E. N. Zalta — first published 14 November 2007; substantively revised in later editions. URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/structural-realism/

Contains: the door for the field. Covers the no-miracles / pessimistic-meta-induction motivation, Worrall's "best of both worlds," the epistemic vs ontic distinction, "relations without relata," and the standing objections (the Newman problem; Demopoulos & Friedman; Psillos). Bibliography is the canonical entry point.

Access: open access at plato.stanford.edu. Author/citation info at plato.stanford.edu/cgi-bin/encyclopedia/archinfo.cgi?entry=structural-realism.
