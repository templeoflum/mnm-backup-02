Full citation — *Sunzi Suanjing* (孫子算經, "Sunzi's Mathematical Classic"), Chinese mathematical text, c. 3rd–5th century CE.

Role — origin: earliest known statement of the congruence problem behind [[object.chinese-remainder-theorem]]. Poses the single example "count by threes, remainder 2; by fives, remainder 3; by sevens, remainder 2 — how many?" with a worked answer (23) but no general method or proof.

Access — translations in standard histories of Chinese mathematics; problem quoted across Wikipedia / Britannica.
