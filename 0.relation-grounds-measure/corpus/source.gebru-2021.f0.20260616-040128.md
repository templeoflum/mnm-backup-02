Timnit Gebru, Jamie Morgenstern, Briana Vecchione, Jennifer Wortman Vaughan, Hanna Wallach, Hal Daumé III, and Kate Crawford, "Datasheets for Datasets," *Communications of the ACM* 64, no. 12 (December 2021): 86–92. DOI: 10.1145/3458723.

Proposes that, by analogy to the datasheet accompanying every electronic component, each machine-learning dataset be accompanied by a datasheet documenting its motivation, composition, collection process, recommended uses, distribution, and maintenance. Frames documentation as a tool for transparency, accountability, bias mitigation, and reproducibility, and for matching datasets to appropriate tasks. The CACM article is the peer-reviewed version of the widely cited preprint.
