Edsger W. Dijkstra, "A Note on Two Problems in Connexion with Graphs," *Numerische Mathematik* 1 (1959), pp. 269–271. DOI 10.1007/BF01386390.

**citation** — a three-page note presenting two algorithms: Problem 1 is the minimum spanning tree (a Prim/Jarník-type method); Problem 2 is the single-source shortest-path algorithm now universally called Dijkstra's algorithm. Dijkstra reportedly designed it in about twenty minutes in 1956 and published it in 1959.
