E. N. Lorenz, "Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow," *Journal of the Atmospheric Sciences*, vol. 20, no. 2 (1963), pp. 130–141.

A three-variable system of ODEs derived from Rayleigh–Bénard convection whose trajectories settle onto a bounded aperiodic set (later the "Lorenz attractor"), displaying sensitive dependence on initial conditions — the butterfly effect. The founding empirical example of deterministic chaos.
