**origin** — Hellenistic astrology (Egypt, ~2nd c. BCE – 2nd c. CE), synthesizing Babylonian celestial omens, Egyptian decans, and Greek philosophy; the twelve tropical signs measured from the vernal equinox. [[source.ptolemy-tetrabiblos]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — Claudius Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos* (c. 150 CE) — the foundational systematic text: signs, planets, aspects, houses, and their significations; the basis of the later Western tradition. [[source.ptolemy-tetrabiblos]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos*.

**lineage** — *tropical* (equinox-anchored, Western) vs. *sidereal* (star-anchored, Vedic — see [[field.vedic-astrology]], to ground) — carried as lineage. The three outer planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) are **modern additions** (post-1781), not in Ptolemy. Held as the tradition's symbolic system (members `status: doctrinal`).
