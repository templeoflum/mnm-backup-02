Full citation — L. Euler, "Theorematum quorundam ad numeros primos spectantium demonstratio" (E54), *Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae* 8, 141–146. Carry the date as "1736 (presented to the St. Petersburg Academy 2 Aug 1736; printed 1741)."

Role — first published proof of [[object.fermat-little-theorem]], via induction on the base. Distinct from Euler's E53 Königsberg-bridges paper, which sits in the *same journal volume* (Commentarii 8) but founds graph theory — see [[source.euler-1736]]; do not conflate the two.

Access — Euler Archive E54 (eulerarchive.maa.org / scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/54).

**[confirmed]** (Euler Archive + secondary sources agree on paper, journal, and that it is the first published proof).
