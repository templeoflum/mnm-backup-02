AICPA, AU Section 350, *Audit Sampling* (pre-Clarity codification; the audit-sampling content was recodified as AU-C 530 — see [[source.au-c-530]]).

Role — the audit literature cites AU 350 as the standard that **describes the audit risk model**, AR = IR × CR × DR, together with the component definitions (inherent, control, detection risk). Retained as a distinct source handle because the model is conventionally attributed here, separate from the current sampling standard.

Access — AICPA Codification (paid); the model and its attribution to AU 350 are reported in the CPA Journal and the Wikipedia "Audit risk" article. **[confirmed as attribution]**
