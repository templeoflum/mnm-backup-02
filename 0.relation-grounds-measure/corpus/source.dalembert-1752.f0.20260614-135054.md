Full citation — J. le Rond d'Alembert, *Essai d'une nouvelle théorie de la résistance des fluides*, Paris, 1752.

Role — earliest appearance of the relations now called the Cauchy–Riemann equations, arising in hydrodynamics (the coupling of velocity-potential components), not yet as a criterion for analyticity.

Access — historical text; the attribution is reported in the Encyclopedia of Mathematics ("Cauchy–Riemann equations"), Wikipedia, and the historical review arXiv:2311.16649. **[confirmed as attribution]**
