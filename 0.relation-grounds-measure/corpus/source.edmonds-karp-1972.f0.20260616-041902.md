Jack Edmonds and Richard M. Karp, "Theoretical Improvements in Algorithmic Efficiency for Network Flow Problems," *Journal of the ACM* 19(2) (1972): 248–264.

Contains: derivation of upper bounds on the number of augmentation steps for network-flow algorithms; the result that choosing a shortest (fewest-arc) augmenting path at each step yields an O(VE^2) maximum-flow algorithm whose running time does not depend on the arc capacities — settling the termination/efficiency gap left by the generic Ford-Fulkerson method.
