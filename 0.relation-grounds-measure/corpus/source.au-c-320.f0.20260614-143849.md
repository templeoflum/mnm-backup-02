AICPA, AU-C Section 320, *Materiality in Planning and Performing an Audit*. The IAASB counterpart is ISA 320.

Role — authoritative source for the **materiality** concept: misstatements are material if they "could reasonably be expected to influence the economic decisions of users taken on the basis of the financial statements"; materiality is a matter of professional judgment, set in planning and used in evaluating misstatements.

Access — AICPA Professional Standards (paid); ISA 320 free in the IAASB Handbook (ifac.org). Definition concurs with Wikipedia "Materiality (auditing)" and ICAEW guidance. **[confirmed]**
