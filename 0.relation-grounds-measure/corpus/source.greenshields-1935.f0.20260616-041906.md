# greenshields-1935

**Citation (verbatim).** Greenshields, B. D. (1935). "A Study of Traffic Capacity." *Highway Research Board Proceedings*, 14: 448–477.

**Contains.** Early empirical study using photographic methods to measure traffic speed, density, and flow. Reports an approximately linear speed-density relation, which yields a parabolic flow-density relation — the first quantitative fundamental diagram. Identifies derived quantities including maximum (capacity) flow and the density at which it occurs.

**Access.** Published in *Highway Research Board Proceedings* (now Transportation Research Board); cited across the field and re-examined in TRB Circular E-C149, "75 Years of the Fundamental Diagram for Traffic Flow Theory."
