Heinz von Foerster, "Objects: Tokens for (Eigen-)Behaviors" (1976).

Role — introduces the concept of **eigenform / eigenbehavior**: objects are tokens for the stable (recurrent) behaviors that arise in the recursive interaction of an observer with its environment. Presented in the mid-1970s (a French version, "Objets: signes stables de comportements (eigen-)," appeared in *Hommage à Jean Piaget: Épistémologie génétique et équilibration*, eds. Inhelder, Garcia & Voneche, 1977); reprinted in von Foerster's collections *Observing Systems* (1981) and *Understanding Understanding* (Springer, 2003).

citation: von Foerster, H. (1976). Objects: Tokens for (Eigen-)Behaviors. *ASC Cybernetics Forum*, 8(3-4), 91-96.

access — reprinted in *Understanding Understanding* (2003); French version in the 1977 Piaget festschrift.
