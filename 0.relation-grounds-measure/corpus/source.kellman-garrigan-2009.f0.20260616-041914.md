Philip J. Kellman & Patrick Garrigan, "Perceptual learning and human expertise," *Physics of Life Reviews*, 6(2), 53–84 (2009). DOI 10.1016/j.plrev.2008.12.001.

Reviews perceptual learning as experience-induced change in information extraction and as a fundamental contributor to expertise in domains such as language, chess, music, mathematics, and medicine. Distinguishes **discovery effects** (changes in what features/relations are selected) from **fluency effects** (changes in the speed, automaticity, and attentional cost of extraction), and motivates the perceptual learning module (PLM) approach to instruction.
