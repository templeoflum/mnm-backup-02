**origin** — the *Yìjīng* (I Ching, *Book of Changes*), one of the oldest Chinese classics; 64 hexagrams = the 8 trigrams paired (8×8), each with a judgment and image, used as an oracle and a book of wisdom. [[source.wilhelm-baynes-1950]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — the King Wén sequence and the Ten Wings (commentaries); Richard Wilhelm's translation (English by Cary F. Baynes, 1950) is the standard Western text. [[source.wilhelm-baynes-1950]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — Wilhelm/Baynes, *The I Ching or Book of Changes* (1950).

**lineage** — each hexagram composes two of the eight [[field.iching-trigrams]] (upper + lower); themes here follow the Wilhelm reading. Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
