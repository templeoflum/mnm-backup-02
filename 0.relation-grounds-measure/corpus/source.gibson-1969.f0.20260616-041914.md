Eleanor J. Gibson, *Principles of Perceptual Learning and Development*. New York: Appleton-Century-Crofts, 1969 (Century Psychology Series). x + 538 pp. ISBN 0-390-36145-3.

The systematic treatise that established perceptual learning as a field. Develops differentiation theory: perceptual learning is the progressive extraction of distinctive features and higher-order invariants from stimulation, increasing the correspondence between perception and the environment. Treats learning across the lifespan, including reading and the development of perception in children.

Reviewed in *Science* 168(3934), 958 (1970). Out of print; available via libraries and used-book channels.
