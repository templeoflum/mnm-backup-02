Full citation — A. F. Möbius, *Der barycentrische Calcul* ("The Barycentric Calculus"), Leipzig: Johann Ambrosius Barth, 1827.

Role — origin: introduces homogeneous coordinates by specifying a point as the barycenter (center of mass) of weights placed at the vertices of a fixed reference triangle; scaling all weights leaves the point fixed, which is exactly the scale-invariance of homogeneous coordinates. This is the first systematic homogeneous-coordinate system.

Same-name / same-author trap — distinct from [[source.mobius-1832]] (Möbius's 1832 Crelle paper introducing the number-theoretic Möbius function μ(n) and its inversion formula). Different work, different field. Do not merge.
