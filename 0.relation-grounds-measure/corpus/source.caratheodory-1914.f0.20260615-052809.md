C. Carathéodory, "Über das lineare Maß von Punktmengen — eine Verallgemeinerung des Längenbegriffs," *Nachrichten von der Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen, Mathematisch-Physikalische Klasse* (1914), 404–426.

Introduces outer measures as an abstract basis for the theory of measurable sets and countably additive measures; the Carathéodory measurability criterion (a set E is measurable iff it splits every test set additively) underlies the modern abstract construction of measures.
