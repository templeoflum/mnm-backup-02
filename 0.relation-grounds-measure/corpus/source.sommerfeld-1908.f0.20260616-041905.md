Full citation — A. Sommerfeld, "Ein Beitrag zur hydrodynamischen Erklärung der turbulenten Flüssigkeitsbewegungen" ("A contribution to the hydrodynamic explanation of turbulent fluid motions"), in *Atti del IV Congresso Internazionale dei Matematici* (Rome, 6–11 April 1908), vol. III, pp. 116–124. Pub. 1909.

Contains — Sommerfeld's derivation of the linear eigenvalue equation governing two-dimensional disturbances to a viscous parallel flow, framing the laminar–turbulent transition as a hydrodynamic-stability problem. With Orr's 1907 work, gives the **Orr–Sommerfeld equation** its name.

Access — historical; congress proceedings digitized (mathunion ICM archive). Citation detail confirmed against the Wikipedia "Orr–Sommerfeld equation" article and Eckert (*Eur. Phys. J. H* 35, 2010). **[confirmed as attribution]**
