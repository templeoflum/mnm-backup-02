Full citation — M. H. Stone, "Applications of the Theory of Boolean Rings to General Topology," *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* **41**(3) (1937), 375–481.

Contains the original, considerably general form of what became the Stone–Weierstrass theorem: density of a separating subalgebra (containing constants) in C(X) for compact Hausdorff X. Stone simplified and re-presented the proof in his 1948 *Mathematics Magazine* exposition.

Access — published by the AMS; widely available through JSTOR / AMS archives. **[confirmed]**
