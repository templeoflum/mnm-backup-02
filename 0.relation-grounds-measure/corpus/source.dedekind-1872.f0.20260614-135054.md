Full citation — R. Dedekind, *Stetigkeit und irrationale Zahlen* (Vieweg, Braunschweig, 1872); English "Continuity and Irrational Numbers" in *Essays on the Theory of Numbers* (trans. W. W. Beman, Open Court, 1901).

Contains the **Dedekind-cut** construction of the real numbers and the explicit characterization of the *continuity* (line-completeness) of ℝ: every cut of the rationals is produced by exactly one real number — equivalently, ℝ has no gaps. This is the construction-side source for the least-upper-bound / completeness property. Distinct from the existing `source.dedekind-1897` (lattice "Dualgruppe" work — different subject).

Access — German original and Beman translation widely available (public domain). **[confirmed]**
