Claudius Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos* (*Apotelesmatika*), c. 150 CE (standard English: F. E. Robbins, Loeb Classical Library, 1940).

The foundational Greco-Roman systematization of astrology — the natures of the planets and signs, elements/qualities, aspects, and houses — on which the Western tradition rests. **[confirmed as attribution]** *(classical primary; cited for the sign/planet significations. The three outer planets are modern, post-Ptolemaic.)*
