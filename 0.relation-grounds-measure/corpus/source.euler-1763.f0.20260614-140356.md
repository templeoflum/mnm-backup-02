Full citation — L. Euler, "Theoremata arithmetica nova methodo demonstrata" (E271), *Novi Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae* 8, 74–104. Carry the date as "1763 (presented to the Berlin Academy 8 June 1758; printed 1763)."

Role — the paper in which Euler proves the generalization of Fermat's little theorem to a composite modulus: a^φ(n) ≡ 1 (mod n) for gcd(a, n) = 1. The result appears as Theorem 11; φ is referred to as "numerus partium ad N primarum" (not yet named the totient). Source for [[object.euler-theorem-totient]].
