*living, fast-moving subfield of data-centric AI — flag on card*

**origin** — emerges as the data-quality counterpart to model-centric ML; consolidated when documentation and data-work were named as first-class concerns. Gebru et al., "Datasheets for Datasets" (preprint 2018, CACM 2021) made dataset documentation a standardized artifact, and Sambasivan et al. (CHI 2021) named **data cascades** and the systematic undervaluation of data work. [[source.gebru-2021]] [[source.sambasivan-2021]] **[confirmed on works/years]**

**codification** — Ng's data-centric AI framing and the 2021 Data-Centric AI Competition reframed the field as improving data under a fixed model; Lee et al. (ACL 2022) gave a concrete, measurable curation operation (deduplication) with quantified effects on quality and memorization. [[source.ng-2021]] [[source.lee-2022]] **[confirmed on documents; framing is recent and living]**

**door** — Zha et al., "Data-centric Artificial Intelligence: A Survey" (ACM Computing Surveys / arXiv:2303.10158, 2023) — taxonomy and entry point: training-data development, inference-data development, data maintenance. [[source.zha-2023]] **[confirmed]**

lineage — the founding is distributed rather than single-authored: documentation (Gebru), data-work/HCI (Sambasivan), data-centric framing (Ng), and large-scale curation operations (Lee) are independent threads that converged into one field. **[confirmed]**

*grade [s] — origin, codification, and door all present.*
