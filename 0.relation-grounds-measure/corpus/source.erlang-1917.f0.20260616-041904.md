# source.erlang-1917

Verbatim citation: Erlang, A. K. (1917). "Solution of Some Problems in the Theory of Probabilities of Significance in Automatic Telephone Exchanges." *Elektroteknikeren*, **13**, 5-13. English translation: *The Post Office Electrical Engineers' Journal*, **10** (1917-1918), 189-197.

Contains: Erlang's loss formula (Erlang B, for blocked-calls-cleared / no-queue systems) and his delay/waiting formula (Erlang C, for blocked-calls-delayed / queued systems), giving the probability of blocking and of waiting as functions of offered load and number of circuits. The mathematical foundation of dimensioning for telephone trunk groups and, by extension, multi-server queues. [confirmed]

Access notes: primary in Danish, with a contemporaneous English translation in the P.O.E.E.J. Bibliographic detail confirmed against multiple independent secondary sources (Erlang biographies; teletraffic histories). Primary not opened here. [confirmed as attribution]
