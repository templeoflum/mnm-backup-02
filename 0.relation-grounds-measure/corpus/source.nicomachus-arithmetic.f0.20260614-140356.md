Nicomachus of Gerasa, *Introduction to Arithmetic* (*Arithmetike eisagoge*), early 2nd century CE (c. 100 CE).

The earliest surviving text that describes the [[object.sieve-of-eratosthenes]] and attributes it to Eratosthenes of Cyrene (c. 276–194 BC). Eratosthenes' own writing of the method does not survive; Nicomachus is the door to the attribution.
