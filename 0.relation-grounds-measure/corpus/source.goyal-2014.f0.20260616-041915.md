Goyal, M., Singh, S., Sibinga, E. M. S., Gould, N. F., Rowland-Seymour, A., Sharma, R., et al. (2014). Meditation Programs for Psychological Stress and Well-being: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. *JAMA Internal Medicine*, 174(3), 357–368. doi:10.1001/jamainternmed.2013.13018

Contains — AHRQ-commissioned systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials of meditation programs. Finds moderate evidence that mindfulness-meditation programs produce small-to-moderate reductions in anxiety, depression, and pain, and low evidence for effects on stress/well-being; little/insufficient evidence that meditation outperforms active controls (e.g. exercise, drugs). The standard bracketed reference for the clinical evidence base.
