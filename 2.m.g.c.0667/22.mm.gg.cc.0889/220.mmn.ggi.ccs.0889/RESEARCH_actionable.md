## THE ONE BIG FINDING (frames everything)
Four independent fields — philosophy (OSR), geometry (shape spaces/canonicalization),
statistics (intrinsic dimension), physics (emergent geometry) — converge on ONE claim:
**identity & dimension are not intrinsic; they are what survives relational measurement.**
This is TS's thesis, arrived at independently from four directions. That convergence is
the signal. It is NOT a theory of everything (a terminus) — it's the opposite: every frame
is a frame, the unit is always self-referential. A theory of pursuit, not of arrival.

## BUILDABLE / RUNNABLE (ranked)

   - ABID / TWO-NN / DANCo measure dimension from neighbor relations (distances, angles).
     Off-the-shelf: `intRinsic` R package; ELKI library; scikit-dimension (Python).
   - Run them on TS's own generated forms. Question: does MEASURED intrinsic dimension

   - If it DOESN'T (a "4D" form reads ~3.2): that is the thesis CONFIRMED, not broken —

   - TS normalization (divide by rmax, recenter COM) IS canonicalization in the technical
     sense: one representative per orbit (Kaba/Ravanbakhsh 2023).

   - Action: TS's normalize-by-rmax MUST have discontinuity points (near-tied outer radii).
     Find them. Same class as the fingerprint's known blind spot. Measure, don't assume smooth.

3. ADD AN INTEGRAL-INVARIANT / CURVATURE SIGNATURE (the parked corner descriptor). [REAL]

   - De-risks the parked corner-descriptor arc: not inventing, adopting a known signature.

4. GROUND THE OPERATION SET (TS + lattice both).  [REAL — deeper foundation]
   - Chasles: every 3D rigid motion = a screw (rotation + translation along same axis).
     TS's [rotate, rotate, translate] is, up to composition, the general rigid motion.
   - Cartan–Dieudonné: every isometry = composition of ≤ n reflections; two reflections =
     a rotation (mirrors intersect) OR a translation (mirrors parallel). So rotate & translate
     are the SAME primitive (reflection) under a limit (mirror angle → 0). Possible reframe
     of the lattice's does/goes as one operation seen two ways.
   - Self-affine / IFS + logarithmic spiral: names TS's self-scaling — "scaling ≡ rotation"
     in a log spiral (Bernoulli, "eadem mutata resurgo"). The third TS operation is the
     IFS fixed-point sense. Words the modifier we couldn't settle.

5. LINGUISTIC GROUNDING FOR THE LATTICE'S GRAMMAR.  [REAL but narrow — SUPERSEDED, see 002]
   - Baker's UTAH: "identical thematic relationships ↔ identical structural relationships."
     Early backing for the lattice's bet that the GRAMMAR POSITION (noun/verb/modifier slot)
     carries the structural role.
   - Fillmore's deep cases include Source/Goal (= Trajectory's origin/destination) and
     Agentive (= the agent·does·effect root). Dowty's Proto-Agent/Proto-Patient = roles as

## THE THREE FINDINGS THAT GROUND THE STRUCTURE
1. The mind/make architecture (motionless navigator vs physical memory-holder) is a NAMED,
   developed position — not idiosyncratic. Exogram/engram (Donald), extended mind (Clark &
   Chalmers), cognitive niche construction + the navigate-build loop (Constant/Clark/Friston).
2. The one-structure-at-every-scale grammar bet has TWO rigorous precedents — X-bar theory
   (Chomsky/Jackendoff: one template, every phrasal level) and categorial/type-logical
   grammar (Montague homomorphism, Lambek) — PLUS measured fractal self-similarity in language

3. The Make/Mind cut and the jobsite register are Aristotle's poiesis-vs-praxis + the whole
   tacit-knowledge tradition (Polanyi, Dreyfus, Kuijpers/Crevels, Merleau-Ponty). The three
   branches map onto Aristotle's epistêmê / phronesis / technê.

      drives of the reader." = test the name against the WHOLE lattice, not local satisfaction.

  (d) NEGATIVE PARAMETERS — "we can say with certainty what cannot be good; the list of what can
      be good is infinite." = our settle-by-surviving: the blade KILLS, never crowns. "Settled"

- ACTION: this is the only genuinely useful import. Optionally name Eco in the DISCIPLINE doc's
