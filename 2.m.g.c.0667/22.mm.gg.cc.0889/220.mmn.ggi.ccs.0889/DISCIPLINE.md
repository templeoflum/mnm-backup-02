# DISCIPLINE — how we work
> The moves that earned their place by catching real errors. Held throughout.

## seeing — find the shape
- **Scaffold to see.** Make the rough, vivid, over-rich version — the dream, the sketch, the figure — to look *through*. It's the tool that finds the shape, not the ground.
- **Don't attach to the scaffold.** When you catch yourself building *with* the rough version instead of seeing *through* it, drop it. And when intent's unclear, ask one line rather than spin up a guess.
- **Navigation vs recall.** A map locates; recall reasons. Keep them apart — no recall in the maps.

## building — make it true
- **Go to the mechanic, not the story.** The code, the actual thing, the literal grammar — not the satisfying narrative or how a name sounds.
- **Re-found, don't patch.** Tidying the surface means the frame is wrong. Build the true thing from what the scaffold revealed.
- **Verify, report negatives.** "Verified" only when it points at a re-runnable check.

## holding — keep it sound
- **Propose, don't crown.** AI proposes & verifies; the human disposes. Propose freely — the constraint is on crowning, not proposing.
- **Settle by use.** Binary: settled or not. Things float by default; they settle only by sitting true through use, and only the human settles them.
- **Advance, don't edit.** Copy forward and freeze the known-good when a change threatens it.
