the dots on the axes are projections — the one motion read along one direction at a time. a projection is a lawful reading, not a shadow: the two readings together are the whole circle, nothing lost. the wave is the vertical reading's diary; change the period mid-turn and your handwriting stays written in it. the faint ticks are recorded turn-completions — events the toy lived through, not arithmetic.
