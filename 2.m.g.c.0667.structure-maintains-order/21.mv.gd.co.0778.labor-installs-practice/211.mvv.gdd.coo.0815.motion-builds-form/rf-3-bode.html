Sweep ζ like a device on a network analyzer.

Put ζ(σ+jω) on the bench and read it the way you'd read any filter: magnitude in decibels, phase in degrees, and group delay. A zero of ζ is, to this instrument, an unmistakable signature — a magnitude notch plunging toward −∞ dB with a sharp phase flip right through it.

The reading. On the critical line (σ=½) the notches go all the way down — |ζ| is exactly zero at each zero, so the dB plunges without bound and the phase snaps through ~180°. Pull σ off ½ and every notch lifts into a finite dip: you're no longer passing through a zero, just near it. That's the analyzer's way of seeing the Hypothesis — on the line the notches are bottomless; off it they're merely dents.
