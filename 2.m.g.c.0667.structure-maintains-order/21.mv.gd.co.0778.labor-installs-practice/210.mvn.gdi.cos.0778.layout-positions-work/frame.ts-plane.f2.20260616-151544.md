## the subject (settled, carried from f1)

The plane is a **geometry** and the unit's **stage**. **Klein** (transformation group) ⊗ **Riemann** (local curvature) = **Cartan** (local symmetry model over a curved base; curvature = deviation from the flat model, the `C/flat` readout). Our knobs ARE the field's three axes:
- **κ (curvature)** = Riemann / local — **the master dial**
- **isometry (the unit's motion)** = Klein / transformation group — strict end of the rigidity spectrum (where the unit circle survives)
- **chart (cartesian/polar)** = method / epistemic — free (gauge, no invariant changes)

**Faithful baseline:** κ's sign fixes the geometry-type, which **derives** the algebra (Cayley–Klein: complex/dual/split) and the available isometries (rotation always; horocycle/translate only κ<0). No unfaithful state is reachable. Built + confirmed (diag sweep + live).
