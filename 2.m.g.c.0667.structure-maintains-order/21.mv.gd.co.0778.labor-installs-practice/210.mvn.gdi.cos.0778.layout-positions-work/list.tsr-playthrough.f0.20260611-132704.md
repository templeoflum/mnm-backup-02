## the rules of the playthrough

- **overlays OFF for every form shot** — overlaysMasterChk off, watch the form itself. overlays may be used to *find* a form, never in its portrait.
- **every screenshot carries its recipe** — formula · level · k · ratio · multipliers (L0…Ln) · timebase · cpf · strobe, recorded beside the shot. a form without its recipe cannot be compared or reproduced.
- **one knob at a time when chasing** — the form's answer must be attributable.

- level 0 is skipped everywhere — always the same (the point seed, already recorded).
