TS → RH · the blind spot, made concrete

The zeros' secret isn't their shape — it's that they refuse to crowd.

TS's fingerprint reads gross shape and radial fill. It is provably blind to one thing: how points are spaced relative to each other. And spacing is exactly where the zeros hide what makes them special — they repel, like the energy levels of a quantum system, never falling close together. Swap between the real zeros, a random spectrum, and a rigid one: a gross-shape measure can't tell them apart, but the spacing can.

What you're seeing. The first two metrics are the kind TS's fingerprint can read — and they stay the same across all three spectra by construction. The third, the fraction of near-touching pairs, is the one the fingerprint is blind to and the one that separates them: the zeros sit near zero small gaps (hard repulsion), random sits near 25%, rigid sits at zero from the other side. The real zeros match the green GUE curve — the same statistics as random-matrix eigenvalues. That correlation structure is the part of RH-adjacent reality an angular/pair-correlation measurement is needed to see.
