The primes are strings. The zeros are the notes.

In log coordinates each prime sits at log p, and its powers p², p³… fall on the overtone ladder 2 log p, 3 log p… — the harmonics of a string. The Euler factor 1 + p⁻ˢ + p⁻²ˢ + … is that harmonic series. Add strings to the orchestra and listen: it resonates at the zeros.
