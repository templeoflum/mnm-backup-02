does a self-adjoint operator exist?  →  real eigenvalues  →  zeros on Re = ½  →  RH

Self-adjoint operators always have real eigenvalues. So if the heights of the zeros were the eigenvalues of one, they’d be trapped on the critical line — and RH would be proved. No such operator is known. This shows only the lever it would pull.
