Where ½ comes from — and where the idea stops. The strip has width exactly 1 because that's the span the reflection folds in half, and ½ is its fixed point: the only real part where σ = 1−σ. That's why this same seam keeps surfacing in every lens — it's a fixed point of a symmetry, so no coordinate system can move it. The Hypothesis reads as: ξ only vanishes on the crease, the line of perfect balance with its reflection — never off-balance. The honest limit: ½ is forced by this reflection and its conserved 1, not by ½ being special in the abstract. The idea is load-bearing exactly as long as it stays tied to the symmetry s↔1−s; detached from it, "the unit is 1, the balance is ½" would fit anything and so explain nothing.
