cell 05 · the unit maker

pour any shape in — it comes out size one, and ½ appears for free.
the chart, declared: subtract the center of mass, divide by the largest radius. position and size are spent to buy comparability; orientation is kept — a tilted specimen stays tilted. once every form has radius 1, the ½ ring is the same address on the bench for all of them — what sits at the ring differs per specimen; the ring itself never does.
