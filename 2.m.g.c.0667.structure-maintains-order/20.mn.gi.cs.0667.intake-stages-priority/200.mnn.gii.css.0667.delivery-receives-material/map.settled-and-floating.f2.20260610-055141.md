**The frame this map serves:** the most lovingly crafted measurement device for the new age — every paradigm relatable on one bench. **The premise (user's, governing):** nothing ever truly agrees from every angle — variation, deviation, noise, vibration always, and no view sees every plane at once. So the device translates, compares across translations, and **measures deviations; it never asserts sameness.** It is a calibration tool that measures the *similarity structure* and the *difference structure* simultaneously, as quantities, and never collapses one into the other — that collapse is the one sin the instrument may never commit. RH is the first patient; TS's full vision is the horizon. **The way:** first principles, cell-level, small steps because they feel good; prediction before build (bath named, dominant term said, derivation checked against one number, ratios divided not felt — see the journal's standing clauses); build → feel → log prediction-vs-actual; toys breed the next generation; two generations, then stop and review.
