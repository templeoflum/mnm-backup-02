# the toy list — dreamed, not reasoned
*f1 (live) · numbered for pointing, nothing built until walked · build numbers are sequential and decoupled from list numbers — the list is checked off by name (see registry below)*

---

## cells — the atoms, reborn tiny

1 · **the spinning arrow** — one arrow walking one circle. the first heartbeat.
2 · **two arrows, stacked** — the little one rides the big one. epicycle!
3 · **the growing turn** — an arrow that swells as it spins. the seashell curve. the primitive itself.
4 · **the clock stretcher** — the same circle on different clocks. fast time, slow time, log time.
5 · **the unit maker** — pour any shape in, it comes out size one. watch ½ appear for free.
6 · **the two ½s** — pin a center by squashing to fit, or by folding to match. same point, different hands.
7 · **the weyl walker** — a dot that steps by √2 forever and never lands twice. the fairest sampler.
8 · **the cloud and its projection** — points become an ellipsoid. squeeze, and watch the three numbers move.
9 · **one mirror** — a cloud and its reflection. do they land on each other? the first yes/no.
10 · **one tone** — a single circling signal, a single spectral spike. the simplest listening.

## organs — the instruments, rebuilt from cells

11 · **the prime staircase** — counting steps that only some numbers get to climb.
12 · **the wave that knows the stairs** — one zero's wave laid on the staircase. then two. then ten.
13 · **the orchestra** — every wave at once; the staircase walks out of the chorus.
14 · **the zero dragger** — pick up γ₁, pull it off the line, hear the primes go sour under your finger.
15 · **the breathing room** — every thread swelling at one rate; one knob to detune a single thread.
16 · **the mirror strip** — s ↔ 1−s, the strip closing on itself like a book.
17 · **the texture dial** — one knob from crystal to static; raindrops at one end, magnets at the other; find where primes and zeros sit.
18 · **gap rain** — prime gaps falling as drops that cluster; zero gaps as drops that refuse to touch.
19 · **the winding counter** — walk a loop, count the turns. the number that survives everything.
20 · **the cross-ratio** — four points, one number, no matter how the view stretches.
21 · **the caliper reborn** — one distance, four rulers, agreeing to the last digit.
22 · **the noise floor** — listen to nothing carefully enough to know what something sounds like.
23 · **the continued-fraction stair** — how close is π to a fraction? climb down and find out.
24 · **the friend finder** — a matched filter as finding one voice in a crowded room.

## new wants — the floats, given toy bodies

25 · **the commutator dial** — two rotations, a knob for how much axis they share, a needle for how much order matters.
26 · **the clock warper** — one orbit under many clocks, side by side. what moves, what stays.
27 · **the ± cradle** — every number on screen wearing its fuzz like a halo.
28 · **the prime player** — the primes themselves in the sample tray, the whole bench reading them.
29 · **the duet** — two specimens on one spine, and a verdict that names the rung: agree, same class, same thing. *(reshaped at walk → **the deviation signature**: similarities, differences, and unseen, each in floor units; "same" not in its vocabulary.)*
30 · **the w-peeker** — a fourth-dimension fuel gauge. how much of you lives in w?
31 · **the fixed-point finder** — turn it, flip it, slide it, scale it: what does this form not notice?
32 · **the floor setter** — thresholds that grow out of the noise instead of out of my head.
33 · **the constellation lattice** — Σnω as stars; spectral peaks landing on them like fireflies.

## wild — heart only, eyes closed

34 · **the loom** — zeros as warp, primes as weft, the cloth weaving itself.
35 · **stereo specimens** — staged in the left ear, zeta in the right; listen for the same song.
36 · **the seam walk** — travel the line where the maps tear; feel the temperature change.
37 · **the chart kaleidoscope** — one form in six coordinate systems at once; turn one, all turn.
38 · **the marble strip** — drop a marble on the critical strip. does it roll to ½? (it shouldn't. does it?)
39 · **the echo well** — drop a number in, hear its factor-lineage come back up.
40 · **the refusal museum** — everything our toys cannot see, each blind spot on display.
41 · **the diophantine ear** — try to hear almost-rational from rational. fail. feel the wall.
42 · **the garden** — forms growing from their formulas, pruned by parameters, seasons of k.
43 · **the body ruler** — the myth grammar as a toy: arrange the figures, feel the meaning move.
44 · **the strobe** — a fast arrow under a tunable shutter; smooth dial, sharp lock. *(born in a walk; deferred until the bench can measure what it makes.)*
