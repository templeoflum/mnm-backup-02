**S3 · The two ½s.** The bench owns the *coordinate-free-ness* of ½ (normalization conserves one unit, so every chart shares one midpoint); only the loaded form decides whether that ½ is a *mirror* (a fixed point of a symmetry). Scaling makes the first; reflection makes the second. — *Earned by:* your pushback and the concession that sharpened it; the-fold (½ as fixed point of s↔1−s); the probe's "mirror?" verdict.

**S5 · The ladder of sameness.** Three rungs, in rising strength: **agreement** (numbers concur across rulers) → **class** (signatures within the measured noise floor) → **identity** (same spectrum). The top rung is modularity's gift: the strongest cross-system statement is two families being *one*, certified by a shared spectral invariant — an identity, not an approximation. — *Earned by:* caliper (agreement); signature + S2 (class); the FLT detour, with the false rhyme ("TS builds modular forms") caught and refused first.

**S6 · L-functions are natively epicyclic.** Read through its zeros, ζ decomposes into TS's own primitive — each term a scaling ∘ rotation — and the dictionary runs both ways (primes reconstruct zeros, zeros reconstruct primes). The clock is log x, not t. — *Earned by:* explicit-formula (staircase = trend − Σ waves), zeros-from-primes, prime-orchestra; amplitude verified as x^β/|ρ|.

**S7 · RH is one shared breathing rate.** What ½ pins is each wave's *growth exponent* — every thread swells in the same proportion as it crosses; their loudnesses differ (1/|ρ|) always. The invariant is the rate, never the height. — *Earned by:* the structure of S6; proven load-bearing when it caught a false thread in our own myth's keystone.

**S8 · Fold, don't listen.** No finite listening can split near-rational from rational (Diophantine); centers are found by symmetry, not spectra. The continued fraction is the right tool because it measures *how close*, not *whether*. — *Earned by:* two honest failures (autocorrelation, then the spectral-lattice test) plus the-fold's success.

**S9 · Refusals are load-bearing.** Every real advance in this work was a registered boundary — the failed tests kept in the ledger, the modular pun declined, the bench denied the mirror. **S11 · Flatness means no view separates it.** The β-extent invariant reads identically across every embedding tried — your reframe, validated: "on the line" is better said as "not canonically separable from it in any chart." — *Earned by:* the headless embedding sweep; rh-flatness, rh-flatness-3d.

**S12 · The seam exists and is measured.** Radial/scaling content maps cleanly between ζ and the staged form; angular/rotational content is obstructed by commutativity structure — disjoint planes commute exactly, overlapping ones don't, ζ's product is abelian (log∏=Σlog), and staged's schedule deliberately *mixes* both kinds. **S13 · The body is a ruler.** The mythic grammar is the device's most extreme coordinate system — lossy in names, lossless in relations — and it is *falsifiable*: the keystone correction proved a myth can be measured wrong and fixed. — *Earned by:* the-crease, written, then corrected by S7.

**S14 · Invariants trade robustness for information.** Winding survives almost anything and says little; cross-ratio says more and breaks sooner.
