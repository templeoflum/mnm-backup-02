## the fold law
everything begins as `name.f0.YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS` (UTC). f0 is immutable ground. the latest layer is alive and accumulates annotations as they come. **a fold happens only when accumulation stops** — never merely because annotations exist; eager folding breeds folds. at a fold: old layer becomes pre-seed; pull forward only what should remain. **never go back.** mistakes are kept as islands — the record of the mistake is the most valuable knowledge there is, and a chain that keeps everything lets the work flow safely around a living error.
