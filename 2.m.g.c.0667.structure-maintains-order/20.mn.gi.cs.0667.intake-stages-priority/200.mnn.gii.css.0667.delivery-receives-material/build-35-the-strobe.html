a fast arrow under a tunable shutter. smooth dial, sharp lock — and an honest meter on the lock.
the refusal: the strobe never says "locked." it says drift ≤ (floor) turns per sample, for this many samples — and the certificate tightens as you watch without ever reaching zero.
