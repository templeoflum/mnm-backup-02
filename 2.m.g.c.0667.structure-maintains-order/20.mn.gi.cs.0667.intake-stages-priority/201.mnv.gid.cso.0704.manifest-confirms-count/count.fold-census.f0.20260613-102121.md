## Three fold-behaviors fall out — and they map to the dream's families

| behavior | types | pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **accumulators** (fold by time/cleanup, go deep) | **schedule (f9), log (f9)** | one long-lived lineage that grows; fold on cleanup/format-change, not per-edit |
| **iterators** (fold as reworked, medium) | **trajectoscope (f6), dream (f4), frame (f4), map (f3), myth (f3), sketch (f2), compass (f2), lattice (f2)** | genuinely evolve — the growth/build kinds |
| **snapshots** (rarely fold, f0/f1) | **count, audit, survey, list, manifest, extract, edge, note, marginalia, essay, template** | written fresh each time; a new census ≠ a fold of the old one |

This is the **growth-vs-record split from the dream, now visible in the fold axis:** the *growth/plan kinds* (dream, frame, sketch, schedule) iterate deep; the *record-keepers* (count, audit, survey, manifest, edge) snapshot and stay shallow. Plus a distinct **accumulator** class (schedule, log) that the fold-discipline note already names. So "type family" and "fold behavior" are two reads of the same structure.

## The collision finding — empirical proof for fold-as-identity

**22 of 137 lineages (16%) have two or more folds sharing one timestamp** — the timestamp *cannot* tell their versions apart; only the fold number can.

- **Concentrated in sketch: 18 of 29 sketch lineages collide.** The `sketch.ts-*` family all carry `20260607-150000`; the `sketch.spline-*` family all carry `20260607-160000`. They were **batch-created** (many sketches dumped in one sitting, one stamp) then folded — so the stamp was never a differentiator to begin with.
- Others: `frame.ts-rebuild` (5 files, one stamp), `float.ts-sc-toys` (4, one stamp), `myth.the-two-flatlanders` (4, one stamp), `frame.warehouse`, etc.

**This is the version-collision the dream is chartered to solve, and the data says it's already real, not hypothetical.** In 22 lineages the timestamp has *already failed* as an identity key. The dream's candidate fix — **fold number = identity (monotonic per lineage), timestamp = provenance metadata** — isn't a precaution; it's a repair for a condition that already exists on the floor. (The /foldrate instrument exposes these as absurd folds/day, e.g. 96/day — its honest tell for same-stamp hand-folds.)
