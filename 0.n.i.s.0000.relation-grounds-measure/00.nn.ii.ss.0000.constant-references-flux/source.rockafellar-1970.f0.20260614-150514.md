**citation** — R. T. Rockafellar, *Convex Analysis* (Princeton University Press, Princeton Mathematical Series, vol. 28, 1970; reissued in Princeton Landmarks in Mathematics, 1997).

**role** — the standard codifying reference for the convex slice of numerical optimization: convex sets and functions, the Legendre–Fenchel conjugate, subdifferentials, Lagrange multipliers, minimax theorems, and duality correspondences (Part III). Door/codification for [[object.convexity-and-lagrange-duality]], alongside the modern teaching door Boyd & Vandenberghe (2004).

**access** — in print (Princeton Landmarks paperback, 1997).
