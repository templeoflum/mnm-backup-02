Frobenius, "Über lineare Substitutionen und bilineare Formen," *J. reine angew. Math.* (Crelle) 84 (1878), 1–63.

Role — first proof of the Cayley–Hamilton theorem in full generality (arbitrary n×n matrices), completing the result that Hamilton (1853, quaternion case) and Cayley (1858, stated and verified 2×2/3×3) had left without a general proof.

Access — historical, German.
