Emmett J. Leahy, US National Archives (joined July 1935), with Philip C. Brooks — credited with conceiving and building out the records life-cycle concept from the late 1930s into the 1940s: records pass from active creation/use, through semi-active storage, to disposition (destruction or permanent archival preservation).

Role — origin/attribution figure for the records life-cycle model. No single canonical 1940s treatise carries the whole concept; it accreted through National Archives records-management practice and Leahy's program. The Emmett Leahy Award (est. 1967) memorializes his role in the life-cycle approach.

Access — secondary literature only (Wikipedia "Records life-cycle," "Emmett Leahy"; JSTAGE Records Management Society of Japan profile). Carried as attribution, not an opened primary source.
