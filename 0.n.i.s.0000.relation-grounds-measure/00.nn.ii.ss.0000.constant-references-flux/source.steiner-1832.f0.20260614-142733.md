Full citation — Jakob Steiner, *Systematische Entwicklung der Abhängigkeit geometrischer Gestalten von einander* (Berlin: G. Fincke, 1832).

Role — one of the three near-simultaneous independent proofs of the projective invariance of the cross-ratio (Möbius 1827, Steiner 1832, Chasles 1837); a foundational text of the synthetic (coordinate-free) school of projective geometry, developing conics and projective ranges/pencils via cross-ratio.

Access — public domain; digitized (e.g. GDZ / archive.org).
