Henry Gordon Rice, "Classes of Recursively Enumerable Sets and Their Decision Problems," *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* 74(2) (1953), 358–366.

Role: origin of Rice's theorem — every non-trivial property of the partial recursive functions (every non-trivial extensional index set) is undecidable. The result first appeared in Rice's 1951 doctoral dissertation at Syracuse University, then in this published paper.

Access — published in *Trans. AMS* (open AMS journal archive). **[confirmed]**
