**citation** — Martin Gardner, "Mathematical Games — The fantastic combinations of John Conway's new solitaire game 'life'," *Scientific American* 223(4), October 1970, pp. 120–123.

**role** — first public appearance of Conway's [[object.cellular-automaton-emergence|Game of Life]]; the column that carried the rules to a mass audience and reportedly drew the largest reader response of any "Mathematical Games" column.

**access** — facsimiles circulate online (ibiblio lifepatterns, Stanford course library); paywalled at the Scientific American archive.

**confidence** — **[confirmed]** (October 1970 issue and column title confirmed by LifeWiki and Wikipedia).
