Kazimierz Kuratowski, "Sur le problème des courbes gauches en topologie," *Fundamenta Mathematicae* **15** (1930), pp. 271–283.

Role — the forbidden-subgraph characterization of planarity (Kuratowski's theorem).

Lineage note — independently obtained but unpublished by Orrin Frink and Paul A. Smith (1930); the cubic case independently by Karl Menger (1930); in the Soviet literature the result is the "Pontryagin–Kuratowski theorem," Pontryagin reportedly having proved it ca. 1927 but never publishing. Published priority is Kuratowski's.
