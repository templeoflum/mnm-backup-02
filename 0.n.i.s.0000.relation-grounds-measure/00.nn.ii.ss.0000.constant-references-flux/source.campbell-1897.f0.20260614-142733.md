J. E. Campbell, papers on the composition of finite transformations of a continuous group, *Proc. London Math. Soc.* (1897–1898).

Role — first to note in print the existence of an element Z with exp(X)exp(Y) = exp(Z) for the composition of finite transformations of a continuous transformation group. Supplies the foundational "Campbell lemma." First of the BCH lineage.
