**citation** — Association for Computing Machinery, "Artifact Review and Badging — Version 1.1," ACM, updated 24 August 2020 (superseding the 2016 / Version 1.0 terminology). acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current.

**role** — documents the contested-terminology turning point. Version 1.0 (2016) defined repeatability/replicability/reproducibility on a same-team / different-team-same-setup / different-team-different-setup axis — placing "reproducibility" opposite to Claerbout. Version 1.1 (24 Aug 2020), advised by NISO, **swapped** the definitions of "reproducible" and "replicable" to align with the National Academies and Claerbout. Primary evidence that the terms genuinely flipped within one standards body.
