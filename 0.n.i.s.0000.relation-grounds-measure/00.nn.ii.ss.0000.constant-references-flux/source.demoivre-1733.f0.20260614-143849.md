Full citation — A. de Moivre, *Approximatio ad Summam Terminorum Binomii (a + b)ⁿ in Seriem expansi* (privately printed Latin pamphlet, 12 November 1733); English version incorporated into *The Doctrine of Chances*, 2nd ed. (London, 1738).

Role — first version of the **central limit theorem**: de Moivre approximated the symmetric binomial distribution (fair coin, p = 1/2) by the normal curve for large n, the earliest instance of the de Moivre–Laplace theorem. Laplace later (1812) extended it to general p and toward the general CLT.

Access — historical; reproduced in editions of *The Doctrine of Chances* and in histories of probability. Attribution and 1733 date corroborated by MacTutor, Wikipedia ("Central limit theorem", "Abraham de Moivre"), and Stigler's history. **[confirmed]**

Note — distinct from [[source.demoivre-1730]] (*Miscellanea Analytica*, the n! asymptotic that is a precursor to Stirling's formula); the CLT precursor is the 1733 *Approximatio*.
