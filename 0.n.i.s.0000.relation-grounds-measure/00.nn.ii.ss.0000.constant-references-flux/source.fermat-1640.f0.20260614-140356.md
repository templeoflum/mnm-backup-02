Full citation — P. de Fermat, letter to Bernard Frénicle de Bessy, 18 October 1640 (in *Œuvres de Fermat*, ed. Tannery & Henry, vol. II).

Role — origin: the first statement of [[object.fermat-little-theorem]]. Fermat asserts the result holds "for all prime numbers" but withholds the proof: "I would send you a demonstration of it, if I did not fear going on for too long." No proof was ever published by him.

Access — reproduced in standard Fermat correspondence editions and quoted across MacTutor / Wikipedia.
