Full citation — P. Lévy, "Sur les lois de probabilité dont dépendent les quotients complets et incomplets d'une fraction continue," *Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France* 57 (1929), 178–194.

Role — independent improvement on Kuzmin's 1928 result for [[object.gauss-kuzmin-theorem]]: Lévy obtained an error term decaying geometrically, O(q^n) with q = 3.5 − 2√2 ≈ 0.6716, sharper than Kuzmin's O(q^√n). The optimal rate constant was later identified by Wirsing (1974) as the Gauss–Kuzmin–Wirsing constant λ ≈ 0.3036.
