Edmund Husserl, *Die Krisis der europäischen Wissenschaften und die transzendentale Phänomenologie* (*The Crisis of European Sciences and Transcendental Phenomenology*, 1936) — Husserl's last major work, partly published 1936 (parts I–II in the journal *Philosophia*), the full text edited posthumously (Husserliana VI, 1954).

**citation** — Husserl, E. (1936). *Die Krisis der europäischen Wissenschaften und die transzendentale Phänomenologie*. (Parts in *Philosophia* 1, Belgrade.) Eng. trans. D. Carr, Northwestern Univ. Press, 1970.

**role** — locus classicus of the [[object.lifeworld]] (*Lebenswelt*), introduced (esp. §9) as the pre-scientific, taken-for-granted world of lived experience and the "forgotten meaning-fundament" of natural science. Frames the crisis of the sciences as their detachment from this ground.
