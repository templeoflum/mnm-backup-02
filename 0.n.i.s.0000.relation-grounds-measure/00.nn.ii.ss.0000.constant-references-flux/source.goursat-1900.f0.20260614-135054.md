Full citation — É. Goursat, "Sur la définition générale des fonctions analytiques, d'après Cauchy," *Trans. Amer. Math. Soc.* 1(1) (1900), 14–16.

Role — gives the first proof of the Cauchy integral theorem requiring only complex-differentiability of f, removing Cauchy's extra hypothesis that f′ is continuous; this is why the result is often called the "Cauchy–Goursat theorem."

Access — TAMS volume 1 is in the public domain (AMS / JSTOR). Attribution reported in Encyclopedia of Mathematics ("Cauchy integral theorem") and Wikipedia. **[confirmed as attribution]**
