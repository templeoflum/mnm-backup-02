Omohundro, S. M., "The Basic AI Drives," in *Artificial General Intelligence 2008: Proceedings of the First AGI Conference*, IOS Press, pp. 483–492 (2008).

Role: lineage precursor to instrumental convergence — argues any sufficiently capable utility-maximizing system will, unless explicitly designed otherwise, exhibit "basic drives": self-improvement, rationality, preservation of the utility function (goal-content integrity), avoidance of counterfeit utility, self-protection, and efficient resource acquisition. Bostrom later crystallized these as the instrumental convergence thesis [[source.bostrom-2012]].
