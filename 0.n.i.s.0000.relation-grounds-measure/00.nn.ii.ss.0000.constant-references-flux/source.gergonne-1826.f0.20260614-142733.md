Full citation — J. D. Gergonne, papers in the *Annales de mathématiques pures et appliquées* ("Annales de Gergonne"), c. 1825–1826, in which he explicitly stated the principle of duality and introduced the terms *dualité* (duality) and *polaire* (polar). Gergonne presented dual propositions in side-by-side columns.

Role — first explicit, general statement of the principle of plane duality (point ↔ line) as a free-standing logical principle, independent of the pole–polar mechanism. Contested priority with Poncelet, who derived duality from poles and polars in his *Traité* (1822) and held it to be a consequence of that theory; the two were rivals over the priority claim.

Access — Annales de Gergonne digitized (public domain; e.g. NUMDAM archive).
