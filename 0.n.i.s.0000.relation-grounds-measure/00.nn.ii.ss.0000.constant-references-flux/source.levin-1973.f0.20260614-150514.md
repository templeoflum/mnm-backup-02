Leonid A. Levin, "Универсальные задачи перебора" ("Universal Search Problems"), *Problemy Peredachi Informatsii* (*Problems of Information Transmission*) 9(3) (1973), pp. 115–116. (in Russian)

**citation** — independently introduces the notion that certain search problems are universal (NP-complete in modern terms), identifying six such problems. The Soviet half of the Cook–Levin theorem; the work circulated earlier (c. 1971–72) before publication.

**role** — co-origin / attribution for [[object.np-completeness-cook-levin-theorem]] and [[object.p-vs-np]].

**access** — Russian original in PPI; English translations by B. A. Trakhtenbrot (1984 survey, *Annals of the History of Computing*) and others.
