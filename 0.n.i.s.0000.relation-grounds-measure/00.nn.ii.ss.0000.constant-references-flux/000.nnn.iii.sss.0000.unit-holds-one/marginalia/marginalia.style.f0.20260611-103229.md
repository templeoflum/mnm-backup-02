# marginalia — the style
**f0 · companion to the frame and the world. the voice speaks, the world is where, this is what the hand draws. a living specimen accompanies this doc — the style demonstrated, not just declared.**

## the inheritance, and the trap beside it
the instrument this style remembers is the oscilloscope: a dark ground, a single luminous trace, drawn by the signal itself. it is the world doc made hardware — the lamp-hour dark, light that must be tended or it fades (phosphor persistence), nothing moving except as the law moves it. the trap beside the inheritance: "near-black with one neon-green accent" is a costume a thousand templated pages wear. the difference is earned in the specifics no template has — **memory of the medium, never a mask of it.** no scanline overlays, no fake screen curvature, no vhs grain, no chromatic-aberration kitsch. what is kept from the crt is *behavior*, not decals: the glow, the persistence, the patience.

## ground & inks
- **ground** `#060907` — the screen at rest; phosphor dark, slightly green-black, never pure #000.
- **ink** `#D6E0DA` — chalk-pale, for the text's voice.
- **dim** `#5D6A63` — graphite-green, the margin's voice; glosses live here.
- **rule** `#1A221D` — hairlines, and the graticule.

## the phosphors (one per piece)
named for real CRT phosphor designations only — honesty extends to naming:
- **P1** `#5BF2A9` — green; the default trace.
- **P3** `#F2B35C` — amber; the warm pieces.
- **P11** `#79C8F0` — blue; the cold pieces.
- **P4** `#E6F0EA` — white; the monochrome pieces.
- **rubric** `#D96459` — *not a phosphor*: red ink, the manuscript rubricator's color, reserved exclusively for corrections and errata pieces. used rarely, it means something.

**the rule:** one phosphor per piece — color is the manicule; where the color is, look. two phosphors only when the mathematics itself has two voices (primes against zeros). distinguishability across color-vision types kept by lineage (the mint/amber/blue set).

## type — the two voices
the body/margin split of the manuscript page, set in faces with real lineage:
- **the text's voice — STIX Two Text** (serif). built by the scientific publishers' consortium for setting mathematics; spiritual ancestor Knuth's Computer Modern, the face mathematics is written in. used for the mathematics' own words: theorem statements, the quoted text. it keeps its own conventions — we do not impose lowercase on the text.
- **the margin's voice — IBM Plex Mono** (mono, true italics). the terminal's honest hand — the second reader's actual medium — humane, unhurried. all glosses, captions, labels. lowercase always. *italic for the nb: teaching lines*, as annotators italicized.
- scale: small and few. two or three sizes per piece. generous leading. restraint is the type system.

## composition
- **mostly margin**: the figure occupies a minority of the frame; emptiness ≥ half, doing the work.
- **the crop is the refusal**: the figure exceeds at least one edge in every piece — the pattern visibly continues past where the drawing stopped. every edge an honest one.
- **the graticule**, when present, at ≤6% opacity — the scope's grid and the manuscript's ruling are one device: faint lines that hold the work without speaking.
- on-frame text: at most a manicule (☞) and six words, in dim. everything else lives in the caption.
- formats: stills 4:5 (1080×1350); loops 1:1 or 9:16, 6–20s, seam invisible.

## motion
tending-pace. things settle, drift, return — nothing snaps, bounces, or arrives with a flourish. one slow change per piece; no gesture under ~4 seconds. **persistence as meaning**: the trace's fading trail is the recent past, kept only while tended — the phosphor decays, the record breathes. loops close so quietly the seam cannot be found. reduced-motion always respected: the still, closed figure stands in.

## the physics
nothing moves except as the law moves it. no particles for flair, no glow beyond the phosphor's own bloom, no easing applied to data. if a thousand points drift, the equation drifts them. the viewer should feel, untold, that nothing in the frame is decoration.

## the caption grammar (the margin, off-frame)
```
☞ what to look at — one breath
nb: the teaching — one or two, lowercase, italic in spirit
kept: x · let go: y        (transformation pieces)
the refusal — one line, plain
```
tags few and literal. no hashtag walls. no emoji. the avatar, if ever needed: a single ☞ in P1 on the ground — nothing else.

## the no-list
scanlines, screen-curve masks, grain filters, vignettes, lens flares, drop shadows, gradients-as-mood, countdown hooks, "wait for it," all caps, exclamation, watermark branding, awe on command.

## the style's own refusal
this document declares the hand; it cannot supply the eye. pieces that follow every rule here and carry no keeping behind them will read as the costume this style exists to refuse. the spec is the floor, not the work.
