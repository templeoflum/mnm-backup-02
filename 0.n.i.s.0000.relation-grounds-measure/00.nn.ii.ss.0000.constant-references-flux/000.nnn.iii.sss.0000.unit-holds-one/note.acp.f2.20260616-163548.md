## TO SETTLE (6) — padding / category-mixing, with recommended resolution

1. **triple_goddess (9 → 3)** — **padded.** Canon is **Maiden · Mother · Crone**. The other 6 ("Enchantress/Amazon" as Maiden aspects, "Queen/Lover" as Mother aspects, "Sorceress/Dark Mother" as Crone aspects) are non-canonical neopagan expansion. → **settle: keep the 3; exclude the 6 aspects.**

2. **alchemy/processes (15 → mixed)** — **two categories in one file.** Real: the 4 color stages (nigredo/albedo/citrinitas/rubedo) + 7 operations (calcination, dissolution, separation, conjunction, fermentation, distillation, coagulation). Not processes: prima materia, philosopher's stone, mercurius, sulphur — these are *substances/principles*. → **settle: keep the 11 processes as the system; the 4 substances are a different object-kind (set aside or separate later).**

3. **kwml (8 → inconsistent)** — the 4 archetypes (King/Warrior/Magician/Lover) are right, but shadows are listed for only King (Tyrant/Weakling) and Warrior (Sadist/Masochist), none for Magician/Lover. Moore & Gillette give each a full bipolar shadow. → **settle: the clean spine is the 4; if shadows are wanted, complete all 4 consistently (4 + 8 = 12), don't carry a half-set.**

4. **jungian/core (12 → mixed, defensible)** — all genuinely Jungian, but two categories conflated: **5 structural** (Self, Shadow, Anima, Animus, Persona) + **figural/image archetypes** (Wise Old Man, Great Mother, Hero, Child, Trickster, Kore/Maiden). "Crone" is borderline (Jung used Kore & Great Mother; "Crone" is more neopagan). → **settle: keep, but mark the structural-vs-figural split; flag "Crone" for a closer look.**

5. **propp_roles (8 → 7 canonical)** — Propp's dramatis personae are **7 spheres**; the "Princess (sought-for person)" and "her Father" are canonically **one sphere**, split into two here. → **settle: trivial — note as 7 (princess+father = one), or keep split with a note.**

6. *(watch)* **astrology/planets (10)** — clean as *modern* astrology (includes Uranus/Neptune/Pluto + Sun/Moon as luminaries). If we ever want *classical/traditional* astrology, that's the 7 classical. Not an error — a tradition-version choice. Noted, no action.
