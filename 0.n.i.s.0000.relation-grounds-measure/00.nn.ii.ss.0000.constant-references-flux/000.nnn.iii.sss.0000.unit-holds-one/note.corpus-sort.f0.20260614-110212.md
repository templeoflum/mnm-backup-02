## the axis — by function, not by subject (settled, operator word 20260614)

Ground's three leaves read as a maturation pipeline, not a subject taxonomy. The corpus subjects (grammar, craft, mind/memory, relational architecture…) don't line up with measurement triads, so we sort by what a thing *is in its processing*, not what it's *about*:

- `00 constant·references·flux` — **raw references still in flux.** The loading dock and the raw material. Where a thing enters.
- `01 gauge·calibrates·accuracy` — **the calibration work.** Comparisons, cross-references, corrections — the pipeline that checks material against itself and against reality.
- `02 magnitude·measures·number` — **distilled, measured findings.** The compiled cards, the settled datapoints — what calibration produced.

The axis doubles as the verification pipeline: **raw (00) → cross-checked (01) → measured (02) → earns root 0.**

## marginalia — the base, at `0` not `000` (settled, operator word 20260614)

The marginalia (our voice, our world — section A of the gather list) is **not research-to-be-measured.** It's the ground the cells stand on. It rises to the **root/branch level of ground** — directly at `0.n.i.s.0000.relation-grounds-measure/` — *above* the measurement shelves, not down at the cellular level `000`. The cells are for material that gets measured; the base is the room itself. Marginalia does not pass through the function pipeline.

## the verification gate — what earns root 0

A thing graduates out of `02` to verified ground only when, per item, shared call:
- sources resolve (citations check out),
- it's been checked **against reality, not against what we expected** (221),
- corrections are closed,
- confidence clears the bar.

Root 0 stays empty until things earn it. The cells are the working shelves; the root is the verified library + the base voice.
