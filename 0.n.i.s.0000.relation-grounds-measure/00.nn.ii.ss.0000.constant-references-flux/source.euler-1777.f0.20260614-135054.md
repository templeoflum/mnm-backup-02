Full citation — L. Euler, paper delivered to the Petersburg Academy of Sciences, 1777 (collected in Euler's *Opera Omnia*), showing the Cauchy–Riemann relations hold for the complex derivative of a function.

Role — first appearance of the CR equations as a criterion connected to (complex) analyticity, the step beyond the purely hydrodynamic context of d'Alembert.

Access — historical text; attribution reported in Encyclopedia of Mathematics ("Cauchy–Riemann equations") and the historical review arXiv:2311.16649. (Some sources loosely cite 1797; the criterion paper is 1777.) **[confirmed as attribution]**
