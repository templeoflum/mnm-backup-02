H. Kahn & A. W. Marshall, "Methods of Reducing Sample Size in Monte Carlo Computations," *J. Operations Research Society of America* 1, no. 5 (1953):263–278 — and the closely related Kahn & Harris work in the NBS *Monte Carlo Method* symposium volume (Applied Mathematics Series 12, 1951:27–30), which lays out importance sampling for neutron-transport problems. Precursor: Goertzel (1949).

Role — earliest formal statement of importance sampling (biased sampling distribution + likelihood-ratio weights for variance reduction) in statistical physics, predating its 1978 introduction to statistics (Kloek & van Dijk).

Access — NBS Applied Mathematics Series 12; J. ORSA.

**[candidate, attribution] — importance-sampling origin is spread across Goertzel (1949), Kahn (1949/1950), and Kahn–Harris/Kahn–Marshall (1951–53); exact "first" citation varies by source. Carried as lineage, not pinned to one paper.**
