Full citation — P. de Fermat, *Ad Locos Planos et Solidos Isagoge* ("Introduction to Plane and Solid Loci"), manuscript written/circulated 1636; first printed 1679 in *Varia opera mathematica* (Toulouse).

Role — origin (co-inventor): independent development of analytic / coordinate geometry, predating in manuscript the 1637 publication of Descartes's *La Géométrie*. Fermat worked from a single reference axis and is reported to have treated loci in three dimensions. Because it was unpublished until 1679, Descartes's printed work carried the priority in influence.

Same-name / same-author trap — distinct from [[source.fermat-1640]] (the 18 Oct 1640 letter on Fermat's little theorem, number theory). This is the analytic-geometry treatise.

Access — Latin text in *Varia opera mathematica* (1679) and *Œuvres de Fermat* (Tannery & Henry); summarized by MacTutor and Wikipedia "Analytic geometry" / "Pierre de Fermat."
