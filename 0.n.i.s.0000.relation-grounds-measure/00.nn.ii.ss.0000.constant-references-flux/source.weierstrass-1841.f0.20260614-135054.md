Full citation — K. Weierstrass, manuscript of 1841 on the representation of an analytic function by a power series (with negative powers on an annulus), unpublished until his *Mathematische Werke*, vol. 1 (Berlin, 1894).

Role — independent and earlier (private) discovery of what is now called the Laurent series; not published until 1894, so Laurent retains publication priority and the name [[source.laurent-1843]]. Carried as lineage.

Access — historical text; in Weierstrass's *Mathematische Werke* (1894). Attribution reported in Wikipedia ("Laurent series") and the Weierstrass bicentenary survey arXiv:1508.02928. **[confirmed as attribution]**
