AICPA, AU-C Section 530, *Audit Sampling* (Clarity recodification of AU 350). IAASB counterpart ISA 530; PCAOB counterpart AS 2315.

Role — authoritative source for **audit sampling**: defines sampling as applying procedures to less than 100% of a population so all units have a chance of selection (AU-C 530.04 objective: a reasonable basis to draw conclusions about the population); distinguishes **statistical** sampling (random selection + statistical evaluation including measurement of sampling risk) from **nonstatistical**; underlies the **attribute** (tests of controls) vs **variables** (substantive) split.

Access — AICPA Professional Standards (paid); ISA 530 free in the IAASB Handbook (ifac.org); AS 2315 free at pcaobus.org. Definitions concur across these. **[confirmed]**
