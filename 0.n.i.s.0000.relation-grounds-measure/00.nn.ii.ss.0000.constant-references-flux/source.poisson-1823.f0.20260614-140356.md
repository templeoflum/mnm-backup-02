Full citation — S. D. Poisson, "Suite du mémoire sur les intégrales définies et sur la sommation des séries," *Journal de l'École polytechnique*, cahier 19, tome **XII** (**1823**).

Role — first publication of the **Poisson summation formula**, Σ f(n) = Σ f̂(k), arising in Poisson's treatment of the summation of trigonometric series and the heat equation. The continuous–discrete (periodization ↔ sampling) duality linking [[object.fourier-series]] and [[object.fourier-transform]]; foundational for lattice sums in number theory (later: Voronoi, Tate).

**Distinct** from [[source.poisson-1837]], which records the Poisson *distribution* — a different Poisson contribution. Do not conflate.

Access — historical journal; the 1823 *Journal de l'École polytechnique* attribution confirmed via Wikipedia "Poisson summation formula" and the survey "Summation Formulas, from Poisson and Voronoi to the Present" (arXiv math/0304187).
