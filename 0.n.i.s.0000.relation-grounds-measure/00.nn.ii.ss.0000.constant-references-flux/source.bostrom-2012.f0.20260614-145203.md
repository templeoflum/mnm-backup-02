Bostrom, N., "The Superintelligent Will: Motivation and Instrumental Rationality in Advanced Artificial Agents," *Minds and Machines* 22(2): 71–85 (2012).

Role: the paper where Bostrom first names and states the **orthogonality thesis** and the **instrumental convergence thesis** (convergent instrumental values: self-preservation, goal-content integrity, cognitive enhancement, technological perfection, resource acquisition). Both are restated in *Superintelligence* (2014, ch. 7) [[source.bostrom-2014]].

Access — author copy free at nickbostrom.com/superintelligentwill.pdf.
