Full citation — R. O. Kuzmin (R. Kuz'min), "Sur un problème de Gauss," *Atti del Congresso Internazionale dei Matematici* (Bologna, 1928), tomo VI, 83–89; companion note in *Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR* (1928).

Role — first solution of "Gauss's problem" for the continued-fraction digit distribution, the proof source for [[object.gauss-kuzmin-theorem]]. Kuzmin established that the distribution of the n-th remainder converges to the Gauss measure with an error bound of order exp(−α√n) (i.e. O(q^√n) for some 0 < q < 1), uniformly in x.

Access — congress proceedings; the result and its bound are reported consistently by Encyclopedia of Mathematics, Wolfram MathWorld, and Khinchin's *Continued Fractions* (which reproduces a version of the proof). **[confirmed as attribution]**
