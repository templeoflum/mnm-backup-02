**citation** — E. P. Wigner, "On the Distribution of the Roots of Certain Symmetric Matrices," *Annals of Mathematics* (2nd series) **67**(2):325–327 (1958).

**role** — extends the 1955 result (which treated a special "bordered" class) to general real symmetric matrices of high dimension, establishing the semicircle law as the limiting eigenvalue distribution. The paper after which the [[object.wigner-semicircle-law]] is named (jointly with the 1955 paper [[source.wigner-1955]]).
