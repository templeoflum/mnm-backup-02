Full citation — B. Riemann, work on the asymptotic evaluation of a hypergeometric-type integral (1863), published in his *Gesammelte mathematische Werke* (collected works / Nachlass).

Role — the **precursor of steepest descent** that Debye explicitly cited as the origin of the method's idea. Siegel (1932) later documented further unpublished Riemann notes (Nachlass) using the same technique, leading to the Riemann–Siegel formula.

Lineage caution — exact dating/contents of Riemann's contribution are contested in the historical literature (the method's roots also reach back to Cauchy, and Nekrasov used it before Debye). Carried as a credited precursor, not the codifying statement.

Access — historical; Riemann's *Werke* (Teubner / Dover reprint). **[candidate, partially confirmed]**
