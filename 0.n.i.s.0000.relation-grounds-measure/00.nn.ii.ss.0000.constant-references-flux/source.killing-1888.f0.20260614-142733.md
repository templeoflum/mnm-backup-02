W. Killing, "Die Zusammensetzung der stetigen endlichen Transformationsgruppen," a series of papers in *Mathematische Annalen* (1888–1890).

Role — first classification of the complex finite-dimensional simple Lie algebras; introduced root systems (and what became the Cartan matrix / Cartan subalgebra) in the course of the classification. The proof was incomplete / not fully rigorous; made rigorous by Cartan (1894).
