Full citation — E. Landau, *Handbuch der Lehre von der Verteilung der Primzahlen*, 2 vols., B. G. Teubner, Leipzig, 1909.

Role — Landau adopted Bachmann's O and **introduced the little-o symbol** (o). Through his heavy use the family became known as the **Landau symbols** / Bachmann–Landau notation. The *Handbuch* is also a landmark in analytic number theory and prime-counting asymptotics.

Access — historical; reprints (Chelsea/AMS) and scans available. **[confirmed as attribution]**
