Full citation — P. L. Chebyshev, "Sur les questions de minima qui se rattachent à la représentation approximative des fonctions," *Mémoires de l'Académie impériale des sciences de St.-Pétersbourg* (7) **7** (1859), 199–291.

The systematic development of best uniform approximation: the characterization of the least-deviating polynomial through the alternation (equioscillation) of the error, extended to rational approximation. This is the memoir most often cited for the alternation criterion; the modern rigorous existence/uniqueness framing was supplied by Borel (1905). **[confirmed]**
