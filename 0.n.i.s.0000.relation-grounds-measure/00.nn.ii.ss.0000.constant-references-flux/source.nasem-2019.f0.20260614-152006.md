**citation** — National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, *Reproducibility and Replicability in Science* (Washington, DC: The National Academies Press, 2019). DOI: 10.17226/25303.

**role** — the U.S. consensus-study definitions that much of science now defers to: reproducibility = "obtaining consistent results using the same input data; computational steps, methods, and code; and conditions of analysis"; replicability = "obtaining consistent results across studies aimed at answering the same scientific question, each of which has obtained its own data." Aligns with Claerbout's sense and (after 2020) with ACM.

**access** — free online (nap.nationalacademies.org / nationalacademies.org).
