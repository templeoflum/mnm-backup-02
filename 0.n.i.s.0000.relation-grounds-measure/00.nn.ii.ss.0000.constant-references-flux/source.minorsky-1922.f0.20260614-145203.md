Nicolas Minorsky, "Directional Stability of Automatically Steered Bodies," *Journal of the American Society of Naval Engineers* 34, no. 2 (May 1922): 280–309 (DOI 10.1111/j.1559-3584.1922.tb04958.x) — derives, from observing how a helmsman steers, the first explicit mathematical three-term (proportional + integral + derivative) control law, applied to automatic steering of U.S. Navy ships.

Role — origin of the formal PID / closed-loop control law (Elmer Sperry's earlier autopilots were intuitive, not mathematical).

Access — paywalled at Wiley Online Library; widely cited in control-theory histories. **[confirmed]**
