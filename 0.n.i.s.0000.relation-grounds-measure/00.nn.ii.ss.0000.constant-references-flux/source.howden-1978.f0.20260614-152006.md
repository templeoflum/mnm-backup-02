**citation** — William E. Howden, "Theoretical and Empirical Studies of Program Testing," *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering*, Vol. SE-4, No. 4 (July 1978), pp. 293–298.

**role** — credited with the first use of the term **"test oracle"** in the program-testing literature; pairs a theoretical line (when testing can prove correctness) with an empirical line (how strategies reveal errors in real programs). Anchors the coinage half of the test-oracle keystone.
