Full citation — É. Borel, "Sur quelques points de la théorie des fonctions," *Annales scientifiques de l'É.N.S.* (3) **12** (1895), 9–55 (developed from his 1894 doctoral thesis).

Contains the first explicit statement and proof of the covering ("Heine-Borel") theorem, restricted to **countable** covers: if a countable family of intervals covers a closed bounded interval, a finite subfamily already covers it. Generalization to arbitrary covers was supplied by Cousin (1895), Lebesgue (1898), and Schoenflies (1900).
