**citation** — Frederick P. Brooks Jr., *The Mythical Man-Month: Essays on Software Engineering* (Addison-Wesley, 1975; anniversary edition 1995).

**role** — lineage touchpoint for the regression-testing keystone. Brooks observes (p. 122) that program maintenance requires far more system testing per statement written than any other programming, and that fixing one fault has a substantial chance of introducing another — the cost structure that makes re-running prior tests after a change necessary. Cited as the named early articulation, not the coiner of "regression testing."

**access** — widely in print; cited by Wikipedia "Regression testing" (p. 122).

**confidence** — **[confirmed as attribution]** for the maintenance-cost framing (Wikipedia cites page 122); used as a lineage anchor, not as the origin of the term.
