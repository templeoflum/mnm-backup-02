Full citation — G. N. Watson, "The harmonic functions associated with the parabolic cylinder," *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society* (2) **17** (1918), 116–148.

Role — the source paper for **Watson's lemma** (stated/proved p. 133): given φ(t) = t^λ g(t) with the right growth and smoothness, the Laplace integral ∫₀ᵀ e^{−xt} φ(t) dt has the asymptotic expansion Σ aₙ Γ(λ+n+1) / x^{λ+n+1} as x → ∞.
