Kenneth Appel and Wolfgang Haken, "Every planar map is four colorable. Part I: Discharging," *Illinois Journal of Mathematics* **21** (1977), pp. 429–490; and K. Appel, W. Haken, and J. Koch, "Part II: Reducibility," same volume, pp. 491–567.

Role — the first proof of the four color theorem; the first major theorem proved with essential aid of a computer.

Note — computer-assisted (non-surveyable) proof: reduced to an unavoidable set of reducible configurations (originally ~1,936/1,834, later trimmed) checked by machine over ~1,200 hours. Result announced at the University of Illinois on 21 June 1976; full papers published 1977.
