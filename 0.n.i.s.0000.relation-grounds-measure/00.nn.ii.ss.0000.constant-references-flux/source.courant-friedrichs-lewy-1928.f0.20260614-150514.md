R. Courant, K. Friedrichs, H. Lewy, "Über die partiellen Differenzengleichungen der mathematischen Physik," *Mathematische Annalen* 100(1) (1928):32–74.

Role: the theoretical foundation for solving partial differential equations by finite differences; states the **Courant–Friedrichs–Lewy (CFL) condition** — a necessary condition for convergence/stability of explicit finite-difference schemes for hyperbolic PDEs (the numerical domain of dependence must contain the analytic one).

Access — German original; English translation in *IBM J. Res. Dev.* 11 (1967):215–234. **[confirmed]**
