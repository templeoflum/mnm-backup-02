Full citation — J. Liouville, exposition in his lectures and in the *Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées*, 1847, of the theorem that a bounded entire function is constant.

Role — Liouville's independent presentation/proof; through this influential exposition the theorem acquired his name, despite Cauchy's 1844 priority [[source.cauchy-1844]]. Distinct from the existing record [[source.liouville-1844]], which concerns Liouville numbers / transcendentals in Diophantine approximation — a different result.

Access — historical text; *Journal de Liouville* 1847. Attribution reported in Wikipedia ("Liouville's theorem (complex analysis)"). **[confirmed as attribution]**
