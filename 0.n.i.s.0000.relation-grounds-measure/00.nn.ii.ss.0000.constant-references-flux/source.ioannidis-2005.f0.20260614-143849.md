Ioannidis JPA, "Why Most Published Research Findings Are False," *PLoS Medicine* 2(8) (2005):e124. DOI 10.1371/journal.pmed.0020124.

Argued via a positive-predictive-value model that, given low pre-study odds, small studies, flexible analysis, and publication bias, a large proportion of published findings are likely false. A foundational precursor to the replication-crisis discourse.

Distinct from Simmons-Nelson-Simonsohn 2011 ("False-Positive Psychology") — different paper, author, venue, and argument.

Access — free (open access) at PLoS Medicine.
