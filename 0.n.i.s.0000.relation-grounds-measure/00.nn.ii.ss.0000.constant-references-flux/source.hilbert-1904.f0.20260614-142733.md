Hilbert, "Grundzüge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen" (first communications from 1904).

Role — introduced the German prefix *eigen-* ("own") for the characteristic values and functions of integral operators (Eigenwert, Eigenfunktion), studying operators as infinite matrices. This terminology became dominant, eventually anglicized to eigenvalue / eigenvector, displacing Cauchy's "characteristic root" and Sylvester's "latent root."

Access — historical, German.
