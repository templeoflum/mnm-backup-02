Mises, R. von (1936). "La distribution de la plus grande de n valeurs." *Rev. Math. Union Interbalkanique* 1:141–160. Reprinted in *Selected Papers of Richard von Mises*, Vol. 2, American Mathematical Society (1964), pp. 271–294.

Role — gave the von Mises sufficient conditions (on the hazard rate / tail) for convergence to each of the three extreme-value types, and an early unified parameterization later identified with the GEV form (commonly attributed to Jenkinson 1955).
