D. Ruelle and F. Takens, "On the Nature of Turbulence," *Communications in Mathematical Physics*, vol. 20, no. 3 (1971), pp. 167–192 (with a note, vol. 23 (1971), pp. 343–344).

Introduces the term "strange attractor" and argues turbulence arises after a small number of bifurcations onto such an attractor (the Ruelle–Takens–Newhouse scenario), displacing the older Landau–Hopf picture of infinitely many incommensurate modes.
