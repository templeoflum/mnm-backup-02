E. Kreyszig, *Introductory Functional Analysis with Applications* (Wiley, 1978).

Role — a standard accessible **door** into [[field.functional-analysis]]: metric, normed, Banach and Hilbert spaces, linear operators and functionals, the fundamental theorems, and spectral theory, with worked applications and minimal prerequisites. Pairs with the more advanced canonical texts (Rudin, *Functional Analysis*, 2nd ed. 1991; Brezis, 2011).

Access — in print; widely held. **[confirmed]**
