**origin** — M. Fréchet, "Sur quelques points du calcul fonctionnel" (*Rend. Circ. Mat. Palermo*, 1906) — abstract metric spaces and the term *calcul fonctionnel*; D. Hilbert, "Grundzüge einer allgemeinen Theorie der linearen Integralgleichungen" (from 1904) — integral equations and the spectral theory of operators as infinite matrices. F. Riesz added the representation theorems and Lᵖ-space theory (1907–1910). Carried as a multi-strand origin. [[source.frechet-1906]] [[source.hilbert-1904]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — S. Banach, *Théorie des opérations linéaires* (1932) — the first monograph unifying the strands into the general theory of complete normed spaces (Banach spaces) and linear operators. [[source.banach-1932]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — E. Kreyszig, *Introductory Functional Analysis with Applications* (1978); canonical: W. Rudin, *Functional Analysis* (2nd ed. 1991). [[source.kreyszig-1978]]

**lineage** — three converging streams: the calculus of variations / "functionals" (Volterra, Hadamard), integral equations (Fredholm, Hilbert), and abstract space theory (Fréchet, Riesz), fused by Banach and the Lwów school into a single discipline in the 1930s. Not flattened to one founder.
