Jon L. Bentley, Dorothea Haken, James B. Saxe, "A General Method for Solving Divide-and-Conquer Recurrences," *ACM SIGACT News* 12(3) (Sept. 1980), pp. 36–44.

**citation** — introduces the unifying method for solving recurrences of the form T(n) = aT(n/b) + f(n); the basis of what CLRS later named the "master theorem." (Co-author Dorothea Haken, née Blostein.)

**role** — origin / attribution for [[object.master-theorem]]. The name "master theorem" was popularized by [[source.clrs]].

**access** — ACM Digital Library (DOI 10.1145/1008861.1008865).
