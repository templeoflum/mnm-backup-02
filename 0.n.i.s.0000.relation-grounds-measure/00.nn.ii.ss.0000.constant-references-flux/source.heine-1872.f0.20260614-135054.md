Full citation — E. Heine, "Die Elemente der Functionenlehre," *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (Crelle) **74** (1872), 172–188.

Contains the proof that a function continuous on a closed, bounded interval is **uniformly continuous**, using a finite-subcover argument that is the geometric germ of the Heine-Borel theorem. Heine *used* the covering idea but did not state the compactness theorem as such; the attribution of his name to the theorem (via Schoenflies) is historically contested — Lebesgue called the association "ridiculous."

Access — Crelle volume 74 is public domain (GDZ / Internet Archive). **[confirmed]**
