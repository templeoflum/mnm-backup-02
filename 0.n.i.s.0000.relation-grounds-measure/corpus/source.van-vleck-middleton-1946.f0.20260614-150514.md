J. H. Van Vleck & David Middleton, "A Theoretical Comparison of the Visual, Aural, and Meter Reception of Pulsed Signals in the Presence of Noise," *J. Applied Physics* 17 (1946):940–971.

Role: wartime radar detection analysis credited with introducing the term **"matched filter"** for the optimal signal-detection filter (independent of / contemporaneous with North's 1943 derivation).

Access: cited from secondary literature (Wikipedia "Matched filter"; Middleton biographical sources), not opened. Naming attribution carried as lineage — the "matched filter" coinage is variously credited to Van Vleck & Middleton.
