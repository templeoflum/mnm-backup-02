**citation** — E. P. Wigner, remarks on nearest-neighbor level spacing presented at the Gatlinburg Conference on Neutron Physics (held 1956; published in the conference proceedings / Oak Ridge National Laboratory report ORNL-2309, 1957). Origin of the "Wigner surmise."

**role** — the conjecture that the nearest-neighbor eigenvalue spacing of a random real-symmetric matrix follows p(s) = (πs/2)·exp(−πs²/4); the seed of [[object.eigenvalue-repulsion-level-spacing]].

**access** — proceedings / ORNL report (historical, harder to obtain); the surmise is reproduced in standard texts (Mehta, [[source.mehta-1967]]).
