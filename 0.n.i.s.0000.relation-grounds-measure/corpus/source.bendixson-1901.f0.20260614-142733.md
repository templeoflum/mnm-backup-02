I. Bendixson, "Sur les courbes définies par des équations différentielles," *Acta Mathematica*, vol. 24 (1901), pp. 1–88.

Supplies the complete, rigorous proof of the planar limit-set result that Poincaré had formulated without a full proof in his 1881–1886 *Sur les courbes définies par une équation différentielle* memoirs. Half of the eponym for the Poincaré–Bendixson theorem.

**[confirmed]** — venue, volume, and pages corroborated by Wikipedia and the de Gruyter/Springer survey "The Poincaré–Bendixson Theorem: from Poincaré to the XXIst century."
