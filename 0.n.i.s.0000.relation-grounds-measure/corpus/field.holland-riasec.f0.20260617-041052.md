**origin** — John L. Holland's theory of vocational choice: people and work environments both sort into six types; fit predicts satisfaction and stability. [[source.holland-1997]]

**codification** — operationalized in the Self-Directed Search and the Strong Interest Inventory; the hexagon orders the six by similarity.

**door** — Holland, *Making Vocational Choices* (3rd ed., 1997).

**standing** — well-supported in vocational psychology; the hexagonal structure replicates broadly. Held `empirical`.
