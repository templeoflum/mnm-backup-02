**citation (verbatim)** — Daniel M. Kan, "Adjoint Functors," *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society*, Vol. 87, No. 2 (1958), pp. 294–329. DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-1958-0131451-0.

**what it contains** — the paper that defined and named *adjoint functors*, one of the central organizing notions of category theory, and in the same work introduced *Kan extensions*. Kan showed that a great many constructions across mathematics (free/forgetful pairs, tensor–hom, limits as adjoints, etc.) are instances of one relation: a pair of functors F ⊣ G with a natural bijection Hom(F A, B) ≅ Hom(A, G B).
