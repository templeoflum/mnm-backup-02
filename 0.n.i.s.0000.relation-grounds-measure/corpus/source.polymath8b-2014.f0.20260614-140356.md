Full citation — D. H. J. Polymath, "Variants of the Selberg sieve, and bounded intervals containing many primes," *Research in the Mathematical Sciences* 1 (2014), Article 12. arXiv:1407.4897 (July 2014). This is the Polymath8b stage of the collaborative project.

Contains the record unconditional bound liminf_{n→∞} (p_{n+1} − p_n) ≤ 246, by generalizing Maynard's multidimensional Selberg sieve and extensive numerical optimization; and ≤ 6 under the generalized Elliott–Halberstam conjecture. (The earlier Polymath8a stage optimized Zhang's argument down to ~4680.)
