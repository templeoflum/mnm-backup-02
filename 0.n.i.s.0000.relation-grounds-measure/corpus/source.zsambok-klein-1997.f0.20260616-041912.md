# zsambok-klein-1997

**Citation (verbatim):** Zsambok, C. E., & Klein, G. (Eds.). (1997). *Naturalistic Decision Making*. Mahwah, NJ: Lawrence Erlbaum Associates. (Expertise: Research and Applications series.) ISBN 0-8058-1874-X.

**Contains:** An edited volume growing out of the second NDM conference (Dayton, Ohio, June 1994) that consolidated the field under its name. Contributions survey NDM's core models — including the recognition-primed decision model — and its applications in management, aviation, health care, nuclear power, military command and control, corporate teamwork, and manufacturing. The volume frames NDM as the study of how people use experience to decide amid high-stakes consequences, shifting goals, incomplete information, time pressure, and uncertainty, and serves as the field's codifying reference for its early models and scope.

**Access:** Published by Lawrence Erlbaum Associates (later Psychology Press/Routledge); reissued in the Routledge catalog. ISBN 9780805818741.
