Seki Takakazu, *Katsuyō Sanpō* (括要算法), published posthumously 1712.

Role — independent discovery of the [[object.bernoulli-numbers]] in the course of computing sums of powers, published a year before Jacob Bernoulli's *Ars Conjectandi* (1713). Seki did **not** frame the result as a single uniform formula keyed to one sequence of constants — that formulation is Bernoulli's — but the underlying numbers appear in his power-sum work, giving him chronological priority of publication. Carried as a co-origin lineage, not a sole attribution.

Access — historical, Japanese (wasan tradition). Attribution corroborated by Wikipedia "Bernoulli number" and the Luschny "Bernoulli Manifesto." **[confirmed as attribution]**
