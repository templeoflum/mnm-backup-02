Anil K. Seth, "Interoceptive inference, emotion, and the embodied self," *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* **17**(11): 565–573 (2013). DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.007.

Contains — the **interoceptive inference** proposal: subjective feeling states (emotions) arise from actively-inferred predictive (generative) models of the causes of interoceptive afferent signals, generalizing appraisal theories of emotion. Extends predictive coding from exteroception to the visceral domain and links interoceptive prediction error to body ownership, conscious selfhood, and presence — with implications for neuropsychiatric conditions (anxiety, depersonalization).

Role — codification of the computational/predictive strand alongside Barrett & Simmons (2015); the founding statement of "interoceptive inference."

Access — open access at Cell Press (Trends in Cognitive Sciences); widely available. **[confirmed]**
