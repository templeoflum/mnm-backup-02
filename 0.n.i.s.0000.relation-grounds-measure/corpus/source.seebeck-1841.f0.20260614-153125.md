August Seebeck, siren-disk experiments on the pitch of periodic acoustic pulses, *Annalen der Physik* (1841 and following, early 1840s).

Role — first systematic documentation of the missing-fundamental phenomenon: rapid airflow interruptions produce a tone at the interruption (repetition) frequency even when little or no energy sits at that frequency; touched off the Seebeck–Ohm controversy over whether the ear is a Fourier analyzer.

Access — 19th-c. German journal; not opened directly.
