F. Hausdorff, "Die symbolische Exponentialformel in der Gruppentheorie," *Ber. Verh. Sächs. Akad. Wiss. Leipzig* (1906).

Role — proved that Z = log(exp(X)exp(Y)) can be expressed purely in terms of iterated commutators (Lie brackets) of X and Y, and connected the result to the Jacobi identity / convergence. Third of the BCH lineage (the "H").
