Tang, Y.-Y., Hölzel, B. K., & Posner, M. I. (2015). The neuroscience of mindfulness meditation. *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 16(4), 213–225. doi:10.1038/nrn3916

Contains — standard review of the neuroscience of mindfulness meditation: organizes effects under attention control, emotion regulation, and self-awareness; surveys associated brain regions and networks (anterior cingulate, prefrontal cortex, insula, default-mode network). Notes that supportive evidence exists but that underlying neural mechanisms remain unclear and more methodologically rigorous studies are needed.

Access — Nature Reviews Neuroscience; an erratum was issued. A door/survey reference for the field. **[confirmed]**
