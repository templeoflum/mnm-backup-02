Ambady, N., & Rosenthal, R., "Thin slices of expressive behavior as predictors of interpersonal consequences: A meta-analysis," *Psychological Bulletin* 111(2) (1992):256–274.

Coins and defines the "thin slice" — a brief sample of expressive behavior, not exceeding five minutes, drawn from the behavior stream. Meta-analysis of 38 results; overall effect size r ≈ .39 for accuracy of predictions of objective outcomes from short observations. Slices under ~30 s predicted as well as 4–5 min observations; longer observation did not improve accuracy. Foundational codification of the thin-slicing literature.
