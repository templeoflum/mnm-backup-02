**origin** — the enneagon figure from G. I. Gurdjieff's Fourth Way; the *enneagram of personality* (nine character types mapped to "passions"/fixations) originated by Oscar Ichazo at the Arica School (~1968–1970). [[source.riso-hudson-1999]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — Claudio Naranjo carried the system to the United States (SAT groups, 1970s) and tied the types to psychological character; Don Riso & Russ Hudson systematized the modern type names, descriptions, and the basic-fear / basic-desire scheme. [[source.riso-hudson-1999]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**door** — Riso & Hudson, *The Wisdom of the Enneagram* (1999).

**lineage** — esoteric origin (Gurdjieff → Ichazo → Naranjo) vs. later popular use as a personality typology; carried as lineage. Held as a *tradition's typology* (members carry `status: doctrinal`), not an empirically validated instrument.
