# klein-1998

**Citation (verbatim):** Klein, G. A. (1998). *Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press. ISBN 0-262-11227-2 (hardcover) / 0-262-61146-5 (paperback, 1999).

**Contains:** The book lays out the recognition-primed decision (RPD) model and the broader naturalistic decision-making framework. It draws on observations of professionals acting under time pressure, high stakes, personal responsibility, and shifting conditions — firefighters, critical-care nurses, pilots, nuclear-power-plant operators, military battle planners, and chess masters. It argues that experienced decision makers typically size up a situation, recognize a workable course of action, and mentally simulate it rather than generate and compare a formal option set. Chapters also treat mental simulation, the power of stories and analogues, and team decision making.

**Access:** Widely held; available via MIT Press (paperback ISBN 9780262611466) and the Internet Archive lending library. A 20th-anniversary edition (2017, ISBN 9780262534291) exists.
