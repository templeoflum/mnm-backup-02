Whitney, Hassler. "Differentiable Manifolds." *Annals of Mathematics*, 2nd ser., 37, no. 3 (1936): 645–680.

Role — codification: gives the modern abstract definition of a differentiable manifold (atlas of charts with smooth transition maps) a firm footing by proving the embedding theorem — every smooth n-manifold (compact, with mild hypotheses) embeds in ℝ^(2n+1) — showing the abstract manifolds of Veblen–Whitehead can always be realized as subsets of Euclidean space.
