Karl E. Weick and Kathleen M. Sutcliffe, *Managing the Unexpected: Assuring High Performance in an Age of Complexity* (Jossey-Bass, San Francisco, 2001) — the work that synthesized two decades of Berkeley-school field research on **high reliability organizations (HROs)** into a portable management framework built on **collective mindfulness**.

Contains — the five principles of mindful organizing: (1) **preoccupation with failure**, (2) **reluctance to simplify interpretations**, (3) **sensitivity to operations**, (4) **commitment to resilience**, and (5) **deference to expertise** (migrating decisions to those with the most knowledge, regardless of rank). Worked examples are the canonical HRO sites — aircraft-carrier flight decks, nuclear power operations, wildland firefighting, hospital emergency departments, air traffic control.

Access — first edition 2001 (ISBN 978-0-7879-5627-1, Jossey-Bass); revised as *Managing the Unexpected: Resilient Performance in an Age of Uncertainty* (2nd ed., 2007) and *…Sustained Performance in a Complex World* (3rd ed., Wiley, 2015).
