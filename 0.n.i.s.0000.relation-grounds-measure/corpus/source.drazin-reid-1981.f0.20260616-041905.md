Full citation — P. G. Drazin and W. H. Reid, *Hydrodynamic Stability*, Cambridge Monographs on Mechanics, Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, 1981 (xi + 525 pp.; ISBN 0-521-22798-4). Paperback 1982; 2nd edition 2004 (Cambridge Mathematical Library).

Contains — the standard graduate-level treatment of linear and weakly nonlinear hydrodynamic stability: thermal convection (Rayleigh–Bénard), rotating and curved flows (Taylor–Couette), and parallel shear flows, with full development of the Orr–Sommerfeld equation, Rayleigh's and Fjørtoft's inviscid criteria, Squire's theorem, and the mathematical theory of the eigenvalue problem. The canonical "door" into the field.

Access — in print (Cambridge); widely held. Bibliographic detail confirmed against Cambridge Core and the *J. Fluid Mech.* review (1982). **[confirmed]**
