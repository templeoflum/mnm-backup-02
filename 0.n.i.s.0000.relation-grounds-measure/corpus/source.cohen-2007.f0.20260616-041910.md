Jonathan D. Cohen, Samuel M. McClure and Angela J. Yu, "Should I stay or should I go? How the human brain manages the trade-off between exploitation and exploration," *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B: Biological Sciences*, vol. 362, no. 1481 (2007), pp. 933–942.

**citation** — reviews the exploration–exploitation trade-off as a general problem of adaptive control, surveys candidate neural mechanisms (notably norepinephrine/locus coeruleus and prefrontal–anterior cingulate circuitry) that may mediate the switch between exploiting a known option and exploring alternatives, and frames the trade-off as a unifying account across decision domains.
