J. J. O'Connor and E. F. Robertson, "Babylonian numerals," MacTutor History of Mathematics Archive, University of St Andrews.

Role — authoritative secondary source for the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) positional system: the first place-value system (inherited base 60 from Sumerians/Akkadians, but the positional advance was the Babylonians' own, c. 2000 BCE), and its initial lack of a zero placeholder (an empty-position symbol came in later Babylonian times).
