Verbatim citation: Goldratt, Eliyahu M., and Robert E. Fox. *The Race*. Croton-on-Hudson, NY: North River Press, 1986. ISBN 0-88427-062-9.

Contains: a concise, heavily illustrated book (built from transparency-style figures with brief text) that formally presents and names the drum-buffer-rope (DBR) scheduling system underlying the constraint-based logistics first dramatized in *The Goal*. The "drum" is the constraint's pace, the "buffer" the time protection placed before it, and the "rope" the release signal tying material flow to the constraint's tempo. [confirmed]
