H. F. Baker, "Alternants and continuous groups," *Proc. London Math. Soc.* (1905).

Role — gave (independently) a complete symbolic solution to the composition problem exp(X)exp(Y) = exp(Z). Second of the BCH lineage (the "B" of BCH). Some accounts date Baker's contributions to 1901–1905.
