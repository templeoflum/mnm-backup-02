**citation (verbatim)** — Emily Riehl, *Category Theory in Context*, Aurora: Dover Modern Math Originals, Dover Publications, 2016. ISBN 978-0-486-80903-8.

**what it contains** — a modern graduate introduction derived from courses at Harvard and Johns Hopkins: categories, functors, natural transformations, the Yoneda lemma, limits and colimits, adjunctions, and monads, revisited across a wide range of mathematical examples from the categorical viewpoint. Prerequisites are only basic set theory and logic. Serves as a contemporary alternative "door" alongside Mac Lane (1971).
