S. Kullback and R. A. Leibler, "On Information and Sufficiency," *The Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 22 (March 1951), no. 1, pp. 79–86. DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177729694 (Institute of Mathematical Statistics; open access via Project Euclid).

Introduces what is now called the Kullback–Leibler divergence / relative entropy. The authors did not use the name "divergence" for the asymmetric quantity — they called it the "mean information for discrimination between H₁ and H₂ per observation," written I(1:2); they reserved "divergence" for the symmetrized sum J = I(1:2) + I(2:1). Distinct from Shannon's 1948 founding paper — this is the *statistical* lineage (sufficiency, discrimination), 1951, not 1948.
