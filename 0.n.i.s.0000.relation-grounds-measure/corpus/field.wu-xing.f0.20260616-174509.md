**origin** — classical Chinese natural philosophy; the five phases named in the "Hóng Fàn" (Great Plan) chapter of the *Shūjīng* (Book of Documents) and systematized by Zōu Yǎn and the Naturalist (Yīn-Yáng) school (Warring States period). [[source.needham-1956-scc2]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — the two governing cycles: *shēng* (generating: wood→fire→earth→metal→water→wood) and *kè* (overcoming/controlling: wood→earth→water→fire→metal→wood); the correlative cosmology mapping the phases to seasons, directions, colors, organs, etc. [[source.needham-1956-scc2]]

**door** — Needham, *Science and Civilisation in China*, vol. 2 (1956).

**lineage** — "five elements" is a common but loose translation; *phases/agents* (dynamic processes) is the truer reading — carried as a translation note. Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
