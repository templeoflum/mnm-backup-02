**origin** — the *Corpus Hermeticum*, Greek philosophical-religious texts attributed to Hermes Trismegistus (2nd–3rd c. CE), revived in the Renaissance by Marsilio Ficino's 1463 translation for Cosimo de' Medici. [[source.corpus-hermeticum]] **[confirmed]**

**codification** — Frances Yates, *Giordano Bruno and the Hermetic Tradition* (1964) — the field-defining account of the Renaissance correspondence-system (macrocosm/microcosm, the *prisca theologia*); extended in *The Art of Memory* (1966). [[source.yates-1964]] **[confirmed]**

**door** — Brian Copenhaver, *Hermetica* (1992, the scholarly translation); Yates (1964).

**lineage** — the **"Yates thesis"** (Hermeticism as a significant stimulus to the Scientific Revolution) is contested by later historians (Vickers, Westman) — carried as lineage, not asserted.
