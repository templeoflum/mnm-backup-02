James J. Gibson & Eleanor J. Gibson, "Perceptual learning: Differentiation or enrichment?" *Psychological Review*, 62(1), 32–41 (1955). DOI 10.1037/h0048826.

Poses the field's defining question: does perceptual learning add to sensory input (enrichment — associations, images, inferences) or does it sharpen the perceiver's extraction of variables already present in stimulation (differentiation)? The Gibsons argue for differentiation — perception comes into closer correspondence with stimulation, getting richer in discriminated variables, not in added images.
