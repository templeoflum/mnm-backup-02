Full citation — William Burnside, *Theory of Groups of Finite Order*, 2nd edition, Cambridge University Press, 1911 (1st edition 1897).

Role — door / early codification for [[object.orbit-stabilizer-theorem]] and orbit-counting: Burnside's treatise gave one of the first systematic textbook treatments of group actions, orbits, and stabilizers, where the orbit–stabilizer relation is made explicit as a counting tool. (The orbit-counting lemma in the same tradition — "Burnside's lemma" — is itself due to Cauchy (1845) and Frobenius (1887); Burnside popularized it.)

Access — public domain; full text on archive.org and Project Gutenberg. Edition/year confirmed by Wikipedia ("Burnside's lemma," "William Burnside"). **[confirmed]**
