Kabat-Zinn, J. (1990). *Full Catastrophe Living: Using the Wisdom of Your Body and Mind to Face Stress, Pain, and Illness*. New York, NY: Delacorte Press.

Contains — describes the secular mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) program developed at the University of Massachusetts Medical Center's Stress Reduction Clinic; lays out an 8-week mind–body curriculum and the early clinical rationale. A landmark of the secular mindfulness movement and the clinical arm of contemplative science.
