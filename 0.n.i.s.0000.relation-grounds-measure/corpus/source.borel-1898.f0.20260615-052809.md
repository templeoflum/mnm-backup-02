É. Borel, *Leçons sur la théorie des fonctions* (Gauthier-Villars, Paris, 1898).

Introduces Borel's theory of measurability for subsets of [0,1] — countably additive measure extending Jordan content — the foundation Lebesgue built his integration theory on (1902). Origin of measure theory.
