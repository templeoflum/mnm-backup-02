R. L. Wegel & C. E. Lane, "The Auditory Masking of One Pure Tone by Another and its Probable Relation to the Dynamics of the Inner Ear," *Physical Review* 23(2) (Feb 1924):266–285.

Role — first systematic quantitative measurement of masking with pure tones; documented the **upward spread of masking** (low frequencies mask high more than the reverse) and tied it to inner-ear mechanics.

Access — Physical Review article (abstract public). Attribution corroborated across two independent secondary sources (APS abstract; masking-history references). **[confirmed as attribution]**
