**origin** — the *Tonalpohualli* ("count of days"), the Aztec/Nahua 260-day ritual calendar: 20 day-signs × 1–13; the Mesoamerican counterpart of the Maya Tzolk'in. [[source.sahagun-florentine-codex]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — read by the *tonalpouhqui* from the *tonalamatl* (day-books, e.g. Codex Borbonicus); the day-signs, their fortunes, and patron deities recorded by Bernardino de Sahagún. [[source.sahagun-florentine-codex]]

**door** — Sahagún, *Florentine Codex* (General History of the Things of New Spain), Book 4.

**lineage** — each day has a patron deity in the tonalamatl, but **patron assignments and fortunes vary by codex** — so omitted here to avoid overclaim (name + theme kept). Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
