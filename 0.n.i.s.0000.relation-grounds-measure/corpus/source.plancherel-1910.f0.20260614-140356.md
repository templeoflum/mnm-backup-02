Full citation — M. Plancherel, "Contribution à l'étude de la représentation d'une fonction arbitraire par des intégrales définies," *Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo* **30** (1910), 289–335.

Role — establishes the **Plancherel theorem**: the [[object.fourier-transform]] extends to a norm-preserving (unitary) map on L²(ℝ), so that ∫|f|² = ∫|f̂|². The L²-theoretic foundation of the Fourier transform; the continuous-variable counterpart of Parseval's identity for [[object.fourier-series]].

Access — historical journal; theorem statement and the 1910 date confirmed via the Encyclopedia of Mathematics article "Plancherel theorem" and Wikipedia.
