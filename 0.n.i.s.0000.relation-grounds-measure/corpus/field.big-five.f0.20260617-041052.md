**origin** — the lexical hypothesis (Allport & Odbert; Cattell), refined by factor-analytic work (Tupes & Christal 1961; Goldberg's Big-Five program). [[source.costa-mccrae-1992]]

**codification** — Costa & McCrae operationalized the factors in the NEO-PI / NEO-PI-R; each factor is a continuous dimension, not a type.

**door** — Costa & McCrae, *Revised NEO Personality Inventory (NEO-PI-R)* (1992).

**standing** — the most empirically supported model of trait structure; the five factors replicate across cultures and methods. Held `empirical`.
