Charles Fefferman, Sanjoy Mitter, Hariharan Narayanan, "Testing the Manifold Hypothesis," *Journal of the American Mathematical Society* 29(4) (2016), 983–1049.

Formalizes the hypothesis that high-dimensional data lie near a low-dimensional manifold: given i.i.d. samples from a distribution on the unit ball of a separable Hilbert space, develops an algorithm with complexity guarantees that tests whether a manifold of bounded dimension, volume, and reach (minimum τ) fits the distribution to within a tolerance. Turns a long-standing motivating assumption of manifold learning into a question one can decide from data.

Published version via AMS (JAMS); preprint arXiv:1310.0425.
