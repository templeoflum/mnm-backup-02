Willis, J., & Todorov, A., "First impressions: Making up your mind after a 100-ms exposure to a face," *Psychological Science* 17(7) (2006):592–598.

Five experiments varying face-exposure time. Trait judgments (attractiveness, likeability, trustworthiness, competence, aggressiveness) after a 100-ms exposure correlated highly with judgments made without time constraint; longer exposure increased confidence and negativity but did not raise the correlation. Extends thin-slicing to the sub-second regime of static-face trait inference.
