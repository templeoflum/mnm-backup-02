A. D. Craig, "How do you feel — now? The anterior insula and human awareness," *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* **10**(1): 59–70 (2009). DOI 10.1038/nrn2555.

Contains — the sequel to Craig 2002. Argues that the anterior insular cortex (AIC) re-represents interoceptive states across successive moments, producing a unified "global emotional moment," and proposes the AIC (with its von Economo neurons) as a candidate substrate for human awareness. Surveys the breadth of AIC involvement — from visceral sensation to emotion, empathy, time perception, decision making and consciousness.

Role — codification / extension; the standard reference for the insular model of interoceptive awareness.
