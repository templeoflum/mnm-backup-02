Bernardino de Sahagún, *Florentine Codex: General History of the Things of New Spain* (c. 1577; English: Arthur J. O. Anderson & Charles E. Dibble, 1950–82).

The foremost primary source on Aztec religion and society, compiled with Nahua informants; Book 4 treats the tonalpohualli day-count and its auguries. **[confirmed as attribution]** *(primary colonial source; cited for the day-signs.)*
