The *Corpus Hermeticum*, a collection of Greek philosophical-religious treatises attributed to Hermes Trismegistus (2nd–3rd c. CE), revived in the Renaissance by Marsilio Ficino's 1463 Latin translation for Cosimo de' Medici. The scholarly English text: Brian Copenhaver, *Hermetica* (Cambridge, 1992).
