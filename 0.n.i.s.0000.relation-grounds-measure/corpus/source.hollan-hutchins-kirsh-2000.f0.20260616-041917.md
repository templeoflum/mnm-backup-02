Verbatim citation: Hollan, James, Edwin Hutchins, and David Kirsh. "Distributed Cognition: Toward a New Foundation for Human-Computer Interaction Research." *ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction* 7, no. 2 (2000): 174–196. DOI 10.1145/353485.353487.

Contains: an argument that distributed cognition supplies a theoretical foundation for HCI; the unit of analysis becomes the functional system spanning people, artifacts, and environment. Frames cognition as the propagation of representational state across media, and lays out methodological commitments (cognitive ethnography) for studying technology-in-use. **[confirmed]**
