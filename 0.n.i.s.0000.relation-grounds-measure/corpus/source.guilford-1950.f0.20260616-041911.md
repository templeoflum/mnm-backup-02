Verbatim citation: Guilford, J. P. (1950). Creativity. *American Psychologist*, 5(9), 444–454.

Contains: Guilford's 1950 presidential address to the American Psychological Association. Argues creativity had been badly neglected by psychology and calls for its systematic study, proposing that creative aptitudes (fluency, flexibility, originality, elaboration) are distinct, measurable intellectual factors — the seed of the *divergent thinking* / Structure-of-Intellect program.
