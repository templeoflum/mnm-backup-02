**origin** — Carl Gustav Jung's analytical psychology: archetypes are inherited, universal predispositions of the collective unconscious, expressed in images, myths, and dreams. [[source.jung-cw9i-1959]]

**codification** — Jung's *Collected Works* vol. 9i, *The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious*.

**door** — Jung, CW 9i (1959).

**split (per the ratified Bin-1 settlement):** two kinds are conflated in the popular set — **5 structural** archetypes (Self, Shadow, Anima, Animus, Persona, which describe the psyche's architecture) and **figural/image** archetypes (Wise Old Man, Great Mother, Hero, Child, Trickster, Maiden/Kore, which appear as characters). Marked per-object.

**Crone flag:** 'The Crone' is **borderline** — Jung used *Kore* and the *Great Mother*; 'Crone' is more a neopagan term. Carried with this flag, not as settled-Jungian.

**standing** — foundational to depth psychology; interpretive, not empirically operationalized. Held `doctrinal`.
