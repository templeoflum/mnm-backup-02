Bishop, S. R., Lau, M., Shapiro, S., Carlson, L., Anderson, N. D., Carmody, J., et al. (2004). Mindfulness: A Proposed Operational Definition. *Clinical Psychology: Science and Practice*, 11(3), 230–241. doi:10.1093/clipsy.bph077

Contains — a consensus, two-component model of mindfulness: (1) self-regulation of attention sustained on immediate experience, and (2) an orientation of curiosity, openness, and acceptance toward that experience. Each component is specified in terms of behaviors, experiential manifestations, and psychological processes, with discussion of measurement.

Access — Wiley Online Library; widely cited operationalization in mindfulness research. **[confirmed]**
