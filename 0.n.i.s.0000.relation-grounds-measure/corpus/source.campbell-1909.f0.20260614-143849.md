N. R. Campbell, "The study of discontinuous phenomena" and "Discontinuities in light emission," *Proc. Cambridge Phil. Soc.* 15 (1909/1910).

Role — origin of Campbell's theorem in point-process statistics. Campbell, a physicist, studied random sums arising from thermionic (shot) noise in vacuum tubes and derived the mean and variance of a sum of a function evaluated over a Poisson set of points. He credited the main mathematical argument to G. H. Hardy, whence the alternate name "Campbell–Hardy theorem."
