Don Richard Riso & Russ Hudson, *The Wisdom of the Enneagram: The Complete Guide to Psychological and Spiritual Growth for the Nine Personality Types* (Bantam, 1999).

The widely-used modern reference for the nine types — the type names (Reformer, Helper, Achiever, …), the three centers (gut/heart/head), and the basic-fear / basic-desire framing. Builds on Oscar Ichazo's Arica structure and Claudio Naranjo's psychological typing. **[confirmed as attribution]** *(primary not opened; cited for the type schema it standardized.)*
