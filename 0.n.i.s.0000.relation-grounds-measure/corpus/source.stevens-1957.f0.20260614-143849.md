S. S. Stevens, "On the psychophysical law," *Psychological Review* 64, no. 3 (1957): 153–181.

Role — origin of Stevens' power law: the paper that revived the power function ψ(I) = k·Iᵃ and marshalled magnitude-estimation / magnitude-production data to support it as a general psychophysical relation, against Fechner's logarithmic law.

Note — distinct from [[source.stevens-1946]] ("On the Theory of Scales of Measurement," *Science*), which is the scales-of-measurement paper (nominal/ordinal/interval/ratio) and belongs to representational measurement theory, not the power law. The famous polemic "To honor Fechner and repeal his law" is a *separate*, later Stevens paper (*Science* 133, 1961).
