Verbatim: Spärck Jones, K. (1972). "A Statistical Interpretation of Term Specificity and Its Application in Retrieval." *Journal of Documentation* 28(1):11–21. DOI 10.1108/eb026526.

Contains: the argument that term specificity should be read statistically — as a function of how a term is used across a collection, not of its meaning — and that terms should be weighted by collection frequency so matches on rarer, more specific terms count for more. This is the conceptual origin of inverse document frequency (idf), the multiplier in tf-idf.
