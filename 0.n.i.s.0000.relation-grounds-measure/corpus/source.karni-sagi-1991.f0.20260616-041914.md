Avi Karni & Dov Sagi, "Where practice makes perfect in texture discrimination: evidence for primary visual cortex plasticity," *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences USA*, 88(11), 4966–4970 (1991). DOI 10.1073/pnas.88.11.4966. PMID 2052578.

Reports long-term learning on a texture discrimination task that is specific for retinal location (retinotopically local), orientation-specific (specific for background element orientation), and strongly monocular (little interocular transfer). The specificity implicates experience-dependent change at an early visual level where monocularity, retinotopy, and orientation are still segregated — early evidence for adult V1 plasticity in perceptual learning.
