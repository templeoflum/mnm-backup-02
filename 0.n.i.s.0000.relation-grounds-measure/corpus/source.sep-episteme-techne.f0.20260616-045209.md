Parry, Richard. "Episteme and Techne." *The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (Winter 2021 Edition), ed. Edward N. Zalta. First published April 11, 2003; substantively revised 2020. URL: plato.stanford.edu/entries/episteme-techne/

Contains — a scholarly survey of *episteme* (knowledge) and *techne* (craft/art) across Greek philosophy: the Presocratics and Sophists, Socrates' use of crafts as models of knowledge, Plato (*Gorgias*, *Republic*, *Philebus*), and Aristotle's systematic distinction of *techne* / *episteme* / *phronesis* in *Nicomachean Ethics* VI and the *Metaphysics*. Serves as the "door" — a free, citable academic survey mapping the primary passages and the secondary debates.
