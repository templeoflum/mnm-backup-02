The three **rune poems** — the Old English Rune Poem (~9th–10th c., the 29-rune futhorc), the Old Norwegian Rune Rhyme, and the Old Icelandic Rune Poem (Younger Futhark) — each a mnemonic stanza per rune.

The primary medieval source for runic names and their glosses; the Elder Futhark meanings are reconstructed back from these. **[confirmed as attribution]** *(primary texts; the Elder Futhark names themselves are scholarly reconstructions.)*
