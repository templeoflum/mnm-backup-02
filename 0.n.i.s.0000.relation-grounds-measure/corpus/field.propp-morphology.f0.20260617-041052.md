**origin** — Vladimir Propp's structural analysis of ~100 Russian fairy tales: beneath surface variety lie a fixed sequence of functions and a fixed cast of seven roles. [[source.propp-1928]]

**door** — Propp, *Morphology of the Folktale* (1928; English trans. 1958/1968).

**settlement applied:** the canon is **7 spheres of action**. The collected set listed eight by splitting 'the Princess (sought-for person)' from 'her Father' — but Propp treats these as **one sphere** (their functions overlap and cannot always be distinguished). Per the ratified Bin-1 settlement, they are **merged into one role**.

**standing** — a foundational, widely-taught result in narratology/structuralism. Held `established`.
