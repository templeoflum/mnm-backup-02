Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (Farrar, Straus and Giroux, New York, 2011). ISBN 978-0-374-27563-1.

Contains — the standard trade synthesis of the heuristics-and-biases and prospect-theory research program. Frames cognition as two systems — **System 1** (fast, automatic, associative) and **System 2** (slow, effortful, deliberate) — and surveys anchoring, availability, representativeness, the conjunction fallacy, framing, loss aversion, the endowment effect, and the experiencing vs. remembering self. Won the National Academy of Sciences Best Book Award (2012) and the LA Times Book Prize.
