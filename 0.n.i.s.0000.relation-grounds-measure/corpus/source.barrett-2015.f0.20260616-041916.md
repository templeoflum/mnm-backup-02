Lisa Feldman Barrett & W. Kyle Simmons, "Interoceptive predictions in the brain," *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* **16**(7): 419–429 (2015). DOI 10.1038/nrn3950.

Contains — an opinion/integration article casting interoception in the predictive-processing (active inference) framework. Proposes the **Embodied Predictive Interoception Coding (EPIC)** model: agranular visceromotor cortices (including anterior insula and anterior cingulate) issue interoceptive *predictions* and allostatic motor commands; granular sensory regions compute *prediction error* against incoming visceral afferents. Unifies allostatic regulation and interoceptive perception within one corticocortical architecture, anchored in anatomical models of laminar/connectional organization.

Role — codification of the computational strand; standard reference for interoceptive predictive coding / EPIC.
