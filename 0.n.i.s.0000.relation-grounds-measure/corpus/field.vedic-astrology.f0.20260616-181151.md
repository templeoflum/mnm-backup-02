**origin** — *Jyotiṣa*, the Vedic/Indian astrological tradition; the twelve *rāśi* (zodiac signs) measured against the fixed stars (**sidereal**), unlike the Western tropical zodiac. [[source.brihat-parashara-hora-shastra]] **[confirmed as attribution]**

**codification** — the *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra* (BPHS), the foundational classical Jyotiṣa text, sets out the rāśi, their lords (graha), elements, and qualities. [[source.brihat-parashara-hora-shastra]]

**door** — *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra*.

**lineage** — **sidereal** (Vedic) vs. **tropical** (Western, [[field.western-astrology]]) — the same twelve signs, differently anchored (~24° ayanāṃśa apart); the rāśi correspond to the Western signs by name (Meṣa = Aries, etc.) but carry the Vedic interpretive layer. The 27 nakṣatras (lunar mansions) ground in a later batch. Held as the tradition's system (members `status: doctrinal`).
