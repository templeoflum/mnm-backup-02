**origin (a)** — C. S. Peirce's triadic sign (sign / object / interpretant), c. 1902–06, reconstructed from *Collected Papers of C. S. Peirce*, 8 vols. (Harvard; vols. 1–6 Hartshorne & Weiss 1931–35, vols. 7–8 Burks 1958). [[source.peirce-collected-papers]] **[confirmed]** *(no single origin paper; cite the Collected Papers.)*

**origin (b)** — Saussure's "semiology" (signifier / signified) in the *Cours* (1916); the two traditions developed independently. [[source.saussure-1916]] **[confirmed]**

**door / codification** — Umberto Eco, *A Theory of Semiotics* (Indiana, 1976; Italian *Trattato di semiotica generale*, Bompiani, 1975). [[source.eco-1976]] **[confirmed]** Accessible alternative: Daniel Chandler, *Semiotics: The Basics* (and the free "Semiotics for Beginners").
