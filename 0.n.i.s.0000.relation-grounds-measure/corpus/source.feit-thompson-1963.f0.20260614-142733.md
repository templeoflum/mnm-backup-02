Full citation — Walter Feit and John G. Thompson, "Solvability of groups of odd order," *Pacific Journal of Mathematics*, vol. 13 (1963), pp. 775–1029 (a single ~255-page paper filling an entire journal issue).

Role — foundation milestone for [[object.classification-of-finite-simple-groups]]: the odd-order theorem (every finite group of odd order is solvable, equivalently every non-abelian finite simple group has even order). Its length and methods set the template and launched the modern classification effort.
