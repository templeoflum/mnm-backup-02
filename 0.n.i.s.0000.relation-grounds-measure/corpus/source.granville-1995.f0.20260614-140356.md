Full citation — A. Granville, "Harald Cramér and the distribution of prime numbers," *Scandinavian Actuarial Journal* 1995, no. 1, 12–28.

Contains the argument that Cramér's random model fails to capture short-interval behaviour (via Maier's theorem, 1985, on irregular prime distribution in short intervals), so the strong form lim sup (p_{n+1}−p_n)/(log p_n)² = 1 is probably false. The refined Cramér–Granville heuristic predicts the lim sup is at least 2e^{−γ} ≈ 1.1229.
