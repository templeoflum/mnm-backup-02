J. E. Hutchinson, "Fractals and Self-Similarity," *Indiana University Mathematics Journal* **30** (1981), no. 5, 713–747.

Contains — the modern foundation of iterated function systems (IFS): given finitely many contraction maps on a complete metric space, the induced "Hutchinson operator" on the space of non-empty compact sets (under the Hausdorff metric) is itself a contraction, hence by the Banach fixed-point theorem has a unique fixed point — the attractor, a self-similar set. Also treats the associated invariant (Hutchinson) measure and dimension. Provides the existence/uniqueness machinery behind the classical fractals.
