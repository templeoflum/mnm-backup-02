Verbatim: Robertson, S., and Zaragoza, H. (2009). "The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond." *Foundations and Trends in Information Retrieval* 3(4):333–389. DOI 10.1561/1500000019.

Contains: a conceptual presentation of the Probabilistic Relevance Framework (PRF) grown from 1970s–80s work; the modelling assumptions behind it; and the ranking algorithms it yields — the binary independence model, relevance-feedback models, BM25, and BM25F. The standard modern reference fixing the form and rationale of the BM25 scoring function.
