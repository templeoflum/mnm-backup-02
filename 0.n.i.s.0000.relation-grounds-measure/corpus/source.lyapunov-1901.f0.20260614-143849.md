Full citation — A. M. Lyapunov, "Nouvelle forme du théorème sur la limite de la probabilité," *Mémoires de l'Académie Impériale des Sciences de St.-Pétersbourg* (8) **12**, no. 5 (1901); preceded by the 1900 note "Sur une proposition de la théorie des probabilités," *Bulletin de l'Académie Impériale des Sciences de St.-Pétersbourg* (5) 13 (1900), 359–386.

Role — first **rigorous proof of the central limit theorem in general form** for sums of independent (not identically distributed) random variables, under what is now the **Lyapunov condition** (a moment condition of order 2 + δ). Lyapunov used the method of characteristic functions, which became central to modern probability.

Access — historical; reprinted in Lyapunov's collected works. Attribution and 1901 (with 1900 precursor) corroborated by MacTutor, Wikipedia ("Central limit theorem", "Aleksandr Lyapunov"), and standard histories. **[confirmed]**

Note — distinct from [[source.lyapunov-1892]] (the doctoral thesis on stability of motion, dynamical systems); the CLT work is the 1900–1901 papers.
