**citation (verbatim)** — Saunders Mac Lane, *Categories for the Working Mathematician*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics, Vol. 5, Springer-Verlag, New York. First edition 1971 (ISBN 0-387-90036-5); second edition 1998 (ISBN 0-387-98403-8), DOI 10.1007/978-1-4757-4721-8.

**what it contains** — the canonical graduate text, written by a co-founder of the subject. Covers categories, functors, natural transformations, constructions on categories, universal arrows and the Yoneda lemma, limits and colimits, adjoint functors and their existence (the adjoint functor theorems), monads/algebras, monoidal and abelian categories, Kan extensions ("all concepts are Kan extensions"), and higher categorical structures. The standard reference and entry point ("door") for the field.
