Pye, David. *The Nature and Art of Workmanship*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1968. (Later reprints: Herbert Press, 1995; Bloomsbury, 2008; with introduction by Ezra Shales in some editions.)

Contains — the distinction between the **workmanship of risk** ("workmanship using any kind of technique or apparatus, in which the quality of the result is not predetermined, but depends on the judgment, dexterity and care which the maker exercises as he works") and the **workmanship of certainty** (found in mass production and automated processes, where the quality of the result is predetermined before a single thing is made). Pye, an English furniture designer, woodworker, and Professor of Furniture at the Royal College of Art, also analyzes "free" vs "regulated" workmanship, diversity, soundness, and the aesthetic role of surface and edge.
