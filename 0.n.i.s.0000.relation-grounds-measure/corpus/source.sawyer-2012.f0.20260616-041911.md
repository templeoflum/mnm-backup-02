Verbatim citation: Sawyer, R. K. (2012). *Explaining Creativity: The Science of Human Innovation* (2nd ed.). New York: Oxford University Press.

Contains: a comprehensive survey of creativity research — definitions and measurement, the creative process (including the Wallas stages and incubation), cognitive and neuroscientific accounts, individual differences, and the sociocultural / systems view of creativity. Serves as the field's "door": a single text mapping the major schools and their evidence.

Access notes: 2nd edition (2012); 1st ed. 2006, 3rd ed. 2023 (with Henriksen) also exist. Publisher Oxford University Press confirmed via OUP catalog. Primary text not opened here.
