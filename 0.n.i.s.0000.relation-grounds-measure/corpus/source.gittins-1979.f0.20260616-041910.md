J. C. Gittins, "Bandit processes and dynamic allocation indices," *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B (Methodological)*, vol. 41, no. 2 (1979), pp. 148–164 (with discussion to p. 177).

**citation** — gives a unified account of bandit processes and dynamic allocation indices, showing that the optimal policy for a family of alternative discounted-reward bandits is to play at each step the arm of highest index — an index computable for each arm in isolation. Read before the Royal Statistical Society with discussion; consolidates the Gittins–Jones (1974) index theorem. Applications to stochastic scheduling, sequential clinical trials, and search are discussed.
