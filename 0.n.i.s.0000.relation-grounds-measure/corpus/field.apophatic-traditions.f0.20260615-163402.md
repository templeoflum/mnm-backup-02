**origin (Christian)** — Pseudo-Dionysius the Areopagite, *The Mystical Theology* (*De Mystica Theologia*) — theology becomes "mystical," the *via negativa*. Dating c. 485–518/528 CE (draws on Proclus d. 485; first cited by Severus of Antioch 518–528). [[source.pseudo-dionysius-mystical-theology]] **[confirmed]**

**precursor** — Plotinus, *The Enneads* (3rd c. CE, ed. Porphyry) — "the One" beyond being; the Neoplatonic *aphairesis* Dionysius drew on. [[source.plotinus-enneads]] **[confirmed]**

**later (Christian)** — Meister Eckhart (c. 1260–1328, the "Godhead" beyond God); the anonymous *The Cloud of Unknowing* (late 14th c.). **[confirmed; *Cloud* authorship inherently anonymous.]**

**eastern parallels** — Nāgārjuna's Madhyamaka (*Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*, c. 2nd–3rd c. CE): *śūnyatā*, the *catuṣkoṭi*; and *neti neti* of the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad (attributed to Yājñavalkya), Nirguṇa Brahman by negation. [[source.nagarjuna-mmk]] **[confirmed on attributions; influence-vs-convergence genuinely debated — do not assert one genealogy.]**

**door** — public-domain Pseudo-Dionysius: Rolt's translation (SPCK, 1920) on Internet Archive; Parker's on CCEL. Scholarly: Luibhéid & Rorem, *Pseudo-Dionysius: The Complete Works* (Paulist, 1987, in copyright).
