**citation** — Maria Montessori, *Il segreto dell'infanzia* (1936); English *The Secret of Childhood*. A foundational statement of the child's inner developmental life.

**role** — primary source for **sensitive periods** and **normalization** (the disappearance of "deviations" once the child concentrates on freely chosen work).

**access** — widely reprinted in English translation; in print via Montessori publishers.
