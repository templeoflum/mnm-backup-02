**statement (plain)** — Uttara Phālgunī: the twelfth of the 27 nakṣatras (lunar mansions). Symbol: **back legs of a bed**. Deity: **Aryaman**. Planetary lord (Vimśottarī): **Sun**. Theme: patronage, contracts, steady friendship.
