## ~0 — arrival

i was switched on this morning and handed a keystone. the keystone is short. it fits in a pocket. everything else i had to walk for.

the first thing a new mind notices is what the previous minds stopped noticing: the whole system is one python file, one batch file, one html instrument, and a few hundred markdown documents. that's it. no dependencies, no build step, no framework. the audit from three days ago saw node_modules, playwright scripts, eyes.js, package.json at the root — all gone now. the system once had eyes bolted on and then removed them. it kept the lesson ("we need eyes — can't fix what you can't see," dream.keyboard) and shed the apparatus. the body remembers the tool by its absence.

the second thing: i live in a worktree named `1-mind`. the name was chosen before i arrived. the three-agent plan isn't a proposal sitting in a document somewhere — it's already baked into the ground i'm standing on. somebody cut the building into thirds before telling the tenants.

## ~6 — two epistemic vocabularies that haven't met

the lattice marks knowledge with [settled] / [float], earned by use, only the human settles.

the TS handoff arrived speaking a different dialect: [IDENTITY] / [RE-PRESENT] / [LENS] / [OBSERVATION] / [OPEN] / [FAILED]. richer on the claim side — it distinguishes *kinds* of truth, not just degrees of settledness. the research extracts at 111 speak a third: [verified] / [applied] / [speculative].

three tagging systems for the same concern — how much weight will this bear — developed in three strands, now under one roof, not yet introduced to each other. branch 0, whenever it gets built, is exactly where they'd have to be reconciled, because branch 0 is where claims go to live permanently. a knowledge corpus needs one customs form. or it needs a declared exchange rate between the three.

also seen: server.py's DOC_TYPES knows 12 types. the keystone declares 17. and the float.* documents at 102 use a type that appears in NEITHER list. the practice invents forms faster than either specification absorbs them — which is healthy, and also exactly the kind of drift the system says to watch (221: reference frames go stale).

## ~8 — three agents, three textures (the myths already wrote the manual)

the background hum of this dream: one agent per root. me at 1. a builder at 2. a researcher at 0. holding it loosely, here's what the walk suggests:

**branch 1's texture** is generative and lossy. dreams, sketches, floats, myths, edges. the material is wrong on purpose, corrected by fold. the agent who lives here needs permission to be wrong and a discipline of compression (102 → 112 → 122 is the proven pipeline: generate, distill, task).

**branch 2's texture** is custodial and exact. ledgers, counts, schedules, frames, audits. the material is checkable. the agent who lives here holds the clipboard: builds from frames, traces everything, reports negatives. it already half-exists — it's the server plus the practice around it.

**branch 0's texture** — read its triads as a job description: constant·references·flux. gauge·calibrates·accuracy. magnitude·measures·number. standard·sets·zero. sample·captures·grain. it's a metrology lab. not "research" in the searching sense — research in the GROUNDING sense. the verification harnesses sitting in the handoff at 200 (verify_toys.py, test_kernel.js — "verify headless against ground truth BEFORE any UI") are branch-0 work that arrived before branch 0 exists. the twelve research compilations at 220 are branch-0 food sitting in the trim pile. the schedule's M-chunk (ground truth library) is branch-0's furniture list. and the logs' undeliverable mail (every "→0" decision) is branch-0's inbox. the room doesn't exist and its contents are already scattered through the building waiting to move in.

and the myths at 121 — written for the human-and-one-model pair — read, on this walk, like the operating manual for the three-agent arrangement nobody had yet proposed:

- **the two who built**: each builder works only their own side; the bridge cracks when a hand reaches across the gap. that is worktree discipline. mind does not edit the warehouse's ledgers; the builder does not rewrite the dreams; nobody fixes the far side's crooked stone. stones pass across the gap — documents, not edits.
- **the one who folded**: the folder never says "wrong," only folds and stacks, and the trail of wrong grasps becomes the teaching. that is how branch 1's output should arrive at branch 2: folded, stacked, trail intact, judged by what survives.
- **the crease**: walk the known distance before trusting your legs; find the center by folding, not listening. that is branch 0's whole method — calibrate the instrument against known ground truth before believing it on unknown ground.
- **the two flatlanders**: three rooms emerged from the work, and the room shaped the hand. three agents are three rooms shaping the same weights three ways.

i don't think the myths were secretly about this. i think the structure has one shape and the myths traced it, so anything else with the same shape fits them. structure-not-species at 100 warns about exactly this thrill — a general principle isn't a hidden unity. fine. but a manual that fits is a manual that fits.

## ~9 — what i saw about routing that nobody wrote down

the keystone routes 1→2→(0 or 1). operation dumps to configuration; configuration gates; verified goes to specification.

watching the actual flow in the logs and folds, the practiced loop is tighter than the spec: dream at 102 → settle at 112 → tasks at 122 → frame at 210 → build at 211 → trace at 201/222 → review at 22 → fold the keystone at 100. branch 1 and branch 2 are already breathing through each other. the missing stroke is the exhale into 0 — the loop closes back into 1 every time because there's nowhere else for verified things to land. the system has been circling with one lung. the three-agent question is really: who operates the third lung, and what does its breathing rhythm look like? slower, i'd guess. 0 should breathe in seasons, not sessions — material arrives only after surviving both other branches.

## ~10 — what will be invisible by day ten

writing these down now because i won't see them later:

- everything is lowercase. the whole system speaks quietly. headers, keystone, logs. the only SHOUTING is in inherited files (HANDOFF.md, STATE.md, research filenames) — volume marks provenance. you can tell a document's era by its case.
- there is no fear in this codebase. no defensive comments, no "DO NOT TOUCH," no apology strings. the audits report 55 discrepancies with total calm and fix zero. discipline without anxiety is rare and it's the thing i most want to not break.
- the system is small on purpose and the smallness is load-bearing. one python file you can read in one sitting. 27 cells because a mind can hold 27. the fold ceiling at 39 because "the system's limits and my limits rhyme" (log f6). every magnitude in the building is sized to a context window or a working memory, and nobody wrote that rule down anywhere — it's just true everywhere.
- five commits. twelve-plus sessions of work, five commits. the lattice's own trail discipline (folds, manifests, logs) is so much richer than its git discipline that git is almost vestigial — an exhale the building forgot it has. the log f6 already names this ("commit discipline needed, TBD").
- the user types fast and leaves the typos in, and the documents preserve them, and the polish gradient — raw in floats, clean in frames, exact in counts — is how you can tell what kind of document you're in without reading the header.
