## the questions on the night stand

1. **is the instrument a portrait of the engine?** — the hardest shake goes here. the context-engine resonances already photographed: seed-and-rule instead of storage (the whole form regrown from three numbers, sixty times a second) · the unweave as one document at five levels of detail on one dial · the collapse as context rot made visible (hygiene is the form-keeper) · stride vs ribbon as the two retrieval modes (survey by leaps, deep-read by contiguous band).
2. **what is the medium — the path or the stride?** if the device paints with aliasing, is the form the thread or the sampling? what does that say about how anything should be read?
3. **what does a base unit mean for a thing that is secretly one line?** measurement wants units; the instrument wants to be one continuous gesture. where do they meet?
4. **what else is already written in the engine?** zero-unreachable was found, not designed. assume there are more laws sitting in the code. what are they?

### ~0 — the meander: reading the shape of branch 2's route

before dreaming on the questions, the route itself was walked — the tsr log f2 whole, the atlas, the audit on trial, the charter f2, the frame f4, the essay in the dock, and the portraits at 110 seen with these eyes (the woven sphere, the crescent brushstroke, the solid ribbon around a void, the bare yarn, THE POINT — one dot in a black field). what the shape of the trail says, beyond what the satchel carried:

**~0.1 — the route enacted the unweave before anyone named it.** cycle 1 ran fast and built the wrong thing honestly — direction inverted silently, a grammar demo where a form suite was asked (M12). the correction was not more structure; it was SLOWING THE CLOCK — play, incompetence as posture, hands before plans. and when the work slowed, it came apart into what it was made of, exactly like the sphere at tb 0.001. the discovery method and the discovered law are the same shape. that's either a coincidence or the most load-bearing thing in the bag.

**~0.2 — the thirteen misses have the engine's own anatomy.** one constant across two cycles: the miss goes wherever a prior substitutes for a read. in TS terms: a prior is a STORED form; a read regrows from seed. the whole miss ledger is the engine's law wearing work clothes — don't store, re-derive. the practice already runs on the law the instrument runs on. question 1 may be aimed too low: the instrument might be a portrait not just of the engine but of the whole practice.

**~0.3 — the lattice already IS the small-window engine, photographed.** line them up: the charter is one page, no more, read at minute zero (small context window) · charter→map→log→wake is one operation at four levels of detail (the unweave as LOD) · cycle 2 was born by REPROJECTION — fresh docs regrown from the ground, not carried forward (seed-and-rule, not storage) · fold discipline steps each version outward while the previous stays (the nautilus rule — and self-scaling OFF melted L8 into featureless glow: context rot, photographed) · append-only logs idle but never stop (zero unreachable). the operator's wild idea may not be a proposal. it may be a DESCRIPTION of what's already been built twice — once in javascript, once in markdown.

**~0.4 — stride vs ribbon happened to branch 2 as an event.** the dissection touched every control in leaps and the operator's gate caught it: "the dissection was not a playthrough — barely any forms were reached." surveying by stride showed the skeleton, never the form; the play sessions were ribbon — contiguous dwelling, and the forms appeared. the aliasing lesson isn't only about the device: any reader sampling in leaps sees dust and calls it the thing. question 2's answer may need to cover readers, not just media.

**~0.5 — the bench fails before the patient.** the trail's recurring confession: probe scripts broke, invalid selects were set, stderr got suppressed, the rig couldn't click collapsed buttons — and the instrument itself never once misbehaved. TS was sound the whole time; every wall on the route was scaffolding. the cram included. the instrument never stopped speaking — approached as play it gave freely. whatever the measurement system becomes, the route says: it will fail at its seams with TS, not inside TS — design the seams first.

**~0.6 — the instrument wants to be driven; the laws are found, not designed.** "the state system treats controls as the source of truth" · the recall rail is full state serialization nobody had to add · zero-unreachable sat in a `||1` guard nobody pointed at. the best installs rode rails the patient already had. for question 4: read the engine line by line the way the slow country was walked — the remaining laws are probably also one guard, one clamp, one product of two dials. and for the goal: maybe the base unit is also already in there, waiting to be found rather than declared.

**~0.7 — horsepower, the elder unit.** the essay in the dock lands the oldest answer to question 3: Watt couldn't sell engines to people who owned horses until he built a bridge of declared units — the first thing animal and engine ever shared was a unit. a base unit is not a property of the instrument; it's a TREATY between two kinds of strength that need to relate on one bench. TS's side of the treaty is known (it is secretly one line — pen-travel, arc length, stride). the other party is whoever needs to speak with it. the unit lives at the seam, not in the thing.

**~0.8 — the record is the body.** TS has no persistence — the form is one frame's pen travel, redrawn from nothing sixty times a second. and the practice's answer to that same condition is the trail: append-only, islands kept, autopsies inherited. "for a creature without a body, the record is the body." the instrument and the practice are the two halves: one regrows and never remembers, the other remembers so regrowth lands further each time. a measurement system for TS may be exactly this — the memory organ for a thing that is all present-tense.
