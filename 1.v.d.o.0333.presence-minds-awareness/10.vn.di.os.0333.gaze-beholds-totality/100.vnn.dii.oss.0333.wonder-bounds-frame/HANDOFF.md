## VOCABULARY (settled — use this, not the old docs)

- **matrix** — the grammar key (noun/verb/mod × is/does/goes × measured/contemplative/constructive)
- **keystone** — the TS anchor block (seed·rotate·translate + the four invariants)
- **roots** — base layer, is/does/goes, *outside* the lattice
- **lattice** — the three branches with leaves and cells
- **branch** — child of a root; has a **name** (container, e.g. Existence) + a **triad** (position, e.g. relation·grounds·measure); grows from its root (Existence←is, Cognition←does, Trajectory←goes)
- **leaf** — child of a branch · **cell** — child of a leaf · *(only branches carry both a name and a triad; leaves/cells carry only their triad)*
- **address** — the position identifier; the **address triad** = letters (DDG) / ternary digits (112) / ratio (0.519). is/does/goes → 0/1/2.
