# marginalia — the animations
**f0 · the channel's first quarry. fresh pieces, cut from scratch against the decree: the channel is a universal transmission vehicle — the record holds in the structure carried, not in the carrier. every piece must transmit even stripped: reposted, uncredited, cropped from its caption, the mathematics still carries the payload. lossy in names, lossless in relations, by construction.**

*each entry: working title · the piece in one breath · the ☞ line as currently felt. all floating; glosses harden at the bench when each is built.*

## the invariance thread (Klein's)
- **four points, one number** — a line projected wildly; every distance changes, the cross-ratio doesn't. *☞ everything moved except one thing.*
- **the triangle reports its world** — angle sums on plane, sphere, saddle. *☞ the triangle knows where it lives.*
- **seven ways to repeat forever** — the frieze groups drawn as manuscript border ornaments; the margins themselves obey group theory. *☞ every border ever ruled is one of seven.*

## the irrationals (closure and refusal)
- **the spiral wearing a circle's name** — twelve fifths stacked against seven octaves; the Pythagorean comma. seen as a spiral that misses, heard as the drift. the dual-projection thesis piece. *(lean: piece 001 — it founds the channel by demonstrating the channel.)*
- **the transparent forest** — sightlines through an integer orchard: rational directions strike trees, irrational ones see forever. *☞ the forest is clear only along the irrationals.*
- **rolling home** — a circle rolling inside a circle: rational ratio closes the petal, irrational never does. *☞ you would watch forever.*
- **at most three** — multiples of any number dropped around a circle; the gaps come in at most three sizes, always (Steinhaus). *☞ chaos was never offered.*

## the ear's marginalia (sound-native; pointing at absence)
- **the tone nobody played** — harmonics 2, 3, 4 sounded; the ear supplies the missing fundamental. the logo's gesture, made audible.
- **one tone breathing** — two tones a hair apart: the eye sees two, the ear hears one, beating. *kept by the eye: the pair · kept by the ear: the breath.*

## chance at tending-pace
- **the walker comes home** — random walks return in one and two dimensions, always; in three, only sometimes. *☞ two dimensions forgive wandering; three do not.*
- **mostly on one side** — the arcsine law: a fair walk spends its life ahead or behind, rarely crossing. *☞ fairness does not look fair.*
- **two digits, ten thousand drops** — Buffon's needle grinding toward π. the refusal is the piece: precision is bought, slowly.

## the refusal pieces (the channel's thesis)
- **seven agreements certify nothing** — the Borwein integrals: π/2, π/2, π/2… then the eighth fails by 10⁻¹¹. certainty runs one way, drawn.
- **it climbs without climbing** — the devil's staircase: rising on a set of measure zero. *☞ where it moves, nothing happens.*
- **the slowest departure** — the harmonic series creeping past 10 after 12,367 terms, diverging anyway. *☞ patience is not convergence.*

## the found world (physics as honesty)
- **the curve in your cup** — the caustic in a coffee mug, drawn by reflection law alone. *☞ the sun has been sketching this forever.*

## the reject pile (held against, by name)
mandelbrot zooms · golden spirals · epicycle drawings · galton boards · double pendulums · modular-times-table cardioids — the genre's claimed clip-art, worn smooth by other hands. the channel transmits quieter and stranger.

## the doc's own note
this is quarry, not schedule. pieces are built at tending-pace, one at a time, each through the full bench: predicted, built, verified, glossed, refused. the list grows by walks and shrinks by keeping.
