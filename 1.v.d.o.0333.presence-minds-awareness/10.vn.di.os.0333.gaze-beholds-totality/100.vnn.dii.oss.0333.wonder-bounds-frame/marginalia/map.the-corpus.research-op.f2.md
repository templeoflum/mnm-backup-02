## rung zero · calibration — PASSED (dive 0)

field: sampling theory. the instrument caught the 1948/1949 seam unaided. result sealed:

- **origin** — H. Nyquist, "Certain Topics in Telegraph Transmission Theory," *Trans. AIEE* 47 (1928), 617–644 — precursor (≈2B samples per bandwidth B; did not treat continuous-signal sampling as such). ✓
- **codification** — C. E. Shannon, "Communication in the Presence of Noise," *Proc. IRE* 37(1) (1949), 10–21 — sampling theorem as Theorem 1, statement + proof. ✓

- **lineage carried** — Whittaker–Kotelnikov–Shannon (Whittaker 1915 · Kotelnikov 1933 · Shannon 1949; Küpfmüller 1928, Raabe 1939, Someya 1949, Gabor 1946 parallel). "why Nyquist's name attached remains obscure" (Meijering). ✓
