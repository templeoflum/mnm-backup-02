# marginalia — the sound
**f0 · companion to the frame, the world, the style. the visual and the audio are two projections of the same object; neither is canonical. a sounding specimen accompanies this doc.**

## the principle
nothing sounds except as the law sounds it. audio here is not soundtrack — it is the mathematics, projected through a second chart. every piece declares its mapping (what becomes pitch, what becomes time, what becomes loudness), because the mapping is the projection and hiding it would be the lie. where the eye and the ear keep different things, the piece says so: that difference is content, not compromise.

## the materials
- **the pure tone is the chalk of audio** — the simplest honest mark. sine first; soft partials only when the mathematics has timbre in it.
- **the ground is not digital zero** — as the dark is `#060907` and never pure black, the quiet is faint room tone, never dead silence. the field breathes.
- **decay is the persistence** — a tone's tail is the recent past, kept only while tended. modest, dark-room decay; never cathedral drama.
- **one voice per piece**, as one phosphor per piece. two voices only when the mathematics itself has two (primes against zeros; the dyad of a ratio).

## the tuning law
**ratios are sounded as ratios.** just intonation is the native chart — a fifth is 3:2, not 2^(7/12). equal temperament appears only when the mathematics under discussion *is* temperament — and then the ~2-cent lie of the tempered fifth is itself the piece.

## the time
tending-pace. tones settle, drift, return. attacks soft (no clicks — unless the mathematics is an impulse: primes may tick), releases long. one slow change per piece. loops close on whole cycles, seam inaudible. silence is the margin: sparse events in a quiet field, mostly rest.

## the loudness
quiet, and honestly quiet: conversational level, integrated around −18 to −16 LUFS, true peak under −1 dB, no loudness-war compression. mixed mono-first — most of this will be heard on a phone speaker, which cannot render fundamentals much below ~200 Hz; either keep the mathematics where the phone can speak it, or accept the vanishing knowingly and say so in the gloss. stereo as gentle width, never ping-pong.

## the ear's own floors
the ear is an instrument with walls, and the wall belongs to the detector: phase of steady tones is inaudible (Ohm, 1843) · differences under the just-noticeable threshold do not exist for the listener · quiet neighbors are masked by loud ones · no finite listening certifies irrationality — past the floor, all ratios sound alike. these are refusals native to audio; pieces state them the way visual pieces state the crop.

## the no-list
risers, drops, braams, whooshes, sidechain pumping, trailer hits, "wait for it" audio bait, vinyl-crackle and tape-hiss costumes (the scanlines of sound — memory of the medium, never a mask of it), narration-as-hype. spoken voice almost never; the margin speaks in text.

## the sound's own refusal
a mapping can be faithful and still partial — every sonification keeps something and lets something go, and so does every drawing. the two projections certify each other where they agree, and nothing more. neither is the object.
