**BIJECTION ↔ REDUNDANCY — and this welds the two dreams into one.** Panel 12, the base knob, is the naming-grammar bone restated in geometry:

So: **a grammar is exactly what a system grows when its bijection fails.** When cover = 1 you need no grammar (the address IS unique); when cover > 1 you need a grammar to say which of the redundant addresses is legal.
