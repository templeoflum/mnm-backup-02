# pin — formal agreement / contract

stamp: 20260617-150908 UTC · seat: clerk · type: pin (first of its kind) · status: **flagged for review, not formalizing now**

## the idea

Trying to formalize, enforce, or track a **plan structure** is too hard — it resists, and
it drifts. An **agreement** is far easier to conceptualize, frame, and get to the point on;
it holds against drift because both sides carry it. Seen in practice this session: the
smart-param frame only clicked once it was written as **"the agreement"** — the operator
edited *that* section directly and it landed.

So: frame work **as an agreement** (a contract between operator and seat), not as a plan to
be policed. Worth formalizing somehow — a document type, a frame convention, or a fixed
section. Not now.

## the meta-idea (the pin mechanism itself)

Instead of one **parked** area that accumulates, a **`pin`** type: `pin.idea-name`, dropped
wherever it's relevant, and **browsed together on review**. Lightweight idea-flags scattered
across the lattice, gathered by a review pass — not a pile in one place. This file is the
**first pin**, and is itself the flag to decide whether `pin` becomes a real type (alongside
or replacing `parked`).
