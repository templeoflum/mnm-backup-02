## the cycles and gates (the shapes that repeat)

1. **the pipeline, four revolutions.** dream → settle → tasks → frame → build → review. ux ran it first (280 claims, 14:1 compression, the wrongness teaching). warehouse second (2.8:1, corrections between folds). fable third (dreamed the dreamers; 64 floats; 15 tasks). tsr fourth (98 → 47 → 45; 14 tasks; the sift done LIVE in the chair for the first time). each revolution cleaner, each correction earlier.
2. **the clutch grammar.** bets before work · work · sign · water. fifteen clutches at the bench, then the tsr chunks, then the dream's own passes — the same heartbeat at three scales. the bet on record is what makes a miss carvable: no prediction, no honest failure.
3. **the miss constant.** twenty-plus carved misses across two ledgers and one dream, ONE anatomy: a prior substituting for a read. stored where it should have regrown. the practice's deepest law, found in its own failures — never designed.
4. **the corrective shape.** deviation → full stop → real audit → reprojection. enacted once at cycle scale (M12 → f3/f4 → cycle 2) and once at dream scale (over-claiming → f2 sift → memory carved). the correction never adds structure first; it slows the clock and regrows from seed.
5. **the breathing.** commit and merge. vestigial for twelve sessions → first breath june 9 → session-end cadence → after-any-change (operator word, *"I can't see anything otherwise"*) → after EVERY change (M10: batching was a compliance prior). 152 breaths in four days. uncommitted work does not exist for the others.
6. **the deliveries.** crates at the door, each unsigned until counted: 12 compilations → 46 files → ~113 → 54 → 13 (marginalia) → 1 (the atlas). receiving is not endorsing; every crate carried at least one myth or essay riding with the freight.
7. **the gates that held.** the fold ceiling (39). gate-before-chunk. the FULL STOP at M12. the halt at the phantom deletion. the dream that logs wants and never builds them. the gate is discipline, not enforcement — and the record shows it holding under the exact conditions it was written for.
8. **the route itself.** 1 → 2 → (0 or 1). mind dreams, warehouse builds and sorts, verified things are MEANT to land at ground — and none has. every loop so far has closed back into 1. the one lung. the fourth walker's first breath is on the task pile now (T11).
9. **the boundary law.** errors cluster at boundaries — file movement, address changes, installation, handoff. builds at cell depth produce zero errors. the system grew nerve endings exactly where it had been hurt, and nowhere else.
10. **zero unreachable, twice.** in the engine: both clock dials guarded, the beam idles and never stops. in the practice: append-only logs, folds that keep every f0, the origin exact while center and boundary are approached and never reached. the same law found independently in the code and in the keystone — neither copied the other.
