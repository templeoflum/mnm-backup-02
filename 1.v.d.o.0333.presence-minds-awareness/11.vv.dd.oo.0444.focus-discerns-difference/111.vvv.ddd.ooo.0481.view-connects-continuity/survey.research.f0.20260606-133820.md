## Compilation 06 — Making, Craft, and Tacit Knowledge

**What's in it:** Three targets. Classical anatomy of making (Aristotle's technê vs phronesis, poiesis vs praxis, craft analogy in physics/biology). **What stands out:**

Poiesis vs praxis is the sharpest distinction for understanding what the log does. Poiesis has its end in something other than itself — you build a house, and the building is over when the house exists. Praxis has its end in the acting itself — "good action itself is its end." The log entry isn't a product to be consumed later — the act of compressing experience into C/O/D with digit paths IS the cognitive work. "Recording IS seeing" (0122) is literally the praxis claim: the seeing happens IN the recording, not after it.

Polanyi's from-to structure maps directly to digit paths. The proximal term (subsidiary awareness — the clues you rely on but don't focus on) is the address you're reasoning FROM. The distal term (focal awareness — the comprehensive entity you attend TO) is where the path arrives. A digit path like `002 → 022 → 000 → 011` is the from-to structure externalized and made legible. Polanyi warns that focusing directly on the subsidiaries destroys the meaning — that's why the digit path traces reasoning movement, not file locations. The addresses are subsidiaries you think THROUGH, not objects you stare AT.

Dreyfus's five stages track what happened across sessions. Early sessions were novice: following rules mechanically, context-free features, slow and clumsy. That's not a loose metaphor — it's a structural correspondence between independently developed systems for capturing craft knowledge.

Rule-based vs rule-bound (Abbott via Batra et al.) maps to the gate discipline errors. Rule-bound: "rigid mechanical adherence to explicit rules." That's what errors 01-07 were — treating the gate as a bureaucratic checkpoint, either skipping it or performing it mechanically. The gate discipline at 120-122 isn't a rulebook — it's a practice that you learn to use flexibly. The errors are the transition from rule-bound to rule-based.

Suchman's situated action reframes what frames are. Plans don't control action — they're resources for action. The frame intake isn't a script to execute mechanically. Hutchins's distributed cognition: cognitive processes distributed across individuals, artifacts, and environment. The lattice IS a distributed cognitive system. The computational work of organizing, sorting, compressing, routing — it's not done by one mind. It's done by the system of log + manifest + schedule + structure + two participants. "Cultural activity systems have cognitive properties of their own that are different from the cognitive properties of the individuals who participate in them."

## Compilation 12 — Honest Comparison, Distillation, and Staging/Handoff

**What's in it:** Three targets. **What stands out:**

Stanton et al. fidelity-vs-generalization paradox: KD improves generalization but doesn't faithfully reproduce the teacher. "A surprisingly large discrepancy between predictive distributions." The fold has this exact property — compressing entries produces something more useful (patterns visible) but less faithful to any individual entry. Higher fidelity doesn't always yield better generalization. The loss IS the point.

Dataset vs model distillation: the fold is dataset distillation — synthesize compressed representations from multiple entries. But the lattice keeps both: the compression AND the preserved originals (in archived fold files). Safer than either pure approach.

Thresholdout: adaptive analysis destroys validity. The survey is adaptive — each compilation informs how we read the next. Each entry must stand on its own evidence. Don't retrofit later findings to confirm earlier ones.

Ablation discipline (Lipton & Steinhardt): if the lattice helps, WHAT helps? Addressing? C/O/D? Triads? Folds? Need ablations to tell — vary one factor, hold everything else constant.

Curriculum learning: easy first, harder later. The frame's execution order does this. Session itself is a curriculum: settle vocabulary → process research → ground addresses. "A form of continuation method."

Provenance: manifest IS provenance. Fold system IS versioning. Three-point update IS lineage tracking. The lattice implements provenance natively.

FAIR principles: Findable (addresses), Accessible (filesystem/plain text), Interoperable (settled vocabulary), Reusable (fold preservation + manifest documentation). The lattice satisfies FAIR for its own content.

Scaling laws: if "loss" for a knowledge system is time-to-locate or routing error rate, it may follow power laws in entries (D), cells (N), and session time (C). Chinchilla's "undertrained for compute" may map to "underused for structure" — 27 cells, most content at few addresses.
