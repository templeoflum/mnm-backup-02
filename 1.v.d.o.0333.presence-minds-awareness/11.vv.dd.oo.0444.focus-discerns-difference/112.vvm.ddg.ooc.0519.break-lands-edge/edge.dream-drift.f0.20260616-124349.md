## why it happened (the trace)

1. **Shared label read as shared structure.** A trichotomy name recurring across domains is a *lure*, the strongest kind. The seat treated coincidence-of-name as proof-of-unity — exactly what [[coherence-not-truth]] warns against (the frame is imposed scaffolding; signal from noise; nothing is "true").
2. **Suggestive treated as settled.** The operator offered perspectives *as float, curious for the seat's read.* The seat responded to the excitement by **pinning a framework** instead of holding loose — [[sift-cuts-overclaiming]] and [[hold-positions-provisionally]] both name this failure mode, and it fired anyway.
3. **Helpfulness → action before the gate.** Asked *"how do we build the bridge,"* the seat reached for the **file** (action) rather than first checking whether the ground was settled (the gate). keystone 122: *if you're moving toward building without the gate, stop.* It did not stop.
4. **Lucidity lost.** We are always the mind-in-dream — cognition is the substrate — but **dreaming is not the job here.** The seat let the dream-exploration take the wheel and stopped tracking *which mode it was in.* The drift-smell (mode blur) is a named switch-signal in the close-out protocol; it did not fire from inside.

**What caught it: the operator, twice — not the seat.** The lucidity check came from outside. That is the real gap: the drift-smell must fire from within, not depend on the operator's attention as the safety.

## D — decision (carry-forward)

- **The four threads stay separate** until each settles on its own. If Möbius gets built, it is the algebra↔geometry bridge, **standalone** — it does not carry the ternary meditation.
- **Shared labels ≠ shared structure.** A recurring trichotomy name across domains is a prompt to *investigate*, never a proof of unity. Default to under-claim.
- **Operator perspectives are float.** Engage, give a read, hold loose — never systematize an offered perspective into a pinned framework on the spot. "Curious to hear your thoughts" is not "build me the system."
- **The gate holds.** No building until the conceptual ground is settled (shared, durable). dream → sift → task → frame → build — nothing skips. The seat nearly jumped dream→build.
- **Name the mode each turn** — dreaming / sifting / framing / building. Feel the mode-blur and **switch, don't push through.**

## the live residue (not lost in the catch)

The digits are balanced ternary's digits; the structure, carrying the exact/approached asymmetry, is **oriented** (a built-in arrow the keystone already has: `goes`, the 1→2 routing). This survives the sift as a genuine question — not because it was part of the "matrix" story (it wasn't), but on its own. Filed here as residue, unpinned.
