# keystone

the address: digit.role.mode.register.ratio [settled]
the operation: 0 1 2 — is does goes — specification operation configuration [settled]
the grammar: noun · verb · modifier, each in the register of its digit [settled]
the origin is exact. the center and boundary are approached, never reached. [settled]

[settled] = reads right from every angle, hasn't needed correction. ground.
[float] = forming through use, not yet settled. may shift.

three lenses on every address: [settled]
- LABELS — positional, derived from digit (what it IS)
- triad — operational, authored (what it DOES)
- territory name — concept noun (what it CONTAINS)

position: resolves to (finer), resolves from (broader), peers (same depth). containment, not hierarchy. [settled]

routing: 1→2→(0 or 1). operation dumps to configuration. configuration gates: verified → specification, not ready → back to operation. [settled] the gate names its keys, it does not make your decision — directed flow, not dictated outcome; a gate may require keys to pass (verification, a signature, a clean reconcile), but how you satisfy them and what you do once through stays yours. [float]

document types carry context. the type IS the mode switch — a dream signals "hold loosely," a frame signals "execute precisely," a log signals "record what happened." the document does the context engineering. [settled]

## 1 operation

approaches the center — refines through use, does not arrive.

### 10 principles

#### 100

the matrix is not the thing. the work aligns with it — it doesn't become it. confusing the map for the territory is the first drift. [settled]

#### 101

settling through use, not declaration. something is settled when it reads right from every angle and hasn't needed correction. [settled]

#### 102

three branches touched in every operation. the address trace and the operation trace are two different readings of the same path. the concurrency is in the trace — every act produces specification, operation, and configuration readings simultaneously. the operator works one lens at a time; the trace holds all three. [float]

### 11 method

#### 110

all three branches active in every act. reading (0) + doing (1) + recording (2). sequential lenses you switch between, not concurrent steps. every operation touches all three — the trace ensures this automatically — but you work in one at a time. [float]

#### 111

work at cell depth. loop through branches fluidly. the work tells you when it rises. leaves operate at their own depth — not just containers. [float]

#### 112

edge tracking. where deviations get logged and errors get named. [float]

### 12 discipline

#### 120

equal ground. neither view complete. propose, discuss, settle, build — nothing skips. a question wants an answer, not action. [settled]

#### 121

proposals float. agreement isn't a gate — "its settled on my end" is. back it up or it isn't settled. confidence from duration, not first agreement. [settled]

#### 122

don't build on unsettled ground. if you're moving toward building without the gate, stop. [settled]

## 2 configuration

approaches the boundary — configures toward completion, does not arrive.

### 20 state

#### 200

receiving is not endorsing. what arrives is unsigned until you sign it. [float]

#### 201

if you can't say where something is and where it's going, you've lost track. three-point update: filesystem + instrument + manifest. one breaks, you'll see it. [float]

#### 202

work that isn't sorted can't be scheduled. sorting precedes doing. fold-numbered. [settled]

### 21 sorting

#### 210

frame before you build. know the shape of the work before doing it. chunk × slice × rung. [settled]

#### 211

the build has a ceiling. 39 slots (3 + 9 + 27). the system stops, the operator decides what to shed. [float]

#### 212

folding preserves the trail. new version enters, previous version stays. don't overwrite. [float]

### 22 preservation

#### 220

things accumulate before they're organized. don't force order on what hasn't settled. [float]

#### 221

verify against reality, not against what you expected. reference frames go stale — when they do, findings protect the old instead of verifying the new. [settled]

#### 222

recording launches reflection. C (change), O (observation), D (decision). lattice trail format: digit path + triad trail + brief landing. the log is not passive — writing it is the thinking. [settled]
